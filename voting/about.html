ℹ️ About the Voting Center

What this is, how we keep it nonpartisan, and how information gets verified.

What this is

The No Spin Media Voting Center is a nonpartisan, standalone section of No Spin Media focused strictly on voter information and education — how registration works, how to vote, and where to find official government election resources. It is built to help you navigate the voting process, not to influence how you vote.

What we will never do

  • We do not recommend candidates, parties, or positions.
  • We do not tell you how to vote on any race or ballot question.
  • We do not collect your voter registration information, home address, birth date, party affiliation, or ID details.

Every actionable voter-service link on this site is intended to point to an official government election source — your state or county election authority, or a federal resource like Vote.gov, the EAC, or FVAP.gov — not a third party.

How we verify information

Voting rules — deadlines, ID requirements, polling procedures — can and do change, and they vary by state. Each state-specific fact on this site carries a Verified, In Review, Needs Review, or Placeholder status and a "last verified" date, so you can see at a glance whether it's been checked against an official source recently, is actively being confirmed, has been flagged for a second look, or is still placeholder content awaiting research.

Current phase: we're populating states one at a time, each researched directly from that state's official election authority (not summarized from other websites) before anything is published. New Hampshire is the first completed, verified state — the rest currently show placeholder content, clearly labeled as such, while we work through them.

Reporting a correction

If you notice information here that looks outdated or incorrect, please let No Spin Media know so we can verify it against an official source and correct it.

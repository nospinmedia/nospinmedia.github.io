❓ Common Voting Questions

Do I need photo ID to vote?

It depends on your state. Some require photo ID, some accept alternatives, some require none.

I recently moved. What do I do?

You may need to update your registration address — the process depends on whether you moved within your state.

What's a provisional ballot?

A backup ballot used when there's a question about your eligibility, counted after it's confirmed.

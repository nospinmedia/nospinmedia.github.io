📍 Find My Polling Place

Your state or county election office publishes the official polling-place locator — here's how it works.

No Spin Media does not ask for or store your home address. To protect your privacy, we send you directly to your state's official polling-place lookup tool, where you enter your address yourself.

How polling place lookup works

Enter your registered voter address into your state or county's official tool, and it will show your assigned polling location for the current election, along with its hours. Polling locations can change between elections, so it's worth checking shortly before each one — even if you've voted at the same place before.

What if I show up at the wrong location?

Poll workers can usually tell you the correct location, or in some states/counties you may be able to cast a provisional ballot, or use a vote center that accepts any registered voter from the county. If you run into trouble at your polling place, see Voting Problems.

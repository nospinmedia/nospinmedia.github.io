AI Transparency

News moves quickly, and major stories develop across many sources at once. That can make it hard to separate confirmed facts from analysis, opinion, or incomplete reporting. We created 🧭 No Spin Media to give readers a clear, structured view of the news: what is known, what remains uncertain, and the context needed to draw their own conclusions.

What Artificial Intelligence Does

AI is a tool in our editorial process. It helps us monitor news sources, research and cross-check facts across multiple sources, draft articles in a consistent and neutral style, and apply our editorial rules on accuracy, attribution, and neutral language.

What People Do

Our editors set No Spin Media's editorial priorities and influence what we cover: they choose and review the sources we monitor, set the rules our AI-assisted process follows, monitor what is published, and handle corrections. We remain responsible for everything we publish.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't reproduce other publishers' articles as our own. Our articles are independently written using researched and attributed information from multiple sources.
  • We don't present AI-generated images as photographs. Illustrations created with AI are labeled "AI-generated illustration."
  • We don't publish opinion pieces or tell readers what to think.

The Limits

No process, human or automated, is perfect. When an error gets through, we correct it openly. See our Corrections Policy.

Our Role

🧭 No Spin Media depends on the work of reporters, researchers, and news organizations that gather and publish information around the world, and on official records and public data. Our role is to bring that information together accurately, clearly, and without spin.

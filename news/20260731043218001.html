Iran Claims Drone And Missile Strike On U.S. Bases In Kuwait

CENTCOM Says Defenses Limited Damage As Gulf Tensions Escalate

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished July 31, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Iran's military and the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said they launched coordinated drone and missile attacks against U.S. military facilities in Kuwait on July 31, 2026, describing the operation as retaliation for recent American airstrikes on Iranian targets. Iranian state media identified Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base and Ali Al Salem Air Base among the locations it claimed were targeted, while U.S. officials disputed Tehran's assessment of the operation's effectiveness.

According to U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM), American and allied air defense systems engaged multiple incoming drones and missiles, with commanders stating that operational capabilities at affected installations remained intact. CENTCOM said it was continuing to assess the attack but rejected Iranian claims that significant U.S. military assets had been destroyed. Kuwait's Ministry of Defense condemned the strikes as a violation of the country's sovereignty and confirmed that its air defense forces responded to the incoming threats while coordinating with allied forces.

The exchange followed a new round of U.S. strikes against Iranian military targets after Washington said it was responding to attacks on American personnel and regional security interests. Iranian officials characterized the Kuwait operation as a direct response to those strikes and warned that additional military action could follow if U.S. operations continue. The latest exchange further expands a conflict that has already affected Jordan, Iraq, the Persian Gulf, and shipping routes connected to the Strait of Hormuz.

Regional governments are increasingly concerned that continued attacks could draw additional Gulf states into the conflict and threaten military logistics, commercial shipping and global energy markets. Diplomats have continued urging restraint, but no new ceasefire initiative had been announced as military operations continued across multiple fronts.

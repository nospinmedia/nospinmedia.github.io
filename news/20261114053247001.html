Trump, Xi and Putin to hold first three-way meeting in China

Nuclear arms reductions will be central to unprecedented talks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 14, 2026 at 10:15 AM ET

President Trump will meet jointly with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in China next week for the first three-way meeting among the leaders of the three countries, with nuclear arms reductions expected to be a central topic.

The White House said the meeting will take place Thursday, November 19, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen, China. Trump is scheduled to meet separately with Xi before the trilateral session, while U.S. officials said no separate Trump-Putin meeting is planned.

White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales said Trump intends to discuss reducing nuclear weapons worldwide because he considers their proliferation dangerous. A senior administration official cautioned that the United States does not expect an agreement at the meeting, describing it instead as the beginning of a potential arms-control discussion involving all three countries.

The talks follow the expiration earlier this year of New START, the last treaty limiting deployed strategic nuclear weapons held by the United States and Russia. Trump has advocated replacing the bilateral framework with one that also includes China. Beijing has resisted joining trilateral nuclear negotiations, arguing that the substantially larger U.S. and Russian arsenals should be reduced first.

The three leaders are also expected to discuss the conflict involving Iran, according to U.S. officials. Russia's war in Ukraine, however, is not expected to be part of the trilateral agenda because U.S. negotiations with Moscow and Kyiv are proceeding separately.

Trump will leave Washington Monday for a four-day Asia trip that includes a stop in the Philippines before traveling to China. The White House said Elon Musk will accompany Trump on portions of the trip but will not participate in the Trump-Xi-Putin meeting.

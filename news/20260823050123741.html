China Orders Tesla Recall of Nearly 3 Million EVs

Emergency Door Releases Raise Post-Crash Safety Concerns

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 23, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Tesla is recalling about 2.98 million electric vehicles in China after regulators determined that emergency mechanical door releases could be difficult to identify or operate after a serious crash or electrical failure. China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) said the action covers China-made and imported Model 3, Model Y, Model S, and Model X vehicles, making Tesla the largest participant in a broader recall involving more than 4 million vehicles from several automakers.

The safety concern centers on electronically operated and concealed door systems. During a severe collision, loss of low-voltage electrical power can disable normal electronic opening mechanisms, forcing occupants or rescuers to rely on mechanical emergency releases. Regulators said those releases may not be sufficiently obvious in some vehicles, potentially delaying escape or access by emergency personnel. Tesla's remedies include software updates and warning labels identifying emergency release locations. Some software changes are designed to lower vehicle windows after a collision, providing another potential path for occupants or rescuers if doors cannot be opened normally.

The Tesla action is part of a much larger Chinese regulatory response affecting roughly 4.3 million vehicles from nine automakers. Other manufacturers involved include Xiaomi, Leapmotor, Geely, and XPENG, with regulators citing similar concerns about emergency door accessibility. China has increased scrutiny of flush and electronically controlled handles following crashes in which occupants were reportedly trapped inside vehicles. The government has announced new rules beginning in 2027 that will prohibit fully concealed exterior door handles and require accessible mechanical opening systems.

The recall reflects a wider safety debate as automakers increasingly replace traditional mechanical controls with electronic systems. Flush handles can improve aerodynamics and vehicle styling, but regulators are focusing on whether those designs remain usable when electrical systems fail during emergencies. Similar door-access concerns have also drawn scrutiny from safety authorities outside China, although no equivalent Tesla recall covering nearly three million vehicles has been announced in the United States.

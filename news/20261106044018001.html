Colorado police officer killed, 3 wounded in shootout

Suspect also killed after gunfire erupts near RTD bus

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 6, 2026 at 5:16 PM ET

One Lakewood Police Department officer was killed and three others were wounded Thursday night after a gunman opened fire on police near a public transit bus in Lakewood, Colorado, authorities said. The suspect was also killed during the exchange of gunfire, and a civilian was injured.

Police said the confrontation unfolded after officers responded to a report involving a suspicious person in the area of West Colfax Avenue and Wadsworth Boulevard, west of Denver. The suspect was near or boarding a Regional Transportation District bus when officers encountered him.

Authorities said the suspect opened fire, striking four Lakewood officers. Police returned fire and fatally shot the suspect. A civilian was also injured during the incident, although authorities had not immediately detailed exactly how that person was hurt.

The four wounded officers were taken for emergency medical treatment. One officer later died from the injuries, while the three surviving officers were hospitalized. Police initially said the surviving officers were expected to recover. The civilian was also taken for treatment.

The shooting prompted a large law enforcement response and temporarily disrupted transit and traffic around the scene. The Regional Transportation District, which operates buses and rail service throughout the Denver metropolitan area, said it was cooperating with investigators.

Authorities had not immediately released the name of the officer who was killed or publicly identified the suspect. Investigators also had not announced a motive or explained what led to the initial encounter before the gunfire began.

The circumstances surrounding the officers' use of force and the suspect's actions remain under investigation. Additional details, including body-camera footage and a more complete timeline, are expected as investigators review evidence and interview witnesses.

Navone Stuns Djokovic in U.S. Open Opener

Serb's Earliest Grand Slam Exit in 20 Years

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 31, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

Mariano Navone of Argentina pulled off one of the biggest upsets of the 2026 U.S. Open, defeating four-time champion Novak Djokovic 7-6 (5), 5-7, 4-6, 6-2, 6-1 in a 4-hour, 36-minute first-round match at Arthur Ashe Stadium. The victory ended Djokovic's tournament before the second round for the first time in his 20 appearances at Flushing Meadows and marked his earliest exit from any Grand Slam event since the 2006 Australian Open.

The 39-year-old Serbian, seeded No. 4, battled significant physical problems throughout the match. Djokovic was visibly ill during the contest, vomiting courtside several times while also dealing with cramping and receiving medical treatment for back and shoulder discomfort. After remaining competitive through four sets, he faded dramatically late in the match, losing 11 of the final 12 games. Following the loss, Djokovic acknowledged that health issues have affected much of his 2026 season.

For Navone, ranked No. 49 in the world, the victory is the biggest of his professional career. The 25-year-old entered the tournament without a victory in four previous five-set matches and had relatively limited success on hard courts compared with his results on clay. He remained composed throughout the match, capitalizing on 70 unforced errors by Djokovic while consistently extending rallies and forcing the veteran into difficult physical exchanges.

The upset dramatically reshapes the men's draw by removing one of the tournament favorites in the opening round. Djokovic entered the tournament as a 24-time Grand Slam singles champion and had won 78 consecutive Grand Slam opening-round matches before Sunday's defeat. Navone advances to the second round with newfound momentum, while questions are likely to continue surrounding Djokovic's health and future schedule as the tennis season progresses.

Serena Williams and Carlos Alcaraz Receive U.S. Open Wild Card

Former Champion Returns for Star-Studded Mixed Doubles Event

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 23, 2026 at 5:16 PM ET

The United States Tennis Association (USTA) has awarded a mixed doubles wild-card entry to Serena Williams and Carlos Alcaraz, creating one of the most anticipated pairings of the 2026 U.S. Open. The announcement marks Williams' return to Flushing Meadows for the first time since stepping away from professional tennis following the 2022 U.S. Open. Alcaraz, the defending men's singles champion, will use the event as part of his return to competition after recovering from a right wrist injury that sidelined him for several months earlier this year.

The revamped mixed doubles championship features a 16-team field competing for a $1 million winner's prize at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center. The tournament is scheduled for August 25-26, with matches played on Arthur Ashe Stadium and Louis Armstrong Stadium before the start of the singles draws. Williams, a 23-time Grand Slam singles champion, previously won the 1998 U.S. Open mixed doubles title with Max Mirnyi and owns nine championship trophies at Flushing Meadows across singles, doubles, and mixed doubles competition.

The field includes several of tennis' biggest stars. Aryna Sabalenka is set to partner with Novak Djokovic, while Iga Swiatek teams with Casper Ruud and Americans Jessica Pegula and Taylor Fritz will also compete. An eight-team qualifying event will determine the final two places in the main draw. The USTA also announced that Sofia Kenin received the final women's singles wild card, confirming Williams will compete only in mixed doubles during this year's tournament.

Tournament organizers say the redesigned mixed doubles format is intended to attract more of the sport's top singles players and generate greater fan interest by featuring marquee partnerships on the main show courts. The pairing of Williams and Alcaraz is expected to be one of the tournament's biggest attractions as one of tennis' most accomplished champions joins forces with one of the game's current stars.

Kentucky state trooper killed during I-65 traffic stop

Suspect captured nearly 100 miles away after highway search

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 29, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

Kentucky State Police Trooper Hayden Phillips, 30, was fatally shot Monday during a traffic stop on Interstate 65 in Warren County, triggering a large search that ended with a suspect being captured nearly 100 miles away in Louisville. Phillips was a three-year veteran of the state police and leaves behind a wife and three children.

Phillips stopped a vehicle while patrolling I-65 on the afternoon of September 28. Kentucky State Police Commissioner Phillip Burnett Jr. said Phillips was shot by an occupant of the vehicle during the stop. The vehicle then fled north, prompting authorities to issue a Blue Alert warning that the suspect was considered armed and dangerous.

Police circulated information about a bluish-gray 2025 Honda Civic with an Alabama temporary tag that was connected to the shooting. Local, state and federal law enforcement agencies joined the search as officers monitored the interstate corridor north of Bowling Green. The suspect was eventually located approximately 94 miles away in Jefferson County, where Louisville police took him into custody.

Kentucky State Police identified the suspect as Emonnie Branch, 51, of Portland, Tennessee. Authorities said Branch is being charged with murder of a first responder or peace officer and will be returned to Warren County to face the charge. Officials had not publicly detailed why Phillips initially stopped the vehicle or precisely how the shooting unfolded as the investigation continued.

Phillips was assigned to Kentucky State Police Post 3 in Bowling Green and graduated with Cadet Class 103 in August 2023. Before joining the state police, he served with the Glasgow Police Department. Gov. Andy Beshear described Phillips as a hero who dedicated himself to protecting Kentucky and asked residents to support his family.

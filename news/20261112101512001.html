Israeli strike on Gaza hospital kills 6, including surgeon

Israel says Hamas commander was targeted as hospital wing suffers heavy damage

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 12, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

An Israeli airstrike hit the surgical wing of Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis in southern Gaza early Thursday, killing six people, including a general surgeon, and wounding at least a dozen others, according to Gaza health authorities. Reuters reported 20 people were wounded, citing Gaza's civil defense agency.

The strike hit the second floor of a building within the hospital complex and caused extensive damage to rooms, corridors, beds and medical equipment. The surgeon killed was identified as Rizk Abu Shaira. Nasser Hospital is among the relatively few medical facilities still operating in Gaza after years of fighting severely damaged the territory's health system.

The Israel Defense Forces and Shin Bet said the operation targeted Muhammad Abu Ismail, whom they identified as the current commander of Hamas's Khan Younis Brigade, along with other Hamas members. Israeli authorities said the results of the strike were still being reviewed and Abu Ismail's fate had not been confirmed.

Israel said Hamas had established a presence at the hospital and used the complex for military activities, including command operations, weapons storage, surveillance and sheltering operatives. The Israeli military said it issued advance warnings before the strike and used precision munitions and aerial surveillance in an effort to reduce civilian harm. The Associated Press reported that Israel did not provide evidence supporting its claims about Hamas's activities at the hospital, while Reuters said it could not independently verify them.

Hamas condemned the attack and described it as a war crime. Hospitals and medical personnel receive special protection under international humanitarian law, although that protection can be affected when medical facilities are used for acts harmful to an enemy outside their humanitarian function.

The attack comes despite a U.S.-backed ceasefire that began in October 2025. Gaza health officials say more than 1,600 Palestinians have been killed by Israeli strikes and gunfire since the ceasefire began, while Israel says Hamas has been rebuilding its forces and preparing attacks.

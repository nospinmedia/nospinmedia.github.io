Liquid Network halts after $320 million Bitcoin withdrawal

Purported white hats drain roughly 95% of reported BTC reserves

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 7, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

Liquid Network halted new transactions after purported white-hat hackers withdrew roughly 4,000 Bitcoin worth about $320 million from the federation wallet backing the Bitcoin sidechain. The withdrawal represented about 95% of the approximately 4,200 BTC held in the wallet before the September 6 security incident, leaving the network and its users facing uncertainty while technology provider Blockstream attempts to contact those responsible.

Liquid said the Bitcoin was withdrawn through SideSwap, a settlement service authorized to process transfers from Liquid back to the Bitcoin network. The transaction used SideSwap's Peg-out Authorization Key (PAK), but Liquid said that key was not compromised and that it had found no evidence other cryptographic keys were compromised. SideSwap said a customer sent 4,000 L-BTC to its peg-out service and the federation subsequently transferred approximately 3,996 BTC to the customer's Bitcoin address. Liquid has not publicly established the complete root cause of the vulnerability.

Following the incident, Liquid disabled bridge nodes used to submit new transactions and asked cryptocurrency exchanges to suspend deposits and withdrawals of Liquid Bitcoin (L-BTC). The network said other assets issued on Liquid, including Tether (USDT) and DePix, were not affected. Liquid is a Bitcoin sidechain designed to provide faster and more confidential transactions while allowing users to move value between Bitcoin and L-BTC through a two-way peg.

The unidentified party left an on-chain message describing themselves as white hats and asking Blockstream to contact them. Blockstream responded through signed on-chain communications, but the claim that those responsible are ethical hackers remains unverified, and Liquid had not confirmed that the Bitcoin would be returned. The incident therefore leaves most of the network's reported Bitcoin reserves outside the federation wallet while efforts to resolve the breach continue.

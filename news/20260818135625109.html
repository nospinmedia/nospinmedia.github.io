House Ethics Opens Probe Into Rep. Jimmy Gomez

Panel cites alleged inappropriate contact with staffer; Gomez says conduct was consensual

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 18, 2026 at 12:31 PM ET

The House Ethics Committee said on August 17, 2026 that it has opened a formal investigation into Rep. Jimmy Gomez (D-Calif.) over allegations of sexual misconduct, including “inappropriate sexual contact with a House staffer.” The panel emphasized that publicly disclosing the review does not, by itself, indicate a rule violation or prejudge the outcome.

In a statement, Gomez acknowledged “personal mistakes outside my marriage,” said his conduct was consensual and did not violate federal law or House rules, and pledged to cooperate fully with investigators. Gomez represents California’s 34th Congressional District, which covers central and northeast Los Angeles.

The House Ethics Committee can investigate potential violations of House standards and, depending on its findings, may recommend disciplinary action to the full chamber. The committee has not announced a timetable for completing its investigation. House ethics inquiries often take months to resolve and may end with dismissal, recommendations for disciplinary action, or referral to other authorities if warranted.

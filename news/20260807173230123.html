Coordinated Houthi Strikes Kill At Least 30 Yemeni Soldiers

Najran shelling injures 11 civilians, including a 4-year-old

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 7, 2026 at 5:16 PM ET

Iran-backed Houthi forces launched coordinated ballistic missile and drone strikes on August 6, 2026, targeting Yemeni government-aligned military positions in Marib and Hadramawt. Yemeni officials said at least 30 soldiers were killed and more than 50 wounded, making it one of the deadliest attacks since a U.N.-brokered truce sharply reduced large-scale fighting in 2022.

Houthi military spokesman Brig. Gen. Yahya Saree claimed responsibility, saying the operation targeted Saudi-backed forces using missiles and drones. Yemen's Defense Ministry condemned the attacks and pledged a response, while the U.S. Mission to Yemen denounced the strikes and expressed condolences to the victims' families. Military and medical sources said one missile struck troops during a morning formation in the Al-Ruwaik area of Marib, contributing to the high casualty toll.

The violence later spread across the border into Saudi Arabia, where shelling in Najran injured 11 civilians, including seven Saudi citizens, one Yemeni, two Egyptians, one Pakistani, and a 4-year-old child, according to coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Malki. Saudi officials accused the Houthis of indiscriminately targeting civilian areas.

Authorities cautioned that casualty figures from the attacks remain preliminary. While Yemeni officials confirmed at least 30 military deaths, other reports citing military sources placed the toll higher as recovery and identification efforts continued. The attacks have renewed concerns that the conflict could intensify after several years of relatively lower levels of fighting.

Israel intensifies targeted killings of Hamas leaders in Gaza

U.S. presses Netanyahu to move forward with ceasefire commitments

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 27, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

Israel has intensified targeted killings of Hamas officials in Gaza, eliminating three senior figures in three days as the Trump administration presses Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to advance commitments under the U.S.-backed ceasefire plan.

An Israeli strike Thursday in Deir al-Balah killed Khaled Abu Nada, a Hamas political bureau member and deputy education minister, along with his wife. Israel said Abu Nada also managed financial networks supporting Hamas' military capabilities.

His death followed Israel's killings of two senior Hamas security officials: northern Gaza internal security chief Muhammad Majed Abu al-Ouf on Wednesday and internal security director-general Naeem Abu Nahel on Tuesday. Netanyahu and Defense Minister Israel Katz said Israeli forces have killed roughly 80 Hamas operatives during the past month.

Katz also threatened a renewed large-scale Israeli military offensive in Gaza if Hamas refuses to disarm. Hamas senior leader Khalil al-Hayya, meanwhile, confirmed the group is consulting other Palestinian factions over how to respond to the targeted killings. Hamas says the attacks violate the October 2025 ceasefire.

The escalation comes as U.S. envoy Jared Kushner presses Netanyahu to move forward with the Gaza framework being coordinated through President Trump's Board of Peace. Kushner urged Israel to move away from what he called a "constant wartime mindset" and toward economic diplomacy as Washington seeks progress on the next stages of the agreement.

The U.S.-backed plan calls for Hamas to disarm, Gaza to be demilitarized and governance transferred to a Palestinian technocratic administration. It also envisions an international stabilization force, a future Palestinian police force and reconstruction efforts.

Netanyahu maintains that further progress depends on Hamas disarming. Hamas has resisted unilateral disarmament without a staged Israeli withdrawal, leaving one of the central elements of the agreement unresolved. The diplomatic push is also unfolding as Netanyahu navigates Israeli coalition negotiations following recent elections.

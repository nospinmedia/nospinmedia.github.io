Trump and Mamdani set to meet Monday at Gracie Mansion

President and NYC mayor will discuss issues affecting New Yorkers

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 21, 2026 at 1:15 PM ET

President Trump is scheduled to meet New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani on Monday, September 21, at Gracie Mansion, the mayor's official residence, as Trump visits New York for the United Nations General Assembly.

Mamdani communications director Anna Bahr confirmed the meeting and said the two leaders will discuss issues affecting New York City and New Yorkers. The White House also confirmed the meeting but did not provide additional details about the agenda. The talks will bring together the president and mayor after several previous meetings and months of political disagreements between the two.

Trump and Mamdani first met at the White House after Mamdani won the November 2025 mayoral election. Despite sharp exchanges during the campaign, that meeting was notably cordial. They met again in February 2026, when Mamdani presented Trump with a mock New York Daily News front page reading "Trump to City: Let's Build," a reference to the famous 1975 headline about President Gerald Ford and New York City's fiscal crisis. Mamdani's aides said the gesture was intended to illustrate the potential public response to new federal investment in city housing.

Monday's meeting comes as Trump joins other world leaders in New York for the annual high-level UN gathering. No specific agreement or policy announcement has been disclosed ahead of the meeting, and neither the White House nor Mamdani's office has provided a detailed list of subjects beyond saying they will address matters affecting the city and its residents.

The meeting provides another opportunity for the federal government and the nation's largest city to discuss areas where their interests intersect despite continuing political differences between their administrations.

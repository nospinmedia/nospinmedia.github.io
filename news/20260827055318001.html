SpaceX plans $100B Starship complex on Louisiana coast

Massive new campus targets thousands of future launches

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 27, 2026 at 12:48 PM ET

SpaceX plans to invest $100 billion in a massive new Starship launch complex on Louisiana's Gulf Coast, creating what the company says will eventually become its largest launch facility. The project, announced August 25 with Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry and SpaceX President and COO Gwynne Shotwell, will occupy approximately 125,000 acres near Pecan Island in Vermilion Parish on property formerly owned by ExxonMobil.

The planned Starbase Louisiana campus is expected to include five launch complexes with two launch pads each, along with vehicle-processing facilities, propellant production and storage, power generation, deep-water shipping infrastructure and employee housing. SpaceX says construction will begin in 2027 and is targeting its first Starship launch from Louisiana in 2029. Louisiana Economic Development gives a slightly more conservative schedule, saying construction should begin by the end of 2027 with initial operations expected in 2030.

State officials say the project will create about 3,000 direct jobs over the next decade with an average annual salary of $92,600, plus more than 8,100 indirect jobs across the region. At peak construction, Louisiana estimates the development could support more than 34,000 construction jobs. Under the project's local tax agreement, SpaceX will provide a $20 million upfront payment and at least $25 million annually for 25 years to local taxing bodies, totaling more than $645 million in direct local payments. The company will also make a $25 million charitable contribution to the Community Foundation of Acadiana.

SpaceX ultimately envisions the Louisiana site supporting thousands of Starship flights annually, though achieving that cadence will depend on engineering progress and regulatory approvals. Environmental groups have raised concerns about development of sensitive coastal habitat, while SpaceX has pledged shoreline restoration, habitat protection and continued environmental monitoring around the property.

Nor'easter floods coastal areas as storm strengthens off East Coast

Wind, heavy rain and dangerous surf spread north toward New England

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 25, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

A strengthening nor'easter is battering parts of the Mid-Atlantic coast with flooding, rough surf and strong winds as the storm moves north toward New York and New England, where its most significant impacts are expected Friday night and this weekend.

The storm was centered several hundred miles off the Mid-Atlantic coast Thursday and is forecast to strengthen as it tracks north. Coastal flooding and erosion were already affecting areas from North Carolina and Virginia through New Jersey, while the National Weather Service issued a combination of coastal flood warnings, advisories, wind alerts and high-surf advisories along portions of the coastline.

In parts of New Jersey, the National Weather Service warned of 1 to 2 feet of water above ground level in vulnerable low-lying areas near shorelines and tidal waterways. Flooding could make some coastal and bayside roads impassable, and repeated high-tide cycles are expected to keep the flooding threat in place into the weekend. High surf and dangerous marine conditions are also expected along exposed beaches.

The storm's focus shifts farther north beginning late Friday. The New York City metropolitan area, Long Island, Rhode Island and eastern Massachusetts face increasing risks from wind-driven rain, coastal flooding and erosion as the system strengthens. Forecasters say the greatest impacts in Massachusetts are expected near the immediate coastline, while communities farther inland should generally experience less severe conditions.

The unusual early-fall storm could produce particularly heavy rainfall in parts of eastern Massachusetts and Rhode Island. Coastal communities also face repeated rounds of high water as persistent onshore winds push water toward shore during successive high tides.

Forecasters caution that the exact track and strength of the offshore low will determine how severe conditions become in New England. Residents along the coast are being urged to monitor updated forecasts and avoid driving through flooded roads as conditions deteriorate.

Police Identify Twin Falls In-N-Out Shooter as 24-Year-Old Chad Williams

Off-Duty Trooper and Armed Citizen Drew Fire Before Gunman’s Suicide

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 3, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Police in Twin Falls, Idaho, on August 2, 2026, identified the In‑N‑Out Burger gunman as 24‑year‑old Chad Williams, who died from a self‑inflicted gunshot wound after the attack. Chief Matthew Hicks said an off‑duty Idaho State Police trooper and an armed civilian fired at the suspect and diverted him away from the crowded restaurant, a response officials credited with preventing further casualties.

- 3 victims were killed; 7 others were injured - The suspect, Chad Williams (24), also died by suicide - One of the deceased was an In‑N‑Out Burger employee, according to owner Lynsi Snyder - As of August 2, two patients had been released, three were stable, and two were in critical condition

The shooting began Saturday afternoon, August 1, 2026, at the newly opened In‑N‑Out Burger in a busy Twin Falls shopping area, with witnesses reporting gunfire in and around the drive‑thru. Officials said the attack occurred “around 2 p.m.” and emphasized that Williams acted alone. The restaurant had opened about a week earlier, on July 24, 2026, drawing large crowds during its first days of operation.

Chief Hicks publicly commended the rapid actions of the off‑duty trooper and the armed bystander, saying their intervention helped steer the gunman away from densely populated areas of the restaurant. Investigators are still working to determine a motive. The FBI asked the public to submit photos and videos to aid the investigation, and officials urged potential witnesses to contact law enforcement.

Authorities also warned about misinformation circulating online, noting that at least one person named on social media had “no involvement in this incident whatsoever.” In a separate statement, Snyder said the company is mourning the loss of its associate and supporting affected families and employees.

With the suspect identified and the preliminary timeline established, investigators continue to examine how the confrontation unfolded and what factors led up to the attack.

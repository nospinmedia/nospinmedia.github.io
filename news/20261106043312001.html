Trump weighs new strikes on Iran after security meeting

CENTCOM prepares as nuclear proposal offers diplomatic path

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 6, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

President Trump is weighing whether to resume major U.S. military strikes against Iran after meeting with his national security advisers on Thursday, November 5, while diplomatic efforts continue to prevent another escalation in the months-long conflict.

Trump said after the meeting that a decision would come soon. Asked whether the United States should go further with strikes to ensure Iran cannot rebuild its nuclear program, Trump said he would prefer not to inflict additional destruction but indicated further military action remains an option. U.S. officials told Axios that U.S. Central Command is completing preparations for a possible return to major combat operations but has not received a new order from Washington.

The military preparations are occurring alongside an active diplomatic effort. Iran sent the United States a new nuclear proposal through Qatar, according to Reuters, while Iranian officials have also been communicating with regional mediators. Tehran has tied renewed negotiations to a series of demands that include sanctions relief, access to frozen Iranian assets and war reparations. Washington maintains that Iran's nuclear program must be part of any negotiations.

Trump has been considering several possible approaches, including reaching a negotiated agreement, maintaining economic pressure or resuming military operations. The decision has taken on greater significance following the November 3 midterm elections, in which Republicans lost control of Congress. Despite the shift in congressional power, Trump retains substantial presidential authority over U.S. military and foreign policy decisions.

Tensions remain high in the region. Tanker incidents have continued in the Strait of Hormuz, and mediators including Qatar are attempting to bridge differences over the waterway, sanctions and Iran's nuclear activities. No peace agreement has been reached, and Trump has not announced a final decision on renewed strikes.

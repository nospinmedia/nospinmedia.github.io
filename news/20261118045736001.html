Iran expands attacks to U.S. base in Kuwait

11 injured and one missing after oil vessel struck

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 18, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

The military confrontation between the United States and Iran expanded across the Persian Gulf as Iran targeted a U.S. air base in Kuwait, additional attacks were directed toward Bahrain, and a strike on a commercial oil vessel in the Strait of Hormuz left 11 crew members injured and one missing.

Iran's army identified the Kuwaiti target as Ali Al Salem Air Base, saying its drones targeted U.S. Patriot air defense systems as well as drone and missile launch platforms. Kuwait's military said it intercepted incoming drones without immediate reports of damage, while Kuwait's Foreign Ministry condemned the attack.

The attacks followed a direct exchange between the United States and Iran. Iran launched its third round of strikes against U.S. targets in Bahrain in five days before American forces struck an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) missile launch site on Qeshm Island. Iranian officials acknowledged the U.S. strike while saying it caused no damage to residential, commercial or vital infrastructure.

The confrontation has also spilled into commercial shipping. ADNOC Logistics & Services (ADNOC L&S) said one of its vessels was struck Tuesday evening, November 17, in the Strait of Hormuz. The company said 11 crew members were injured, one remained missing and the remaining crew were safely evacuated. Search-and-rescue operations followed the attack.

Separately, the Saudi-led coalition intercepted a Houthi cruise missile launched toward Riyadh, adding to the wider regional military activity.

The United States is also increasing its forces in the region. The USS Theodore Roosevelt carrier strike group is being deployed to join the USS George H.W. Bush and USS George Washington, bringing the American presence to three carrier strike groups alongside more than 50,000 U.S. troops in the region.

With U.S. facilities in multiple Gulf states and commercial shipping now caught in the escalating hostilities, the confrontation is extending beyond direct U.S.-Iran military targets.

Binance hit by record $6.3 billion crypto heist

About $3 billion in affected assets has been frozen

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 30, 2026 at 10:15 AM ET

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, says a security breach had an estimated impact of about $6.3 billion, making it the largest cryptocurrency heist on record. The company has worked with blockchain partners to freeze affected assets, with roughly $3 billion located or frozen so far.

Binance temporarily suspended deposits and withdrawals while conducting security checks and investigating the breach. The exchange said its initial investigation indicates that a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop may have allowed an attacker to access a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawal transactions. Binance said it has found no evidence that wallet private keys were leaked and plans to release additional technical details after its investigation is complete.

The company says it will cover losses from the apparent hack. Binance co-founder Yi He said the exchange would cover all hacking losses, while founder and former CEO Changpeng Zhao said he expects user funds to be fully covered. Binance has also said it is working with law enforcement, blockchain intelligence companies and other industry partners to trace and freeze additional assets.

The incident has already affected cryptocurrency markets. Bitcoin fell to a six-week low following news of the breach, while other cryptocurrencies and crypto-related stocks also came under pressure. The scale of the incident far exceeds previous major crypto thefts, including the roughly $1.5 billion stolen from the Bybit exchange in 2025.

Blockchain intelligence firms are now examining who carried out the attack as investigators work to determine exactly how the breach occurred and whether more of the affected assets can be recovered or frozen.

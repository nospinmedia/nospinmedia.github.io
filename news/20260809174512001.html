B.C. Declares Provincial Emergency As Okanagan Wildfire Triggers Mass Evacuations

Entire Summerland Ordered To Evacuate; Thousands Flee Peachland

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 9, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

British Columbia declared a provincewide state of emergency on August 8, 2026, after a fast-moving wildfire along Okanagan Lake forced more than 20,000 people to flee. Premier David Eby said the measure was needed as extreme heat, wind, and drought drove dangerous fire behavior and cut off escape routes, prompting air rescues and widespread evacuation orders.

Officials ordered the entire District of Summerland—home to about 12,000 residents—to evacuate overnight, while roughly 8,000 people in and around Peachland also left as the fire advanced toward the lakeshore. The Bald Range wildfire, first reported on August 7, expanded to about 50 square kilometers within hours and raced roughly 15 kilometers toward Summerland, according to incident officials. Helicopters were diverted from firefighting efforts to rescue trapped residents, and the RCMP said they were investigating a possible wildfire-related fatality near Summerland, with hazardous conditions delaying confirmation.

Emergency reception services were established in Penticton, where traffic and shelter demand surged as evacuees arrived. With more than 100 wildfires burning across the province—nearly half listed as out of control—authorities warned that additional communities could face rapid evacuations if winds shifted. Emergency Management Minister Kelly Greene said the declaration allows the province to impose travel restrictions and secure accommodations so hotels and other facilities can be prioritized for evacuees.

Officials were still assessing damage as initial videos showed homes burning while residents drove through heavy smoke. Fire leaders with the BC Wildfire Service described conditions as “explosive,” saying drought and strong winds were enabling long-range spotting and rapid fire growth capable of overwhelming roads and emergency response efforts. Residents under evacuation alert were urged to prepare essential supplies, keep vehicles fueled, and leave immediately if ordered.

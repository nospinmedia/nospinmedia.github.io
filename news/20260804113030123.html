Michigan Confirms First U.S. Deaths Linked To Cyclospora Outbreak

Taylor Farms recall widens as investigators trace 9-state cases

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 4, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

Michigan Department of Health and Human Services said on August 3, 2026, that two people in the state who contracted Cyclospora have died, marking the first U.S. fatalities linked to this summer’s nationwide surge. Officials noted both individuals had significant underlying conditions and severe dehydration, underscoring heightened risk for medically vulnerable patients during protracted gastrointestinal illness.

The announcement comes amid the largest recorded U.S. cyclosporiasis wave. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention said it is aware of more than 18,000 confirmed or suspected cases nationwide, while state tallies indicate the total may exceed 20,000 as reports are updated. Federal investigators have identified shredded iceberg lettuce served at Taco Bell as a source of infections across nine states, and traceback work has converged on supplier Taylor Farms de Mexico. The company voluntarily recalled iceberg lettuce grown in central Mexico, and Taco Bell said it stopped using that lettuce on July 17, 2026. As of late July, at least 1,947 illnesses and 98 hospitalizations were tied to Taco Bell exposures; officials stress the broader national surge also includes cases unrelated to the chain and that additional channels may be identified as the probe continues.

For consumers, health agencies emphasize that cyclosporiasis is typically treatable and rarely fatal, but persistent diarrhea can lead to dangerous dehydration, especially in older adults and those with chronic conditions. Public health departments advise seeking care for prolonged symptoms and following FDA food-safety guidance while the investigation continues and additional data are gathered.

- States with Taco Bell–linked cases identified by federal investigators: Indiana, Illinois, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, West Virginia

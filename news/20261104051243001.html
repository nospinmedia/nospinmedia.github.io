Troy Jackson unseats Susan Collins in Maine

Democrat ends Republican senator's nearly 30-year tenure

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 4, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

Democrat Troy Jackson defeated five-term Republican Sen. Susan Collins in Maine's closely watched U.S. Senate race, ending Collins' nearly three decades in Washington and giving Democrats another seat as the party won control of the chamber in Tuesday's midterm elections.

Jackson, a former president of the Maine Senate and a fifth-generation logger from Allagash, becomes the first Democrat elected to a U.S. Senate seat from Maine since George Mitchell won reelection in 1988. Collins, first elected in 1996, was seeking a sixth term and had built a national reputation as a moderate Republican willing to break with her party on some major votes.

The contest became one of the country's most closely watched Senate races as Democrats sought seats needed to overturn the Republican majority. Jackson emphasized his working-class and rural Maine background during the campaign, while Collins pointed to her seniority, bipartisan record and influence in Washington. The result ultimately became part of a much broader Democratic showing nationally, with the party winning control of both chambers of Congress.

Collins' defeat also changes Maine's representation in Washington after nearly 30 years with her in the Senate. Her longevity and seniority had given Maine influence disproportionate to its population, including through her role as chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee. Jackson will enter the Senate as part of a new Democratic majority when the next Congress convenes in January 2027.

The result represents a major change from Maine's recent Senate history. While the state has supported Democratic presidential candidates for decades, voters repeatedly reelected Collins, including during strongly Democratic election years. Jackson's victory breaks that pattern and turns what had been one of New England's remaining Republican-held Senate seats Democratic.

Bolsonaro’s party recognizes Lula victory

President Trump calls Brazil election result ‘rigged’

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 27, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

The political party of defeated Brazilian presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro has recognized President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva’s election victory, even as Bolsonaro continues to challenge the result and President Trump publicly questioned the integrity of the vote.

The recognition by Bolsonaro’s Liberal Party adds to pressure for the election result to be accepted after Lula secured a historic fourth term. Bolsonaro has refused to concede and is seeking to have the result annulled, but his party acknowledged Lula’s victory and said it would serve as an opposition force in Congress. Lula, meanwhile, used his victory speech to defend the legitimacy of the result and signal that he intends to move ahead with his governing agenda despite the closely divided electorate.

Trump added an international dimension to the dispute by saying the Brazilian election looked ‘rigged’. His comments contrasted with recognition of Lula’s victory from governments across Latin America, including several led by conservative politicians. Trump has been a political ally of the Bolsonaro family, and the Brazilian election has also drawn attention in the United States because of comparisons between the political movements surrounding the two leaders.

The election result also triggered significant volatility in Brazilian financial markets. The Brazilian real fell more than 8% against the U.S. dollar, while stocks in São Paulo declined sharply as investors assessed the implications of another Lula administration. Some individual shares suffered much larger losses amid concerns about future fiscal policy, state-controlled companies and the direction of economic policy.

Lula faces the challenge of governing a politically divided country while dealing with an opposition presence in Congress and financial-market concerns about his economic agenda. The formal recognition of the result by Bolsonaro’s party does not end Bolsonaro’s legal challenge, but it further separates the party’s institutional response from the defeated candidate’s refusal to concede.

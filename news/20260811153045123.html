Zelensky Says Russia Used North Korean Missiles in Deadly Strike

Seven steelworkers killed as Ukraine urges faster air-defense aid

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 12, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

President Volodymyr Zelensky said Russia used North Korean-supplied ballistic missiles during a large overnight assault into August 11, 2026, including a strike on the Zaporizhstal steel plant in Zaporizhzhia that killed seven workers as they headed toward bomb shelters after an air-raid alert. Ukraine says the attack highlights growing military cooperation between Moscow and Pyongyang as Russia intensifies its long-range strikes.

Ukrainian officials said the overnight barrage included Zircon hypersonic cruise missiles, Iskander ballistic missiles, and more than 120 attack drones launched against targets across the country. Metinvest, which operates the Zaporizhstal plant, said 21 workers were also injured. Elsewhere, strikes damaged buildings in Kyiv and hit communities in the Dnipropetrovsk and Donetsk regions. Russia's Defense Ministry said its attacks targeted defense-industrial facilities and transportation infrastructure supporting Ukraine's military.

Zelensky said the alleged use of North Korean missiles demonstrates that Russia is expanding its military cooperation with Pyongyang while continuing large-scale attacks despite international diplomatic efforts. He again urged Ukraine's allies to accelerate deliveries of Patriot air-defense systems and interceptor missiles, arguing they remain the country's most effective protection against ballistic missile attacks.

The strike came as both Ukraine and Russia continued long-range attacks on each other's territory, with Ukraine also reporting successful drone strikes against infrastructure inside Russia. Independent analysts and Western governments have previously said North Korea has supplied Russia with missiles and other military equipment, although Moscow and Pyongyang have denied transferring weapons in violation of United Nations sanctions.

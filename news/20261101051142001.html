Pregnant woman killed in Spirit Halloween store shooting

Six others wounded as police search for a single suspect

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 1, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

A 31-year-old pregnant mother of four was killed and six other people were wounded in a targeted shooting inside a Spirit Halloween store at the Solano Town Center in Fairfield, California, police said. No arrest had been made as investigators searched for a single suspect.

Police identified the woman who died as Constance Hages, 31. Her family said she was pregnant and was the mother of four children. Gunfire erupted at about 8:30 p.m. Friday, October 30, inside the seasonal store as shoppers were preparing for Halloween. Hages died at the scene, while the six other victims were taken to hospitals.

Three male victims, ages 16, 19 and 32, remained in critical condition. A 23-year-old man was hospitalized in critical but stable condition, while two other men, ages 19 and 43, were treated for gunshot wounds and released. Fairfield police said preliminary evidence indicated the shooting stemmed from a targeted incident involving one suspect, but authorities had not publicly identified the shooter or disclosed a motive.

Detectives were reviewing extensive surveillance footage from the mall and store while working with federal authorities to identify the lone suspect. The FBI's Sacramento office was assisting Fairfield police with the investigation. Authorities said there was no continuing threat to the public following the shooting.

Spirit Halloween said it was cooperating fully with law enforcement and that its Solano Town Center location would remain closed until further notice as the investigation continued. The mall also canceled its planned Halloween trick-or-treating event. Fairfield Mayor Rick Vaccaro called for community unity and vigilance during Halloween events following the shooting.

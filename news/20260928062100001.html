3 killed, 4 wounded in Detroit after-hours shooting

Police say fight led to gunfire as investigators pursue leads

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 28, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

Three men were killed and four other people were wounded in a shooting at an after-hours location on Detroit's west side early Sunday, September 27, according to police. Officers responded at approximately 7:21 a.m. near Eaton Avenue and Prest Street, where they found three men with fatal gunshot wounds.

Detroit Police Chief Todd Bettison said the three men who died were approximately 30 years old or older. Four additional victims — two men and two women — were taken to hospitals in private vehicles. Police said their injuries were not considered life-threatening. Authorities had not publicly identified any of the seven victims as of Sunday afternoon.

Investigators believe the shooting began with a fight at the after-hours establishment. Bettison said detectives have developed leads and are working to identify the people responsible, but police had not announced an arrest. He also said investigators did not believe the shooting represented an ongoing danger to the surrounding community.

Police described the location as an after-hours establishment, while other reporting identified it as a strip club. Authorities had not publicly released detailed information about what happened during the confrontation, how many people fired weapons or what prompted the initial dispute. The investigation remained active Sunday as detectives worked to determine the sequence of events and identify those involved.

The shooting adds three homicide victims to Detroit's violent-crime toll, but police emphasized that the circumstances appeared connected to the confrontation at the establishment rather than a continuing threat to residents in the area. Investigators are asking anyone with information about the shooting to contact Crime Stoppers of Michigan at 1-800-SPEAK-UP.

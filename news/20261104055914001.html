Chris Pappas wins New Hampshire Senate race

Democrat defeats John Sununu to keep seat in party hands

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 4, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

Democrat Chris Pappas won New Hampshire's U.S. Senate election Tuesday, defeating Republican John E. Sununu and keeping the seat in Democratic hands during a midterm election that shifted control of the Senate nationally.

Pappas, who has represented New Hampshire's 1st Congressional District in the U.S. House since 2019, will move to the Senate when the new Congress convenes in January 2027. His victory follows a closely watched campaign in a state known for competitive elections and voters willing to support candidates from different parties on the same ballot.

Sununu brought substantial political experience and a familiar name to the contest. He previously represented New Hampshire in both the U.S. House and U.S. Senate, serving one Senate term from 2003 to 2009 before losing to Democrat Jeanne Shaheen. Constitution Party candidate Edmond Laplante was also on the ballot.

The race was for the seat held by retiring Democratic Sen. Jeanne Shaheen, making it an important defensive contest for Democrats rather than a party pickup. Pappas' victory allows Democrats to retain the seat while the party made gains elsewhere that ultimately gave it control of the chamber.

The result also continues Pappas' record of winning competitive federal races in New Hampshire. His House district has historically been one of the more closely contested districts in New England, repeatedly changing party control before Pappas first won the seat in 2018.

Pappas' Senate victory comes amid a split political picture in New Hampshire, where voters have regularly elected both Republicans and Democrats to major statewide and federal offices rather than consistently favoring one party.

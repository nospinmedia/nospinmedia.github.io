15-year-old to face adult charges in FAMU shooting

11 people were shot after fight near stadium

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 27, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A 15-year-old arrested after a shooting near Florida A&M University will be prosecuted as an adult, according to State Attorney Jack Campbell, as investigators continue examining the gunfire that wounded 11 people during the university's homecoming weekend.

Police identified the suspect as Anthony Rivers III. He faces 11 counts of attempted second-degree murder and a charge of possession of a firearm by a delinquent. Rivers was arrested Sunday and ordered to remain in custody during a detention hearing Monday. Authorities have said the investigation remains active and additional arrests are possible.

The shooting happened Saturday night near Perry and Manatee streets, just outside Bragg Memorial Stadium in Tallahassee. Police said preliminary evidence indicates a physical altercation occurred among people who knew one another before shots were fired. Investigators said none of the people who were shot appear to have been the intended targets.

Six of the gunshot victims were minors, with the youngest 11 years old. At least three victims suffered serious injuries, including one who was reported in critical condition. An additional person, the grandmother of one of the victims, was injured while fleeing, bringing the total number of people injured in the incident to 12.

The shooting occurred hours after Florida A&M's homecoming football game against the University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff. Authorities have not established that the shooting was connected to the university or the football game. Police Chief Lawrence Revell said the area was crowded when the shooting occurred, making it difficult for ambulances to reach victims and requiring officers to carry some of the wounded to emergency vehicles.

Police are reviewing surveillance footage and other digital evidence while continuing to interview witnesses and determine whether other people were involved.

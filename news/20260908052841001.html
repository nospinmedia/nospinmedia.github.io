Florida family missing after plane vanishes off Bahamas

Bad weather disrupts search for four people

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 8, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

A search is underway for four members of a South Florida family after their small plane disappeared Monday while flying from the Bahamas to Miami Executive Airport. Bahamian authorities and the U.S. Coast Guard are searching waters near Andros Island, although poor weather has disrupted the operation.

The missing aircraft is a five-seat Piper PA-34, registration N212ML, according to Bahamian police. It departed Great Harbour Cay in the Berry Islands at approximately 11:12 a.m. on September 7. Air traffic control later lost contact with the plane approximately 13 miles west of Andros Island, near waters off Red Bays, North Andros, where authorities believe it may have gone down.

Family members identified those aboard as pilot Mario Lamar Sr., 80, his wife Lili Lamar, 79, and their grandchildren Sofia Sosa, 28, and Christian Sosa, 22. The family said Lamar has more than 40 years of flying experience. They had spent the Labor Day weekend at a family home on Great Harbour Cay and were returning to South Florida when the aircraft disappeared.

Flight-tracking data showed the aircraft experiencing significant changes shortly before contact was lost, including a rapid descent from roughly 10,000 feet to 3,700 feet in about three minutes. Severe weather was reported in the area, but authorities have not established what caused the aircraft to disappear.

The Royal Bahamas Defence Force requested U.S. assistance Monday afternoon. The Coast Guard deployed an HC-144 Ocean Sentry aircraft to help Bahamian authorities search the area, but officials said poor weather forced search operations to be suspended. It was not immediately clear when the full search would resume. The family has publicly appealed to pilots and boaters in the region for any information that could help locate the aircraft and its occupants.

FBI searches donor’s Beverly Hills mansion in Eric Swalwell investigation

Raid marks latest escalation in federal probe of former congressman

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 28, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

Federal agents searched a Beverly Hills mansion belonging to developer and political donor Stephen Cloobeck on October 27 as part of an ongoing criminal investigation involving former U.S. Rep. Eric Swalwell, according to people familiar with the investigation.

The FBI confirmed that agents conducted an operation in the Los Angeles region related to an active criminal investigation but declined to provide additional details. The property belongs to Cloobeck, founder of Diamond Resorts International, who contributed more than $23,000 to Swalwell's congressional campaigns since 2017. Swalwell spent time at the property before resigning from Congress in April 2026. Cloobeck said earlier this year that he had ended his association with Swalwell as allegations against the former congressman mounted.

The Beverly Hills search is the latest federal investigative action involving Swalwell. In August, agents seized electronic devices from him at San Francisco International Airport and searched his Washington residence. Authorities have not publicly disclosed what agents were seeking at Cloobeck's property or what evidence, if any, was recovered during Tuesday's search.

Swalwell resigned from Congress after multiple women accused him of sexual misconduct, including sexual assault and rape. He has denied criminal wrongdoing. The Manhattan District Attorney's Office has separately confirmed an investigation after a former staffer alleged that Swalwell raped her while he was in New York for a charity event in 2024. Prosecutors in Los Angeles have also been reviewing allegations involving him.

Cloobeck has not been accused of participating in Swalwell's alleged misconduct. He is separately facing a witness intimidation charge filed in May in an unrelated case and has pleaded not guilty.

Swalwell represented a California congressional district for seven terms and served on the House Judiciary and Intelligence committees. He also served as one of the House impeachment managers during President Trump's second impeachment trial.

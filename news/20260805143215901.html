Armed Suspect Arrested at Trump’s LA Golf Course

Federal firearms complaint filed; weapons and notes seized

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 5, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Authorities arrested Jeanine John Taele, 38, at Trump National Golf Club Los Angeles in Rancho Palos Verdes after plainclothes federal agents observed him taking photos and appearing to watch security preparations ahead of President Trump’s August 4, 2026 fundraiser. Deputies with the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department said Taele had a 16‑round magazine with hollow‑point ammunition in his pocket; a search of his vehicle recovered a loaded pistol and an additional loaded magazine. Investigators said there is no credible ongoing threat to the community as they continue to assess intent.

According to officials, agents first noted Taele during a security assessment on July 31. He returned on August 2, 2026, when LASD detained him and searched his truck, then executed a search warrant at his Downey, California home on August 3. Items seized included an illegally modified AR‑platform rifle and a 1911 .45‑caliber pistol. Taele was already under investigation by the El Segundo Police Department in a separate robbery case, authorities said.

Prosecutors charged Taele in state court with carrying a concealed firearm and possession of armor‑piercing/prohibited ammunition, and a federal criminal complaint was filed on August 4, 2026 alleging possession of an unregistered short‑barreled rifle. The investigation is being conducted in coordination with the FBI Joint Terrorism Task Force, the U.S. Secret Service, ATF, and LASD. Officials said Taele remains in custody while both cases proceed.

Recovered items described by investigators included: - Illegally modified AR‑style rifle; AR upper receiver - Loaded 9mm pistol in vehicle; 1911 .45‑caliber pistol at residence - Body armor; high‑capacity magazines; bulk ammunition - Two radio signal devices; earpiece; binoculars; a badge labeled “security protection agent” - Multiple notebooks with “concerning statements”

The RNC dinner at the Rancho Palos Verdes course went forward on August 4, with enhanced federal‑local coordination. Officials emphasized that assessing potential pre‑event surveillance remains central to protective security.

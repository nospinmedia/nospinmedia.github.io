White House blocks CNN from Air Force One press pool

Real America's Voice takes its place on Trump's Tennessee trip

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 27, 2026 at 6:32 PM ET

The White House blocked CNN from traveling aboard Air Force One as the designated television pool outlet for President Donald Trump's Saturday trip to Knoxville, Tennessee, extending a dispute over media access just days after a federal judge temporarily restored White House credentials for CNN and two other news organizations.

CNN had been scheduled to handle the rotating television pool assignment for the trip, which required the network to gather video and information on behalf of other major television organizations. White House guidance instead listed Real America's Voice, a conservative network that is not part of the traditional five-network television pool, for the trip.

The change came shortly after the regular television pool resumed operations following several days without normal coverage. ABC, CBS, NBC and Fox News had previously declined to replace CNN when it was removed from its scheduled assignments, effectively suspending the television pool in solidarity with the excluded network.

A federal judge had temporarily restored White House grounds access for CNN, MS NOW and Politico after the outlets challenged the administration's decision to revoke their credentials. However, that court order addressed access to the White House grounds and did not specifically require the administration to include CNN in the traveling pool or aboard Air Force One.

A pool report from Saturday confirmed there was no regular television pool traveling on the Tennessee trip. Reuters photographs showed a Real America's Voice reporter working beside Air Force One after CNN's exclusion.

The latest dispute raises a separate question from the fight over White House credentials: how much authority an administration should have to determine which news organizations participate in the independent press pool that provides shared coverage of the president.

Scottish police investigate Andrew and Epstein's Balmoral visits

Inquiry follows new information about time spent at royal estate

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 31, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

Police in Scotland are conducting active inquiries into time Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell spent at Balmoral, the British monarch's private estate in Aberdeenshire.

Police Scotland confirmed Friday that it had received new information and said its inquiries were at a "very early stage." The force has not disclosed precisely what information prompted the inquiry, what potential offenses are being examined or whether anyone is currently considered a suspect.

British media have reported that the inquiries relate to allegations that women were trafficked to the Scottish estate. Police Scotland has not confirmed that allegation, saying it would be inappropriate to provide details while inquiries are continuing. The force said its work is evidence-led and being conducted with the needs of victim-survivors in mind.

Epstein and Maxwell are reported to have stayed at Balmoral in 1999 as guests of Andrew, who was then Prince Andrew. Photographs previously released by the U.S. Department of Justice appear to show Andrew and Epstein together on a shooting trip at the estate. Other images introduced during Maxwell's criminal trial showed Epstein and Maxwell at a cabin on the Balmoral estate. Their presence at Balmoral does not by itself establish criminal wrongdoing.

The Scottish inquiry comes amid broader British police examinations of information contained in records released by the U.S. Justice Department concerning Epstein. The National Police Chiefs' Council says several police forces have investigations connected to the released material, although there is currently no single national investigation.

Andrew was separately arrested by British police in February 2026 on suspicion of misconduct in public office over allegations connected to his relationship with Epstein and his former role as a U.K. trade envoy. He was released while that investigation continues and has not been charged. Andrew has consistently denied criminal wrongdoing related to Epstein.

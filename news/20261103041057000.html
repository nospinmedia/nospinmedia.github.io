U.S. strikes Iran's Qeshm Island

Officials call operation limited and defensive

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 3, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

The United States carried out limited strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, with U.S. officials describing the operation as a defensive response after Iranian forces used surface-to-air missiles against American assets.

U.S. officials said the strikes targeted Iranian positions associated with the missile activity. Iranian state media reported several explosions on Qeshm Island, the largest island in the Strait of Hormuz. U.S. officials characterized the military action as limited and defensive rather than the start of a broader offensive operation against Iran. No confirmed casualty or damage figures were immediately available.

Additional U.S. reporting indicated that the action followed an incident involving an American military aircraft. ABC News, citing a U.S. official, reported that the strikes followed an Iranian attack on a U.S. aircraft. Iran separately claimed that it had shot down two American RQ-170 reconnaissance drones, but a U.S. official disputed that assertion.

The incident adds another direct military encounter between the United States and Iran around the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most strategically important shipping routes. The immediate scope of the U.S. response remained limited, according to officials, while the situation continued to carry the possibility of further military action if attacks on American forces or assets continue.

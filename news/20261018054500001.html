Zelenskyy Says Starlink Restrictions Inside Ukraine Appear Deliberate

Tests Found Terminals Worked at Higher Speeds Outside Country

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 18, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says new tests indicate speed restrictions affecting Starlink and Starshield terminals are limited to Ukraine, leading him to conclude that the restrictions appear deliberate rather than the result of a technical problem.

The Ukrainian military has been dealing with a speed limit of approximately 161 kilometers per hour, or 100 mph, on satellite terminals used aboard moving vehicles and drones. When the threshold is exceeded, Ukrainian officials say the connection shuts down. The restriction has interfered with reconnaissance, supply and drone-interception missions.

Zelenskyy said Ukraine tested the explanation by sending vehicles equipped with the satellite terminals into European Union countries. According to the president, the systems continued operating while the vehicles traveled at speeds exceeding 180 kilometers per hour. When tested inside Ukraine, however, the terminals disconnected at approximately 161 kilometers per hour.

"The restrictions apply inside Ukraine but do not apply in European Union countries," Zelenskyy told reporters, describing the results as evidence of what he called a "planned operation." His conclusion has not been independently established, and questions remain over who imposed the restrictions and why.

SpaceX has attributed the problem to technical issues, according to Ukrainian officials. President Trump has denied that the White House ordered the restrictions. Secretary of State Marco Rubio also said nobody in the U.S. government ordered Starlink slowed and disputed Zelenskyy's characterization of the situation.

The dispute is complicated by the use of two SpaceX systems. Starlink is a commercial service controlled by SpaceX, while Starshield is used for military operations and is controlled by the U.S. government. Elon Musk previously said any Starshield speed or usage limits are determined by the U.S. military and ultimately the president.

Ukraine says the restrictions remain in place despite earlier indications that full service had been restored.

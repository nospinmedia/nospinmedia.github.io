Woman Killed, Five Injured in Swiss Rave Shooting

Police launch major manhunt with German assistance

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 30, 2026 at 11:45 AM ET

Swiss authorities launched a large-scale manhunt after a gunman opened fire during an overnight rave at the Schachen horse racing grounds in Aarau, killing a 22-year-old woman and injuring five other people. Police said emergency calls were received at about 2:30 a.m. on Sunday, shortly after the music program ended at the event, which drew approximately 3,500 attendees. Witnesses initially believed the loud noises were fireworks before realizing shots had been fired, sending crowds fleeing for safety.

The five injured victims—four men and one woman, ranging in age from 24 to 27—were transported to hospitals for treatment. Authorities said all six victims are Swiss residents. Emergency responders secured the venue, evacuated attendees to a nearby sports facility and established a wide security perimeter while forensic investigators began examining the scene. Police appealed for witnesses to submit photos and video recordings that could help identify the shooter and reconstruct the sequence of events.

The suspect escaped before officers arrived, prompting an extensive search involving cantonal police, specialized tactical units, police dogs and an Army Super Puma helicopter. Because the shooting occurred near Switzerland's northern border, German police joined the search and coordinated with Swiss authorities to monitor possible escape routes. Investigators have not released a description of the suspect, identified the weapon used or announced a possible motive.

The shooting is one of Switzerland's deadliest violent incidents of the year and remains under active investigation. Officials urged anyone with information to contact police immediately and asked the public to avoid spreading unverified information while investigators continue collecting evidence. Authorities said additional updates will be released as the investigation progresses.

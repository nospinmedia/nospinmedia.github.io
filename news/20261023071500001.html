President Trump says Elon Musk restored Ukraine's Starlink access

Ukraine says service has improved but restrictions have not been fully lifted

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 23, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

President Trump says Elon Musk has restored Ukraine's access to SpaceX's Starlink satellite network after restrictions disrupted Ukrainian military operations, but Ukrainian officials say service has improved rather than returned completely to its previous capabilities.

Asked Wednesday whether he had spoken directly with Musk about the restrictions, Trump declined to say. His comments followed a Wall Street Journal report that Trump personally asked Musk to remove limits affecting Ukraine. Reuters reported that Starlink performance improved after the reported conversation but that Ukrainian officials said the restrictions had not been fully removed.

The dispute centers partly on the distinction between Starlink, SpaceX's commercial satellite internet service, and Starshield, a military-focused system developed for government customers. Ukrainian forces depend heavily on satellite connections for battlefield communications, reconnaissance and drone operations.

Ukrainian officials reported earlier this month that terminals used aboard moving systems had been limited to about 100 mph, disrupting missions involving interceptor drones. Ukraine's Defense Ministry reported that service began improving on October 15, but questions have continued over who ordered the restrictions and which capabilities remain unavailable.

Musk has said Starshield systems are controlled by the U.S. government and that limits on their speed or use are decided by the military and ultimately the president. The White House has denied ordering the slowdown. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has instead said decisions involving commercial Starlink service are controlled by SpaceX and Musk.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy disputed Trump's description of a complete restoration, saying the system was working better but that problems remained. He said Ukraine planned to continue discussing the issue with Rubio.

The controversy has also drawn congressional scrutiny because satellite communications have become important to Ukraine's ability to defend against Russian attacks. Lawmakers have sought answers about who controls restrictions on systems used by Ukrainian forces and whether the U.S. government played a role in the disruption.

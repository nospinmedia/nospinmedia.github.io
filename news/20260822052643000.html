Russian Drone Strike on Kryvyi Rih Mall Kills 16

EU Signals New Sanctions After Attack on Civilians

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 22, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

At least 16 people were killed and 130 others were injured after a Russian drone attack struck the Soniachna Halereya shopping center in Kryvyi Rih, Ukrainian officials said on August 22. Authorities said the strike occurred in two waves, with an initial attack igniting a massive fire before a second drone struck approximately 30 minutes later as emergency crews and police responded. Ukrainian officials reported that 23 children were among the injured, while rescue teams continued searching through the rubble for at least nine missing people, including two children.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy described the assault as a deliberate "double-tap" attack targeting both civilians and first responders. Officials from the Dnipropetrovsk Regional Military Administration and the Kryvyi Rih Defense Council said 29 of the injured remained in critical condition. The fire damaged roughly 9,000 square meters of the shopping complex before firefighters brought it under control.

The attack prompted swift international condemnation. European Union foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said the bloc would move forward with a new sanctions package against Russia, calling the strike another example of attacks on civilian infrastructure. United Nations Humanitarian Coordinator for Ukraine Matthias Schmale said attacks that deliberately endanger emergency responders raise serious concerns under international humanitarian law. The mall strike coincided with additional Russian attacks reported in Kyiv, Mykolaiv, and Zaporizhzhia, highlighting the continuing intensity of aerial operations across Ukraine.

Kryvyi Rih, President Zelenskyy's hometown, has repeatedly been targeted since Russia's full-scale invasion began in 2022. Ukrainian officials said continued shortages of advanced air-defense interceptors have made it increasingly difficult to defend cities against large-scale drone and missile attacks. As rescue operations continue, officials warned that the death toll could rise if additional victims are found beneath the collapsed sections of the shopping center.

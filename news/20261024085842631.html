Two Former FBI Officials Charged With Lying to Congress About Hunter Biden Probe

Criminal Complaint Alleges They Downplayed Their Roles in FBI Investigations

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 24, 2026 at 11:45 AM ET

Two former senior FBI officials have been charged with making false statements to Congress about their involvement in investigations concerning Hunter Biden and his laptop before the 2020 presidential election.

Nikki Floris, 46, and Bradley Benavides, 56, former leaders of the FBI's now-disbanded Foreign Influence Task Force, surrendered Friday and appeared in federal court in Washington. Each faces one count of making false statements stemming from testimony given to lawmakers in 2023.

The criminal complaint alleges Floris and Benavides were substantially more involved in FBI investigations concerning Hunter Biden than they acknowledged during their congressional testimony. Prosecutors say the task force hosted classified briefings involving agents and prosecutors working on the Hunter Biden investigations and that both officials participated in discussions and communications about the cases.

The complaint specifically alleges Benavides falsely testified that he learned through Wikipedia that the FBI possessed Hunter Biden's laptop. Prosecutors allege Floris falsely denied knowing that Hunter Biden had been discussed during task force meetings.

Both defendants deny wrongdoing. Attorneys for Floris called the accusation false and criticized the timing of the prosecution, while Benavides' attorneys described the charges as baseless and politically motivated.

The case was filed through a criminal complaint rather than a grand jury indictment, meaning prosecutors have not yet secured an indictment. During Friday's court appearance, U.S. Magistrate Judge Zia Faruqui questioned the unusual procedure and noted that the alleged conduct occurred years ago. He scheduled a preliminary hearing for Tuesday, when prosecutors will face an early test of whether there is sufficient probable cause to continue the case.

The charges come less than two weeks before the November 3 midterm elections and revive scrutiny of how the FBI handled investigations involving Hunter Biden during the 2020 election period.

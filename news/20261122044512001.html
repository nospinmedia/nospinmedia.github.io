Iranian Attack Sparks Fire At Bahrain Industrial Facility

Air Defenses Intercept More Attacks As IRGC Claims Strikes On U.S. Positions

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 22, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

Bahrain says an Iranian attack struck a civilian industrial facility on Saturday, November 21, sparking a fire as the country's air defenses intercepted additional incoming attacks. Authorities reported material damage but no injuries, while Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps separately claimed it had targeted U.S. military positions in Bahrain.

Bahrain's Interior Ministry said Civil Defence crews responded to the industrial-site fire after authorities warned residents to move to safe locations. Officials did not identify the facility, but witnesses told Agence France-Presse (AFP) they saw smoke rising over Sitra Island on Bahrain's eastern coast, an industrial area that includes a refinery operated by state oil company Bapco. The Bahrain Defence Force said its air defenses intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks and that falling shrapnel caused additional material damage without casualties.

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) claimed it launched missile and drone strikes against U.S. positions at Al-Riffa and Awali and alleged that two U.S. Army Patriot radars were destroyed. Those claims have not been independently verified. A U.S. official told CBS News that the latest Iranian attack attempts caused no impacts to U.S. personnel or military assets in the region. Bahrain hosts the headquarters of the U.S. Navy's Fifth Fleet.

Iranian state news agency IRNA circulated a competing, unverified account alleging the Sitra fire resulted from a malfunction involving a U.S. Patriot interceptor. Bahrain, however, attributed the strike on the civilian industrial facility to Iran.

The incident also prompted a diplomatic response. Bahrain urged the United Nations to take firm action and called for compensation from Iran. Jordan condemned the attack, while Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Secretary-General Jassim AlBudaiwi expressed solidarity with Bahrain.

The attack adds another flashpoint to continuing hostilities involving Iran, the United States and regional partners, with Bahrain now reporting damage to civilian industrial infrastructure even as its defenses intercepted other incoming threats.

Oregon School Shooter Identified as 16-Year-Old Former Student

Police Say Shotgun Was Purchased by His Mother

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 18, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

Authorities have identified the 16-year-old who killed three children and a teacher at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, as Griffin J. John II, a former student at the school. Law enforcement sources told CBS News that John used a shotgun purchased by his mother, providing a significant new detail as investigators continue examining how he obtained the weapon and ammunition.

Police said John approached the school on October 15, threw his shotgun over a fence and climbed onto the property. He then fired multiple rounds through glass in locked entrance doors and went directly into a nearby special education classroom. John had previously been a special-needs student at Silver Rail. Police have not announced a motive or said whether his previous connection to the school or classroom played a role in the attack.

Investigators recovered a Maverick 88 pump-action 12-gauge shotgun and said John fired as many as 15 rounds. He had approximately 44 additional 12-gauge rounds on his person or in a backpack, including birdshot, buckshot and slugs. Police said all six victims were shot in the classroom. Three children, ages 5, 8 and 11, and 25-year-old teacher Megan Lambert were killed. A 7-year-old student and 54-year-old teacher Becky Drury were wounded and survived.

Police also provided additional details about the efforts to stop the attack. An unidentified teacher threw her cellphone at John, tackled him and fought to take away his shotgun. Kaylub Giamanco, a 36-year-old father of two Silver Rail students, had seen the gunman outside, retrieved a .40-caliber pistol from his vehicle and followed him into the school. Police said Giamanco entered the classroom while the teacher was struggling with John and fatally shot the teenager.

Authorities are continuing to examine John's electronic devices, interviews and other evidence as they investigate his motive and determine exactly how he obtained the shotgun and ammunition.

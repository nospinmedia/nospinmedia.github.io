Suspect Charged In Deadly Lexington Park Shooting

Teen killed and four others wounded during community event

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 18, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

An 18-year-old man has been charged with murder following a shooting at Charles Young Park in Lexington, Kentucky, that left a 16-year-old dead and four others wounded during the city's annual East End Day community celebration. Police arrested Jametrius Griffin on August 16, charging him with one count of murder, four counts of first-degree assault, and tampering with physical evidence.

The shooting occurred shortly before 7 p.m. on August 15, when gunfire erupted during the neighborhood event. The four surviving victims—including a 4-year-old child, a 14-year-old, and two adults—were hospitalized with non-life-threatening injuries. Investigators said evidence collected at the scene and information provided by witnesses helped identify the suspect.

Lexington Police Chief Lawrence Weathers said detectives continue to investigate the motive and whether additional individuals were involved. City officials, including Mayor Linda Gorton and Kentucky Gov. Andy Beshear, condemned the violence and pledged support for the victims, their families, and the surrounding community. Local violence-prevention organizations also renewed calls for stronger efforts to reduce youth gun violence.

Authorities said the investigation remains active as detectives continue reviewing evidence and interviewing witnesses. While an arrest has been made, prosecutors are expected to present additional details as the criminal case moves forward through the court system.

Steel plant explosion in Turkey kills 16 workers

Thirteen others injured after crane falls into furnace

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 10, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

An explosion and fire at an iron and steel plant in western Turkey killed 16 people and injured 13 others, authorities said, after a crane fell into a furnace during maintenance work.

The explosion occurred Monday at the HABAS iron and steel plant in Aliaga, in Turkey's western Izmir province. President Recep Tayyip Erdogan said two of the injured were in critical condition as emergency crews responded to the fire and searched the industrial site for casualties.

Izmir Gov. Suleyman Elban said workers were replacing a boiler when a crane fell into a furnace, triggering an explosion in a nearby steel mill. Production had been stopped for the maintenance operation, meaning about 200 workers were in the area instead of the roughly 300 normally present.

Witnesses described a powerful blast that shook the surrounding area. Images from the aftermath showed flames inside the facility and a large column of smoke rising above the plant. One witness quoted by Turkey's state-run Anadolu news agency said the ground shook so strongly that he initially thought an earthquake had occurred.

Erdogan said both judicial and administrative investigations had been opened to determine the circumstances of the explosion and whether negligence contributed to the disaster. Justice Minister Akin Gurlek said four prosecutors were assigned to the investigation. A private Turkish news agency also reported that five people had been detained in connection with the explosion.

The HABAS facility was established in 1987 and has the capacity to produce about 4.5 million tons of steel annually. Aliaga is a major industrial and port area north of Izmir and is also known for its large shipbreaking industry.

Initial reports varied on the death toll, but the latest figures confirmed by Erdogan and reported by both the Associated Press and Reuters put the number killed at 16.

Trump to Mark 25th Anniversary of 9/11 at Pentagon

Vance will represent administration at Ground Zero

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 30, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

President Trump plans to commemorate the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks at the Pentagon rather than the annual ceremony at Ground Zero in New York City, while Vice President JD Vance is scheduled to represent the administration in Manhattan. The White House said administration officials will also attend the Flight 93 National Memorial in Shanksville, Pennsylvania, providing federal representation at all three sites struck during the attacks that killed nearly 3,000 people.

Asked on August 28 why he would not attend the New York ceremony, Trump said he was going to the Pentagon. The decision does not break presidential precedent. During Trump's first term, he regularly marked 9/11 at either the Pentagon or Flight 93 memorial instead of attending the official Manhattan ceremony. He last participated in the Ground Zero commemoration in 2024, when he attended alongside then-President Joe Biden and then-Vice President Kamala Harris during the presidential campaign.

The Manhattan observance traditionally differs from ceremonies at the other attack sites. Since 2012, elected officials have not delivered formal speeches during the Ground Zero ceremony. Instead, relatives of victims read the names of those killed, with moments of silence marking the times when hijacked aircraft struck the World Trade Center and Pentagon, when the towers collapsed and when United Airlines Flight 93 crashed in Pennsylvania. Presidents have therefore frequently divided anniversary responsibilities among senior administration officials or attended ceremonies outside New York.

The White House described the upcoming anniversary as an opportunity to honor the victims, their families and the first responders who entered the burning buildings and attack sites. The September 11, 2026 commemorations will mark a quarter-century since 19 al-Qaida hijackers seized four passenger aircraft, crashing two into the World Trade Center, one into the Pentagon and a fourth into a Pennsylvania field after passengers fought back.

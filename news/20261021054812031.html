Amazon plans more than 300,000 holiday hires

Seasonal recruitment rises about 20% from last year

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 21, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

Amazon plans to recruit more than 300,000 workers for the U.S. holiday shopping season, increasing its seasonal hiring for the first time in three years as the retailer prepares its fulfillment and delivery network for the busiest part of the year.

The planned hiring represents an increase of roughly 20% from the 250,000 positions Amazon offered for the 2025 holiday season. The jobs include full-time, part-time and seasonal positions across the company's fulfillment centers, sorting facilities and delivery stations throughout the United States.

Amazon said average starting pay for the positions will exceed $22 per hour. Available jobs and pay rates vary by location and role, and the company is also promoting flexible scheduling and training opportunities as part of the recruitment campaign.

The increase is notable because Amazon had held its holiday hiring target at about 250,000 workers for the previous three seasons. This year's expansion comes as the company continues investing in its logistics network and prepares for the surge in orders traditionally associated with the final months of the year.

Seasonal hiring by major retailers and delivery companies provides temporary employment for hundreds of thousands of workers each year, although companies increasingly use automation and year-round logistics capacity to handle changes in demand. Amazon's decision to increase its hiring target indicates that the company expects to need substantially more workers across its network than it recruited during the previous holiday season.

The hiring announcement does not guarantee that all of the positions will become permanent jobs after the holidays. Seasonal employment levels can also vary by market depending on customer demand and staffing needs.

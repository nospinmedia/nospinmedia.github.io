Gunman kills 8 at Pennsylvania family home

Two children and pregnant woman among victims in Erie

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 11, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

A 45-year-old gunman killed eight people, including two children and a pregnant woman, at a home in Erie, Pennsylvania, before apparently taking his own life. The shooting occurred on Friday, October 9, 2026, leaving nine people dead in total, including the suspected shooter. Police confirmed the death toll Saturday as investigators worked to determine what led to the violence.

Officers responded to reports of gunfire around 5 p.m. Friday near 7th and Perry Streets. Witnesses reported seeing a man firing a weapon before entering a residence. Police subsequently discovered two women dead outside the home and additional victims inside. The suspected gunman was found dead on the second floor with an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound.

Erie Police Chief Rick Lorah said the victims included members of the same family and a family friend. Investigators believe the shooter entered the residence and later came outside, where he shot two women who appeared distressed and were seeking assistance from neighbors. Authorities have not established a motive, and the suspect's identity had not been publicly released in the initial police briefing.

Among those killed was Angelina Stone, a pregnant woman who, according to family members interviewed by local reporters, was just four days away from giving birth. Relatives and neighbors described the devastating impact on a family that lost multiple members, including young children.

Police said all nine deaths occurred before officers arrived. Authorities also clarified that loud noises heard during the police response came from tactical flash-bang devices, not additional gunfire. Officials confirmed that the suspect was deceased and there was no continuing threat to the public.

Erie Mayor Daria Devlin expressed condolences to the victims' families and praised emergency responders. Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro said he had spoken with the mayor and offered state assistance. Shapiro also renewed his call for legislative action on gun violence prevention, including expanded background checks.

Investigators continued examining the Perry Street residence Saturday, gathering evidence and interviewing witnesses to establish the sequence of events and determine what motivated the killings.

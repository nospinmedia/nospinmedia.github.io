Cambridge’s Youngest Black Professor Resigns Amid New Credentials Probe

University opens investigation into qualifications; college launches separate review

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 9, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Professor Jason Arday resigned from the University of Cambridge with immediate effect after the university announced a new investigation into information concerning his academic qualifications and honorary appointments. Arday, appointed in 2023 at age 37 as Cambridge's youngest Black professor, stepped down on August 5, 2026, from both his professorship and his fellowship at Jesus College, saying sustained public scrutiny had taken a personal toll.

Cambridge said its latest inquiry concerns newly received information about Arday's credentials and honorary roles, while Jesus College announced a separate review of his fellowship and appointment. University officials have not publicly described the new information, and both investigations remain ongoing.

The new review follows earlier questions surrounding Arday's academic work and public biography. Liverpool John Moores University, where he earned his doctorate in 2015, previously concluded that citation problems in his thesis reflected "honest and reasonable error" rather than academic misconduct. More recent media reports have raised additional questions about his qualifications and aspects of his public biography, which Arday disputes.

Despite the investigations, publisher Simon & Schuster said it still plans to release Arday's memoir, "Great and Unfortunate Things," later this month. Cambridge has not indicated when its review will conclude or whether additional findings will be released publicly.

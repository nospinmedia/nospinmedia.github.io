Lenovo Revenue Climbs 43% To Record $26.9 Billion

AI servers drive growth despite accounting-related quarterly loss

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 13, 2026 at 9:15 AM ET

Lenovo Group reported record fiscal first-quarter revenue of $26.94 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, a 43% increase from a year earlier that exceeded analyst expectations. Adjusted net income rose 176% to $1.075 billion, reflecting continued demand for artificial intelligence infrastructure, enterprise computing, and related services.

The company's Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) led the growth, with revenue rising 98% to $8.5 billion and operating profit reaching $777 million. Lenovo said AI-related revenue climbed to $9.3 billion, accounting for about 35% of total company sales. The Intelligent Devices Group (IDG) generated $17.1 billion in revenue, up 27%, driven by business demand for AI-capable personal computers and enterprise hardware.

Despite the strong operating performance, Lenovo reported a headline net loss of $609 million under generally accepted accounting principles after recording a $1.69 billion non-cash fair-value adjustment tied to warrants issued in 2025. Company executives said the accounting adjustment did not reflect day-to-day operating performance and emphasized that underlying earnings remained strong.

Lenovo also reported its AI server order backlog grew 157% from the previous quarter to $54 billion, driven by demand from cloud service providers and enterprise AI deployments. Company leadership said that, based on its current revenue trajectory, Lenovo is on pace to surpass its long-term goal of $100 billion in annual revenue ahead of schedule. Executives said continued investment in Hybrid AI products, infrastructure, and services is expected to support future growth.

- Quarter ended: June 30, 2026 - Revenue: $26.94 billion (+43% year over year) - Adjusted net income: $1.075 billion (+176% year over year) - Headline GAAP net income: -$609 million after a $1.69 billion non-cash accounting adjustment - AI server backlog: $54 billion (+157% quarter over quarter) - AI-related revenue: $9.3 billion (about 35% of total sales)

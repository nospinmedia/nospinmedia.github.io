Russia warns NATO of nuclear response over Kaliningrad

Moscow claims alliance is preparing Baltic blockade

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 1, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

Russia has warned NATO that it is prepared to use its full arsenal, including nuclear weapons, if the alliance attempts to isolate the Russian exclave of Kaliningrad, according to a diplomatic document sent to NATO member states on September 30.

The Russian document claimed Moscow had information that NATO was preparing an air and naval blockade of Kaliningrad. Russia said that if the territory were isolated from the rest of the country, it could respond with the forces and capabilities available to it, including nuclear weapons. The document also raised the possibility of strikes against decision-making centers in NATO countries from the outset of a conflict. NATO has rejected Russia's allegation that the alliance is preparing threatening action against Russian territory.

NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte said the alliance is defensive and called on Russia to stop making nuclear threats. NATO's response said its exercises and military activities do not threaten Russian territory. Lithuania, which borders Kaliningrad, separately rejected Moscow's claims and said Russia's threats were intended to undermine NATO unity and Western support for Ukraine.

Kaliningrad is Russian territory on the Baltic Sea separated geographically from mainland Russia and positioned between NATO members Poland and Lithuania. The exclave is an important Russian military outpost and hosts elements of the Baltic Fleet as well as nuclear-capable Iskander missile systems. Its location has long made it a potential flashpoint in any confrontation between Russia and NATO.

The warning comes amid heightened tensions around the Baltic region and Russia's continuing war in Ukraine. Poland has recently increased its air-defense readiness following reported airspace incursions, while NATO countries have continued military exercises and surveillance operations in the region. Russia has denied Western accusations that it is conducting sabotage and other destabilization operations in Europe.

GTA VI Shatters Records With $3.79 Billion In First-Day Sales

Rockstar Says Blockbuster Game Had Biggest Entertainment Launch Ever

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 21, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Grand Theft Auto VI generated approximately $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day, according to Rockstar Games, setting a record for the biggest launch in the entertainment industry and demonstrating the enormous demand surrounding one of gaming's most anticipated releases.

The sales figure puts GTA VI well beyond the opening-day performance of previous major video game releases and places its launch alongside — and by Rockstar's measure ahead of — the largest debuts across entertainment. Reuters reported the figure at approximately $3.8 billion, while Bloomberg described it as the biggest entertainment launch ever.

The record follows years of anticipation for the next installment in the Grand Theft Auto franchise. Its predecessor, Grand Theft Auto V, became one of the most commercially successful entertainment products ever released after debuting in 2013, giving Rockstar an unusually large established audience for the sequel.

The new game's launch also carries significance for the broader video game industry. Major releases increasingly require enormous development budgets and years of production, making blockbuster titles financially important not only to their publishers but also to console makers, retailers and other companies connected to the gaming market.

The first-day number represents retail sales reported by Rockstar, rather than an independently audited accounting of the publisher's revenue. The amount consumers spend at retail is not necessarily the same as the money ultimately retained by Rockstar or parent company Take-Two Interactive after platform fees, retailer shares and other costs.

Even with that distinction, the scale of the launch shows how large the video game business has become within the wider entertainment industry. A single game generated several billion dollars in consumer spending in approximately one day.

With $3.79 billion in first-day sales, GTA VI now faces the longer-term question of whether it can match the extraordinary staying power of its predecessor.

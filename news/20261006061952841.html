Massachusetts man admits importing plutonium from Russia

Federal prosecutors say he sold radioactive material to about 67 customers

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 6, 2026 at 5:16 PM ET

Jacob D. Miller, 44, of South Hadley, Massachusetts, pleaded guilty on October 5 to illegally importing plutonium from Russia and selling the radioactive material to customers in the United States and abroad. Federal prosecutors said Miller sold plutonium to approximately 67 customers between December 2020 and March 2025.

Miller operated a business called Collect The Periodic Table (CTPT) from his residence, selling samples of elements directly and through the online marketplace Bonanza. Prosecutors said he arranged to have plutonium mailed to him from Russia and then shipped it to customers through the U.S. mail, even though the radioactive material was prohibited from being mailed. He also sold highly toxic thallium to approximately 71 customers between 2017 and 2025.

The guilty plea went well beyond the radioactive-material sales. Miller admitted to one count each of unlawfully importing nuclear material, mailing hazardous materials, being a felon in possession of ammunition, being a felon in possession of explosive materials and possessing child pornography. The last charge carries a mandatory minimum sentence of 10 years because Miller had a previous child-pornography conviction.

Miller was convicted in Massachusetts state court in 2004 of possessing child pornography and an explosive device. During an April 2025 search of his residence, investigators recovered bottles of explosive powder, numerous rounds of ammunition and six rifles. Prosecutors said his laptop and cellphone also contained child sexual abuse material that he had accessed as recently as the day before the search.

The investigation also revealed earlier concerns about Miller's radioactive-material sales. According to federal court records, the Massachusetts Radiation Control Program ordered him in 2014 to stop selling depleted uranium on eBay. Prosecutors said he later established CTPT and continued selling radioactive and hazardous substances.

Miller has remained in federal custody since his April 9, 2025 arrest. U.S. District Judge Mark G. Mastroianni scheduled sentencing for January 21, 2027.

U.S. approves potential $24.3 billion F-35 sale to Saudi Arabia

Package includes 48 stealth fighters and 49 engines

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 18, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

The U.S. State Department has approved a potential $24.3 billion sale of 48 F-35A stealth fighter jets and 49 engines to Saudi Arabia, clearing a major step in a proposed weapons package that could significantly expand the kingdom's air-combat capabilities. The proposed sale has been formally notified to Congress, which now has an opportunity to review it.

The package would make Lockheed Martin the principal contractor for the F-35 aircraft, while RTX's Pratt & Whitney would supply the engines. State Department approval does not mean the transaction has been completed, and the final value and number of aircraft could change during negotiations and congressional review.

The proposal carries broader significance because Israel is currently the only country in the Middle East operating the F-35. U.S. arms-sale policy is also subject to a legal requirement to preserve Israel's qualitative military edge, meaning proposed sales of advanced weapons to other Middle Eastern countries must be evaluated for their potential effect on Israel's military advantages.

Saudi Arabia has sought to modernize its armed forces while deepening its defense relationship with Washington. The kingdom initially requested the F-35s from President Trump in 2025, and the administration has pursued expanded defense cooperation with Riyadh. The proposal comes during heightened regional tensions involving Iran, fighting between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis in Yemen, and continuing threats to energy infrastructure and shipping routes.

The F-35 is designed to combine stealth, advanced sensors and networked combat capabilities, making its potential introduction into the Saudi air force a substantial upgrade from the kingdom's existing fighter fleet. Congressional review will determine whether lawmakers attempt to block or modify the proposed transaction before it advances toward a final agreement.

Arrest Made In Virginia State University Shooting

Five wounded as classes prepare to begin Monday

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 16, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

Authorities have arrested a 19-year-old suspect in connection with a shooting that wounded five people at Virginia State University (VSU), as the campus prepares to begin the fall semester. Chesterfield County Police announced that Camron Harris, of Henrico, Virginia, was taken into custody after officers found him hiding inside a dormitory room on August 15. Investigators said Harris is not a VSU student.

The shooting occurred shortly after 1:30 a.m. outside residence halls near the university's Quad Annexes in Ettrick, Virginia. Victims ranged in age from 17 to 23, with one person remaining in critical condition. Several others were treated for injuries, including a 20-year-old VSU student who was later released from the hospital.

Police obtained eight felony warrants against Harris, including multiple counts of malicious wounding and use of a firearm in the commission of a felony. Investigators have not announced a motive for the shooting. Earlier alerts indicated officers were searching for multiple possible suspects, but following Harris's arrest, authorities said there is no immediate threat to the campus while the investigation continues.

The investigation is being led by the Chesterfield County Police Department with assistance from the Virginia State University Police Department, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF) and other law enforcement agencies. Detectives continue reviewing evidence and interviewing witnesses to determine what led to the shooting.

University officials said residence halls have reopened and preparations for the start of classes on Monday remain on schedule. They encouraged students and employees to remain alert and report any information that could assist investigators as the case moves forward.

Russia Launches Massive Overnight Missile And Drone Assault Across Ukraine

Kyiv And Multiple Cities Hit As Air Defenses Face One Of Summer's Largest Attacks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished July 30, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Russia launched one of its largest combined aerial assaults of the summer overnight on July 30, firing 74 missiles and 284 drones at Kyiv and multiple regions across Ukraine. According to the Ukrainian Air Force, air defense systems intercepted 55 missiles and 265 attack drones, but dozens of weapons penetrated defenses or falling debris caused widespread destruction across several cities.

The nationwide attack killed at least eight people and injured more than 50 others. Ukrainian officials said a ballistic missile strike on a residential area near Kryvyi Rih killed two girls, ages 5 and 12, along with four adults, while additional casualties were reported elsewhere as emergency crews responded to damage across the country. In Kyiv, thousands of residents sheltered inside underground metro stations as repeated waves of Kh-101 cruise missiles, Iskander-M ballistic missiles, and attack drones targeted the capital, sparking fires and damaging residential neighborhoods.

The bombardment also struck the Lviv, Dnipropetrovsk, and Poltava regions. Lviv Governor Maksym Kozytskyi reported injuries and significant damage to residential buildings after missile impacts. As the attacks unfolded, Poland scrambled fighter aircraft to protect NATO airspace because the strikes occurred near the alliance's eastern border.

The assault came just hours after President Volodymyr Zelenskyy warned that intelligence indicated Russia was preparing a large-scale attack and shortly after his meeting in Washington with President Trump, where Ukraine renewed requests for additional Patriot air defense interceptors. Ukrainian officials said the latest barrage demonstrated Russia's continued use of long-range missiles and drones to pressure Ukraine's air defenses and critical infrastructure.

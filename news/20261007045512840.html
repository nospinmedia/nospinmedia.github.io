Whistleblower says Trump pushed FBI probe after seeing protesters on TikTok

Former analyst says agents found no evidence of a crime

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 7, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

A former FBI supervisory intelligence analyst alleges President Trump demanded an investigation of anti-immigration-enforcement protesters in Southern California after seeing them in a TikTok video, despite concerns inside the bureau that their conduct was protected by the First Amendment.

The allegation comes from Jill Fields, formerly a supervisory intelligence analyst in the FBI's Los Angeles field office. According to her whistleblower disclosure, protesters in Alhambra, California, were using megaphones during a February 2025 demonstration to warn residents about the presence of ICE and Customs and Border Protection officers and tell people about their legal rights. Fields said an initial FBI assessment found the protesters' conduct — including recording officers, using a megaphone and verbally objecting — did not cross into criminal activity.

Fields alleges that FBI Director Kash Patel subsequently told senior bureau officials that President Trump was angry about the incident and wanted the protesters investigated and arrested. Her disclosure says personnel were ordered to conduct checks on roughly two to four protesters even though investigators did not yet have evidence of a federal crime. Fields also described a separate incident in which then-Acting Deputy Attorney General Emil Bove allegedly pushed for an investigation of protesters outside a federal building.

The White House disputes the allegations, saying the First Amendment does not protect violent rioting, obstruction or assaults on law enforcement. The Justice Department said federal authorities have a responsibility to investigate potential violations of federal law while protecting Americans' First Amendment rights.

Democratic Sen. Alex Padilla of California has asked Patel and Attorney General Todd Blanche for information about how many protest-related investigations were opened, their outcomes and whether President Trump directed other investigations. Fields alleges she lost responsibilities after raising objections and eventually resigned from the FBI.

Sweden Election Poll Shows Race Tightening

Government bloc cuts opposition lead ahead of September vote

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 13, 2026 at 9:25 AM ET

A Demoskop poll released on August 13, 2026, shows Sweden's center-left opposition maintaining its lead ahead of the country's September 13 general election, but by a narrower margin than earlier this summer. The survey found the opposition bloc leading 50.6% to 46.9%, reducing its advantage to 3.7 percentage points after a 7.6-point lead in June.

The poll found the opposition, led by Magdalena Andersson's Social Democrats, continuing to outpace the governing coalition headed by Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson's Moderate Party. Among coalition partners, the Christian Democrats, led by Ebba Busch, rose to 7.5%, their strongest showing in six years, while the Liberals remained below Sweden's 4% parliamentary threshold at 2.4%. The Sweden Democrats, another governing bloc partner, polled 20.2%.

The survey questioned 2,016 voters between July 29 and August 10. Based on the results, Demoskop projected the opposition would win approximately 186 seats in the 349-seat Riksdag, compared with 163 for parties aligned with the government. Because parties must receive at least 4% of the national vote to enter parliament, the Liberals' performance could influence the final distribution of seats.

The campaign has focused on issues including the cost of living, crime and gang violence, immigration, energy policy, and national security. With one month remaining before election day, additional polling will help indicate whether the narrowing gap reflects a lasting shift in voter sentiment or normal movement during the campaign.

- Election date: September 13, 2026 - Poll: Demoskop (2,016 respondents) - Opposition lead: 50.6% to 46.9% - Parliamentary threshold: 4% of the national vote - Projected seats: 186 opposition, 163 government-aligned parties

White House blocks Politico from Air Force One press pool

No primary print reporter travels after other outlets decline seat

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 6, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

The White House blocked Politico from traveling aboard Air Force One on October 5, preventing the outlet from serving in its scheduled role as the primary print pool reporter during President Trump's trip to Nebraska. Other news organizations declined to take Politico's place, leaving the presidential flight without a primary print pool reporter.

The presidential press pool is a rotating group of journalists that provides reports to the broader White House press corps when space or security restrictions prevent all reporters from attending an event. Politico had been assigned the primary print position for Monday's trip. The Washington Post, which was serving as the secondary print pooler, provided updates during the flight, while the television pool also distributed information to reporters.

The exclusion comes amid an ongoing lawsuit involving Politico, CNN and MS NOW over access to the White House. President Trump announced in September that the three organizations would be barred from the White House complex. U.S. District Judge Timothy J. Kelly subsequently issued a temporary restraining order requiring the administration to restore their access to the White House grounds while the legal dispute proceeds.

That order did not explicitly require the administration to include the outlets in limited-capacity presidential pool assignments. The White House has argued in court that the president has broad authority to control access to restricted areas such as Air Force One and the Oval Office. Lawyers representing the news organizations contend that excluding them because of their coverage amounts to unconstitutional viewpoint discrimination and violates their due-process rights.

Judge Kelly is scheduled to consider the outlets' request for a longer-lasting preliminary injunction on October 8. The organizations are seeking restoration of the access they had before the September restrictions, including participation in presidential press pools.

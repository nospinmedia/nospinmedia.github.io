Philadelphia officer killed, partner shot 12 times responding to gun call

10-year veteran Shane Seiber killed as suspect faces murder charges

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 11, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

A Philadelphia police officer was killed and another was shot 12 times after they responded to a report of an armed man in West Philadelphia early Saturday, October 10, 2026. Police identified the slain officer as Shane Seiber, a 40-year-old, 10-year veteran of the department and father of two.

The surviving officer, a 33-year-old whose name had not initially been released, suffered multiple gunshot wounds and underwent surgery. Authorities said he was stabilized following treatment.

Police identified the suspect as 35-year-old Eric Lewis, a Philadelphia Water Department employee. Lewis was charged with murder and attempted murder in connection with the shooting.

Authorities said officers were responding to reports of a man with a gun when they encountered Lewis on an enclosed porch. Police said Lewis opened fire on the responding officers, striking Seiber and his partner. Investigators said Lewis fired at 11 officers in total during the confrontation, although only two were struck.

Seiber and the wounded officer were transported to Penn Presbyterian Medical Center. Seiber later died from his injuries, while doctors treated his partner for the numerous gunshot wounds he sustained during the attack.

The shooting prompted a large police response and an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the confrontation. Detectives are reviewing evidence from the scene, including available body-worn camera footage and witness accounts, as prosecutors prepare the case against Lewis.

Philadelphia officials and members of the police department mourned Seiber following confirmation of his death. His killing represents a line-of-duty death for a veteran officer who had served the city for a decade.

The investigation remains active as authorities work to establish the complete sequence of events and determine what led to the initial report of an armed man and the subsequent attack on responding officers.

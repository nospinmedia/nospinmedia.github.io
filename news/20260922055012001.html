Iran threatens to expand war if U.S. launches new attack

Tehran warns different weapons and new areas could be targeted

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 22, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has warned that another U.S. attack would prompt Tehran to use different weapons and expand the conflict into new areas, as President Trump considers whether to intensify military action or pursue another diplomatic effort to end the nearly seven-month war.

IRGC spokesman Hossein Mohebbi said Iran would change both its weaponry and the "geography of the war" if the United States launches another assault. He also said Tehran is prepared for a prolonged conflict. The warning did not specify which weapons Iran might use or what additional locations could become targets.

The statement comes as President Trump weighs his next move toward Iran. Trump said Sunday that he was in "deciding mode" and indicated that his options include renewed military action, continued economic pressure or another attempt at a negotiated settlement. He also said he would probably be willing to meet Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian while both leaders are in New York for the United Nations General Assembly this week.

The tensions extend beyond Iran itself. U.S. embassies across the Middle East issued security alerts advising Americans to exercise heightened caution and prepare for possible travel disruptions because of the potential for further regional escalation. Fighting has also intensified between Iran-backed Houthi forces in Yemen and a Saudi-backed coalition, adding another active front to the broader conflict.

There are simultaneous signs of possible diplomacy. Pezeshkian is expected to travel to New York for the U.N. gathering, while regional governments including Pakistan, Qatar and Egypt have been involved in diplomatic efforts during the conflict. Whether direct U.S.-Iran talks will occur remains uncertain.

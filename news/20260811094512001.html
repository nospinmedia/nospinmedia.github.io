Trump Media Reports $238M Quarterly Loss

Company pivots back to core media business and Truth API

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 11, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

Trump Media & Technology Group (TMTG), the parent company of Truth Social, reported a $238.1 million net loss for the second quarter of 2026, compared with a $20 million loss during the same period a year earlier. The company reported a per-share loss of $0.86, while quarterly revenue increased to about $1.7 million. Shares of DJT fell about 8% following the earnings report.

The company said most of the quarterly loss resulted from unrealized declines in the value of digital assets and equity investments, including holdings of Bitcoin and Cronos, rather than day-to-day operating expenses. Interim Chief Executive Officer Kevin McGurn said the company will scale back many recent initiatives outside its core media operations, including most cryptocurrency and online betting efforts, and instead focus on expanding Truth Social, Truth+, and its new Truth API data-licensing business.

McGurn said Truth API has signed more than 10 institutional customers, primarily high-frequency trading firms, with subscription prices ranging from $60,000 to $100,000 per month. The company said the service is intended to provide a recurring source of licensing revenue by offering low-latency access to public posts from influential Truth Social accounts.

TMTG ended the quarter with approximately $400 million in cash and short-term investments and about $1.2 billion in bitcoin and bitcoin-related assets. The company also said it continues working toward its previously announced merger with TAE Technologies, a nuclear fusion company, while maintaining that its primary strategic focus is now its media platforms and related data services.

Northern Cyprus Ferry Disaster Kills 8

Captain and Crew Detained as Search Continues

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 31, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

Authorities in Northern Cyprus detained the captain and seven crew members of the passenger ferry Filo Jet after the vessel capsized and sank shortly after departing Kyrenia for Taşucu, Turkey, leaving at least eight people dead and 18 others missing. Officials said the catamaran was carrying 267 people, including 259 passengers and eight crew members, when it reportedly struck an object or suffered a serious impact near its bow before rapidly taking on water and overturning on August 30.

Emergency responders, the Turkish Coast Guard, nearby commercial vessels, and private boats rescued 241 survivors, with many passengers pulled from the overturned hull before the ferry sank into waters estimated to be more than 500 meters deep. Search-and-rescue operations continued overnight and into August 31, using helicopters, patrol vessels, and specialized diving teams to search for those still unaccounted for. Officials warned that the vessel's depth makes recovery efforts significantly more difficult.

Police launched a criminal investigation into the sinking while detaining the captain and crew for questioning. Survivors told local media they encountered serious problems during the evacuation, alleging that access to life jackets was limited, some cabin doors were locked, and evacuation procedures were poorly coordinated. Investigators have not yet determined the cause of the disaster and said all claims will be examined as part of the inquiry.

Northern Cyprus leaders declared three days of national mourning, calling the tragedy one of the territory's deadliest modern maritime disasters. Authorities pledged a full investigation into whether mechanical failure, navigational error, weather conditions, or safety violations contributed to the sinking. Rescue teams remain focused on locating the missing passengers while support services continue assisting survivors and the families of those affected.

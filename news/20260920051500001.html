White House begins enforcing Trump media ban

CNN, MS NOW and Politico reporters lose access

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 20, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

The White House began enforcing President Trump's ban on CNN, MS NOW and Politico on September 19, one day after he announced the restrictions but provided no details about how they would be implemented. Reporters from all three organizations were denied entry Saturday, turning what had initially been an announced ban into an enforced restriction on their White House access.

CNN senior White House reporter Betsy Klein and MS NOW White House reporter Akayla Gardner said their press credentials had been deactivated when they arrived for work. Gardner said she passed through two gates before an officer told her at a badge scanner that her credential was disabled and asked her to surrender it. Klein said her credential was also deactivated and confiscated. CBS News independently observed Gardner and Klein being turned away. Politico said White House reporter Cheyenne Haslett was stopped by the Secret Service, denied entry and had her pass confiscated.

The restrictions did not appear to affect every employee of the organizations in the same way. Gardner reported that an MS NOW producer was allowed to enter while she and a photographer were denied access. The White House referred questions about the restrictions to Trump's previous comments, in which he said the outlets were being banned because he considered their reporting to be "fake news." He did not identify specific reports as the basis for the decision.

The three organizations said they would continue covering the administration and defend their First Amendment rights. Politico Editor-in-Chief Jonathan Greenberger said the organization would "vigorously defend" those rights, while MS NOW said it would take necessary steps to defend its journalists. White House Correspondents' Association President Jacqui Heinrich called for the reporters' access to be restored. No lawsuit by the three organizations had been announced in the reporting reviewed for this update.

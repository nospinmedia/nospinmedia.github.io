Angie Nixon Defeats Alex Vindman in Florida Democratic Senate Primary

Progressive upset sets up November race against Sen. Ashley Moody

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 19, 2026 at 7:48 AM ET

Florida State Representative Angie Nixon won Florida's Democratic nomination for the U.S. Senate on August 18, 2026, defeating former Army Lt. Col. Alexander Vindman in one of the cycle's biggest primary upsets. Unofficial results showed Nixon receiving about 55.9% of the vote to 44.1% for Vindman, who later conceded the race and pledged to support Nixon in the general election.

The victory came despite a significant fundraising disadvantage. Through late July, Nixon had raised roughly $975,000, compared with approximately $16.3 million for Vindman, a more than 16-to-1 gap. Nixon built her campaign around progressive priorities including expanded healthcare access, affordable housing and childcare while relying heavily on grassroots organizing rather than large fundraising networks.

Nixon now advances to the November 3, 2026, special general election against Republican Sen. Ashley Moody, who was appointed to the Senate in January 2025 after Marco Rubio resigned to become U.S. Secretary of State. Republicans currently hold roughly a 1.5 million voter registration advantage in Florida, making the state one of the GOP's strongest electoral battlegrounds. Florida has not elected a Democrat to the U.S. Senate since 2012, giving Nixon a difficult path despite her primary victory.

The outcome highlights ongoing debate within the Democratic Party over the strength of progressive grassroots campaigns versus establishment-backed candidates. While Nixon's upset demonstrated that well-funded campaigns are not always decisive in primary elections, the November contest will test whether that coalition can compete statewide in a Republican-leaning electorate.

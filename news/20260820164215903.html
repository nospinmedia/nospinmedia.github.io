IDF Opens Criminal Probes Into Hind Rajab Killing and Gaza Medics' Deaths

Army also closes World Central Kitchen and MSF cases without charges

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 20, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) on Aug. 19, 2026, ordered criminal Military Police investigations into two wartime incidents in Gaza, including the January 2024 killing of 5-year-old Hind Rajab, six relatives, and two responding medics. For the first time, the military acknowledged its troops fired on the family's vehicle and a Palestine Red Crescent Society ambulance sent to rescue them, reversing earlier assessments that Israeli forces were not operating in the immediate area.

The Military Advocate General also directed a criminal investigation into the March 2025 killings of 15 emergency rescue workers in southern Gaza. Both cases were referred after reviews by the military's Fact-Finding and Assessment Mechanism, which has examined roughly 150 incidents involving civilian harm during the conflict to determine whether criminal investigations are warranted.

At the same time, the IDF closed three other high-profile investigations without filing criminal charges, including the April 2024 strike that killed 7 World Central Kitchen aid workers and two separate incidents that killed 4 Doctors Without Borders (MSF) staff members. The military concluded those cases involved operational failures rather than criminal misconduct, a finding sharply criticized by World Central Kitchen founder José Andrés, humanitarian organizations, and legal advocates who continue to question the effectiveness of internal military investigations.

Rights groups, including the Israeli legal organization Yesh Din, noted that internal military investigations have historically resulted in very few indictments. Victims' families and Palestinian officials renewed calls for independent accountability through the International Criminal Court (ICC), while the IDF said it would provide updates as the newly opened criminal investigations progress and additional evidence is gathered.

Trump's economic approval falls to 26% in new AP-NORC poll

65% of Americans say his policies bear more blame for high prices

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 2, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

Americans' approval of President Trump's handling of the economy has fallen to a new low, with a new AP-NORC Center for Public Affairs Research poll finding that just 26% of U.S. adults approve of his overall economic performance as concerns about groceries, gasoline and other living expenses remain widespread.

The poll found an even lower 17% approval rating for Trump's handling of the cost of living. About 65% of U.S. adults said Trump's policies deserve more blame for persistently high prices than factors outside his control. His economic approval has fallen 14 percentage points from 40% in March 2025, shortly after he began his second term. About 7 in 10 Americans said his handling of the cost of living has been worse than they expected.

Financial concerns extend beyond assessments of the president. About half of adults said they are extremely or very concerned about being able to afford gasoline, up from 39% in July, and roughly half expressed the same level of concern about paying for food. The dissatisfaction reaches into Trump's own party: roughly half of Republicans said his handling of living costs has been worse than they expected, although Republican views of his overall performance remain substantially more positive than those of Democrats and independents.

The survey was conducted September 24-28 among 2,140 U.S. adults using NORC's probability-based AmeriSpeak Panel. The margin of sampling error was plus or minus 2.9 percentage points.

The results arrive just over a month before the November 3 midterm elections, as Trump campaigns for Republican candidates and argues that his economic policies are succeeding. The poll measures public opinion at a specific point in time and does not by itself predict how Americans will vote in November.

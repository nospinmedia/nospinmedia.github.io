Masked Israeli Settlers Attack European Delegation in West Bank

Diplomats and Officials Targeted During Visit to Palestinian Olive Harvest

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 18, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Masked Israeli settlers attacked a European delegation and Palestinians during an olive-harvest visit in the occupied West Bank on October 17, according to European officials and witnesses. The delegation included diplomats and other European officials who had traveled to the area to observe conditions facing Palestinian farmers during the annual harvest.

The confrontation occurred near Taybeh, northeast of Ramallah, where European representatives were accompanying Palestinians harvesting olives. Witnesses said masked settlers arrived and confronted the group, attacking people and damaging vehicles. Video from the scene showed masked individuals striking vehicles as members of the delegation attempted to leave. European officials said some participants were injured, but no life-threatening injuries were reported.

Belgian Foreign Minister Maxime Prévot said members of a Belgian delegation were among those attacked and condemned the violence. Representatives from several European countries were participating in the visit, including officials connected to Belgium, France and Poland. French local official Bernard Baudoux, mayor of Aulnoye-Aymeries, was among those reported assaulted during the incident.

The attack occurred during the Palestinian olive harvest, a period that has repeatedly produced confrontations between Palestinians and Israeli settlers in parts of the West Bank. European governments and human-rights organizations have raised concerns about violence against Palestinian farmers and access to agricultural land during the harvest.

Israeli authorities condemned the attack. The Israel Defense Forces described violence against civilians and members of the delegation as unacceptable, while Israeli police opened an investigation. Israeli authorities also said the delegation's visit had not been coordinated in advance with security forces.

No arrests had been publicly announced in the immediate aftermath of the attack. European officials called for those responsible to be held accountable and for greater protection of Palestinians and international visitors during the harvest.

Democrats score historic victories in Texas

Senate and governor races break decades of GOP dominance

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 4, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Texas voters delivered two major Democratic victories Tuesday, electing James Talarico to the U.S. Senate and Gina Hinojosa as governor in results that broke decades-long Republican winning streaks in statewide races.

Talarico defeated Republican Ken Paxton, the state's attorney general, becoming the first Democrat to win a U.S. Senate election in Texas since 1988. His victory also contributed to the Democratic takeover of the Senate nationally. Talarico, a state representative and former public-school teacher, entered a race that had drawn national attention after Paxton emerged as the Republican nominee.

Hinojosa separately defeated three-term Republican Gov. Greg Abbott, giving Democrats control of the Texas governor's office after more than three decades of Republican victories. Abbott had served as governor since 2015. Hinojosa, a Democratic state representative from Austin, had challenged Abbott in a state where Republicans had consistently won statewide offices for decades.

The two victories were part of a much broader Democratic showing in Texas. Political coverage described the results as a statewide sweep, transforming what had long been one of the Republican Party's most reliable large-state strongholds. The significance extends beyond Texas: the state has 40 electoral votes, the second-largest congressional delegation in the country and a major role in national Republican politics.

The results do not necessarily establish that Texas has permanently shifted toward Democrats. One election can reflect candidate matchups, turnout and the national political environment as much as a lasting partisan realignment. But victories in both the governor's race and a U.S. Senate contest represent a sharp break from recent Texas history and will give Democrats major governing and political footholds heading toward the 2028 presidential election.

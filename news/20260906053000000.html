MAGA Inc. puts $10 million into Texas Senate race

Trump-aligned group makes first major general-election ad buy

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 6, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

President Trump's allied super PAC, MAGA Inc., is spending $10 million on advertising in the Texas U.S. Senate race, its first significant investment in a major general-election contest of the 2026 midterms. A Federal Election Commission filing disclosed September 5 shows the spending divided evenly: $5 million for advertising supporting Republican nominee Ken Paxton and $5 million for advertising opposing Democratic nominee James Talarico. The advertisements are slated for digital and connected television platforms.

The intervention comes after Paxton, the Texas attorney general, defeated incumbent Republican Sen. John Cornyn in a primary runoff with Trump's endorsement. Talarico, a 37-year-old state representative, has substantially outraised Paxton and dominated general-election advertising through much of the summer. During the second quarter, Talarico raised more than $30 million, compared with more than $9 million for Paxton, according to campaign finance figures reported by The Texas Tribune.

MAGA Inc.'s advertisements focus heavily on taxes. The pro-Paxton messaging promotes proposals for tax relief involving health care and housing expenses, while its anti-Talarico advertising criticizes the Democrat's tax record. Talarico's campaign disputes the characterization, pointing to his support for property-tax reductions and an economic plan that would increase taxes on corporations and billionaires while expanding tax credits for parents and lower-income workers.

The $10 million purchase adds to substantial outside spending already flowing into the contest. America PAC, backed by Elon Musk, has spent about $1.4 million opposing Talarico, while other Republican-aligned organizations have also supported Paxton. Before MAGA Inc.'s new advertisements began running, Democrats had spent about $41.7 million on the Texas general-election race compared with $24.3 million by Republicans, according to advertising data reported by The Texas Tribune. MAGA Inc. had more than $400 million available at its latest reporting point, leaving it with substantial resources for additional congressional races before November.

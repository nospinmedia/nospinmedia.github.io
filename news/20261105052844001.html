U.S. Marine dies after training incident in Japan

25-year-old Maryland Marine identified as investigation continues

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 5, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

A U.S. Marine died Tuesday after sustaining fatal injuries during fast-rope training at Camp Schwab in Okinawa, Japan, military officials said. The circumstances surrounding the training accident remain under investigation.

Officials identified the Marine as Lance Cpl. Devin J. Hernandez, 25, of Maryland. Hernandez was a 1371 Combat Engineer assigned to 2nd Battalion, 6th Marines, 2nd Marine Division, and was attached to the 3rd Marine Division in Okinawa at the time of the incident.

Fast-rope training involves service members descending from a hovering aircraft using a thick rope, allowing troops to deploy quickly in locations where an aircraft cannot land. Military officials have not released additional details about what occurred during Hernandez's exercise or what caused his fatal injuries.

Maj. Gen. Kyle Ellison, commanding general of the 3rd Marine Division, expressed condolences to Hernandez's family and said the division's focus remains on supporting them while investigators determine the circumstances surrounding the incident.

Military records show Hernandez had received the Navy Arctic Service Ribbon during his service. Camp Schwab is one of several U.S. military installations on Okinawa and supports Marine Corps training and operations throughout the Indo-Pacific region.

The Marine Corps has not announced the results of its investigation or whether equipment, procedures or other factors contributed to the accident. No other casualties have been reported in connection with the training incident.

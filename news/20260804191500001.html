SpaceX Reports First Earnings as a Public Company Today

Lockup to free 911.5M shares Thursday as investors eye Starlink, AI

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 4, 2026 at 5:45 PM ET

SpaceX will release its first quarterly results as a public company after the U.S. market close on August 4, 2026, a closely watched debut for Elon Musk’s newly listed rocket-and-connectivity firm. The milestone comes less than two months after the company’s IPO at $135 per share on June 12, 2026. Shares have since cooled from an intraday peak of $225.64 on June 16 and hovered near $110 on Monday, focusing attention on whether guidance, cash needs, and subscriber growth can steady sentiment.

The company said it will publish results after the bell and hold an analyst call this evening. Ahead of the print, analysts expect roughly $6.9 billion in second‑quarter revenue, with the connectivity business led by Starlink viewed as the primary driver, according to S&P Global’s Visible Alpha. In filings, SpaceX reported $18.7 billion in 2025 revenue and a net loss of more than $4.9 billion, framing tonight’s update as a test of how quickly operating losses in launch, exploration, and AI initiatives can narrow. The company also disclosed that its AI segment produced $818 million in revenue in Q1 2026.

The near‑term stock setup is unusually sensitive because a major lock‑up expiration lands two days later. On Thursday, August 6, more than 911.5 million Class A shares become eligible to trade, materially increasing supply and potentially amplifying volatility as early holders gain the option to sell. Market participants will parse management’s commentary on capital needs, margin trajectory, and deployment plans to assess how the added float might interact with fundamentals in the weeks ahead.

Investors will look for concrete signals on several fronts: - Revenue and margin direction versus the roughly $6.9B consensus - Starlink subscriber and churn trends and any ARPU commentary - AI spending cadence and commercialization milestones following the Q1 2026 revenue disclosure - Balance‑sheet items, including liquidity, cash burn, and any update to capital expenditure plans

With the first public earnings and a sizable share unlock landing within 48 hours, how management frames growth, spending, and the path to profitability may matter as much as the numbers themselves.

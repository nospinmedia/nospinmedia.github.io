Delta automation outage disrupts Thanksgiving travel nationwide

Atlanta ground stop gives way to more than 1,900 delays

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 23, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A failure in Delta Air Lines' automated weight-and-balance system disrupted flights across the United States on November 22, triggering an Atlanta ground stop and contributing to more than 1,900 delays as Thanksgiving travel accelerated.

The system calculates how passengers, baggage, cargo and fuel are distributed aboard aircraft before departure. Delta said crews and dispatchers temporarily used manual weight-and-balance procedures while the automation was unavailable. The airline said the disruption was not caused by a cyberattack or hack.

At Delta's request, the Federal Aviation Administration issued a ground stop for Delta flights headed to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport at 7:46 a.m. ET. The FAA replaced it with a ground-delay program at 8:32 a.m. and lifted the restrictions at 11:26 a.m. The FAA said its own air traffic control systems were not responsible for Delta's technical problem.

Delta and Delta Connection had roughly 5,000 flights scheduled Sunday. FlightAware recorded more than 1,900 delays by Sunday evening and 202 U.S. cancellations, representing about 4% of the airline's schedule. Delta said roughly 70% of its FlightAware-tracked delays and cancellations resulted from the automation problem, with weather and Northeast air traffic programs contributing to the remainder.

In New York, 42 Delta flights headed for LaGuardia Airport were diverted amid a combination of weather and air traffic restrictions. Delta said its automated system was restored Sunday afternoon, although delays continued as aircraft and crews were repositioned.

The disruption occurred as AAA projected nearly 6 million Americans would travel domestically by air during the Thanksgiving holiday period. Delta said affected travelers were being rebooked and that refunds, meals and hotel accommodations were being provided when applicable.

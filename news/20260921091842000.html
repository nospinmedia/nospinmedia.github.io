ICE agent shoots DoorDash driver during Austin pursuit

Damaged car and protests add new questions about encounter

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 21, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

A U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent shot and wounded Wilber Rafael Garces Perez, a 28-year-old Venezuelan man, during an encounter in Austin, Texas, on September 20. His attorney and DoorDash confirmed that Garces Perez was actively making a food delivery when the encounter with federal agents occurred.

The Texas Department of Public Safety said ICE requested assistance with a vehicle pursuit involving a blue Toyota Corolla shortly before 1 p.m., but the shooting had already occurred when state troopers arrived. Austin City Council Member Mike Siegel, who went to the scene, said an ICE Ford Expedition rammed the Corolla and that an ICE officer then fired multiple shots. Siegel said he saw at least two apparent bullet holes in the car's rear passenger-side window. Those details describe Siegel's account of the scene; federal authorities had not publicly provided their own detailed sequence of events leading to the shooting.

Garces Perez suffered a gunshot wound to the torso and was initially hospitalized in serious but stable condition. His attorney, Kate Lincoln-Goldfinch, said he later was released from the hospital into what the family believed was ICE custody. She said Garces Perez called his wife after being shot and told her what had happened. His family and attorney said they initially struggled to obtain information about his condition and whereabouts.

The shooting drew demonstrators to the scene throughout the afternoon as a large law-enforcement presence remained nearby. Authorities later used non-lethal crowd-control measures as tensions increased around the demonstration.

Local officials also raised questions about who would investigate the shooting. Siegel said the crime scene was controlled by federal and state authorities and that Austin Police were not permitted to conduct the crime-scene investigation. Texas Rangers were assisting with the investigation, while Austin officials continued calling for an independent and transparent review of the agent's use of force.

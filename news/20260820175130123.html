Russian Missile and Drone Barrage Hits Kyiv, Killing at Least 13

Children’s facility and homes damaged; city declares mourning

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 20, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

A large overnight Russian missile-and-drone strike hit Kyiv and surrounding areas on August 20, 2026, killing at least 13 people and wounding about 40, according to Ukrainian officials. The barrage damaged residential buildings, schools, industrial facilities, and a children’s medical facility across multiple districts, prompting Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko to declare August 21, 2026, a citywide day of mourning.

Emergency services reported strikes across at least a dozen locations spanning the Solomianskyi, Darnytskyi, and Sviatoshynskyi districts. In Solomianskyi, authorities said seven residents died after a missile tore through the upper floors of a nine-story apartment building, igniting fires and trapping occupants. Utility operator DTEK said roughly 90,000 households initially lost power before emergency crews began restoring electricity.

The Ukrainian Air Force said Russia launched 168 Shahed attack drones along with cruise missiles, ballistic missiles, and hypersonic missiles in a coordinated effort to overwhelm Ukraine's air defenses. Officials said many drones and cruise missiles were intercepted, but faster ballistic and hypersonic weapons penetrated defenses over the capital. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy again appealed to allies for additional Patriot interceptors and other air-defense munitions, warning that shortages leave civilians increasingly vulnerable during mass attacks.

Search-and-rescue operations continued throughout the day as firefighters and emergency crews worked through damaged apartment blocks and other impacted sites. Officials cautioned the casualty toll could rise as debris is cleared and additional victims are located. The strike marks one of the largest combined missile-and-drone assaults on Kyiv in recent months, underscoring the continuing escalation of Russia's long-range campaign against Ukraine's capital.

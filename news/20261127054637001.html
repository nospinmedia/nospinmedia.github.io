UK contacts roughly 100,000 military reservists

Defense secretary calls move a precaution amid 'heightened threat environment'

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 27, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

Britain's Ministry of Defence is trying to contact roughly 95,000 to 100,000 former military personnel on its Strategic Reserve, asking them to update their contact information and civilian skills as the government increases military readiness amid what officials describe as a "heightened threat environment."

The outreach covers former personnel from the British Army, Royal Navy and Royal Air Force, but the MoD currently lacks valid email addresses or up-to-date contact information for roughly half of them. Around 50,000 have so far been reached by post, prompting the heads of all three armed services to publish open letters seeking updated details from those the ministry cannot directly reach.

The letters ask reservists to confirm information including their employment and skills acquired since leaving military service. Up to 1,000 Strategic Reservists could also be invited to participate voluntarily in NATO's Steadfast Defender exercise in spring 2027.

Defence Secretary Wes Streeting described maintaining accurate reserve records as a "sensible, necessary precaution," saying Britain and its NATO allies are operating in a heightened threat environment amid concerns involving Russia and hybrid warfare.

Government officials stressed that the outreach is not an immediate mobilization order. Veterans Minister Calvin Bailey described the effort as "good housekeeping" intended to give the government an accurate picture of where former personnel are and what skills they could provide if additional military capacity were needed.

The effort also follows recommendations in the UK's 2025 Strategic Defence Review and comes as Parliament considers the Armed Forces Bill 2026. The proposed legislation would raise the maximum recall age for eligible former non-officers from 55 to 65 and expand the circumstances in which the Defence Secretary could authorize reservists to be recalled when preparations for warlike operations are underway.

Strategic reservists are former regular service members who remain potentially subject to recall for a period after leaving active service. The current outreach seeks to improve readiness and records rather than order them back into service.

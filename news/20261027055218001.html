DOJ moves to charge James Comey for third time

New case alleges disclosure of national defense information

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 27, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

The Justice Department has taken steps toward bringing a third criminal case against former FBI Director James Comey, filing two counts accusing him of transmitting national defense information to someone not authorized to receive it.

The latest case is procedurally unusual. Prosecutors filed the charges through an information, rather than obtaining an indictment from a grand jury. A Justice Department spokesperson said the filing was made to preserve the case before the applicable statute of limitations expired. Comey has not yet been indicted by a grand jury or charged through a criminal complaint in this latest case.

The allegations concern information Comey is accused of disclosing in 2016. His attorney, Patrick Fitzgerald, said the charges relate to an October 5, 2016 New York Times article that had previously been investigated by federal prosecutors. Fitzgerald said Comey maintains his innocence and challenged both the substance of the allegations and the government's use of an information to preserve the charges.

The new action marks the third attempt by the Trump administration to prosecute Comey. An earlier case accusing him of making false statements to Congress was dismissed after a judge found that the prosecutor who secured the indictment had been unlawfully appointed. Comey was indicted again in April 2026 over an Instagram photograph showing seashells arranged as ‘86 47,’ which prosecutors allege constituted a threat against President Trump. Comey is challenging that prosecution.

Comey's lawyers argue the latest allegations were previously investigated without charges and contend the statute of limitations has expired. Prosecutors maintain their filing preserved the government's ability to pursue the case. The latest charges would ultimately require further legal proceedings before the government could prosecute Comey on them.

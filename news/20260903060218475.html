Gunman kills 2 in Minneapolis high-rise shooting

Three officers wounded before suspect dies

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 3, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

Two civilians were killed and three Minneapolis police officers were wounded after a gunman opened fire inside a downtown Minneapolis apartment building on September 2, prompting a large emergency response and an hours-long search of the high-rise. Authorities said the suspected shooter was later found dead from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound inside the building.

Police responded shortly after 4:30 p.m. to reports of gunfire at Shoreline Plaza in the Loring Park neighborhood. Officers entered the building while shots were still being fired and worked to evacuate residents as smoke and limited visibility complicated the response. One civilian was pronounced dead at the scene, while a second died after being transported to Hennepin County Medical Center. One officer underwent emergency surgery after being shot in the abdomen, another suffered a gunshot wound to the leg, and a third officer received treatment for injuries that police said were not caused by gunfire.

Authorities identified the suspected shooter as Carlton Neal Johnson II, 35. Court records show he was facing an eviction case that had been scheduled for a hearing later this month. Property management records cited in local reporting alleged previous incidents involving threats, firearm-related complaints, and confrontations with building staff. Investigators have not said whether the eviction proceedings directly motivated the shooting.

Interim Police Chief Bill Peterson and Mayor Jacob Frey praised the officers and emergency responders who entered the building under active gunfire to rescue residents and secure the scene. Investigators continue to examine evidence, interview witnesses, and determine the sequence of events that led to the attack.

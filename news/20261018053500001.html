Truck Drives Through Kansas Youth Soccer Game, Killing Two

Police Investigate Why Driver Entered Field With Children Inside Vehicle

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 18, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

A 6-year-old boy and a 35-year-old man were killed after a pickup truck drove through a youth soccer game at the Scheels Stryker Sports Complex in Wichita, Kansas, on October 17. Eleven other people were taken to hospitals as police investigated why the vehicle entered the field.

Emergency crews received multiple 911 calls at approximately 3:25 p.m. Police said the two people killed were both from McPherson, Kansas, and were pronounced dead at the scene. They were not related.

Of the 11 people hospitalized, 10 suffered traumatic injuries ranging from bruises to extremely severe injuries, but police said they were expected to survive. The injured ranged in age from 5 to 68 and included five children. One additional adult was hospitalized for a non-traumatic medical issue, accounting for differences in reports describing 10 people injured and 11 hospitalized.

Police identified the driver only as a 34-year-old woman from Garden City, Kansas. She remained with the truck after it stopped and was taken into police custody. Two juvenile girls were inside the vehicle when it drove through the field. Police said the woman also had a child playing soccer elsewhere at the complex, but not on the field where the deaths occurred.

Authorities have not determined whether the incident was intentional or accidental. Wichita Police Capt. Aaron Moses said investigators were working to determine how and why it happened. Police interviewed more than 30 witnesses and asked spectators to submit photos and videos that could assist the investigation.

Police Chief Joe Sullivan praised parents and other bystanders who provided lifesaving assistance before emergency crews arrived. Officials said there was no continuing threat to the public, and events at the sports complex were canceled while the investigation continued.

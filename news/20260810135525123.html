Taylor Farms Recalls Jalapeño Foods in 26 States

Salsas, dips and guacamole sold at major retailers are affected

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 10, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Taylor Fresh Foods, which does business as Taylor Farms, is recalling prepared foods containing jalapeños in 26 states because the peppers may be contaminated with Salmonella. The recall includes products sold through Walmart, Target, Kroger, Whole Foods, Trader Joe’s, Hannaford and Stop & Shop, with affected "Best If Used By" dates extending through August 16, 2026.

The recall was announced after supplier Coast Citrus Distributors recalled fresh jalapeños linked to an ongoing multistate Salmonella outbreak. Federal investigators identified a grower in Sinaloa, Mexico, that supplied Coast Citrus as a potential source. Taylor Farms said it has stopped sourcing from that grower and is filling orders through alternative suppliers. As of August 10, the company said it was not aware of any illnesses specifically linked to its recalled prepared foods.

Among the recalled items are Freshness Guaranteed Hot Pico de Gallo and Mild Pico de Gallo sold at Walmart; Taylor Farms Pico de Gallo, Authentic Guacamole, Spicy Guacamole, Mango Pico de Gallo and Taco Dip sold at Target; Trader Joe’s Fiesta Style Salad with Shrimp; Kroger Private Selection Spicy Roast Beef Sandwich, Spicy Jarlsberg Dip and Spicy Pimento Cheese Dip; and several salsas sold at Whole Foods. Hannaford and Stop & Shop products include diced jalapeños, a rice and bean burrito and pico de gallo. Exact products and date ranges vary by retailer and state.

The broader outbreak linked to fresh jalapeños has sickened 345 people across 27 states, including 36 hospitalizations, according to federal health officials. Those figures were already part of the earlier outbreak investigation; the new development is the recall of specific retail foods made with the affected peppers.

Consumers who have recalled products should not eat them. Taylor Farms and federal officials advise discarding the products or returning them to the place of purchase for a refund.

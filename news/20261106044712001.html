Iran sends U.S. nuclear proposal through Qatar

Tehran sets seven conditions for renewed negotiations

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 6, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Iran has sent the United States a new proposal concerning its nuclear program through Qatar, offering a potential diplomatic path as Washington considers whether to launch additional military strikes against Tehran. A senior Iranian official told Reuters that the proposal was delivered through Qatari mediators and includes seven conditions Iran wants addressed before renewed negotiations.

The demands include lifting U.S. sanctions on Iranian oil exports, releasing frozen Iranian assets abroad and compensation for damage caused during the war. Tehran has also linked negotiations to restrictions affecting shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, a critical route for global energy supplies.

Iranian officials have said they could reopen the strait to unrestricted commercial traffic within 48 hours if an agreement addressing their economic demands is reached. Iran has maintained restrictions on shipping through the waterway during the conflict, contributing to higher energy prices and disruptions to international trade.

Significant disagreements remain over Iran's nuclear program. Tehran has resisted demands that it immediately allow international inspectors unrestricted access to nuclear facilities damaged during the conflict or transfer its remaining stockpile of highly enriched uranium outside the country. The United States has maintained that nuclear restrictions and verification must be part of any agreement.

The proposal comes as President Trump weighs the possibility of renewed U.S. military action. Trump met with his national security team Thursday to consider options involving Iran, while U.S. military planners have prepared for the possibility of additional operations. No final decision on new strikes has been announced.

Qatar has played a central role in efforts to keep diplomatic channels open between Washington and Tehran. The latest proposal does not mean formal negotiations have resumed, and neither side has announced that the seven Iranian conditions have been accepted.

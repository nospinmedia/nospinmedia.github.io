12 injured near Florida A&M stadium

Six juveniles among victims after fight erupts into gunfire

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 25, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

At least 12 people, including six juveniles, were injured Saturday night in a shooting near Florida A&M University's Bragg Memorial Stadium in Tallahassee, Florida, during the university's homecoming weekend, according to police. Eleven of the 12 victims were shot, while another person was injured during the incident.

The Tallahassee Police Department said its preliminary investigation indicates that a fight broke out before the shooting in a crowded area near the stadium following a football game. Police Chief Lawrence Revell said no arrests had been made as of the latest briefing. The shooting occurred near the intersection of Perry and Manatee streets, close to Bragg Memorial Stadium.

Police said six adults and six juveniles were among those injured. At least three victims suffered serious injuries, while the remaining nine were reported in stable condition. Authorities have not reported any deaths. The youngest person shot was 11 years old, according to police information reported by multiple outlets.

The shooting occurred during Florida A&M's homecoming weekend, when large crowds had gathered around the university and stadium. Florida A&M said the violence happened near its campus and emphasized that the campus itself remained safe. Police have not said the shooting was directly connected to the university or the football game beyond occurring nearby after the game.

Investigators with the police department's violent crimes unit are working to determine what led to the altercation and subsequent gunfire. Authorities had not publicly identified a suspect or announced a motive as of early Sunday.

U.S. disables ship trying to run Iran blockade

Pacific Archer struck as Tehran vows to break restrictions

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 28, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

The U.S. military disabled a commercial ship in the Gulf of Oman after it attempted to run the American naval blockade of Iran, adding another direct military confrontation as Iranian officials publicly vow to break the restrictions.

U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) said a military aircraft fired precision munitions that struck the stern of the Palau-flagged bulk carrier M/V Pacific Archer on November 26. CENTCOM said the vessel had departed Bandar Abbas, Iran, and continued despite multiple warnings. Its propulsion was disabled, while the civilian crew was unharmed. CENTCOM released video on November 27 showing a projectile striking the stern of the vessel.

Independent maritime tracking adds context to the military's account. AIS data reviewed after the strike showed no recent record of the Pacific Archer calling at Bandar Abbas or another Iranian port. Tracking data also indicated the vessel initially turned back after receiving blockade warnings before attempting another transit the following day. CENTCOM maintains that the ship departed Bandar Abbas before attempting to breach the blockade.

CENTCOM said U.S. forces have redirected 158 commercial vessels that complied with blockade instructions and disabled six that refused since the blockade resumed in July. At least 15 vessels have been publicly acknowledged as disabled by U.S. forces since blockade enforcement began in the spring.

Iran responded on November 27 with increasingly explicit promises to challenge the restrictions. Mohammad Mokhber, an adviser to Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei, said Iran would not allow the blockade to continue. Iranian Navy Commander Rear Adm. Shahram Irani said efforts to break the blockade were underway and claimed Iranian forces were escorting commercial vessels and oil tankers.

Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Aerospace Force Commander Maj. Gen. Majid Mousavi separately warned of operations in the air and at sea as Iran seeks to break the blockade. No such operation was announced with his statement.

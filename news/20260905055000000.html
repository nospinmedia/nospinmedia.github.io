Fox News abruptly parts ways with Maria Bartiromo

Her attorney disputes reports that the longtime anchor was fired

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 5, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Fox News Media abruptly ended Maria Bartiromo's run as one of its most prominent anchors after more than 12 years, announcing that she was no longer with the company effective September 3. Fox later described the departure as a "business decision" but did not publicly explain its reasoning. A person familiar with the matter told The Washington Post that the decision was Fox's, while Bartiromo's attorney subsequently disputed reports that she had been fired and asserted that she remained employed by the company.

Bartiromo hosted Mornings With Maria and Maria Bartiromo's Wall Street on Fox Business and Sunday Morning Futures on Fox News. Fox said her morning program would become Mornings With Fox Business, initially using rotating anchors, while former U.S. Representative Jason Chaffetz was scheduled to guest-host her Sunday program as the network searched for a permanent replacement. Bartiromo joined Fox in 2014 after approximately two decades at CNBC, where she became widely known for her financial-market reporting.

The departure comes as Bartiromo remains associated with litigation over Fox's coverage following the 2020 presidential election. She was among the Fox personalities whose broadcasts were scrutinized in Dominion Voting Systems' defamation case, which Fox settled for $787.5 million in 2023, and in the continuing $2.7 billion Smartmatic lawsuit. Fox has not said those cases played a role in its decision.

Separate media reports have alleged that Bartiromo shared confidential Fox editorial guidance concerning election-related claims with White House officials. Her attorney, Bryan Freedman, called reports that she had been fired "absolutely and unequivocally false" and suggested evidence and witnesses could emerge in potential legal proceedings. The Washington Post said it had not independently corroborated the allegation involving internal Fox communications, and Fox declined to provide additional comment on Freedman's statement.

President Trump publicly praised Bartiromo following Fox's announcement and expressed disappointment that her programs were ending. Despite speculation about a possible administration role for Bartiromo, no such appointment had been announced.

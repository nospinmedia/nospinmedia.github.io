Father of Two Identified as Bystander Who Stopped Oregon School Shooter

Kaylub Giamanco Followed 16-Year-Old Gunman Into His Daughters' School

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 17, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

The armed community member who stopped the deadly shooting at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, has been identified as 36-year-old Kaylub Giamanco, a father of two girls who attend the school.

Giamanco was driving near the school on October 15 when he encountered the unfolding attack. Police said the teenage gunman shot through glass in a locked entrance before entering the elementary school. Giamanco, who was armed, followed the attacker into the building.

Authorities identified the gunman as 16-year-old Griffin J. John II, a former student at Silver Rail. Police said he was armed with a pump-action shotgun. During the attack, three children, ages 5, 8 and 11, and a 25-year-old teacher were killed. Two other people were critically wounded.

Police said a teacher inside the school also confronted the gunman as the attack unfolded. Giamanco eventually reached the attacker and fatally shot him, ending the shooting before additional people could be harmed. Authorities have credited the actions of both Giamanco and school employees with helping prevent further casualties.

Giamanco's family later confirmed his identity while asking for privacy as he and his family process what happened. The identification adds new details to the shooting after authorities initially described the person who killed the gunman only as an armed community member.

The Bend Police Department, assisted by federal authorities, continues to investigate the attack and the gunman's motive. Investigators are also examining how the teenager obtained the weapon and the circumstances leading up to the shooting.

Russia pounds Kyiv as Ukraine sends hundreds of drones toward Moscow

Major strikes continue as both sides discuss restarting peace talks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 23, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

Russia launched waves of ballistic missiles at Kyiv while Ukraine sent hundreds of drones toward Moscow and other parts of Russia on October 22, as both countries continued large-scale attacks despite renewed efforts to restart negotiations aimed at ending the war.

Ukraine said Russia fired at least 21 ballistic missiles in three waves. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said three of the missiles carried cluster munitions, which disperse smaller explosives over a wide area. Russia's Defense Ministry did not immediately respond to that claim. Zelenskyy said Russian attacks across about a dozen Ukrainian regions killed at least two people and injured 34, including five children, while regional officials reported additional casualties from strikes elsewhere in the country.

Russia, meanwhile, said its air defenses intercepted 808 Ukrainian drones overnight across the country. Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 630 drones were directed toward the Russian capital, where airports temporarily restricted flights. Two people were wounded near Moscow, according to Russian officials. Ukraine does not routinely provide details about all of its long-range drone operations, meaning Russia's interception figures could not be independently verified.

The attacks came as diplomatic efforts showed signs of restarting. Russian President Vladimir Putin confirmed that Russia was prepared to resume U.S.-mediated negotiations with Ukraine next month after meeting U.S. presidential envoy Steve Witkoff in St. Petersburg. Zelenskyy has also said Ukraine is prepared for talks and hopes negotiations could take place in early November. The meetings would be the first U.S.-mediated negotiations between the two countries since February.

The two sides remain far apart on the terms of any settlement. Russia continues to demand Ukrainian territorial concessions and restrictions on Ukraine's military and alliances, while Ukraine rejects those conditions as unacceptable. Previous rounds of negotiations produced prisoner exchanges but no agreement to end the fighting.

Russia and Ukraine also exchanged war dead Thursday, with 252 bodies returned to Ukraine and 25 returned to Russia, according to reporting citing Belarusian state media.

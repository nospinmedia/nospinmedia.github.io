Russell Brand sentenced to 10½ years in prison

British comedian convicted of rape and sexual assaults

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 10, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

British comedian and actor Russell Brand has been sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison after being convicted of rape and sexual assault involving four women.

A jury at Southwark Crown Court in London convicted Brand on November 5 of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving incidents between 2004 and 2009. Jurors acquitted him of two additional rape charges and one attempted indecent assault charge. Brand had denied the allegations and maintained that his sexual relationships were consensual.

Judge Joel Bennathan imposed the sentence on November 9, describing Brand as a sexual predator who had exploited his fame and disregarded women's right to refuse sexual contact. The judge sentenced him to nine years for the rape conviction, with additional consecutive sentences for the sexual assault convictions. Brand, 51, was taken into custody following sentencing.

The court also heard victim impact statements describing the long-term effects of the assaults. One woman said she had felt like "damaged goods," while another described the distress of seeing Brand continue to receive public attention and professional opportunities in the years after the offenses. The women cannot be identified under British law because they are victims of sexual offenses.

Brand rose to prominence in Britain as a comedian, television and radio presenter before appearing in Hollywood films. In recent years, he developed a large online following focused on political and cultural commentary. The criminal investigation followed allegations made publicly by several women beginning in 2023.

London's Metropolitan Police have said investigations into additional allegations involving Brand remain underway. His convictions and sentence therefore do not necessarily conclude the broader police investigation.

Russia pounds Kyiv after brief pause for U.S. peace talks

Two killed as missiles and drones strike across Ukraine

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 8, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

Russia launched a large overnight missile and drone attack on Kyiv and other parts of Ukraine early Tuesday, killing two people and injuring at least 10 in the capital. The barrage ended a brief pause in attacks on Kyiv while U.S. peace envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner visited Russia and Ukraine in a renewed attempt to advance negotiations over the war.

Ukraine's Air Force said Russian forces launched 166 drones and 32 cruise missiles, along with ballistic and other missiles. Ukrainian defenses shot down or suppressed 142 drones, 31 cruise missiles and two ballistic missiles, while strikes were recorded at 29 locations nationwide. Officials did not disclose the total number of ballistic missiles launched. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the attack involved multiple types of weapons and appeared designed to cause maximum damage.

Damage and fires were reported in at least five Kyiv districts, with civilian infrastructure damaged at 16 locations. Authorities said at least 14 high-rise buildings, a dormitory, school, clinic and warehouses were damaged. Emergency crews rescued at least 10 people from a five-story residential building in the Solomianskyi district. Russian attacks also struck the Odesa, Kharkiv, Sumy, Zaporizhzhia and Dnipropetrovsk regions, including a market in Odesa and a locomotive operating on the Kyiv-Sumy railway route.

The attack followed the departure of Witkoff and Kushner after meetings with Zelenskyy in Kyiv and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow. The diplomatic effort produced no announced breakthrough. Russia and Ukraine had temporarily refrained from attacking each other's capitals during the envoys' visit. Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha called for additional international pressure on Moscow and more interceptors capable of defending against ballistic missiles.

Russia's Defense Ministry said its strikes targeted military and logistics facilities in and around Kyiv and targets near the Black Sea. Neighboring Poland activated military aircraft as a precaution, and flights at Lublin Airport were temporarily suspended during the military operation.

Israeli Strike Kills 3 In Vehicle Carrying Food In Gaza

IDF Says Those Killed Were Militants Posing As Aid Workers

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 22, 2026 at 10:15 AM ET

An Israeli strike killed three Palestinians in a vehicle carrying food in Gaza City on Saturday, November 21, with witnesses saying the vehicle appeared to be distributing aid and Israel later identifying those killed as Palestinian Islamic Jihad (PIJ) operatives posing as aid workers.

The strike occurred in the Tel al-Hawa area. Witnesses said the vehicle contained packaged food, including cooked rice meals, along with materials bearing the logo of World Central Kitchen (WCK). Witness Abu Zein Al-Daya told Reuters that the vehicle was preparing to distribute food and drinks to children when it was struck. Food was visible scattered around the wreckage afterward.

World Central Kitchen said the vehicle did not belong to the organization and the three people killed were not members of its team. WCK founder José Andrés reiterated that the organization had no connection to those killed, while warning about the continuing humanitarian conditions facing Gaza families.

The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) subsequently said the three men were members of Palestinian Islamic Jihad who had been "falsely presented as aid workers." According to the IDF, they were using an unmarked vehicle to transport food and other supplies to fellow militants. The military also said one of the men had previously been dismissed by an aid organization because of alleged militant ties. The Israeli claims about the men's affiliations have not been independently verified.

The incident comes amid continuing violence despite the October 2025 U.S.-backed ceasefire. Palestinian authorities say more than 1,600 Palestinians have been killed since the truce began, while Israeli figures show five Israeli soldiers killed during the same period. The figures come from the respective sides and are not directly comparable casualty counts.

The competing accounts leave an important distinction: witnesses described a food-carrying vehicle apparently preparing to distribute supplies, WCK says neither the vehicle nor its occupants belonged to the organization, and Israel says those inside were militants using the appearance of humanitarian activity as cover.

Ukrainian Drone Strike Kills 5 Near Moscow

Warehouse fire in Chekhov as cross-border barrages intensify

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 4, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

A Ukrainian drone strike on August 4, 2026 killed at least 5 people and injured 10 in the Moscow region city of Chekhov, according to regional governor Andrey Vorobyov. Officials said several fires broke out at the Novosyolki industrial zone, where a warehouse was hit, marking a lethal deep‑rear strike amid ongoing cross‑border aerial attacks.

Local authorities reported additional overnight drone activity against logistics hubs. Wildberries, Russia’s largest online retailer, said a warehouse in the Leningrad Region caught fire, while acting Tver Region governor Vitaly Korolyov said drone debris caused partial damage to a wall at a Wildberries site in the village of Emmaus; no injuries were reported at either location. Ukraine did not immediately claim responsibility for Tuesday’s attacks.

Russia’s Defense Ministry said air defenses intercepted 320 Ukrainian drones overnight across Russian regions and annexed Crimea. On the same day inside Ukraine, officials reported multiple Russian strikes. In the northern city of Sumy, regional chief Oleh Hryhorov said guided aerial bombs killed three civilians, including two girls aged 5 and 10. Authorities also reported additional Russian attacks elsewhere that damaged civilian sites and port infrastructure. Some battlefield claims from both sides could not be independently verified.

The Chekhov strike fits a weeks‑long pattern of Ukrainian long‑range drone operations targeting industrial and logistics nodes that support Russia’s war effort, including repeated hits on Wildberries facilities. Tuesday’s casualties in the Moscow region—together with concurrent Russian bombardments in Sumy and other areas—underscore the expanding human toll and the risks to commercial supply chains as both countries pursue distance strikes beyond front‑line zones.

Michigan Manhunt Ends With Suspect Dead After 5 Killed

Multi-scene investigation continues in Missaukee County

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 15, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

A series of shootings across rural Missaukee County, Michigan, left five victims dead, another person critically injured and the suspected gunman dead on August 14, 2026, after a large-scale police search involving tactical, aviation and canine units. Michigan State Police identified the suspect as Chad Hickman, 39, whose body was eventually found in a wooded area near Whitlock Lake along with a fifth victim.

Troopers were dispatched at approximately 11:40 a.m. to a home on South LaChance Road in Lake Township after receiving a report that someone had been shot. Officers found three people dead inside and another person with life-threatening injuries who was taken to a hospital. Police said Hickman had fled before troopers arrived, prompting authorities to warn residents that he was considered armed and dangerous and should not be approached.

As officers expanded the search, investigators discovered a fourth victim dead at a separate home in Missaukee County. Police later located a vehicle associated with Hickman near a wooded area close to Whitlock Lake in Caldwell Township. Search teams subsequently found both Hickman and another person dead there, bringing the death toll to six people, including the suspect. Authorities have not publicly identified the victims, pending notification of their families, and had not announced a motive as of early Saturday.

Lt. Ashley Miller of the Michigan State Police described the case as heartbreaking for the community and challenging for investigators, saying teams would continue processing the multiple scenes and provide updates as additional information becomes available. Governor Gretchen Whitmer said she had been briefed on the shootings and was monitoring the investigation. Police have not yet released details explaining the relationships among Hickman and the victims or the sequence of events connecting the different locations.

Suspect in Oregon quintuple homicide found dead in Washington

Investigation continues after 5 victims discovered on rural property

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 24, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

Authorities investigating the deaths of five people at a rural property near Forest Grove, Oregon, have identified 36-year-old Benjamin Charles Parker as the primary suspect after he was found dead inside a vehicle in Enumclaw, Washington, in what investigators believe was an apparent suicide. Officials said Parker was known to the victims and had previously lived at the property, but a motive for the killings has not yet been established.

Deputies with the Washington County Sheriff's Office responded early August 22 to assist firefighters with a vehicle fire at a property on Northwest Otis Lane, about 30 miles west of Portland. While investigating the fire, first responders discovered human remains near the burned vehicle, prompting a wider search that uncovered four additional victims and several deceased domestic animals across the property. The victims' identities have not been released pending autopsies and notification of next of kin.

The investigation has been turned over to the Washington County Major Crimes Team, with assistance from the Oregon State Police Crime Lab, the Oregon State Medical Examiner, the Forest Grove Police Department, and the Beaverton Police Department. Sheriff Caprice Massey said detectives believe Parker was the only outstanding suspect and confirmed there is no continuing threat to the public following the discovery of his body in Washington state.

Authorities have not released details about how the victims died or what led to the violence, saying investigators are continuing to process the extensive crime scene and collect forensic evidence. Officials also have not disclosed the relationships between Parker and the victims. Detectives said additional information, including the victims' identities and preliminary autopsy findings, will be released as the investigation progresses and families are notified.

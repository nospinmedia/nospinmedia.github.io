Explosion sparks fire at world’s largest oat mill in Iowa

All Quaker Oats workers escape without injuries

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 26, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

An apparent explosion sparked a fire at the Quaker Oats plant in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, sending heavy smoke over the city before firefighters brought the blaze under control. Despite the size of the emergency response, officials reported no injuries among plant workers or firefighters.

The Cedar Rapids Fire Department received the first call at about 4:44 p.m. Saturday, October 24. Firefighters arriving at the plant encountered heavy smoke and flames on the north side of the building and found signs that an explosion had occurred. Crews from Cedar Rapids received assistance from fire departments in Marion, Hiawatha and Linn County. The fire was considered under control by about 6:15 p.m., with crews remaining inside afterward to extinguish hot spots and complete overhaul work.

Cedar Rapids Mayor Tiffany O'Donnell said all plant employees evacuated safely and were accounted for. The fire occurred in a product-handling section of the facility that normally has relatively few workers. Officials had not determined what caused the explosion and fire, and the incident remained under investigation.

The size of the facility makes the incident notable beyond Cedar Rapids. PepsiCo Foods US, which owns Quaker Oats, describes the Cedar Rapids operation as the largest milling facility in the world, processing more than 2 million pounds of oats per day. The company said emergency responders were called to the facility and that the cause of the incident would be investigated.

Officials had not announced the extent of the damage or how long affected portions of the plant might remain out of operation. There was also no confirmed indication that the fire would disrupt Quaker Oats production or supplies.

White House Press Secretary Katie Zacharia Confirms She Failed Bar Exam

Zacharia Says She Never Retook Test After Previously Describing Herself as a Lawyer

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 16, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

White House Press Secretary Katie Zacharia confirmed that she failed the California bar exam and never took it again, addressing questions about her legal credentials after previously describing herself publicly as a lawyer and being called an attorney by President Trump.

Zacharia graduated from Pepperdine University School of Law in 2011 and took the California bar exam that year. During an interview with Fox News host Sean Hannity on October 15, she was asked directly whether she had failed the exam and answered, "Correct." When Hannity asked whether she had taken it a second or third time, Zacharia said she had not.

Zacharia said she had already planned to move to China with her husband after graduating and considered the bar exam results inconsequential because she did not expect to practice in the United States. She subsequently worked on the international-practice team of Zhong Lun Law Firm in Beijing, where she has said she handled international transaction work.

The questions arose because Zacharia has repeatedly referred to herself as a lawyer during television appearances and other public statements. President Trump also described her as an "attorney" when announcing her appointment as White House press secretary. Zacharia is not licensed to practice law in the United States.

Zacharia has defended her use of the term lawyer, saying she sought advice and was told that her law degree allowed her to describe herself that way while distinguishing the term from "attorney," which she said would indicate someone licensed to practice law. Legal experts interviewed by news organizations have disputed that interpretation, noting that rules governing professional titles vary but generally restrict representations suggesting someone is authorized to practice law.

The position of White House press secretary does not require a law degree or a license to practice law. The controversy concerns Zacharia's previous descriptions of her professional background rather than a qualification required for her current government position.

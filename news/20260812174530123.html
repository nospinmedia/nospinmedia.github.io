U.S. Helicopter Disables Panama-Flagged Cargo Ship

CENTCOM says crew ignored repeated warnings near Iran blockade

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 12, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) said a U.S. military helicopter disabled the Panama-flagged container ship M/V Vela Nova in the Gulf of Oman on August 11, 2026, after the vessel allegedly ignored repeated warnings while attempting to transit toward an Iranian port in violation of the U.S. naval blockade. U.S. officials said the helicopter fired two Hellfire missiles into the ship's engine room, leaving it unable to continue its voyage.

Maritime security officials reported the incident occurred about 71 nautical miles off the coast of Pakistan. Authorities said there were no immediate reports of injuries among the ship's 17 civilian crew members. Early reports indicated the crew later began transferring to another civilian vessel while the damaged ship remained disabled. Officials have not released information about the ship's cargo, ownership, or intended destination beyond saying it was bound toward an Iranian port.

CENTCOM said the action was part of its ongoing enforcement of the U.S. blockade on Iranian ports, which was reimposed in July 2026 after tensions with Iran escalated. According to the command, U.S. forces have now redirected 55 vessels, boarded 2, and disabled 3 ships that failed to comply with blockade enforcement measures. Officials said U.S. forces will continue intercepting vessels that ignore lawful military instructions while approaching restricted Iranian ports.

The incident highlights the continuing security risks facing commercial shipping near the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most important maritime trade routes. Insurance costs, shipping schedules, and regional energy markets have remained under pressure as military operations and diplomatic negotiations continue. Officials said the investigation into the Vela Nova incident remains ongoing and additional details could be released as they become available.

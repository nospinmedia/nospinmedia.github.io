Attorney General Blanche Defends Pirro After Trump Criticism

Says prosecutors must follow the evidence in Reflecting Pool case

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 17, 2026 at 7:39 PM ET

Attorney General Todd Blanche publicly defended Jeanine Pirro, the U.S. attorney for the District of Columbia, following criticism from President Trump over her office's decision to dismiss charges in the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool vandalism case. During an appearance on NBC's Meet the Press on August 16, Blanche said he supports Pirro's handling of the case and emphasized that prosecutors must base charging decisions on the available evidence.

The controversy began after federal prosecutors moved to dismiss felony property damage charges against former Olympian David Hearn, concluding that damage to the Reflecting Pool resulted from construction defects during a $14.7 million renovation rather than intentional vandalism. President Trump criticized the decision publicly and urged the Justice Department to reconsider the case.

Blanche said prosecutors are obligated to pursue cases only when the evidence supports criminal charges, regardless of political attention surrounding a case. The dispute has renewed discussion over prosecutorial discretion and the relationship between the White House and the Justice Department, particularly after Blanche's separate comments declining to promise complete DOJ independence from the White House.

The dismissal remains before the court as Hearn's attorneys seek to ensure the case cannot be refiled. The episode is expected to continue drawing attention as lawmakers and legal observers examine how high-profile criminal cases are handled within the Justice Department.

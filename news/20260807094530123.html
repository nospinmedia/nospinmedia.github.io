Student Opens Fire at Thai High School, At Least 6 Dead

Police say suspected gunman also died as investigation begins

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 7, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

A student opened fire at Debsirin Nonthaburi School in Nonthaburi, just northwest of Bangkok, on August 7, 2026, killing at least six people before authorities said the suspected gunman also died. Police evacuated classrooms, secured the campus, and reunited students with their families as emergency crews responded to one of Thailand's deadliest school shootings in recent years.

The Royal Thai Police said the victims included three teachers and three students, with numerous others injured. Officials said hospitals were continuing to treat victims and cautioned that injury totals could change as patients were evaluated. Authorities also reported that the suspected shooter killed two grandparents earlier the same day before arriving at the school. Investigators believe the handgun used in the attack belonged to a family member.

Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul expressed condolences to the victims' families and ordered a full investigation into the shooting, including how the student obtained the firearm. Deputy Interior Minister Polapee Suwunchwee said authorities are reviewing the sequence of events and coordinating with police, schools, and health officials as the investigation continues. School records show Debsirin Nonthaburi serves approximately 3,100 students and employs 147 teachers, requiring a large emergency response to secure the campus.

Thailand has comparatively strict firearms laws but one of the higher rates of civilian gun ownership in Southeast Asia. The attack follows another school shooting in Hat Yai earlier this year and is expected to renew debate over firearm access, school security, and mental health support for young people. Officials said the investigation remains active as they work to confirm the full timeline and casualty figures.

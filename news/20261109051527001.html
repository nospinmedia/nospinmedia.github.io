Arrest made after deadly shooting at Winston-Salem State

Two separate shootings leave one dead and three injured during homecoming

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 9, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

Police have arrested a 20-year-old man in connection with one of two shootings at Winston-Salem State University during homecoming weekend that left one person dead and three others injured. Authorities said the shootings occurred less than an hour apart Saturday night on the campus of the historically Black university in North Carolina.

Officers already working homecoming events heard gunfire at about 9:45 p.m. and found a 19-year-old man suffering from a gunshot wound. Officers attempted to save him, but he died at the scene. A 22-year-old woman who was also wounded was taken to a hospital, treated and later released.

Police arrested Ziyaire Spinks, 20, who authorities said is not a Winston-Salem State student. He was being held without bond on charges including murder, assault with a deadly weapon and possession of a firearm on educational property. Investigators said the shooting followed an argument between two groups of people.

Less than an hour later, police responded to a second shooting on campus after hearing additional gunfire. Two people were injured following another altercation. Authorities said they were still investigating whether the two shootings were connected, and no arrests had been announced in connection with the second incident.

University officials said neither the person killed nor the accused shooter was a Winston-Salem State student. The university canceled the remainder of its homecoming activities and later canceled Monday classes to give students and employees time to process the violence and use available support services. Classes are scheduled to resume Tuesday morning.

The university said the campus is secure and there is no active threat, although additional law enforcement officers are remaining on campus while the investigations continue. Counseling services have also been made available to students, faculty and staff.

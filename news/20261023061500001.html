Judge orders President Trump to testify under oath in $10 billion BBC lawsuit

Deposition must be completed by December 11

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 23, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

A federal judge has ordered President Trump to sit for a deposition under oath by December 11 in his $10 billion defamation lawsuit against the BBC, rejecting an effort to postpone his testimony while the British broadcaster seeks to have the case dismissed.

U.S. District Judge Roy Altman issued the order in federal court in Miami, setting an accelerated schedule for the case. Trump sued the BBC in December 2025, alleging that an episode of the broadcaster's Panorama program deceptively edited portions of his January 6, 2021 speech to make it appear that he directly called for violence at the U.S. Capitol. The lawsuit seeks $5 billion for defamation and another $5 billion under Florida's deceptive and unfair trade practices law.

The BBC has apologized for the edit and acknowledged that the way the excerpts were combined created a misleading impression that Trump had made a direct call for violent action. However, the broadcaster has rejected Trump's demand for compensation and disputes that he has a valid defamation claim. The BBC is seeking dismissal of the lawsuit, arguing among other things that the Florida federal court lacks jurisdiction and that the program was not broadcast in the United States through BBC channels.

The deposition dispute arose as Trump's lawyers sought to delay his testimony until after the court considers the BBC's motion to dismiss. Altman rejected that request and ordered Trump's deposition completed by December 11, while the BBC must make a representative available for deposition by December 18. The judge set a January 4, 2027 deadline for completing fact discovery.

The ruling does not determine whether the BBC defamed Trump or whether he is entitled to damages. It governs the discovery process, during which both sides gather testimony and evidence before the court determines whether the lawsuit proceeds toward trial.

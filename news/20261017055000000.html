Elon Musk Settles Lawsuits Over $1 Million Election Giveaways

Voters Alleged America PAC Misrepresented How 2024 Winners Were Selected

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 17, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

Elon Musk and his political group America PAC have agreed to settle lawsuits accusing them of misleading voters about the $1 million daily giveaways they promoted during the 2024 presidential election.

The lawsuits were brought by people who signed America PAC's petition supporting the First and Second Amendments after the group promoted what plaintiffs described as a chance to be randomly selected for a $1 million award. The campaign targeted registered voters in battleground states during the final weeks before the election.

Plaintiffs alleged that the promotion was presented as a random lottery but that the recipients were actually selected in advance to serve as spokespeople for America PAC. They argued that they provided personal information and signed the petition based on the belief that every eligible participant had a random chance of receiving the money.

Musk and America PAC disputed the allegations. During earlier litigation over the giveaway, lawyers representing Musk said recipients were selected based on their suitability to represent the political organization rather than through a random drawing.

The settlement resolves two federal lawsuits stemming from the promotion. The cases had continued after the 2024 election, with plaintiffs pursuing fraud and related claims over how the giveaway was advertised and operated. A federal judge had also ordered Musk to participate in a deposition as the litigation moved forward.

The parties have now agreed to resolve the cases rather than continue toward trial. The financial terms of the settlement have not been publicly disclosed, and the agreement does not amount to an admission of wrongdoing by Musk or America PAC.

The settlement closes another legal dispute stemming from Musk's extensive political activity during the 2024 election, when America PAC spent heavily to support Donald Trump's presidential campaign and Republican candidates.

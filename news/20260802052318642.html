Bomb explodes outside Moscow restaurant during private event

Three killed and at least 21 injured as investigators probe attack

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 2, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

An improvised explosive device detonated outside the Balzi Rossi Italian restaurant on Kudrinskaya Square in central Moscow on the evening of August 1, killing three people and injuring at least 21 others. Russia's National Anti-Terrorism Committee said the blast occurred shortly before 8:00 p.m. local time after an unidentified woman attempted to enter the restaurant during a private event while carrying the explosive device. A security guard stopped her at the entrance before the explosion occurred.

Authorities said those killed included the woman carrying the bomb, the security guard who confronted her, and a restaurant patron. Emergency responders, bomb disposal teams and heavily armed security personnel quickly secured the area while investigators searched the scene for evidence. The Investigative Committee of the Russian Federation opened a criminal investigation, and several of the injured were reported to be in serious condition after being transported to Moscow hospitals.

Officials have not identified who organized the attack or announced a motive. Investigators are examining multiple possibilities, including whether the woman knowingly carried the device or whether it may have been detonated remotely by another party. Russian authorities have not publicly confirmed either scenario and said the investigation remains in its early stages. Reports circulating on social media regarding the intended target or the nature of the private gathering have not been officially verified.

The bombing comes amid heightened security concerns inside Russia as the war in Ukraine continues and a series of attacks, sabotage incidents and drone strikes have increased pressure on domestic security services. While previous high-profile attacks have targeted military or political figures, authorities have not linked this explosion to any organization or foreign actor. Investigators are expected to review surveillance footage, forensic evidence and witness statements to determine who planned the bombing and whether additional suspects remain at large.

Apple unveils first foldable iPhone starting at $1,999

New iPhone Duo opens to 7.6-inch display and arrives Oct. 23

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 10, 2026 at 3:47 PM ET

Apple has unveiled the iPhone Duo, its first foldable iPhone, introducing a new form factor that opens into a 7.6-inch inner display while retaining a 5.4-inch outer screen for use when closed. The device starts at $1,999 in the United States and will be available beginning October 23, with preorders opening October 16.

Apple says the iPhone Duo is the thinnest iPhone it has made when unfolded. The inner display uses a nano-textured surface designed to reduce glare, while the outer display provides about 90% of the screen area of the iPhone 18 Pro in a smaller footprint. The phone uses a precision hinge and titanium construction, with Apple emphasizing durability in the folding display and frame.

The Duo is powered by Apple's new A20 Pro processor and runs a version of iOS 27 adapted for the two-screen design. When opened, users can display two Home Screen pages and use apps across the larger display for multitasking. Apple says applications including Netflix, Zoom and Slack have already been adapted to take advantage of the folding layout.

The camera system includes a 48-megapixel main camera with an optical-quality 2x telephoto option and a 48-megapixel ultra-wide camera. The foldable design also allows users to preview photos on the outside display while using the higher-resolution rear cameras for selfies or group shots.

Apple says the dual-battery design provides up to 31 hours of video playback using the inner screen or 44 hours using the outer display. Storage options range from 256GB to 2TB, and the phone will be sold in star white and night sky.

The launch also marked John Ternus' first major product announcement since becoming Apple's CEO, succeeding Tim Cook earlier this month.

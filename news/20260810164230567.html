Bezos, Saverin in group nearing 30% Liverpool stake

FSG could announce this week as valuation nears $5.9B

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 11, 2026 at 9:11 AM ET

⚽ Bezos, Saverin Group Nears 30% Liverpool Stake FSG could announce deal this week as club valuation nears $5.9B 👇

An investor consortium led by British-Indian businessman Amit Bhatia and including Jeff Bezos and Eduardo Saverin is nearing an agreement to acquire roughly a 30% minority stake in Liverpool FC. The proposed transaction is reportedly worth about £1.35 billion, implying a valuation of approximately £4.5 billion ($5.9 billion) for the Premier League club. An announcement could come this week, although negotiations remain subject to completion.

Current owner Fenway Sports Group (FSG) would retain majority ownership and control of Liverpool if the transaction is completed. FSG purchased the club in 2010 for about £300 million and has repeatedly maintained that it is not seeking a full sale, while remaining open to outside investment that could strengthen the club's finances.

The proposed investment would represent a substantial increase in Liverpool's valuation since FSG's acquisition. The club previously brought in outside capital in 2023, when U.S. private investment firm Dynasty Equity acquired a minority stake. FSG said at the time that proceeds from that investment would primarily be used to reduce bank debt and expenses associated with infrastructure and player transfers.

Reporting on the latest negotiations has not identified specific operational changes that would accompany the proposed 30% investment. There is also no indication that the consortium would gain majority control of Liverpool. Details about potential board representation, voting rights or other governance powers associated with the minority stake have not yet been publicly disclosed.

Bhatia has extensive experience in professional football through his involvement with Queens Park Rangers. Public filings show that he left QPR's board in July 2026. He is now reported to be leading the consortium pursuing the Liverpool investment, with Bezos, the founder of Amazon, and Saverin, a co-founder of Facebook, among the high-profile investors involved.

The potential deal comes during a period of change at Liverpool. Andoni Iraola was appointed head coach in June 2026, succeeding Arne Slot, as the club prepares for the 2026–27 season.

Until an agreement is formally announced, the proposed investment remains a transaction under negotiation. If completed on the terms currently reported, however, FSG would retain control while bringing significant new outside capital into one of the world's most valuable football clubs.

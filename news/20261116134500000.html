Zelensky says Starshield limits are allowing Russian strikes through

Ukraine says 100 mph cutoff prevents interceptor drones from catching faster Shaheds

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 16, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is urging the United States and SpaceX to remove restrictions on the military-grade Starshield satellite network, saying they are preventing Ukrainian interceptor drones from catching some Russian attack drones and leaving energy infrastructure more vulnerable.

Zelensky said Starshield terminals being used along the front line and in Russian-occupied Ukrainian territory lose connectivity when moving faster than 161 kilometers per hour, or 100 mph. Ukraine uses the satellite system to maintain communications with interceptor and reconnaissance drones.

According to Zelensky, the restriction means Ukrainian interceptor drones can lose contact with their operators before catching faster, jet-powered versions of Russia's Shahed attack drones. He said Russian forces are targeting electricity substations and that some strikes are getting through because of the limitation.

Ukraine has asked the U.S. government and SpaceX to resolve the issue. Starshield is based on SpaceX's Starlink technology but is provided to Ukraine through a U.S. government arrangement. Responsibility for the restrictions remains disputed, and SpaceX has denied making a recent change to the service.

Similar speed restrictions were reported in October before service was restored on October 24. Ukrainian forces began reporting renewed problems in November.

The dispute comes as Russia intensifies attacks against Ukraine's energy system ahead of winter. Ukrainian officials reported strikes on energy infrastructure in Zaporizhzhia, Sumy, Odesa, Zhytomyr and Kirovohrad, along with attacks on railway infrastructure in several regions.

Zelensky said protecting lives and Ukraine's energy infrastructure depends directly on restoring unrestricted Starshield operations.

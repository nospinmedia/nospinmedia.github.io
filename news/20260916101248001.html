House Democrat investigates Russian oligarch's funding of Trump Jr. wedding events

Records sought from White House and president's son

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 16, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

The top Democrat on the House Oversight Committee has opened an inquiry into Russian businessman Umar Kremlev's financial support for wedding celebrations held for Donald Trump Jr. and Bettina Trump, seeking records from both the White House and the president's son about their relationship with Kremlev. Rep. Robert Garcia of California sent separate letters to White House chief of staff Susie Wiles and Trump Jr. requesting documents and communications related to Kremlev and the wedding events.

The inquiry follows reporting that Kremlev, president of the International Boxing Association, spent hundreds of thousands of dollars on celebrations in the Bahamas following the couple's wedding earlier this year. PBS NewsHour reported that the expenses included the rental of a private island and a fireworks display. Bettina Trump subsequently confirmed Kremlev's involvement, saying he hosted two nights of celebrations after the couple's family-only wedding and describing the spending as an extraordinarily generous wedding gift from a friend.

Garcia is seeking communications between Trump Jr. and Kremlev, communications involving the White House, financial records concerning purchases made by foreign nationals and a complete list of wedding gifts from foreign nationals. Garcia said the inquiry is intended to examine possible foreign entanglements and whether Kremlev received anything in return. Those concerns are allegations being investigated and do not establish that Kremlev received favors or improperly influenced the administration.

President Trump said he does not know Kremlev and emphasized that Kremlev funded an afterparty rather than the wedding ceremony itself. Attorney General Todd Blanche also said on September 15 that he had no information suggesting grounds for a Justice Department investigation. Trump Jr. and his wife have characterized Kremlev as a personal friend and the celebrations as a gift.

Kremlev has ties to Russian President Vladimir Putin and Russian state-linked business interests. The congressional requests are part of an inquiry by committee Democrats and are not findings of wrongdoing by Trump Jr., Kremlev or the White House.

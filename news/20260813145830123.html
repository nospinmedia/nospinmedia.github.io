Police Link Minnesota Daycare Killings To Same Family

Burnsville homicide tied to deadly Hopkins attack

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 13, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

Minnesota authorities say a deadly attack at the licensed in-home Brown Bear Childcare daycare in Hopkins on August 12, 2026, is connected to the killing of a family member in Burnsville earlier the same day. Investigators said the suspect fatally attacked his partner and their child at the daycare before taking his own life. Police also believe the same suspect was responsible for the death of a 78-year-old female relative whose body was later found at a residence in Burnsville.

A parent arriving to drop off a child witnessed part of the attack and immediately called 911, according to Hopkins Police Chief Brent Johnson. Six other young children were inside the home during the incident. All six were safely evacuated by first responders and reunited with their families. Johnson described the tragedy as every parent's worst nightmare and emphasized there is no continuing threat to the public.

Investigators said the Burnsville homicide and the Hopkins daycare killings are part of a single family-related case. Police believe the suspect had been staying at the Burnsville residence before traveling to the daycare, located about 20 miles away. Authorities have not released the identities of the victims, and the Hennepin County Medical Examiner will determine the official causes and manners of death. Detectives from Hopkins and Burnsville continue coordinating interviews, forensic evidence collection, and reconstruction of the suspect's movements.

State licensing records identify Brown Bear Childcare as a licensed family child care provider serving children in Hopkins. Authorities said the investigation remains active and cautioned that additional information about the timeline, motive, and family relationships may be released as investigators complete interviews and review evidence.

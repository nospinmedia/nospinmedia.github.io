4 killed in helicopter crash outside Montreal

Birthday flight ends in tragedy as investigators examine cause

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 28, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

Four people were killed when a private helicopter crashed and caught fire in Sainte-Brigide-d'Iberville, Quebec, about 50 kilometers southeast of Montreal, on Saturday, September 26. The aircraft went down in a field near Rang des Écossais shortly before 2:30 p.m., and all four people aboard were pronounced dead at the scene.

Family members and friends identified the victims as pilot Suzanne Jutras, Gabriel Jasmin, Chantal Raymond and Loucas Perreault. Raymond, Jasmin and Perreault were taking the helicopter ride to celebrate Raymond's birthday, according to her daughter, Océane Perreault, who lost her mother, brother and partner in the crash. Jasmin was a radio host at 106.1 Énergie in Sherbrooke and was the father of a young daughter.

Quebec provincial police initially said the helicopter appeared to have experienced difficulties shortly after takeoff. In an update Sunday, however, a Sûreté du Québec spokesperson said the crash occurred while the aircraft was returning. Authorities have not determined what caused the helicopter to go down, and investigators have not publicly identified a mechanical failure or other specific factor.

The helicopter was burning when first responders reached the crash site. Firefighters brought the blaze under control, while the Sûreté du Québec Major Crimes Unit and crime-scene technicians began investigating the circumstances surrounding the crash.

The Transportation Safety Board of Canada also deployed investigators to Sainte-Brigide-d'Iberville. The TSB is responsible for determining the causes and contributing factors of transportation accidents and making safety recommendations when warranted. No official determination about the cause of Saturday's crash had been announced as of Sunday.

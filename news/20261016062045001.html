Two Women Sue Former Rep. Matt Gaetz, Alleging Sex Trafficking

Federal Lawsuit Accuses Gaetz and Others of Using Money, Drugs and Travel to Obtain Sex

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 16, 2026 at 10:15 AM ET

Two women filed a federal civil lawsuit in Florida accusing former U.S. Rep. Matt Gaetz, former Seminole County Tax Collector Joel Greenberg and others of sexually exploiting them in violation of federal anti-trafficking law. The women, identified as Jane Doe 1 and Jane Doe 2, say they were in their early 20s when the alleged conduct began in 2017.

The lawsuit alleges Greenberg recruited the women online and introduced them to Gaetz. According to the complaint, the defendants coordinated payments, drugs and travel to obtain commercial sex acts between 2017 and early 2019. The women allege Gaetz provided drugs and paid for sex, lodging, travel and other expenses. The complaint describes trips to locations including Miami, Las Vegas, New York, the Florida Keys and the Bahamas.

Former Florida state lawmaker Chris Dorworth is also named as a defendant. The lawsuit alleges Dorworth knowingly participated in events involving the women and made his home available for a gathering. Businesses associated with Greenberg's family are also named, with the plaintiffs alleging they provided funding connected to the operation.

Greenberg previously pleaded guilty to six federal crimes, including sex trafficking of a minor, and was sentenced to 11 years in federal prison. Gaetz has never been criminally charged in connection with the allegations against him.

The lawsuit follows a 2024 House Ethics Committee investigation that found substantial evidence that Gaetz paid women for sex and used illicit drugs while serving in Congress. Gaetz resigned from Congress in 2024 after President Trump nominated him for attorney general. He later withdrew from consideration and has consistently denied wrongdoing.

Gaetz denied the new allegations, saying much of the conduct described in the lawsuit is false. He said the case will give him an opportunity to confront his accusers in court. The allegations in the civil complaint have not been proven, and the defendants will have an opportunity to respond.

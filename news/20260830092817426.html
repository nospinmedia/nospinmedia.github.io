Philadelphia Officer Investigated After Leaving Man in Roadway

Victim later killed in hit-and-run as driver remains sought

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 30, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

Philadelphia police are investigating an officer who allegedly left a 48-year-old man lying partly in a traffic lane shortly before the man was struck and killed by a hit-and-run driver. Surveillance video from the 5500 block of Rising Sun Avenue shows the officer stopping to speak with the man during the early morning hours of August 27, after witnesses and police said the man had been walking with an unsteady gait. The officer drove away while the man remained on the pavement, according to police.

Philadelphia Police First Deputy Commissioner John Stanford said another motorist later stopped as the man remained in the roadway, but a second vehicle entered the bike lane and struck him at about 1:45 a.m. Two witnesses moved the victim from the road and called 911. Paramedics transported him to a hospital, where he was pronounced dead. Police are searching for the driver of a green 2023-2026 Kia Sportage that investigators say fled eastbound on Tabor Avenue after the collision.

The same officer who had spoken with the victim before the crash later became the first officer to respond to the hit-and-run scene, Stanford said. Police allege the officer then failed to secure the scene, initiate a report or investigation, or provide medical care. Investigators did not learn about the fatal crash until later that morning, when the medical examiner contacted the department seeking information about the victim. The officer, a 35th District member with about two years on the force, has been placed on administrative duty while an internal investigation proceeds.

Stanford described the officer's reported conduct as "very disturbing" and said it went against the department's expectations of empathy and compassion. Police have not publicly identified either the officer or the victim. Separate investigations remain underway into the officer's actions and the driver who fled the scene.

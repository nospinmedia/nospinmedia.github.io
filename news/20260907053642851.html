Armed man rushes toward Amy Acton at Ohio fair

Two pistols and brass knuckles recovered after arrest

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 7, 2026 at 2:45 PM ET

An armed man was arrested after forcing his way through a crowd toward Amy Acton, the Democratic candidate for Ohio governor, during a campaign appearance at the Canfield Fair on September 6, according to law enforcement. Several people were injured as the man pushed through attendees before members of Acton's Ohio State Highway Patrol security detail stopped him. Acton was not injured.

Authorities identified the suspect as Patrick Havas, 38, of Canfield. The Ohio State Highway Patrol said Havas knocked over multiple people while moving toward Acton. Mahoning County Sheriff Jerry Greene said Havas was holding up a cellphone as he aggressively pushed through the crowd and knocked over two older people using mobility scooters, causing one to fall to the ground. Acton's security detail then stopped Havas and took him into custody.

Authorities recovered two pistols, a Taser and brass knuckles from Havas. The Mahoning County Sheriff's Office said none of the weapons were brandished or removed from their holsters during the incident. Havas was taken to the Mahoning County Jail and booked on two misdemeanor assault counts and one count of disorderly conduct. Authorities had not publicly established a motive, and the investigation remained ongoing.

The incident drew condemnation from officials and candidates across Ohio's political spectrum. Republican Gov. Mike DeWine said violence or threats of violence at political and public events are unacceptable. Acton's Republican opponent, Vivek Ramaswamy, also condemned the incident, saying candidates should be able to meet voters without worrying about violence. Republican Sen. Jon Husted and Ohio Attorney General Andy Wilson issued similar statements condemning political violence and thanking law enforcement for its response.

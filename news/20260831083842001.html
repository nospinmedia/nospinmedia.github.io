Separate Mass Shootings Kill 3 and Wound 22

Suspects Remain at Large in Chicago and Trenton

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 31, 2026 at 11:45 AM ET

Separate mass shootings in Chicago, Illinois, and Trenton, New Jersey, left a combined 3 people dead and 22 wounded overnight as investigators in both cities searched for those responsible. The shootings occurred within roughly 15 minutes of each other in real time late August 29 and early August 30, although they happened hundreds of miles apart and authorities have reported no connection between them. No arrests had been announced in either investigation as of August 31.

In Chicago, police said gunfire erupted around 11:37 p.m. CDT at a large gathering in Washington Park near South Cottage Grove Avenue while officers were attempting to clear multiple gatherings in the area. A 35-year-old man, identified in local reporting as Rontonio M. Guterz, was killed and 14 other people were wounded. 20th Ward Alderman Jeanette Taylor said the shooter was positioned across the street from the park and fired toward the crowd. The gathering included a reunion involving former residents of the Robert Taylor Homes public housing complex. Cook County Crime Stoppers is offering up to $10,000 for information leading to an arrest as detectives review evidence and seek witnesses.

In Trenton, multiple gunmen approached a large gathering on the 100 block of Locust Street shortly after 12:20 a.m. EDT and opened fire into a crowd of about 100 people, according to authorities. A 33-year-old woman and a 42-year-old man were killed, while eight others, ranging in age from 23 to 43, were wounded and later stabilized. Trenton Mayor Reed Gusciora described the gathering as an unauthorized street block party. Investigators recovered more than 50 spent shell casings, indicating numerous rounds were fired and potentially multiple firearms were involved.

The Mercer County Homicide Task Force and Trenton Police Department are leading the New Jersey investigation, while Chicago detectives continue pursuing leads in the Washington Park shooting. Authorities in both cities have asked witnesses and anyone with video or other information to contact investigators as they work to identify the shooters and determine what led to the attacks.

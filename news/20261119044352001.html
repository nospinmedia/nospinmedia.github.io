Anthropic raises record $81.3 billion in massive IPO

Claude maker's total deal reaches $107.3 billion with private investment

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 19, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company behind Claude, raised $81.3 billion in the largest initial public offering on record, pricing 980 million Class A shares at $83 each before its shares begin trading Thursday on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

The offering was upsized from an initial 825 million shares. About 956 million shares were sold by Anthropic itself, with existing stockholders selling the remainder. Underwriters also have a 30-day option to purchase as many as 147 million additional shares. The IPO values Anthropic at roughly $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis and surpasses the previous record set by SpaceX in its June 2026 market debut.

A concurrent $26 billion private placement brings the combined transaction to approximately $107.3 billion. Google and Nvidia are investing $10 billion each, AMD is investing $2.5 billion, and Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft are contributing $1 billion each. TSMC is investing another $500 million.

Founded in 2021 by siblings Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, Anthropic is the first major frontier AI model developer to enter the public markets. The company generated $33.24 billion in revenue during the first nine months of 2026, while spending $18.8 billion on computing and infrastructure and posting a $1.26 billion operating loss.

Those computing costs remain a major financial risk. Anthropic has about $70.7 billion in compute commitments due during the next 12 months, part of much larger long-term commitments required to train and operate increasingly powerful AI models. The company also carries about $6.2 billion in debt.

The listing gives public investors direct exposure to one of the leading developers of frontier AI while testing whether Anthropic's rapid revenue growth can eventually support the enormous capital and computing costs required by its business.

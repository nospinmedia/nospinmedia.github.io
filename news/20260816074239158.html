Ukrainian Drones Strike Moscow Region, Killing 1

Warehouse fire follows one of war's largest reported drone attacks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 16, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Ukrainian long-range drones struck the Moscow region overnight on August 16, 2026, killing one person and igniting a large fire at a major Wildberries logistics center, according to Russian officials. Moscow Oblast Governor Andrey Vorobyov said an 83-year-old man was killed and several others were injured after drones hit the Koledino distribution complex near Podolsk, about 45 kilometers (28 miles) south of central Moscow. Emergency crews worked for hours to contain the fire while damage assessments continued.

Officials also reported damage to a second warehouse in Domodedovo, another key logistics hub serving the Moscow metropolitan area. The Russian Defense Ministry said air-defense systems intercepted 822 Ukrainian drones across the country overnight, including approximately 600 approaching the Moscow region. Those figures could not be independently verified, and Ukraine had not publicly commented on the operation as of August 16.

The strikes extended well beyond the capital region. Authorities in Rostov Oblast reported at least five additional deaths during overnight attacks, highlighting the broad geographic scope of the assault. Analysts say repeated strikes against warehouses, transportation hubs and energy infrastructure reflect Ukraine's continuing effort to disrupt military and commercial logistics deep inside Russia.

Wildberries is Russia's largest online retailer, and its Koledino complex is one of the country's busiest distribution centers. Damage to facilities near Moscow could affect regional freight movement and order fulfillment while repairs are completed. The attack also demonstrates Ukraine's ability to reach strategic targets hundreds of miles from the front lines despite Russia's extensive air-defense network.

Military analysts caution that battlefield claims from both sides are difficult to verify independently while operations are ongoing. Investigators continue assessing the full extent of the damage, casualty figures and the effectiveness of Russia's air defenses during what officials described as one of the largest drone attacks since the war began.

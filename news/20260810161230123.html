Liberty Island Boat Capsize Kills Mother, Infant

Captain arrested on 13 charges as Coast Guard investigates

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 10, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

A small recreational vessel carrying 14 people capsized near Liberty Island at about 10:30 p.m. Saturday, killing 27-year-old Sara Sanchez and her 5-month-old daughter, Antonella Garcia, according to the NYPD and U.S. Coast Guard. Police arrested the boat's operator, Manuel Hernandez, 46, on August 9, 2026, charging him with 13 counts of reckless endangerment as investigators continue examining the circumstances surrounding the crash.

Authorities said the overturned boat was spotted near the Statue of Liberty, where the sightseeing vessel Sea the City II rescued passengers from the water before emergency crews arrived. Twelve survivors were transported to hospitals with minor injuries. NYPD divers later recovered Sanchez and her daughter, who were taken to NYU Langone Hospital but were pronounced dead. The U.S. Coast Guard, NYPD, and other agencies are jointly investigating the incident.

Federal investigators are examining whether the trip operated as an unauthorized passenger-for-hire charter. Carrying paying passengers requires a properly credentialed captain and compliance with federal safety regulations, including required lifesaving equipment and, in some cases, vessel inspection certificates. Officials have not yet determined whether those requirements were met or whether they contributed to the capsize.

Captain Doreen McCarthy, commander of Coast Guard Sector New York, thanked the responding agencies and the crew of Sea the City II for their rapid rescue efforts. Authorities have not announced a cause of the capsize, and the investigation remains active. Hernandez's criminal case will proceed separately from any potential federal maritime enforcement actions.

Kraków gunman dies after killing six in city square

Prosecutors investigate attack as terrorist offense

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 9, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

The 24-year-old gunman who killed six people and wounded 15 others in Kraków, Poland, died in a hospital Monday after being critically wounded by police during the attack, authorities said. Prosecutors are investigating the mass shooting as an act potentially constituting a terrorist offense, while investigators examine the attacker's possible ideological motivation and whether anyone helped or inspired him.

The attacker drove into Main Square in Kraków's Old Town on Saturday wearing military-style clothing and a bulletproof vest before opening fire in the crowded tourist area. Police said he continued shooting even after officers wounded him. The six people killed were four women and two men, including Polish, British and Austrian citizens. Five foreign nationals were among the wounded. Two police officers who confronted the attacker were also injured, with one remaining in serious condition.

Authorities have said the gunman held far-right and ultranationalistic views, although officials have not established publicly that he belonged to an organized extremist group. Prosecutors are examining his electronic devices, how he obtained his weapons and ammunition, whether another person assisted him and whether his ideology played a role in the attack. Officials have cautioned that the investigation remains underway and conclusions about his motivation have not been finalized.

The investigation also extends to Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski, about 93 miles northeast of Kraków, where police found the bodies of the attacker's mother, father and 21-year-old sister at the family's home. Authorities said they appeared to have suffered gunshot wounds and believe the same attacker was responsible, although forensic examinations are continuing.

Poland began two days of national mourning Sunday. Residents gathered in Main Square to leave candles and flowers near the scene as investigators continued working through one of the country's deadliest mass shootings in decades.

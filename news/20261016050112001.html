Three Children and Teacher Killed in Oregon Elementary School Shooting

Armed Community Member Fatally Shoots 16-Year-Old Gunman

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 16, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

Three children and a teacher were killed after a 16-year-old gunman opened fire at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on October 15, 2026, before an armed community member followed the attacker into the school and fatally shot him. Two other people were critically injured.

The children killed were 5, 8 and 11 years old, according to police. A 7-year-old student and a 54-year-old adult were taken to St. Charles Medical Center Bend for treatment. Authorities had not publicly identified the victims or the teenage suspect as the investigation continued.

Police said the first emergency call came at about 12:59 p.m. local time, reporting that someone with a long gun was firing at a vehicle outside the school. Additional 911 calls then reported that the shooter had entered the building and was firing inside. Police said the gunman gained entry by shooting at locked doors.

Before officers reached the attacker, a community member who had seen the teenager enter the school armed followed him inside. The community member confronted and fatally shot the suspect, according to Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz. Oregon Governor Tina Kotek praised the intervention and said the person's actions prevented further loss of life while acknowledging the emotional burden the person now carries.

The Bend Police Department is leading the investigation with assistance from state and federal agencies, including the FBI. Investigators are examining evidence and working to determine a motive and whether the teenager had any connection to the school. Authorities said the shooter used a long gun, a category that includes rifles and shotguns.

The shooting left five people dead in total, including the gunman. The attack prompted a large emergency response and a community vigil in Bend as families and residents mourned the three children and teacher who were killed.

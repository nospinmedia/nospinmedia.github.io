Protests and clashes continue across France

Police use tear gas as unrest enters a fourth week

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 8, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

Protesters and police clashed again in Paris and other parts of France as anti-government and cost-of-living demonstrations entered a fourth week, with authorities deploying about 70,000 security personnel nationwide.

Demonstrators gathered across France on Saturday, November 7. The French Interior Ministry estimated that 146,000 people participated nationwide, including 46,000 in Paris. Labor unions placed turnout substantially higher, estimating approximately 250,000 demonstrators nationwide and 75,000 in Paris. The differing figures reflect separate estimates from authorities and protest organizers.

In Paris, confrontations broke out between some protesters and police, who used tear gas as crowds moved through parts of the capital. Fires and property damage were reported in some areas. Authorities also surrounded an unauthorized Yellow Vests demonstration near Place de la Concorde, preventing the gathering from proceeding. Police arrested movement figures including coordinator Benjamin Ball and spokeswoman Azelma Sigaux.

Clashes were not limited to Paris. Security forces used tear gas to disperse unauthorized demonstrations in Lyon and Montpellier, while protests took place in other communities across the country.

The demonstrations are part of a broader movement challenging the government over economic and political issues, including concerns about the cost of living. Many participants demonstrated peacefully, while some gatherings were accompanied by confrontations with police, fires and property damage.

The deployment of roughly 70,000 security personnel reflects the scale of the government’s response after several consecutive weekends of demonstrations. Authorities have maintained a large police presence while attempting to manage permitted protests and prevent unauthorized gatherings from developing into further unrest.

With official and union estimates differing substantially, Saturday’s demonstrations nevertheless showed that large numbers of people continue to participate after four weeks of protests.

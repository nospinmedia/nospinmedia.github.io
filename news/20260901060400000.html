SCO leaders meet in Kyrgyzstan for annual summit

Xi, Putin and Modi discuss security, trade and regional cooperation

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 1, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

Leaders from the 10-member Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) gathered in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, on September 1, 2026, for the organization's 26th Heads of State Summit, marking the bloc's 25th anniversary. Among those attending were Chinese President Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin, Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and other regional leaders, with discussions focused on security cooperation, economic integration, counterterrorism, and regional stability.

Hosted by Kyrgyz President Sadyr Japarov, the summit included bilateral meetings between Putin, Xi, and Modi alongside broader multilateral sessions. During talks with Xi, Putin reaffirmed Russia's commitment to expanding economic and strategic cooperation with China, while Xi described bilateral relations as resilient despite global uncertainty. Modi emphasized that regional disputes should be resolved through dialogue and diplomacy while calling for stronger cooperation against terrorism and greater economic connectivity among member nations.

The summit also advanced work on the Bishkek Declaration, addressing regional security, trade, transportation links, and cooperation across Eurasia. Leaders discussed expanding commercial ties, improving cross-border infrastructure, and strengthening coordination among member states. The gathering followed recent diplomatic efforts between India and China aimed at reducing tensions along their disputed border through new communication and confidence-building measures.

The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation includes China, Russia, India, Pakistan, Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, and Belarus, representing approximately 3.4 billion people and about one-quarter of global economic output. The summit concluded with preparations for the organization to transfer its rotating chairmanship to Pakistan for the 2027 summit cycle as member states pledged continued cooperation on shared regional priorities.

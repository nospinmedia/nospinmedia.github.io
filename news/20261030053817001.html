Russian drone strike kills 14 at Ukraine train station

Other attacks push Thursday's death toll to at least 20

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 30, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A Russian drone struck a crowded railway platform in Dnipro, Ukraine, on Thursday, October 29, killing at least 14 people as a commuter train was preparing to depart, according to Ukrainian officials. At least 14 others were wounded, including an 11-year-old boy, and six of the injured were reported in serious condition.

Ukraine's state railway operator Ukrzaliznytsia said a jet-powered drone hit a platform at Dnipro's main station. A commuter train bound for Zaporizhzhia was preparing to leave when the strike occurred. Ukrainian officials said the attack hit civilians using the country's passenger rail system. Russia has repeatedly attacked Ukraine's rail network during the war but denies deliberately targeting civilians.

The Dnipro strike was part of a deadly day across Ukraine. Two 15-year-old girls were killed in an attack in Chernihiv, while three people were killed when a drone struck residential buildings in Kremenchuk. A later missile strike killed another person in Kyiv, bringing the reported death toll from Thursday's attacks to at least 20. Strikes in Chernihiv, Kremenchuk and other areas also wounded dozens of people.

The attacks came while a limited six-day humanitarian truce remained in effect in the southern Kherson region. The U.S.-brokered arrangement applies to the area around Russian-held Oleshky and is intended to allow humanitarian assistance to reach residents and civilians to leave. It does not constitute a broader ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine.

The contrast underscored the limited scope of the agreement: humanitarian operations were proceeding in one part of the country while Russian drone and missile attacks continued elsewhere. New U.S.-backed diplomatic talks involving Ukraine and Russia are expected as efforts continue to find a path toward ending the war.

Pentagon fires top military planner over alleged leaks

Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia accused of sharing classified information

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 30, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth has fired Army Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia, the three-star general who led strategy, plans and policy for the Joint Chiefs of Staff, as the Pentagon investigates allegations that he leaked classified information to journalists.

A Pentagon official told the Associated Press that Sylvia is accused of sharing classified information involving the Iran war, the Russia-Ukraine conflict and other subjects. The official said Sylvia failed three polygraph examinations related to the leak investigation. Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said the department took personnel action to protect sensitive information and referred the matter to the Justice Department for potential further legal action.

A separate report from Axios, citing an internal investigative report and a U.S. official, said Sylvia admitted during questioning to providing classified information about Iran and Ukraine to two journalists from separate news organizations over approximately a year. Axios reported that the admissions were recorded on video. Sylvia has not publicly responded to the allegations.

Sylvia held one of the Pentagon's most important military planning positions. His directorate develops and coordinates strategy and military plans for the chairman of the Joint Chiefs and senior U.S. leadership. Before taking the position, Sylvia commanded the 101st Airborne Division and served more than 32 years in the Army.

The Pentagon has not publicly detailed exactly what classified information was allegedly disclosed. Reuters reported that Sylvia's dismissal followed an investigation by the Air Force Office of Special Investigations and comes amid a broader Pentagon effort to identify unauthorized disclosures to the news media.

President Donald Trump, asked about the case Thursday, said the administration was actively looking for leakers and warned that Sylvia could face serious consequences. No criminal charges against Sylvia had been announced as of Thursday.

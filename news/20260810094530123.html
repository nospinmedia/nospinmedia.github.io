Ukrainian Drone Strike Kills 13 in Russia's Tatarstan

75 injured as TANECO refinery and nearby hostel are hit

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 10, 2026 at 1:15 PM ET

A Ukrainian drone attack struck the Russian city of Nizhnekamsk in Tatarstan on August 10, 2026, killing 13 people and injuring 75, according to the latest reporting from regional authorities. The city lies about 1,200 kilometers (750 miles) from Ukraine and is home to major oil-refining and petrochemical facilities.

Ukraine acknowledged carrying out the attack and said its forces struck the TANECO oil refinery, where a fire broke out. Russian regional authorities confirmed that Nizhnekamsk came under a large-scale drone attack, although Russian and Ukrainian accounts differed over some details of the targets and damage. The attack also damaged a nearby hostel where casualties were reported. A child was among those killed, and Tatarstan declared a day of mourning.

At least seven Uzbek citizens were among the dead, according to Uzbekistan's consulate in Kazan. Other foreign nationals were also among the casualties. Emergency crews continued working at damaged locations while authorities treated the injured and assessed the extent of the destruction.

Nizhnekamsk is a major Russian energy center containing the TANECO refinery, another oil refinery and a large petrochemical plant. Ukrainian forces have repeatedly targeted oil and industrial facilities inside Russia with long-range drones. The August 10 strike follows earlier drone attacks on industrial facilities around Nizhnekamsk, but the latest attack produced a substantially higher casualty toll.

The 75 injured reported in the latest Associated Press update is a material increase from the 39 injuries cited in earlier reports. With the death toll remaining at 13, the attack is among the deadliest Ukrainian strikes reported inside Russia since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine began in 2022.

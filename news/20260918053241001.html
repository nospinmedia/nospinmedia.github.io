Trump weighs return to large-scale attacks on Iran

Gulf leaders set for talks as U.S. considers war's next phase

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 18, 2026 at 6:16 AM ET

President Trump says he is approaching a major decision over whether the United States should resume large-scale military attacks against Iran, as the conflict enters a critical period ahead of meetings with regional leaders at the United Nations General Assembly in New York.

Trump told Axios on September 17 that he is considering whether to dramatically intensify attacks against the Iranian government. The comments come ahead of a planned September 22 meeting with leaders from Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman. Trump said he wants to hear directly from the Gulf states about their positions and conditions as he considers the next phase of U.S. strategy.

The administration has held back from another major round of attacks since August, when Saudi Arabia and Qatar raised concerns that Iran could retaliate against Gulf oil and gas infrastructure. The United States has instead maintained economic pressure, a naval blockade of Iranian ports and military operations aimed at increasing tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz. U.S. officials told Axios that American forces have been ordered to maintain their current Middle East presence through the end of 2026, preserving the ability to return to large-scale combat if ordered.

Trump has also recently expressed hope that the war may be approaching an end. Reuters reported that the conflict began with the February 28 U.S.-Israeli attack on Iran and has continued for nearly seven months, while disruptions to regional energy production and shipping have contributed to elevated oil and fuel prices.

The upcoming Gulf meeting could help determine whether Washington makes another push for diplomacy or intensifies military operations. Trump said Iran remains in direct contact with the United States and continues to seek an agreement, but he has not announced when he will make his decision.

Actress Hayden Panettiere Dies At 36

Family confirms death as authorities investigate circumstances

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 17, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

Actress Hayden Panettiere, best known for starring in Heroes and Nashville, died on August 16 at the age of 36, her family confirmed. Her father, Skip Panettiere, described her as "an incredible light and a force of nature" while requesting privacy for the family. Authorities in South Carolina confirmed they are investigating the circumstances surrounding her death but have not released an official cause of death.

Panettiere began acting as a young child before rising to international recognition as Claire Bennet in the NBC series Heroes. She later earned two Golden Globe nominations for portraying country singer Juliette Barnes in Nashville and appeared in films including Remember the Titans, A Bug's Life, and the Scream franchise. Earlier this year, she published the memoir This Is Me: A Reckoning, detailing her struggles with addiction, domestic abuse, and the loss of her younger brother, Jansen Panettiere, in 2023.

Representatives said additional information may be released as authorities continue their investigation. Officials have not indicated that foul play is suspected, and no further details about the circumstances of her death have been made public. News of her passing prompted tributes from colleagues and fans across the entertainment industry.

Panettiere is survived by her 11-year-old daughter, Kaya. Her death marks the loss of an actress whose career spanned more than three decades, from childhood television roles to leading performances in some of television's most recognizable series of the 2000s and 2010s.

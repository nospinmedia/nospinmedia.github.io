Ukraine Targets Yandex Data Centers in New Front of War With Russia

Drone Strikes Put Critical Internet Infrastructure and Online Services at Risk

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 17, 2026 at 6:52 PM ET

Ukraine is increasingly targeting Russian data centers linked to Yandex, the technology company often described as "Russia's Google," expanding its long-range strike campaign into infrastructure that supports some of the country's most widely used digital services.

Recent Ukrainian drone attacks have targeted data centers in several Russian regions, including facilities associated with Yandex's cloud computing and internet operations. The company operates search, mapping, navigation, cloud and other digital services used by millions of people and businesses across Russia.

The attacks represent a shift from Ukraine's more familiar long-range targets such as oil refineries, fuel depots and military facilities. Data centers contain servers, networking equipment and other specialized hardware needed to keep online services operating, making them potentially important economic and communications infrastructure.

Some Russian internet services experienced disruptions following the attacks. However, claims circulating online that the strikes had disabled large Russian disinformation or automated social-media networks have not been established. Analysts examining changes in internet traffic said at least some of the decline in automated activity was connected to Yandex's own search-crawling systems going offline rather than evidence that foreign influence networks had been eliminated.

The strikes also highlight a potential vulnerability created by Russia's reliance on large centralized technology providers. Damage to specialized computing equipment could be difficult to repair quickly because Western sanctions have restricted Russia's access to some advanced technology and replacement components.

Russia has repeatedly targeted Ukraine's energy, communications and other infrastructure during the war, while Ukraine has expanded the range and variety of targets it strikes inside Russia using domestically produced long-range drones.

The attacks on Yandex facilities show how the conflict is increasingly extending beyond traditional military and energy targets into the digital infrastructure that supports everyday economic and communications activity.

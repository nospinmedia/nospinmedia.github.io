Bills Support Ed Oliver After Death of 2-Year-Old Son

Team Mourns as Family Grieves Texas Drowning Tragedy

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 23, 2026 at 12:15 PM ET

The Buffalo Bills have expressed their support for defensive tackle Ed Oliver following the death of his 2-year-old son, Ed Oliver Jr., in what authorities have described as an accidental drowning in Texas. General manager Brandon Beane released a statement calling the loss an "unthinkable tragedy" and said the organization would continue to support Oliver and his family during their time of grief. Oliver did not travel with the team for its preseason game as teammates and coaches rallied around him.

According to the Harris County Sheriff's Office, deputies responded to a June 28 emergency call reporting an unresponsive child at a residence in Huffman, Texas. The toddler was transported to a nearby hospital, where he was pronounced dead. Authorities have continued to treat the case as an accidental drowning, and investigators have not announced any criminal allegations. Officials have also declined to release certain records while the investigation remains open under provisions of the Texas Public Information Act.

Following Buffalo's preseason victory over the Cleveland Browns, quarterback Josh Allen said the team's focus was on supporting Oliver and respecting the family's privacy. Bills players and coaches described the tragedy as heartbreaking and emphasized that the organization would provide whatever assistance Oliver and his family need during the grieving process. Messages of condolence also came from across the NFL and the broader football community.

Oliver, the No. 9 overall pick in the 2019 NFL Draft out of the University of Houston, has become one of the cornerstones of Buffalo's defense during his NFL career. While football remains secondary amid the family's loss, the Bills said their priority is ensuring Oliver has the time, privacy, and support needed as he mourns the death of his young son.

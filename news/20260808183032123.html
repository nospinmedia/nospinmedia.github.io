Norway FA Calls for Infantino’s Resignation

UEFA keeps boycott threat; FIFA denies payoff allegation

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 8, 2026 at 11:33 AM ET

Norway’s Football Federation (NFF) president Lise Klaveness on August 6, 2026 formally called on FIFA president Gianni Infantino to resign, citing a breakdown of trust after his abandoned plan to sell a stake in World Cup commercial rights via a new subsidiary. The NFF decision followed a board meeting, with Klaveness saying leadership must be reassessed when trust is gone.

The collapsed proposal—structured around the FIFA Forward Enterprise (FFE)—has already prompted a rare escalation from UEFA, which said its threat to boycott FIFA competitions remains in place even after FIFA apologized and pulled the plan. The English Football Association has withdrawn support for Infantino’s re‑election, while Mexico’s federation has voiced backing. The dispute centers on governance and control of FIFA’s money‑making operations; FIFA has confirmed the FFE idea is off the table.

Infantino faces this turmoil ahead of a leadership vote expected in March 2027 in Rabat, Morocco. The Norwegian move intensifies pressure on national associations to declare their positions as UEFA weighs next steps. A boycott by Europe—football’s most commercially significant confederation—would have sweeping implications for global tournaments, broadcasters, sponsors, players, and fans, making the governance standoff more than an internal spat.

Separately, Infantino on August 8, 2026 “strongly” denied fresh corruption allegations stemming from a newspaper investigation into his UEFA tenure from 2009 to 2016. A FIFA spokesman said the claims are “categorically untrue” and defamatory. In its own statement, UEFA said a “departure payment” was made to a former junior employee and that MBA tuition was covered, asserting the actions complied with rules at the time; UEFA did not characterize the payment as hush money. The reporting does not state that formal complaints were filed against Infantino, and key details remain under dispute, so the allegations should be treated as unproven.

Man charged with murder after woman found dead in Central Park

63-year-old victim suffered stab wounds and head trauma

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 28, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

A 36-year-old man has been arrested and charged with murder after a 63-year-old woman was found dead inside New York City's Central Park on Sunday morning. Police said Jonathan Robles was charged with murder and criminal possession of a weapon following questioning by investigators.

The NYPD received a 911 call shortly after 9:30 a.m. on September 27 reporting an unconscious person near a gazebo around East 74th Street and East Drive, close to the Central Park Boathouse. Responding officers found the woman unconscious and unresponsive with trauma to her head. EMS pronounced her dead at the scene. Subsequent reporting citing police said the victim had suffered multiple stab wounds.

Robles was questioned by investigators Sunday before police announced his arrest Monday. Authorities had not publicly disclosed the victim's identity while they worked to notify her family. Police also had not announced what relationship, if any, existed between Robles and the woman or identified a motive for the killing.

The discovery initially prompted an investigation into an unexplained death. Early Sunday reports said only that the woman had suffered significant trauma, while later information established that she had been stabbed. The Office of Chief Medical Examiner is responsible for formally determining the cause and manner of death.

The killing occurred while a nor'easter was affecting New York City, bringing heavy rain to the area as detectives examined the scene. Police cordoned off the gazebo and surrounding area while investigators searched for evidence. Authorities have not indicated that the killing represented an ongoing threat to other people using the park.

The investigation remains active as detectives work to establish what happened before the woman was killed and the circumstances surrounding the encounter.

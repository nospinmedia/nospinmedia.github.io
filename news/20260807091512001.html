Trump-Endorsed Rep. Andy Ogles Loses Tennessee GOP Primary

Charlie Hatcher advances to face Columbia Mayor Chaz Molder

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 7, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Rep. Andy Ogles lost the Republican primary in Tennessee's 5th Congressional District on August 6, 2026, conceding to former Tennessee Agriculture Commissioner Charlie Hatcher. The defeat ends Ogles' bid for another term despite an endorsement from President Trump and sets up a November general election between Hatcher and Columbia Mayor Chaz Molder, the Democratic nominee.

With just over 80% of ballots counted Thursday night, Hatcher led Ogles by more than six percentage points, prompting the incumbent to concede. Ogles, a member of the House Freedom Caucus first elected in 2022, received public support from President Trump, who urged Republican voters to back him on Election Day. Hatcher was endorsed by Gov. Bill Lee and former Gov. Bill Haslam, while outside groups spent heavily in support of his campaign. Campaign finance filings also showed Hatcher outraised the incumbent during the race.

The election was the first held under Tennessee's newly redrawn 5th Congressional District, adopted in May 2026. The revised boundaries shifted the district away from its previous Nashville-centered map and added large numbers of new voters. According to an Associated Press analysis, roughly 82% of the district's voters had not previously been represented by Ogles, reducing the traditional advantages of incumbency.

Ogles also faced months of public scrutiny over campaign-finance questions and past social media posts. In 2024, the FBI seized his phone as part of a campaign-finance investigation before later returning it without criminal charges being filed. Hatcher will now face Molder in the November 3 general election as Republicans seek to retain the seat.

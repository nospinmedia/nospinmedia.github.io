Katie Zacharia Out as White House Press Secretary After Five Days

Abrupt Departure Follows Rocky Debut and Scrutiny Over Her Legal Credentials

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 17, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

Katie Zacharia is out as White House press secretary after just five days in the job, ending an unusually brief tenure following criticism of her first briefing and questions about her legal credentials.

Multiple news organizations reported on October 16 that Zacharia had been removed from the position. President Trump had publicly cast doubt on her future earlier in the day, telling reporters that he had encouraged her to reconsider whether she wanted the pressure associated with the job. Zacharia later confirmed her departure and thanked Trump for the opportunity to serve in the administration.

Zacharia's short tenure attracted attention almost immediately. Her first White House briefing drew criticism after she took only a small number of questions, while subsequent reporting examined statements she had made about being a lawyer. Zacharia acknowledged that she had graduated from law school but failed the California bar examination and did not take it again.

Her departure represents a significant change from the story No Spin Media reported a day earlier, when Zacharia confirmed the failed bar exam but remained White House press secretary. The latest development ends her tenure altogether.

Zacharia had taken over one of the administration's most visible communications positions only days earlier. Her removal leaves the White House needing another public spokesperson just over two weeks before the November 3 midterm elections.

The White House has not announced a permanent replacement. Zacharia's five-day stint ranks among the shortest tenures for a White House press secretary.

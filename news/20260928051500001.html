27 killed in two South Africa mass shootings

Police hunt gunmen after attacks hours apart

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 28, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

Police in South Africa are searching for gunmen after two separate mass shootings killed 27 people and wounded 26 others within hours near the country's two largest cities. The attacks occurred in Wedela, southwest of Johannesburg, and Lwandle, near Cape Town, as authorities investigate possible motives ranging from illegal-mining disputes to a business rivalry.

In Wedela, police said eight gunmen armed with AK-47-style rifles and pistols attacked a bar late Saturday, September 26. The assailants first opened fire on people outside before entering the bar and continuing to shoot. 17 people — 13 men and four women — were killed, while at least 15 others were injured. Police said some patrons were searched and had their mobile phones taken, and two vehicles outside the bar were set on fire as the attackers fled.

About four hours later, another shooting occurred at a store connected to an entertainment venue in Lwandle township. Police said 10 people were killed and 11 wounded. Multiple suspects were being sought, and no arrests had been announced in either case. Specialized police units were deployed to both areas, while soldiers were also present at the Wedela scene as part of an existing national operation against violent crime.

Authorities have not established definitive motives. Initial investigations cited by Reuters indicated the Wedela attack could involve a turf dispute among illegal miners, while the Lwandle shooting could be connected to a business rivalry. Police were continuing to investigate those possibilities rather than treating either explanation as established fact.

The shootings came just five days after gunmen killed at least 11 people, including a pregnant woman, at a house party in KwaMakhutha near Durban. South African police recorded 5,427 homicides during the three months ending June 30 — an average of nearly 60 killings per day.

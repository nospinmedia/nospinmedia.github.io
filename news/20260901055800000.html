NYPD fatally shoots Times Square stabbing suspect

Random rush-hour attack leaves one woman dead and one man injured

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 1, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

A woman armed with two kitchen knives was fatally shot by New York City Police Department (NYPD) officers after allegedly carrying out a random stabbing attack in Times Square during the evening rush on August 31, 2026. Police identified the suspect as Pamela Cisneros, 49, of Queens. Authorities said the attack killed a 32-year-old woman and seriously injured a 68-year-old man before officers confronted the suspect near West 42nd Street and Seventh Avenue.

According to NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch, the attack began shortly after 4:20 p.m. when Cisneros allegedly pulled two large kitchen knives from a shopping bag and stabbed the victims without provocation. Officers from the nearby NYPD Times Square substation responded within moments and repeatedly ordered her to drop the weapons. Police said officers also deployed Tasers during an approximately four-minute confrontation, but the devices failed to stop her. Authorities said Cisneros then advanced toward officers while still armed, prompting two officers to fire their service weapons.

All three individuals were transported to Bellevue Hospital. The female victim and Cisneros were pronounced dead, while the injured man remained hospitalized in stable condition after suffering abdominal wounds. Tisch said investigators have found no indication that the attack was connected to terrorism and described it as an apparently random act of violence. She added that Cisneros had no prior criminal arrest record but had previous documented encounters with police involving mental health evaluations.

The NYPD Force Investigation Division has opened the standard review required after any police shooting, while detectives continue investigating the stabbing. Mayor Zohran Mamdani praised the officers' rapid response, saying their actions likely prevented additional casualties in one of the city's busiest pedestrian areas.

Harvey Weinstein sentenced to 15 years in prison

New York sentence follows retrial over 2006 sexual assault

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 24, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

Former Hollywood producer Harvey Weinstein has been sentenced to 15 years in prison for sexually assaulting former Project Runway production assistant Miriam Haley at his Manhattan apartment in 2006. The sentence also includes five years of post-release supervision, and Weinstein will be required to register as a sex offender.

A New York jury convicted Weinstein in 2025 of committing a criminal sexual act against Haley. The case had previously resulted in a conviction in 2020, when Weinstein was found guilty of assaulting Haley and of raping actress Jessica Mann. He received a 23-year sentence, but New York's highest court overturned that conviction in April 2024, ruling that the original trial judge improperly allowed testimony about allegations that were not part of the charges. Prosecutors then retried the case.

During the sentencing hearing, Haley described the lasting personal and professional effects of the assault and the repeated legal proceedings that followed. Prosecutors had requested a 20-year sentence, arguing that Weinstein had failed to accept responsibility. His attorneys sought a nine-year sentence, pointing to his age, health and the more than six years he has already spent incarcerated.

Weinstein addressed the court before sentencing, expressing remorse for how his past conduct affected others and specifically saying he felt remorse toward Haley, while continuing to maintain that he was innocent of the crime. The judge ultimately imposed the 15-year sentence.

The New York case is separate from Weinstein's California conviction. A Los Angeles jury convicted him in 2022 of three sexual assault counts, including rape, and he received a 16-year prison sentence in that case. In New York, prosecutors dropped the remaining rape charge involving Mann in June 2026 after juries repeatedly failed to reach a unanimous verdict on that count.

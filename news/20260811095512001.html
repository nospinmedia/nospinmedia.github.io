Report Details Secret Flight Used During Trump's Return From NATO Summit

Investigation says decoy aircraft helped counter reported Iranian threat

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 11, 2026 at 5:16 PM ET

A Washington Post investigation published on August 10, 2026 reports that President Donald Trump returned from last month's NATO summit in Ankara, Türkiye, aboard a military C-32A aircraft as part of a security operation designed to reduce risks from a reported Iranian assassination threat. According to the report, Air Force One was used as a decoy while the president traveled separately under heightened security measures.

The report says Trump publicly boarded the older presidential aircraft before secretly transferring to the C-32A using airport service equipment outside public view. Both aircraft later stopped at RAF Mildenhall in the United Kingdom, where Trump reportedly continued his journey aboard the newer presidential aircraft donated by Qatar. Members of the traveling press remained on the decoy aircraft and were instructed to keep window shades closed during departure, according to the newspaper.

White House Communications Director Steven Cheung did not address the reported security operation directly but said the president faces serious threats and that every available measure is used to protect him. The U.S. Secret Service declined to comment on security procedures. A former Secret Service official told the newspaper that while such deception operations are uncommon, they fall within established presidential protection practices when intelligence indicates elevated risks.

The report expands on earlier disclosures that U.S. officials had taken additional precautions because of threats linked to tensions with Iran and its regional proxies. The White House has not publicly confirmed the specific operational details described in the investigation, and several aspects of the reported deception plan remain based on unnamed sources familiar with the operation.

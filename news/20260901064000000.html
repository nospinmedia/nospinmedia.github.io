Met cancels planned John Galliano exhibition

Museum withdraws 2027 Met Gala theme after public backlash

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 1, 2026 at 6:50 PM ET

The Metropolitan Museum of Art and fashion designer John Galliano have jointly canceled the planned 2027 Costume Institute exhibition, "John Galliano: Horizons," which had been selected as the inspiration for the 2027 Met Gala. Museum Director and Chief Executive Officer Max Hollein said the decision followed discussions between the museum and Galliano after criticism surrounding the designer's past antisemitic and racist remarks intensified following the exhibition's announcement.

In a public statement, Galliano apologized again to the Jewish and Asian communities, saying he remained deeply remorseful for his past conduct and did not want the planned exhibition to overshadow the work of the museum or the Costume Institute. The Metropolitan Museum said it respected the joint decision and confirmed that a new exhibition and Met Gala theme for 2027 will be announced at a later date.

The controversy centered on incidents from 2010 and 2011, when Galliano was dismissed as creative director of Christian Dior after videos surfaced showing him making antisemitic remarks in a Paris café. In 2011, a French court found him guilty of public insults based on race, religion, or ethnicity and imposed suspended fines. Galliano later completed treatment for addiction, rebuilt his career, and served as creative director of Maison Margiela from 2014 through 2024, but the planned retrospective renewed debate over whether institutions should honor public figures whose past conduct caused significant harm.

The cancellation follows objections from Jewish organizations and other critics who argued that the exhibition's timing, close to Yom HaShoah, would be inappropriate despite Galliano's public apologies and professional rehabilitation. Museum officials emphasized that the decision was made jointly with the designer and said planning has already begun for a replacement exhibition that will serve as the centerpiece of the 2027 Met Gala.

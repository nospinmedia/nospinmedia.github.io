Trespasser stopped at Kamala Harris' Malibu home

Private security intervenes before person reaches residence

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 13, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

A person illegally entered the property of former Vice President Kamala Harris and her husband, Doug Emhoff, in Malibu, California, late Friday night, but was stopped by private security before reaching the residence. Neither Harris nor Emhoff was at the property during the incident, and authorities reported no injuries.

The Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department responded around 10:30 p.m. after receiving a report of a suspicious person at the property. Harris spokesperson Eduardo Negrón said the person had illegally accessed the grounds but was intercepted by Harris' private security detail before reaching the home. Deputies arrived after security personnel had already detained the individual outside the residence.

Sheriff's officials said deputies questioned the person and advised them about trespassing. The individual then agreed to leave voluntarily and was not arrested. Authorities completed a trespassing report and said patrols in the surrounding area would be increased as a precaution. Officials had not publicly identified the person or disclosed a suspected motive as of the latest reporting.

The incident comes after Harris lost U.S. Secret Service protection in 2025. Federal law generally provides former vice presidents and their spouses Secret Service protection for up to six months after leaving office. Before departing office, President Joe Biden extended Harris' protection beyond that period to a total of 18 months, but President Trump revoked the additional protection in August 2025. California and Los Angeles law enforcement initially provided supplemental protection while alternative arrangements were developed, and Harris subsequently moved to private security.

Friday's incident did not result in an encounter between the trespasser and Harris or Emhoff, and law enforcement had not reported evidence that the person intended to harm either of them.

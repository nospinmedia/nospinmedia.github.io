WNBA Launches Executive Review Of Gender-Eligibility Policy

League Leaders To Discuss Current Rules At Next Week’s Meeting

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 9, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert told teams in a league memo sent on Friday, August 7, 2026, that an executive task force of team presidents and general managers will review the league's gender-eligibility policy during a previously scheduled meeting next week. The memo states the league will continue handling the issue “thoughtfully” while prioritizing competitive integrity and gathering input from league stakeholders.

The current eligibility standard is governed by the WNBA Collective Bargaining Agreement. Article XIII, Section 1(a) states that “only players who are women are eligible to play in the WNBA,” but it does not define biological sex, gender identity, or establish medical or hormone-based eligibility criteria. Unlike policies used by organizations such as the International Olympic Committee and World Athletics, the WNBA's agreement leaves those details undefined.

The review follows weeks of public discussion after Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham voiced support for restricting transgender participation in women's sports, prompting demonstrations during recent Fever games. On Aug. 7, former NBA players Enes Kanter Freedom and Royce White also announced they considered themselves eligible for a future WNBA Draft, though the league has announced no rule changes or individual eligibility decisions. There has never been a publicly out transgender woman on a WNBA roster.

League officials said team executives will discuss the issue next week and continue working with the Women's National Basketball Players Association as part of any future review. Any substantive change to player eligibility would likely require action under the current collective bargaining agreement or future negotiations with the union. Until then, the league's existing eligibility rules remain in effect.

U.S. Intelligence Warns Iranian Plots to Kill Americans Have Reached Advanced Stages

Memo Says Tehran Has Increased Efforts to Target U.S. Personnel Abroad

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 24, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A new U.S. intelligence warning says Iran has substantially increased efforts to target American personnel abroad and that plots to kill U.S. citizens have reached "advanced stages" in parts of the Middle East and other countries.

The warning was contained in an unclassified October 23 memo from Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton to Secretary of State Marco Rubio. The memo was obtained by CBS News and Axios. It does not identify specific targets or countries where the alleged plots are underway, and it does not say that an attack is imminent.

Clayton said the intelligence community is working to downgrade sensitive intelligence so information about the threats can be shared with more State Department personnel who could be at risk. CIA Director John Ratcliffe was copied on the memo.

Separate reporting based on U.S. officials indicates intelligence assessments in recent weeks have also examined potential threats involving Iranian operatives inside the United States, including possible threats against officials such as ambassadors. Those details were not contained in the October 23 memo, and officials have not publicly identified specific targets.

The warning comes as the United States reduces its diplomatic presence across parts of the Middle East. The State Department ordered non-emergency government employees and family members to leave Saudi Arabia, while operations and staffing have also been restricted at U.S. embassies in Jordan, Lebanon, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates.

U.S. military officials have also reduced personnel at some locations in the region amid continuing threats from Iran and Iran-backed groups.

The intelligence warning provides additional context for those precautionary moves while underscoring an important limitation: U.S. officials say the Iranian threat has intensified, but the publicly disclosed memo does not reveal who is being targeted, where specific plots are underway or whether an attack is imminent.

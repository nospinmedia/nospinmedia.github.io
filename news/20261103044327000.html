2 dead, 2 injured in California beach shooting

Investigators say circumstances and motive remain unknown

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 3, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Two people were killed and two others injured after gunfire erupted near Moss Landing State Beach in California early Monday, prompting authorities to close the beach while investigators worked to determine what happened.

Deputies responded to Jetty Road in Moss Landing shortly before 2 a.m. following reports of multiple gunshots, according to the Monterey County Sheriff's Office. Officers found two people dead at the scene with apparent gunshot wounds. Authorities had not publicly identified either person as of the latest reporting.

A third person was transported from the scene to a hospital with non-life-threatening injuries. Authorities later learned that a fourth person had arrived separately at a hospital with gunshot wounds. Officials had not disclosed that person's condition.

Investigators said several important details remain unknown, including whether the people involved knew one another, what led to the gunfire and whether there is a suspect. Sheriff's officials also stopped short of formally classifying the deaths as homicides while the circumstances are still being investigated.

Authorities were examining an area near the beach where a gathering may have taken place, but officials said they had not established a connection between that gathering and the shooting. The beach normally closes at sunset, meaning those present were there after normal operating hours.

California State Parks closed Jetty Road and Moss Landing State Beach while sheriff's detectives processed the scene. State park officers and the California Highway Patrol also responded to assist with the investigation.

Officials said there was no active threat to the Moss Landing community and described the investigation as likely to be lengthy as detectives reconstruct the events surrounding the shooting.

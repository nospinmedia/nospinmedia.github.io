Congress Demands Answers Over Starlink Disruptions in Ukraine

Rep. Mike Turner Says Investigation Is Needed Because Lives Are at Risk

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 19, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

Republican Rep. Mike Turner of Ohio is calling for a congressional investigation into disruptions of satellite communications used by Ukraine, saying the explanation provided by the Trump administration does not adequately account for why the service was restricted during Russian attacks.

Ukraine reported problems last week with Starlink and Starshield, the military version of the SpaceX satellite network provided under a U.S. defense arrangement. Ukrainian forces rely heavily on satellite connectivity for communications and drone operations. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said some restrictions remain and have interfered with Ukraine's ability to operate interceptor systems against Russian drones.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the United States did not order Starlink speeds reduced. He told reporters that nobody in the administration directed the slowdown and that it had, in his words, "sort of happened." Rubio said his understanding was that service had subsequently been restored.

Turner, a member of the House Armed Services Committee, rejected that explanation during an appearance on CBS News' Face the Nation. He said there needs to be a congressional investigation to determine who was responsible, adding that "lives are at risk and lives are lost over these things." Turner said reports indicate service has returned in some areas while remaining restricted in others and called for it to be completely restored.

His demand adds to bipartisan scrutiny already underway in the Senate. Nine senators, including Republicans and Democrats, asked the Department of Defense inspector general to investigate disruptions to U.S.-maintained Starshield systems supporting Ukraine. The senators asked investigators to determine whether U.S. officials approved or knew about the disruptions and whether actions by the Defense Department or its contractors contributed to them.

The senators also raised concerns that disruptions to satellite service, intelligence sharing and early-warning capabilities may have endangered U.S. diplomatic, military and security personnel during Russian air attacks.

The congressional pressure represents a new development after earlier reports established that Ukraine was experiencing unexplained Starlink restrictions.

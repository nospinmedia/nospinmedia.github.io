School Roof Collapse in Pakistan Kills 34 Children and Teacher

Dozens More Injured as Authorities Investigate Building Safety

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 16, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

At least 34 children and a teacher were killed when the roof of a private school collapsed in Peshawar, Pakistan, as students were attending classes on October 15, 2026, according to local authorities. Dozens of other children were injured in one of the country's deadliest recent school-building disasters.

The collapse occurred at the Al-Khair Education Center in Peshawar, the capital of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province in northwestern Pakistan. About 110 students were inside the school when the roof gave way, trapping children and staff beneath concrete and other debris.

Emergency crews and residents worked through the rubble to reach those trapped inside. The narrow streets surrounding the school complicated access for rescue vehicles and heavy equipment. Injured students were transported to hospitals across Peshawar, including Lady Reading Hospital, as medical facilities treated victims for fractures and other injuries.

Authorities opened an investigation into the cause of the collapse and the condition of the building. Police detained the school's owner as investigators examined whether structural problems, construction standards or other safety failures contributed to the disaster. Any determination of criminal responsibility will depend on the results of that investigation.

The disaster has renewed scrutiny of building safety at private schools in Pakistan, particularly facilities operating in structures that were not originally designed as schools. Officials ordered inspections of school buildings following the collapse as authorities sought to determine whether similar risks exist elsewhere.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif expressed condolences to the families of those killed and directed authorities to provide medical assistance to the injured. President Asif Ali Zardari also expressed grief over the deaths and called for measures to prevent similar tragedies.

Rescue and recovery operations continued as authorities worked to establish a final casualty count and determine why the school structure failed.

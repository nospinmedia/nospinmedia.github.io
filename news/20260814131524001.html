USS George Washington Heads To Middle East To Relieve USS Lincoln

Move Comes After Extended Deployment Raises Crew Welfare Concerns

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 14, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

The U.S. Navy has ordered the aircraft carrier USS George Washington (CVN-73) to the Middle East to relieve the USS Abraham Lincoln (CVN-72) after one of the Navy's longest recent carrier deployments. Navy officials said the George Washington departed the Asia-Pacific after leaving Da Nang, Vietnam, and is now transiting toward the region, allowing the Lincoln to conclude an extended deployment supporting U.S. military operations involving Iran.

The deployment follows more than 260 days at sea for the Abraham Lincoln strike group. According to U.S. Central Command, the carrier has flown more than 10,000 combat sorties and expended approximately 1.5 million pounds of ordnance during operations since the conflict escalated earlier this year. The Lincoln departed San Diego on November 21, 2025, and has remained deployed through multiple mission extensions as regional tensions continued.

The prolonged deployment has drawn increased congressional scrutiny over sailor welfare and operational readiness. On August 12, Sen. Richard Blumenthal requested information from Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao regarding deployment extensions, mental health services, and logistical support for the carrier strike group. Some family members and military news organizations have also raised concerns about the effects of the lengthy deployment on sailors. Navy officials acknowledged one sailor went overboard in early August and was safely recovered while maintaining they have not identified an increase in suicidal ideation among the crew.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth has disputed reports suggesting widespread problems aboard the carrier, while Navy leaders say the relief deployment reflects normal force management and ongoing efforts to maintain operational readiness. The arrival of the George Washington will allow the Abraham Lincoln to begin returning home after months of sustained operations in one of the world's most active military theaters.

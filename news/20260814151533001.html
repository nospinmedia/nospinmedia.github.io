Kennedy Center Board Approves Trump Inscription And Two-Year Renovation Closure

Vote Sets Up New Legal Fight Over Court Order

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 14, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

The John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts Board of Trustees voted on August 13, 2026, to approve adding an exterior inscription reading, "Restored and Renovated by President Donald J. Trump," and to close most of the performing arts complex for approximately two years during a major renovation project. The action comes as litigation continues over the center's signage, governance, and renovation plans.

The board also approved renaming the main entrance plaza as the President Donald J. Trump Plaza, while stating that limited performances and public programming would continue at the adjacent REACH campus during construction. The meeting was conducted virtually. Earlier this year, U.S. District Judge Christopher R. Cooper ruled that only Congress has authority to change the official name of the national memorial and ordered previous Trump-related signage removed while litigation proceeds. Following Thursday's vote, ex officio trustee Rep. Joyce Beatty said the new action was "a transparent effort to circumvent the Court's ruling."

Center officials maintain the renovation project is necessary to modernize aging infrastructure and preserve the performance venue for future generations. Supporters of the plan argue the inscription recognizes the administration that authorized and funded the renovation work. Critics contend the newly approved inscription and plaza designation could conflict with the court's earlier ruling and further complicate the ongoing litigation.

The Kennedy Center remains a federally chartered memorial governed by statutory requirements that limit changes to its official name and commemorative features. Additional court proceedings are expected to determine whether the newly approved inscription, plaza designation, and renovation plans can move forward as approved by the board.

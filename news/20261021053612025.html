UK sends fighter jets to help defend Saudi Arabia

RAF deployment responds to Houthi missile and drone attacks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 21, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

The United Kingdom is deploying Royal Air Force fighter jets to Saudi Arabia to help defend the kingdom against missile and drone attacks by Yemen's Houthis, expanding Britain's military role as conflict in the Middle East threatens regional infrastructure and shipping.

British Defence Secretary John Healey said the deployment was requested by Saudi Arabia and is intended to strengthen the country's air defenses. The RAF aircraft will operate alongside Saudi forces as part of a defensive mission focused on intercepting incoming aerial threats. British officials said the deployment does not involve strikes inside Yemen.

The decision follows renewed Houthi attacks against Saudi territory amid wider regional fighting. The Iran-aligned group has used drones and missiles against targets across the region and has also attacked commercial and military vessels around the Red Sea and nearby waterways. Saudi Arabia has invested heavily in air-defense systems, but the continuing attacks have increased pressure on regional governments and their allies to strengthen protection of civilian and economic infrastructure.

The deployment also places additional British military assets in a region already experiencing heightened international military activity. The UK has previously used RAF aircraft and naval forces to intercept drones and missiles and protect shipping routes during periods of intensified Houthi attacks.

The Houthis control much of northern Yemen, including the capital, Sanaa, following years of civil war. Saudi Arabia led a military coalition against the group beginning in 2015, although large-scale fighting inside Yemen later declined as diplomatic efforts sought to establish a lasting settlement.

British officials have described the new deployment as defensive and intended to protect Saudi territory rather than reopen Britain's involvement in offensive operations connected to Yemen's war.

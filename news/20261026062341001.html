Israel expands West Bank operation after attack kills two soldiers

Military closes crossings into Israel through Tuesday’s national election

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 26, 2026 at 1:15 PM ET

The Israeli military is continuing a major operation in the occupied West Bank after two Palestinian gunmen killed two Israeli soldiers and wounded three others near Hebron, while new movement restrictions will remain in place through Israel's national election on Tuesday, October 27.

The operation began after Friday's attack at a military checkpoint near Hebron. Israeli forces said the two gunmen were killed during the confrontation and that weapons found in their vehicle indicated they may have been planning additional attacks. No Palestinian armed group has claimed responsibility for the shooting.

By Sunday, the Israeli military said its forces had operated at more than 400 locations across the West Bank and arrested 40 Palestinians it said were affiliated with Hamas or Palestinian Islamic Jihad. The military said searches were continuing in areas connected to the attackers.

The operation has been particularly extensive in Hebron. The Hebron municipality said more than 2,600 Palestinians had been displaced, while Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz previously put the number at about 2,000. Israeli forces sealed entrances and exits to parts of the city and ordered residents to leave areas where troops were conducting searches. The military has described the operation as targeted and intended to dismantle militant infrastructure and prevent additional attacks.

Israel also imposed additional restrictions ahead of Tuesday's election. Most Palestinian workers with permits were barred from entering Israeli settlements in the West Bank for work on Sunday and Monday, and the military said crossings into Israel would remain closed until after election day.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu pledged a forceful response following the checkpoint attack. The military operation comes as Israelis prepare to vote in an election in which security policy and Netanyahu's leadership during the past three years of war have been major issues.

Amazon suspends cargo carrier after fatal Miami crash

21 Air partnership paused as federal investigation continues

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 14, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Amazon has suspended its partnership with cargo airline 21 Air following the September 6, 2026, crash of an Amazon Air flight at Miami International Airport that killed five people and injured five others. Amazon said it paused operations with the carrier while the National Transportation Safety Board continues investigating the Boeing 767 runway overrun.

The 21 Air Boeing 767-300, operating as Amazon Air Flight 7598, was arriving from San Juan, Puerto Rico, when it overran Runway 30 by about 1,300 feet. The aircraft struck navigational equipment and a van carrying workers from an airline cleaning company before breaking through the airport perimeter and hitting another vehicle. All five people killed were on the ground. The two pilots survived and were treated at a hospital.

Preliminary NTSB information shows the aircraft approached and landed at excessive speed. Cockpit recordings captured one pilot repeatedly warning that the aircraft was too fast. Flight data also showed the crew increased the throttles after touchdown in what investigators said appeared to be an attempted go-around before abandoning the maneuver. The aircraft's speed brakes and thrust reversers were not deployed during the landing sequence. Investigators have not determined the probable cause of the crash and are examining the crew's actions, weather, aircraft maintenance and 21 Air's operating procedures.

The investigation has also brought renewed attention to allegations made by former 21 Air employees before the airline began flying for Amazon in 2024. Former pilots and safety personnel alleged in legal proceedings and interviews that managers discouraged safety reports and pressured crews over scheduling and rest requirements. Former company leadership disputed allegations of retaliation and unsafe practices. The NTSB has not established any connection between those earlier allegations and the Miami crash.

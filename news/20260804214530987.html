S&P 500, Dow Close at Record Highs

AI-fueled profits and sub-$80 oil lift Wall Street

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 5, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

U.S. stocks rallied on August 4, 2026, sending the S&P 500 up 1.8% to a record 7,736.52 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average up 907.47 points (1.7%) to a record 54,085.88. The Nasdaq Composite climbed 2.6% to 26,584.99. A sharp pullback in energy prices supported risk appetite as Brent crude slid 5.3% to $79.36 per barrel, easing inflation worries while investors digested a fresh wave of stronger-than-expected corporate results.

Earnings from AI-linked and industrial leaders anchored the advance. Palantir Technologies surged 29.5% after CEO Alex Karp said second‑quarter revenue jumped 93% year over year and the company raised its 2026 revenue outlook. Heavy‑equipment maker Caterpillar rose 5.6% after reporting its first‑ever quarter with more than $20 billion in sales and revenue; CEO Joe Creed cited strong order rates and a growing backlog, with demand tied to turbines powering artificial‑intelligence data centers.

Gains extended across key technology suppliers: Nvidia added 2.6%, Broadcom 6.6%, and Micron Technology 7.6%, bolstering the broader market. On the profits front, companies in the S&P 500 are on track to deliver nearly 50% year‑over‑year earnings‑per‑share growth for the second quarter of 2026, according to FactSet, the strongest surge since 2021. With oil easing and profit growth accelerating, Treasury yields also edged lower, helping equity valuations look less stretched than earlier in the summer.

Tuesday’s move pushed the S&P 500 above its prior record set in June 2026, while the Dow logged a second straight record close. Markets continue to wrestle with persistent inflation pressures and uncertainty tied to the war in Iran, but today’s combination of robust earnings and a drop in crude prices proved decisive. Investors will watch whether subsequent reports from major tech and industrial names confirm the momentum implied by today’s leaders.

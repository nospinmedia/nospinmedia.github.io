Tucson Gay Bar Shooting Investigated as Possible Hate Crime

Police Find List of LGBTQ+ Venues

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 2, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

Police in Tucson, Arizona, are investigating whether the fatal shooting outside an LGBTQ+ bar was motivated by bias after investigators discovered a list of additional LGBTQ+ venues in the suspect's possession. Authorities said the evidence prompted a formal hate crime investigation following the shooting outside Venture-N, where two men were killed shortly after midnight on Aug. 31.

According to the Tucson Police Department, 44-year-old Ousman Ceesay confronted patrons in the parking lot before opening fire. Vincent Anthony Siqueiros, 42, and Cameron Davis Capara, 33, were pronounced dead at the scene. Ceesay was later found with a gunshot wound and died after being transported to a hospital. Investigators have not publicly detailed how he sustained the fatal injury. Police said a handwritten list recovered during the investigation contained the names or addresses of several other LGBTQ+-oriented businesses and gathering places in the Tucson area.

Police Chief Monica Prieto said detectives are examining whether the list indicates the victims or the location were intentionally selected because of their association with the LGBTQ+ community. Investigators are working with federal law enforcement partners as they review the suspect's communications, digital records, firearm history and other evidence to determine whether additional crimes had been planned or whether other people may have been at risk. Authorities have also increased patrols around LGBTQ+ businesses and community gathering places while the investigation continues.

Tucson Mayor Regina Romero condemned the attack and expressed support for the victims' families and the city's LGBTQ+ community. Community leaders held vigils honoring the victims while urging anyone with information to cooperate with investigators. Police emphasized that the hate crime investigation remains active and that no final determination regarding motive has been announced.

Star Trek's Walter Koenig dies at 90

Chekov actor remembered for decades of work beyond the Enterprise

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 26, 2026 at 4:53 PM ET

Walter Koenig, the actor best known for portraying Ensign Pavel Chekov in the original Star Trek television series and its films, died on October 25, 2026, at age 90. His death was announced the following day by his representative, Zachery McGinnis. Koenig died at his home in North Hollywood, Los Angeles, surrounded by his daughter and niece. His death was attributed to natural causes.

Born on September 14, 1936, in Chicago, Koenig began his television career in the early 1960s before joining Star Trek in 1967, during its second season. As Chekov, the young Russian navigator aboard the USS Enterprise, he became part of a television series that imagined international cooperation during the Cold War. His character served alongside Captain James T. Kirk, portrayed by William Shatner, and Hikaru Sulu, portrayed by George Takei.

Koenig continued playing Chekov in seven Star Trek films, beginning in 1979, and remained involved with the franchise through additional productions, books and fan conventions. He wrote Chekov's Enterprise, published in 1980, and maintained a connection with generations of viewers who embraced the original series long after its initial broadcast ended in 1969.

His career extended well beyond Star Trek. Koenig portrayed Alfred Bester, a powerful and morally complicated telepathic officer, in the science-fiction series Babylon 5. He also wrote television scripts for programs including The Incredible Hulk and Family, worked in theater, taught acting and participated in computer-game productions. His work demonstrated a range of creative interests beyond the character that made him internationally recognizable.

Koenig's longtime friend and former castmate George Takei paid tribute to him, recalling a friendship lasting nearly six decades. Koenig served as best man when Takei married Brad Altman in 2008. Takei remembered him not only as Chekov but also as a writer, teacher and loyal friend.

Koenig was preceded in death by his wife, actress Judy Levitt, in 2022, and their son Andrew Koenig, in 2010. He is survived by his daughter, Danielle Koenig.

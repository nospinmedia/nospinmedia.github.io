Armed man arrested near White House

DHS says suspect told officers he planned to kill Trump

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 7, 2026 at 1:15 PM ET

U.S. Secret Service officers arrested an armed man near the White House late Thursday, November 5, after finding him inside a stolen pickup truck containing three firearms and ammunition, according to federal authorities.

The suspect was identified as Ronny Yordrix Guanipa Chirinos, 26, a Venezuelan national. Secret Service officers encountered Chirinos at approximately 11:42 p.m. while he was asleep inside a blue Ford F-150 parked on Constitution Avenue near the White House complex. A check of the vehicle determined that the truck had been reported stolen.

According to the Department of Homeland Security, Chirinos acknowledged having weapons inside the vehicle while officers questioned him with the assistance of Google Translate. Authorities said a subsequent search uncovered an AR-15-style rifle, a MAC-10, a Glock 43X handgun and ammunition.

DHS said Chirinos told investigators that he had heard voices directing him to travel to Washington, D.C., and kill President Trump. The statement is an allegation reported by federal authorities; officials have not said that Chirinos reached the White House grounds or made contact with the president.

Federal officials identified Chirinos as a Venezuelan national who entered the United States through Texas in March 2024 without authorization. He appeared in D.C. Superior Court on Friday, November 6, facing charges including firearms offenses, unauthorized use of a motor vehicle and theft. He was ordered held without bond and remained in custody.

The arrest comes amid heightened security surrounding the White House and other federal facilities. The investigation is continuing, including examination of the suspect's actions before arriving in Washington and the weapons recovered from the stolen vehicle.

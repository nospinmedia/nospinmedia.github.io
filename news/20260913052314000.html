Iranian commercial ship struck near Strait of Hormuz

One killed as authorities investigate projectile attack

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 13, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

An Iranian commercial vessel was struck by a projectile early Sunday off Qeshm Island near the Strait of Hormuz, killing one person as tensions continue around one of the world's most important shipping routes. Iranian state media initially reported three people were wounded, while later reporting said four were injured among the vessel's 10-member crew.

The strike occurred around 5 a.m. local time near Hengam and Qeshm islands, according to Iranian officials. Authorities said they were still investigating what type of projectile hit the vessel. Iranian officials characterized the incident as an attack by hostile forces, but independent confirmation of who launched the projectile was not immediately available. The U.S. military had not immediately commented on the incident.

Separately, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations, a British military maritime-monitoring organization, reported that a vessel transiting the Strait of Hormuz had been struck by an unknown projectile. UKMTO said a fire was reported and authorities were responding. It was not immediately clear whether the UKMTO alert and the Iranian account described the same vessel, so the two reports have not been treated as a confirmed single incident.

The strike comes amid continuing military confrontation and severe disruption to commercial traffic through the strait. The waterway connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and has historically carried roughly one-fifth of the world's petroleum liquids consumption, making threats to shipping there significant well beyond the region. Commercial traffic through the passage has fallen sharply during the current conflict.

The incident also comes as Iran and Oman pursue efforts to establish arrangements for managing maritime transit through the strait. Iranian officials said foreign ministers from regional countries were expected to discuss the issue on Monday, while Iran has maintained that restrictions imposed on its own shipping must be addressed as part of any agreement.

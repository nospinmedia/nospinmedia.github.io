Pregnant U.S. Citizen Pepper-Sprayed During ICE Encounter

Video shows agents forcing woman face-down before detaining her

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 17, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Video recorded outside the Stanislaus County Jail in Modesto, California, on Sunday, November 15, shows federal immigration agents pepper-spraying and restraining Lizbeth Vargas Andrade, a 23-year-old U.S. citizen who said she was 12 weeks pregnant, during an encounter in the jail parking lot. She was later hospitalized and released from federal custody without criminal or immigration charges.

Vargas Andrade, her husband Adolfo Talavera and attorney Patrick Kolasinski said the couple was observing and recording immigration enforcement activity after agents stopped a minivan driven by her sister-in-law. Cellphone video shows Vargas Andrade filming plainclothes agents around the vehicle before she and an agent made physical contact while moving around it.

The agent accused Vargas Andrade of hitting him and grabbed her arms. Video then shows another agent deploying pepper spray as Talavera repeatedly warned officers that his wife was pregnant. Agents forced Vargas Andrade face-down onto the pavement and handcuffed her behind her back before turning her onto her side and pouring water over her face.

Vargas Andrade, who has diabetes and reported two previous pregnancy losses, was taken to an ICE facility in Stockton. Her sister called emergency medical responders to check on her, and Vargas Andrade was transported by ambulance to a hospital after experiencing elevated blood pressure and blood sugar. An ultrasound showed the pregnancy had not been harmed. Vargas Andrade alleged that two agents remained with her at the hospital and restricted her access to an attorney before she was released without being formally booked at the Stockton facility.

The Stanislaus County Sheriff's Office said its deputies did not participate in the detention. The Department of Justice declined to comment, while DHS and ICE had not publicly provided a detailed account of the encounter. Local community members and advocates also scheduled a town hall in Modesto following the incident.

Minnesota Primary Sets November's Top Statewide Races

Flanagan wins Senate primary; Demuth captures GOP governor nomination

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 12, 2026 at 8:34 AM ET

Minnesota voters finalized two of the state's highest-profile statewide races during the August 11, 2026 primary election. Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan won the Democratic–Farmer–Labor nomination for the open U.S. Senate seat, while House Speaker Lisa Demuth secured the Republican nomination for governor, setting the stage for two closely watched general election contests on November 3.

Flanagan defeated U.S. Rep. Angie Craig to earn the Democratic nomination for the Senate seat being vacated by retiring Sen. Tina Smith. She will face Republican Michele Tafoya in November. If elected, Flanagan would become the first Native American woman elected to the U.S. Senate. The Senate primary drew national attention as Democrats chose between one of the party's leading progressive voices and a more moderate member of Congress.

In the governor's race, Lisa Demuth defeated Mike Lindell, the MyPillow founder who was endorsed by President Trump, along with other Republican candidates to secure her party's nomination. Demuth will face Democratic nominee Sen. Amy Klobuchar, who is seeking to succeed outgoing Gov. Tim Walz. The race will determine whether Republicans can reclaim the governor's office for the first time since 2006.

The primary results conclude months of campaigning and shift attention to the fall election, where Minnesota voters will choose leaders for two of the state's most prominent offices. Both races are expected to draw significant campaign spending and national political attention in the weeks leading up to Election Day.

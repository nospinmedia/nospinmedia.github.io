Russian attacks knock out power and water in Kyiv

Death toll from village bombing rises to 40 with dozens still missing

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 5, 2026 at 8:46 AM ET

Russian attacks caused hours-long power and water outages in Kyiv and surrounding areas Wednesday, while the death toll from a separate Russian bombing in southern Ukraine rose to 40, with dozens of people potentially still buried beneath the rubble.

Russia launched 310 drones overnight, including 81 jet-powered drones, according to Ukraine's air force. Ukrainian officials said 272 were downed or suppressed. The attacks disrupted critical infrastructure in and around the capital before crews restored water service and ended emergency power shutdowns.

The rising death toll came from Monday's attack on Komyshuvakha, a village near the front line in the Zaporizhzhia region. Multiple Russian guided bombs struck a former school and a nearby underground shelter as residents were waiting for humanitarian aid. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Wednesday that 40 people were believed killed and about 30 more could still be trapped beneath the rubble.

Search operations have been complicated by continued Russian drone activity. Officials said some victims could not be visually identified, requiring investigators to collect DNA samples from relatives. Body-camera footage recovered from a police officer killed in the attack captured the moments before another bomb struck the shelter where people had taken cover.

The attacks come as officials from the United States, Germany, France, Britain, NATO and the European Union meet in Brussels to discuss security arrangements and reconstruction plans for Ukraine ahead of another round of U.S.-brokered peace negotiations.

The latest strikes also continue Russia's campaign against Ukraine's energy infrastructure as colder weather approaches, while fighting and long-range attacks continue on both sides of the conflict.

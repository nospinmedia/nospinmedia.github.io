White House launches 24/7 Trump TV stream

New channel mixes past speeches, executive actions and live events

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 23, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

The White House has launched a new 24/7 streaming channel called Trump TV: The Essentials Station, giving President Trump an official government-operated platform for continuously distributing administration video without relying on traditional television networks or news organizations.

The stream went live Monday through the White House's digital platforms, including its website and YouTube account. Programming observed after the launch consisted largely of previously recorded Trump appearances, speeches and executive actions, although the channel can also carry live administration events. Early programming included Trump's July 3 Mount Rushmore speech and footage from his 2025 inauguration. On Tuesday, it carried a briefing by Vice President JD Vance, while Trump's address to the United Nations General Assembly was not shown live and the channel instead replayed an earlier presidential speech.

Unlike a conventional television news channel, Trump TV currently has no anchors, news ticker or regular headline graphics. The White House describes it as a continuously updated collection of major remarks, announcements and administration highlights. White House digital officials say the purpose is to provide Trump's speeches and accomplishments directly to viewers without what they contend is unfair treatment by traditional media.

The launch comes during an escalating dispute over independent White House coverage. Trump recently barred CNN, MS NOW and Politico reporters from White House grounds, and those organizations have filed a federal lawsuit challenging the restrictions. Major television networks subsequently suspended their rotating White House television pool coverage. That pool normally supplies independent video and audio of presidential events to news organizations across the country. The administration continues to provide its own video streams, while the new Trump TV channel gives the White House an additional outlet for distributing material selected by the administration.

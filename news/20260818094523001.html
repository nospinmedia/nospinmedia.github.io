Two Dead in Livestreamed Shooting at Philippine School

Grade 9 gunman dies by suicide; campus evacuated

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 18, 2026 at 10:39 AM ET

A Grade 9 student opened fire at the junior high school building of Ateneo de Zamboanga University in Zamboanga City on August 18, 2026, killing a fellow student before taking his own life. Officials said the attack was livestreamed using a body-worn camera and posted on Facebook, prompting a rapid evacuation of hundreds of students and staff.

Zamboanga City Mayor Khymer Adan Olaso said the suspect first fired at a teacher seated at a desk but apparently missed, then moved into an adjacent classroom on the fourth floor and fatally shot a student. The gunman went up one more floor and died by suicide. Police said he brought two handguns onto campus. Authorities added that at least two other people were injured in the chaos and transported for treatment, and that the scene was secured shortly after the shooting.

Investigators have not released a motive and are reviewing evidence as they reconstruct the timeline. President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. ordered a nationwide investigation and directed education and law enforcement officials to strengthen school security following the attack. School officials said there was no continuing threat after police cleared the building, and classes were suspended while counseling and support were arranged for students and staff.

The incident is the country’s second fatal school shooting in less than two months. On June 22, 2026, a separate attack at San Jose National High School in Tacloban City left 3 students dead and about 20 wounded, according to authorities. School shootings remain rare in the Philippines, but the back-to-back attacks have renewed debate over student safety, campus security procedures, and access to firearms.

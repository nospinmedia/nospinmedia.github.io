Ukrainian drone hits Russian warship in Gulf of Finland

Strike near Gogland Island triggers wider Baltic security concerns

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 13, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

Ukraine's Security Service says it targeted the Russian Navy's Naro-Fominsk missile corvette with a drone in the Gulf of Finland, extending Ukrainian military operations into a strategically sensitive Baltic waterway near NATO members Finland and Estonia.

The strike occurred near Gogland Island around 5:30 a.m. November 11, during a wider Ukrainian drone operation targeting Russia's Ust-Luga port. The Naro-Fominsk is a Buyan-M-class small missile corvette assigned to Russia's Baltic Fleet and capable of carrying cruise missiles. Smoke was reported rising from the vessel after the strike, although it remained able to move toward St. Petersburg.

The operation also targeted Ust-Luga, a major Russian Baltic port. Reports said five oil storage tanks and processing facilities there were damaged during the broader drone attack. The full extent of damage to the warship and port facilities remained under assessment.

The incident had effects beyond Russian territory. Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania issued civilian warnings as drones entered or approached their airspace, while NATO aircraft were scrambled in response to the activity. Finnish authorities also detected an unauthorized drone over the eastern Gulf of Finland.

The location makes the attack particularly sensitive. The Gulf of Finland connects the Baltic Sea with St. Petersburg and is bordered by NATO members Finland and Estonia. Ukraine's maritime drone campaign has previously focused heavily on Russian military assets around the Black Sea, making an operation this far north a significant expansion of its reported reach.

The strike comes as U.S. envoys continue diplomatic talks with Russian and Ukrainian leaders aimed at finding a path toward ending the war.

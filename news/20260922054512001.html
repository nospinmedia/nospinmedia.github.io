Russian drones strike homes, university and mall in Zaporizhzhia

Five wounded after Ukraine's massive weekend barrage on Russia

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 22, 2026 at 11:46 AM ET

Russian drones struck the southern Ukrainian city of Zaporizhzhia, hitting apartment buildings, a university and a shopping mall and wounding five people, Ukrainian officials said. The attack followed a weekend in which Ukraine launched more than 1,000 drones at Russia, including hundreds directed toward Moscow.

The Zaporizhzhia strikes occurred late Sunday, September 20, with damage reported across civilian areas of the city. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia also attacked nine other regions of Ukraine. Ukraine's air force reported shooting down or suppressing 148 Russian drones during the overnight assault. Russia's Defense Ministry said its forces targeted Ukrainian logistics hubs, warehouses, ports and other military-related facilities.

The Russian attack came after Ukraine carried out an exceptionally large long-range drone campaign from Saturday into Sunday. Hundreds of drones targeted the Moscow region, while strikes also reached Russian energy infrastructure. The Moscow oil refinery was damaged during the barrage, and Russian authorities reported deaths and injuries from attacks in the Moscow region. Russia's Defense Ministry said Monday that its air defenses subsequently downed another 244 Ukrainian drones over Russian regions and the Black Sea.

The continuing exchanges are also creating concerns beyond Russia and Ukraine. Moldova temporarily closed part of its northern airspace after detecting an unidentified aerial object crossing into the country overnight. Moldova has reported repeated airspace violations and discoveries of drone debris as the war has increasingly involved large numbers of unmanned aircraft.

The latest attacks come as world leaders gather in New York for the United Nations General Assembly, where the war and efforts to reach a diplomatic settlement are expected to be discussed. Zelenskyy has said Ukraine remains willing to pursue diplomatic steps toward ending the conflict while calling for additional international pressure on Russia.

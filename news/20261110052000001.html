FBI investigates suspected homicide aboard Caribbean cruise

MSC Seascape ordered back to Texas after passenger dies

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 10, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

The FBI is investigating a suspected homicide aboard the MSC Seascape after authorities ordered the cruise ship back to Galveston, Texas, following the death of a passenger shortly after it departed on a Caribbean voyage.

The ship left the Port of Galveston on Sunday for a scheduled weeklong cruise before what MSC Cruises described as a "guest incident" prompted authorities to direct the vessel back to port. The ship returned Monday morning, allowing federal investigators to board and begin investigating the death.

The FBI confirmed that a suspect is in custody, but authorities have not publicly identified that person or announced criminal charges. The bureau said it was withholding identifying information while the victim's family was being notified. Officials have also released few details about how the passenger died or what investigators believe occurred aboard the ship.

That makes some details circulating in early reports premature. Although local reporting has identified a person being held in connection with the investigation, the FBI has not publicly confirmed the suspect's identity or formally announced a murder charge.

MSC Cruises said it is cooperating fully with authorities and providing support to those affected. After investigators completed their initial work aboard the vessel, the MSC Seascape was cleared to resume its voyage and departed Galveston again Monday afternoon.

The Seascape is a large cruise ship with 2,270 cabins, more than 1,600 crew members and capacity for nearly 5,900 passengers. It operates five- and seven-night Caribbean cruises from Galveston after moving its home port there from Miami last year.

Crimes aboard cruise ships can involve complicated jurisdictional questions depending on where a vessel is located, where it is registered and the nationalities of those involved. In this case, the FBI has taken the lead in the suspected homicide investigation.

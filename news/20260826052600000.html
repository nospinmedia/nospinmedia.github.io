Pennsylvania Reports Nation's First Measles Deaths of 2026

Two unvaccinated Lancaster County residents die as cases continue to climb

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 26, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

The Pennsylvania Department of Health has confirmed that two Lancaster County residents died from complications associated with measles, marking the first reported measles-related deaths in the United States during 2026. State officials said both individuals were unvaccinated and did not release their names or ages to protect their families' privacy. The deaths are Pennsylvania's first attributed to measles in 35 years and come as the state experiences its largest outbreak in decades.

Health Secretary Dr. Debra Bogen urged residents who are not fully vaccinated to receive the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine, noting that the recommended two-dose series provides approximately 97% protection against measles. Officials said nearly 20% of Pennsylvania's confirmed measles patients have required hospitalization during the current outbreak, underscoring the seriousness of the highly contagious disease.

Pennsylvania has reported 393 confirmed measles cases across 28 counties in 2026, with Lancaster County accounting for 185 of those infections. Nationally, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) has confirmed 2,777 measles cases across 47 jurisdictions, the highest annual total since 1991 and already exceeding the total number reported during all of 2025. Public health officials have linked many recent outbreaks to declining childhood vaccination rates and increased community transmission among unvaccinated populations.

Health authorities warned that falling vaccination coverage could jeopardize the United States' measles elimination status, which was achieved in 2000 after sustained interruption of domestic transmission. Officials continue to encourage vaccination, rapid identification of new cases, and prompt isolation of infected individuals to limit additional spread while outbreak investigations remain underway in Pennsylvania and other affected states.

UAE calls Flydubai cockpit axe attack an attempted terrorist act

Co-pilot tried to seize control of Israel-bound flight

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 4, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

Authorities in the United Arab Emirates say the co-pilot of Flydubai flight FZ1073 attacked the captain with a cockpit crash axe and tried to seize control of the Israel-bound aircraft in what the country's prosecutor general described as an attempted terrorist attack.

The September 30 flight was traveling from Dubai to Tel Aviv when the attack occurred. The suspect, identified by sources familiar with the investigation as Hammam al-Hammami, an Omani national, assaulted Captain Smit Machchhar inside the cockpit as the aircraft began a steep descent. Passengers and crew members ultimately subdued the co-pilot, and other pilots aboard the aircraft helped regain control. The flight was diverted to Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, where it landed safely. The suspect was detained and later transferred to Abu Dhabi as the investigation continued.

UAE Prosecutor General Hamad Saif Al Shamsi said investigators are examining the circumstances of the attack, the suspect's motives, possible connections and physical and digital evidence. The statement marked the first time Emirati authorities publicly characterized the incident as an attempted terrorist attack. Officials have not announced a final determination about the suspect's motive or whether anyone else was involved.

The investigation is also drawing scrutiny to how the co-pilot was cleared to work the flight. Reporting from AP News says al-Hammami previously had been barred from flying for Oman Air because of concerns that he had adopted extremist views. Oman and Israel do not have formal diplomatic relations, adding questions about how an Omani pilot was assigned to a Tel Aviv-bound flight. Flydubai has said it is cooperating with authorities while the investigation remains underway.

The incident adds a new dimension to the extraordinary midair emergency: what was initially reported as passengers and crew preventing an aircraft from crashing is now officially being investigated by UAE authorities as an attempted terrorist attack.

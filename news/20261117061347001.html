Chase Software Bug Disrupts Banking Transactions Nationwide

Services restored after delays ripple across customers at other banks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 17, 2026 at 2:31 PM ET

A software bug at JPMorgan Chase caused transaction delays on Monday, November 16, affecting Chase customers and some customers at other U.S. banks who were attempting transactions involving Chase. The bank said the problem was an internal technical issue rather than a cyberattack and confirmed Monday evening that transactions were processing normally again.

User outage reports began rising around 9:53 a.m. Eastern Time and peaked around 2 p.m. at nearly 5,000 reports for Chase on Downdetector. Chase spokesperson Joe Evangelisti said the problem originated with a software bug in an "on-premise hardware system" and described it as "not a systemic issue." Chase serves more than 86 million U.S. customers.

Reports also increased for Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo, Truist and other financial institutions. However, those reports did not necessarily mean each bank was experiencing its own outage. Chase said other institutions were affected only when they were involved in transactions with Chase. Bank of America and Citigroup said their banking systems were operating normally, while U.S. Bank said its systems were not directly affected.

Users also reported problems involving Zelle, but the payment network said it remained fully operational. Zelle said technical problems at individual financial institutions could delay payments for some customers. U.S. Bank similarly warned that customers sending or receiving Zelle payments involving another affected institution could experience delays.

At approximately 6:30 p.m., Chase said the issue had been resolved and transactions were posting normally again. User-submitted outage reports also declined substantially from their earlier peaks.

The disruption followed separate major technology failures affecting Microsoft services and the Federal Aviation Administration, but there was no evidence connecting the incidents. The Chase problem was attributed to an internal software bug, while those disruptions had separate technical causes.

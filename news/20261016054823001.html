Rifle Jams During Alleged Attempted Mass Shooting at Illinois Hindu Temple

19-Year-Old Accused of Targeting Indian Worshippers Is Held on Terrorism and Weapons Charges

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 16, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

A 19-year-old Illinois man accused of white supremacist beliefs allegedly drove to a Hindu temple in Aurora, Illinois, intending to carry out a mass shooting before his AR-style rifle malfunctioned, according to prosecutors. Alexandros Paporakis, of Palatine, was arrested later that day and is being held on felony charges of making a terrorist threat and unlawfully possessing a rifle.

Authorities say a red Acura RDX registered to Paporakis entered the parking lot of the Sri Venkateswara Swami (Balaji) Temple at about 12:25 p.m. on October 11 and left less than 15 minutes later. Prosecutors allege Paporakis had an AR-style rifle, ammunition and tactical equipment and attempted to fire the weapon, but it jammed. He later allegedly told a co-worker about the malfunction and indicated he intended to try again.

The investigation began after Paporakis' co-workers at a Taco Bell contacted police about threatening communications. Prosecutors say he had told colleagues that he wanted to kill Indian people and described Sunday as a special day. Authorities also recovered an eight-page document that police say expressed white supremacist and neo-Nazi beliefs and described plans for mass violence.

Police searching Paporakis' residence recovered an AR-style rifle, loaded magazines, approximately 300 rounds of ammunition, body armor, tactical equipment and items displaying Nazi and white supremacist imagery. Authorities say the rifle had a barrel shorter than 16 inches, resulting in an additional unlawful-weapons charge.

Palatine Police said investigators believe Paporakis was targeting a Hindu place of worship in Aurora. The temple has increased its armed security following the alleged attempted attack. The FBI's Chicago Field Office is assisting local authorities with the investigation.

Paporakis has been ordered detained while the case proceeds and is scheduled to return to court on October 30. The charges remain allegations, and he has not been convicted.

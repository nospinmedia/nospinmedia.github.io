Sen. Chris Murphy hospitalized after stroke

Doctors optimistic he will make a full recovery

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 2, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Connecticut Sen. Chris Murphy was hospitalized after suffering a stroke while attending his son's hockey game, his office said Sunday. The 53-year-old Democrat is awake and alert, responding well to treatment and expected to make a full recovery.

Murphy began experiencing numbness in his arm Saturday while at the hockey game, according to his chief of staff, Allison Herwitt. He was taken to a local hospital, where doctors determined that he had experienced a stroke. Murphy was subsequently transferred to Johns Hopkins for additional monitoring.

"The doctors are encouraged by his progress, he is awake and alert, and we are optimistic he will make a full recovery," Herwitt said in the statement announcing his condition.

Murphy has represented Connecticut in the U.S. Senate since 2013 after previously serving three terms in the House of Representatives. He currently serves on the Senate Appropriations Committee and has been a prominent congressional voice on gun policy and immigration.

The senator is not on the ballot in the 2026 midterm elections, but he had been traveling and campaigning for Democratic candidates as the party seeks control of Congress. His recent campaign activity included appearances in Connecticut and Minnesota. His hospitalization comes just before Tuesday's elections, interrupting that campaign schedule while he receives treatment.

Lawmakers from both parties offered messages of support following the announcement. Connecticut's other senator, Richard Blumenthal, said he was thinking of Murphy and his family and expressed confidence that his colleague would return to the Senate. Republican senators including Chuck Grassley of Iowa and Pete Ricketts of Nebraska also offered well wishes.

Murphy's office has not announced when he may be released from the hospital or resume his Senate duties.

Oregon Father Who Stopped School Shooter Describes Decision to Run Inside

‘I Have to Go In,’ Kaylub Giamanco Told 911 as Gunfire Continued

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 24, 2026 at 8:45 PM ET

Kaylub Giamanco, the armed father who confronted and killed the gunman during the Silver Rail Elementary School shooting in Bend, Oregon, has described his decision to enter the school as the attack was unfolding.

Giamanco was near the school when he heard gunfire during the October 15 attack. He called 911 and told a dispatcher what he was seeing while deciding whether to intervene. "I have to go in," he told the dispatcher. "People are going to die."

Giamanco, whose two children attended the school, said he saw the teenage gunman enter the building after shooting through glass. Despite being warned by the dispatcher not to enter, Giamanco said his thoughts were focused on the children inside. He was legally carrying a firearm and moved toward the school.

Inside, Giamanco encountered the 16-year-old shooter and fatally shot him, ending the attack. Authorities have credited his intervention with preventing additional deaths.

Four people were killed before the gunman was stopped: 5-year-old Rhoam Summer, two other children and teacher Meghan Lambert. Others were wounded in the attack. NSM previously reported on the identification of the victims; Giamanco's account provides new details about how the shooting ended.

In interviews following the attack, Giamanco said he does not consider himself a hero and believes he was simply in the right place when children needed help. He has described hearing children inside the school and feeling that waiting outside was not an option.

The shooting remains under investigation as authorities reconstruct the gunman's actions and the response. Giamanco's intervention has also become part of the broader examination of the attack, including how quickly the shooter entered the school and how the violence was ultimately stopped.

Apple Maps Adopts 'Lake America' for U.S. Users

Change Follows Trump's Direct Outreach to Apple

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 2, 2026 at 5:16 PM ET

Apple updated Apple Maps on Sept. 1 to display "Lake America" for users in the United States, becoming the second major digital mapping provider after Google to adopt the Trump administration's new federal name for Lake Ontario. The change appeared one day after Interior Secretary Doug Burgum said President Trump had personally contacted Apple and urged the company to use the new designation. Apple has not publicly said whether Trump's outreach directly caused the change.

Apple confirmed that U.S. users will see Lake America, while users in Canada will continue to see Lake Ontario. Users elsewhere will see both names. The company said its mapping service follows updates to the U.S. Geographic Names Information System (GNIS), which was changed after President Trump issued an executive order directing federal agencies to use the new name within the United States.

The timing drew attention because Burgum disclosed Trump's direct outreach to Apple on Aug. 31, when the company had not yet changed its maps. By the following day, the new label had begun appearing for U.S. users. Google had already implemented a similar location-based naming system, showing Lake America to U.S. users, Lake Ontario in Canada and both names in many other countries.

The federal renaming does not change Canada's official designation for the lake, which forms part of the international boundary between the two countries. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and other Canadian officials have rejected the new U.S. name. Meanwhile, mapping service MapQuest publicly said it would continue using Lake Ontario, highlighting the differing approaches private technology companies are taking to the federal naming policy.

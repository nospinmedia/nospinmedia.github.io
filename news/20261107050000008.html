Thomas Markle arrested on federal child sexual abuse material charge

Judge orders house arrest pending proceedings in California

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 7, 2026 at 6:31 PM ET

Federal agents arrested Thomas Wayne Markle, the estranged father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, in Florida on Friday, November 6, on a federal charge involving possession of child sexual abuse material.

Markle, 82, was arrested at a senior living facility in Lakeland following an investigation originating in California. According to a federal criminal complaint, investigators searched two storage units in the Los Angeles area that Markle had rented since 2008 and seized approximately 78 items, including slides, film negatives and printed materials. Prosecutors allege some of the seized material contained sexually explicit images involving prepubescent children.

The charge is contained in a federal criminal complaint filed in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California. The allegations have not been proven in court, and Markle is presumed innocent unless convicted.

Following his arrest, Markle appeared in federal court in Florida. A judge approved his release under restrictive conditions that include home detention, travel restrictions and required medical and psychological evaluations while proceedings continue to transfer the case to Los Angeles. The house-arrest order represents a subsequent development in the same federal case as Friday's arrest rather than a separate criminal proceeding.

Federal authorities have charged Markle with possession of child sexual abuse material. If convicted, the offense carries the possibility of a substantial federal prison sentence, though any sentence would ultimately depend on the charge, federal sentencing rules and decisions by the court.

Markle is a retired television lighting director who has been publicly estranged from his daughter for years. Meghan and her husband, Prince Harry, have not been accused of any wrongdoing in connection with the investigation.

The federal case is now expected to proceed in California as prosecutors present the evidence underlying the criminal complaint.

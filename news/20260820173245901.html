State Farm Sends Out $5B Auto Dividend Refunds

Average payout about $100 per vehicle as rollout expands nationwide

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 20, 2026 at 5:15 PM ET

State Farm is distributing a record $5 billion cash-back dividend to eligible auto policyholders across the United States, marking the largest policyholder payout in the company's history. The distribution applies to customers who held an active State Farm auto policy at any point during 2025 and covers approximately 49 million insured vehicles nationwide.

Refund amounts vary by state but average about $100 per vehicle, generally representing 4% to 10% of the auto premiums paid during 2025. As a mutual insurance company, State Farm returns excess profits directly to policyholder members rather than outside shareholders when financial performance allows.

The company said more than 7.2 million dividend checks were mailed between July 31 and mid-August, with another 3.8 million checks and digital payment notifications now entering the next phase of distribution. Customers with an email address on file may choose electronic payment through services such as Zelle, Venmo, or PayPal, while others will automatically receive paper checks if no digital option is selected.

State Farm said customers do not need to submit an application to receive the dividend. Eligible policyholders are being contacted directly, and those who wish to confirm eligibility or payment status can use the company's official verification site, sfdividend.com, or contact State Farm customer service. The company said the historic payout reflects stronger underwriting results following several years of elevated premiums and challenging market conditions across the auto insurance industry.

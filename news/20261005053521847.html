2 killed, 35 injured at Georgia block party

More than 50 shots fired as police search for answers

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 5, 2026 at 1:16 PM ET

Two people were killed and 35 others injured when gunfire erupted during a crowded neighborhood block party in Vienna, Georgia, early Sunday, sending people fleeing through the streets as authorities struggled to determine who was shooting and why.

Dooly County Sheriff Craig Peavy identified those killed as Jaquevion Jackson, 21, of Cordele, and Macaleb Jackson, 23, of Hawkinsville. The injured ranged in age from 14 to 51, with conditions ranging from stable to critical Sunday evening. Authorities said some victims suffered gunshot wounds while others were injured as people tried to escape the violence.

More than 1,000 people had gathered for the party, which stretched across roughly four blocks. Peavy said more than 50 shots were fired in at least two locations about a block apart. Deputies were already patrolling nearby when the first gunfire erupted around 1:25 a.m., and additional shots were heard after officers reached the scene. Victims were transported to several regional hospitals for treatment.

The gathering took place during Dooly County High School's homecoming weekend, but Superintendent Sherrod Willaford said it was not a school-sponsored event. The school system planned to reopen Monday with additional security measures because of the shooting's impact on students, families and staff.

The Georgia Bureau of Investigation is assisting the Dooly County Sheriff's Office with the investigation. As of Sunday evening, authorities had announced no arrests, identified no motive and had not said how many shooters may have been involved. Investigators were continuing to interview witnesses and examine evidence from the sprawling scene.

Vienna is a small rural community about 130 miles south of Atlanta, making the scale of the violence particularly unusual for the area.

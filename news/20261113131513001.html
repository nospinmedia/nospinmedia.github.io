Doctor identified as gunman in deadly D.C. shooting

Building manager killed and four wounded before suspect died by suicide

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 13, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Dr. Matt Wade Lambert, 55, has been identified as the gunman in a Washington, D.C., shooting that killed building manager James Stuart, 46, and wounded four other people before Lambert died by suicide during a police standoff.

Police said the attack began around 8 a.m. Thursday, November 12, inside the Vida Fitness gym at The Metropole condominiums near 15th and P Streets NW in the Logan Circle area. Investigators believe Lambert specifically targeted Stuart, the building's manager, inside the gym before retreating to his seventh-floor condominium.

Authorities said Lambert then fired from his balcony toward people on the street below, wounding two men and two women, including people working at a construction site across the street. One of the four remained in critical condition Thursday afternoon, while the other three suffered injuries described as non-life-threatening.

Metropolitan Police Department officers arrived within about 30 seconds of the first emergency calls. Interim Police Chief Jeffery Carroll said Lambert barricaded himself inside his apartment for more than an hour while negotiators attempted to communicate with him. Police said Lambert fired through his door at officers, damaged a natural gas line and started a fire inside the unit before fatally shooting himself. The building's sprinkler system helped contain the fire.

Police recovered three unregistered firearms — a handgun, rifle and shotgun — along with gasoline from the apartment. Carroll said investigators found evidence suggesting some level of planning.

Lambert had been involved in a prolonged legal dispute with the condominium association and management over water leaks and mold in his penthouse. Investigators are examining that dispute as they work to establish the motive for Stuart's killing and the subsequent shootings.

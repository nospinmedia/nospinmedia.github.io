Two Iranian men charged over alleged Manchester terror plot

Jewish community was targeted ahead of Yom Kippur, police say

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 3, 2026 at 3:46 PM ET

Two Iranian nationals have been charged with terrorism offenses over an alleged plot targeting the Jewish community in Manchester, England, British authorities announced October 2. Salam Ahmadyan, 36, of Liverpool, and Rahman Salehi, 34, of Salford, were arrested in Manchester on September 20, shortly before Yom Kippur, and have been remanded in custody.

The Crown Prosecution Service said both men were charged under Section 5(1) of the Terrorism Act 2006 with engaging in conduct in preparation for committing an act of terrorism or assisting another person to commit one. Prosecutors said the alleged conduct occurred on or before September 20. The charges are allegations, and the CPS emphasized that both defendants have the right to a fair trial and that guilt can only be determined by a court.

Counter Terrorism Policing said investigators believe the alleged plot was directed at Manchester's Jewish community. Authorities also allege that the two men were in contact with an overseas third party who may be located in Iran in connection with the plot. Police have said the suspected plot was disrupted and that they do not believe there is an ongoing threat to the public from this case.

The arrests came during the Jewish High Holy Day period and close to the anniversary of the October 2, 2025, terrorist attack at Manchester's Heaton Park Hebrew Congregation Synagogue, where two people were killed. Counter Terrorism Policing said the timing and wider increase in antisemitic incidents in Britain were likely to heighten concerns within the Jewish community.

Ahmadyan and Salehi are scheduled to appear at Westminster Magistrates' Court on October 3. Authorities have urged the public to avoid reporting or commentary that could prejudice the active criminal proceedings.

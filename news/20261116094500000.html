Estonia to close all road crossings with Russia after sabotage attempt

Authorities say Russian GRU ordered attack targeting Estonian drone maker

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 16, 2026 at 11:45 AM ET

Estonia says it will close all three of its road border crossings with Russia beginning December 1 after authorities accused Russia's military intelligence service of ordering a failed sabotage attack against an Estonian drone manufacturer.

Estonian authorities said eight suspects were detained following an attempted attack early Saturday, November 14, at Threod Systems in Viimsi, north of Tallinn. Prosecutors said the group attempted to break into the company's property, pour flammable liquid inside and start a fire intended to cause an explosion.

The company's security team intervened before the attack succeeded. Authorities said nobody was injured, significant damage was avoided and Threod's production and delivery capabilities were unaffected.

Estonia's Internal Security Service, KAPO, said its counterintelligence investigation determined the operation was coordinated by Russia's GRU military intelligence service. Russia has denied involvement in sabotage operations. Russian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova described Estonia's planned border closure as another example of "Russophobia."

Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna announced that the road crossings at Narva, Koidula and Luhamaa will close on December 1. The crossings currently handle pedestrians and freight, and officials have not yet said whether the closure will be temporary or indefinite.

The attempted attack is the second known incident targeting an Estonian defense company this year. Estonian authorities previously attributed an August arson attack against defense contractor Milrem Robotics to Russian special services.

Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Russia's objective is to intimidate Estonia, weaken its defense industry and reduce support for Ukraine. Estonia is a member of both NATO and the European Union and has been a strong supporter of Ukraine since Russia's full-scale invasion.

Body Found Ends Search in Worcester Officer's Death

Investigators believe case was a murder-suicide

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 25, 2026 at 2:47 PM ET

Massachusetts authorities have found the body of Karen Solomon, the woman sought in connection with the fatal stabbing of her husband, Worcester Police Officer Kurt Solomon, bringing a six-day search to an end. Worcester Police Chief Paul Saucier said two employees from Fairlawn Rehabilitation Hospital discovered her body in a wooded area near the hospital shortly before 1 p.m. on August 24. Investigators also recovered a firearm near her body that was not a department-issued service weapon.

Police said the evidence currently indicates the deaths were the result of a murder-suicide, although the investigation remains active pending final findings from the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner. Authorities said there had been no previous domestic violence calls involving the couple. Before her body was located, prosecutors had obtained an arrest warrant charging Karen Solomon with assault and battery with a dangerous weapon causing serious bodily injury in connection with her husband's death.

The investigation began during the early morning hours of August 18, when officers responding to the couple's Wildwood Avenue home found 57-year-old Kurt Solomon suffering from fatal sharp-force injuries. Solomon had served with the Worcester Police Department for 31 years. Surveillance video later showed Karen Solomon leaving the neighborhood on foot, prompting an extensive search involving local, state and federal law enforcement agencies. Search efforts focused on areas around Coes Pond, Mill Street and nearby wooded terrain before her body was ultimately discovered.

Karen Solomon was widely known as a co-founder of First H.E.L.P., a nonprofit organization that supports law enforcement officers and their families following line-of-duty deaths and suicides. Officials expressed condolences to the Solomon family and said investigators are continuing to review evidence to complete the case.

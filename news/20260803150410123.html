229 Rescued After Indonesian Ferry Fire; 5 Dead As Missing Count Remains Unclear

Unmanifested passengers complicate search off Madura Island

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 3, 2026 at 8:13 PM ET

Monday, August 3, 2026 — Indonesian rescuers said 229 people have been saved and 5 are confirmed dead after a major fire aboard the passenger ferry KMP Mutiara Sentosa 2, with the total number of missing still uncertain because some people on board were not listed on the manifest. Search operations continued off Madura Island amid rough seas as authorities worked to reconcile reports from families and survivors with official records.

Officials said the vessel, operated by PT Atosim Lampung Pelayaran, was sailing from Surabaya (East Java) to Makassar (South Sulawesi) when the blaze erupted between 6:00 a.m. and 7:00 a.m. local time on Sunday, August 2. The ferry’s manifest recorded 232 passengers and 39 crew. Rescuers located the incident area about 19 nautical miles (35 km) north of Buruan Sapudi Island in Sumenep regency, deploying assets from Basarnas (Indonesia’s National Search and Rescue Agency), the Indonesian Navy, and nearby commercial ships to pull people from the water and upper decks.

Rescue officials cautioned that discrepancies between manifests and actual headcounts are common on inter-island routes, complicating accountability during emergencies. The cause of the fire remains under investigation. The ferry was carrying numerous vehicles—mostly heavy trucks—and an excavator on its lower deck, and survivors described flames spreading rapidly in strong winds, forcing many to crowd the bow before abandoning ship.

Figures evolved over time as evacuations progressed. By Sunday afternoon, authorities reported roughly 225 people rescued and 41 unaccounted for; by Monday, the confirmed rescue total had risen to 229 while officials emphasized that the precise number of missing remained unknown due to unmanifested passengers. Families gathered at ports and rescue posts for updates as additional patrols and naval units widened sea searches north of Madura.

President Trump says Iran could “take out” Los Angeles or San Diego

California leaders condemn remarks made during Nebraska rally

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 6, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

President Trump drew criticism from California leaders after saying during an October 5 campaign rally in Grand Island, Nebraska, that Iran could “take out” Los Angeles or San Diego while he defended the costs associated with the ongoing war with Iran.

Speaking about the conflict and higher prices, Trump told the crowd that the economic effects were “a small price to pay for keeping the world safe, keeping our country safe.” He then added: “They can take out a city. Let them take out Los Angeles. Let them take out San Diego.” Rally attendees could be heard cheering after the remarks. Trump then repeated that it would be “a very small price to pay.”

The comments prompted responses from several California officials. Gov. Gavin Newsom called Trump “deranged and dangerous.” Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass said a president's role is to protect the country rather than encourage attacks on American lives. San Diego Mayor Todd Gloria said his city was “not expendable” and should not be treated as collateral damage.

Both cities also have major national-security infrastructure. Los Angeles is home to the U.S. Space Force's Space Systems Command headquarters and major aerospace and defense operations. The San Diego region includes Naval Base San Diego, Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton and Naval Amphibious Base Coronado, along with a large population of active-duty military personnel and their families.

Trump's comments came while he was campaigning for Republican candidates ahead of the November midterm elections and discussing the Iran war and its economic effects. The Los Angeles Times reported that it was unclear whether his remarks were based on any specific intelligence about a potential Iranian attack. The outlet also noted that Iran's known ballistic-missile capabilities do not establish an ability to strike the U.S. mainland directly.

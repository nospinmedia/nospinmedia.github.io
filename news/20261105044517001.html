Vance blames Iran war for Republican midterm losses

Trump agrees conflict hurt GOP but says he would do it again

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 5, 2026 at 10:15 AM ET

Vice President JD Vance says the war with Iran was the primary reason Republicans lost control of Congress in the 2026 midterm elections, while President Trump acknowledged the conflict hurt his party but defended the decision to go to war.

In a lengthy post on November 4, Vance wrote that "the reason we lost is the war" and said Republicans should directly confront its political cost. Vance said polling he reviewed before Election Day showed some voters who supported the Trump-Vance ticket in 2024 planned to vote Democratic because they wanted the administration to end the war, address inflation and the cost of living, prioritize domestic concerns and change the U.S. relationship with Israel.

Vance's assessment came after Democrats won control of both the House and Senate. He argued that Republican turnout itself was not the central problem, pointing instead to voters who switched parties. His conclusion represents Vance's own interpretation of the election results rather than a definitive finding about why individual voters cast their ballots.

Trump told reporters Wednesday that Vance's assessment was "largely true," saying the Iran conflict had pushed gasoline prices higher in the short term. However, Trump said he would make the same decision again, arguing that military action was necessary to prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon.

The exchange puts the political consequences of the Iran war at the center of the Republican Party's post-election debate. Vance has previously defended parts of the administration's Iran policy but has also been among officials urging a negotiated end to the conflict. Trump, meanwhile, continues to defend the decision to launch the military campaign despite acknowledging its domestic political costs.

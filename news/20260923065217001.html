Teen gunman wounds 11 students outside Turkish school

Suspect and three relatives detained as investigators examine weapon access

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 23, 2026 at 2:30 PM ET

A 15-year-old student opened fire outside a high school in western Turkey on Tuesday morning, wounding 11 students, including two seriously, before being detained by police, according to Turkish authorities. The attack occurred around 8 a.m. near the İnci Üzmez Vocational and Technical Anatolian High School in Turgutlu, a district of Manisa province near İzmir.

Justice Minister Akın Gürlek said the suspected attacker was quickly apprehended. Investigators also detained the teenager's mother, father and grandfather as authorities examine how the shotgun used in the attack came into the minor's possession and how it had been stored and supervised. Officials are also investigating allegations that bullying may have preceded the shooting. Authorities have not announced a motive.

Security-camera footage reported by news organizations appeared to show a teenager carrying a long-barreled firearm near the school before the attack. Police, emergency medical crews and other responders were sent to the scene, and security around the school was increased. Turkey's Education Ministry suspended classes there for the remainder of the day and dispatched investigators to Manisa.

The shooting is the third school shooting reported in Turkey this year, an unusual series of attacks for the country. Two school shootings on consecutive days in April prompted the government to introduce additional security measures for the 2026-2027 school year. Those measures include police assigned to schools considered at higher risk, liaison officers for lower-risk campuses, increased patrols and approximately 31,000 school grounds supervisors deployed nationwide. The latest attack occurred one day after Turkey's education and interior ministries announced additional school-security measures for the new academic year.

Swat Police Station Bombing Death Toll Climbs To 17

Updated count follows antimilitant rally blast in Kabal

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 3, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

The death toll from a suspected suicide bombing outside a police station in Pakistan’s Swat Valley rose to 17 on August 3, 2026, after several critically injured victims died in hospital. The blast occurred on August 2 in Kabal, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, as a large crowd gathered near the Kabal police station for an antimilitant demonstration, according to local police and rescue officials.

District Police Officer Umar Khan said officers attempted to stop and search the attacker at the station’s entrance, where the bomb detonated, killing police and civilians at the gate and injuring dozens nearby. Malakand regional police official Fida Hussain confirmed that law enforcement personnel were among the dead. Authorities reported that the updated fatality count reached 17 on Monday as hospitals treated additional casualties from the scene.

- Location: Kabal, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa - Casualties: 17 dead as of August 3, 2026; officials reported more than two dozen injured on August 2, with later tallies indicating a higher injury total as hospital admissions were updated - Agencies involved: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police, Rescue 1122 - Medical response: Patients taken to Central Hospital Saidu Sharif and other local facilities

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif condemned the attack and called for the best possible care for the wounded. Police cordoned off the area and opened an investigation. Later on August 3, the Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan (TTP) claimed responsibility for the suicide bombing, according to Reuters. Officials and analysts said the attack reflects renewed militant activity targeting security forces and public gatherings in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Sunday’s explosion struck as residents rallied against resurging militant violence in the region, where attacks on security forces and civilians have increased this year. Initial reports on August 2 listed 14 fatalities, but the rise to 17 deaths on August 3 resulted from additional victims succumbing to their injuries rather than a separate incident. Investigators continue working to determine the full sequence of events and assess any additional security measures needed for future public gatherings.

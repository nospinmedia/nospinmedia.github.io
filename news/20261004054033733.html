Trump's top security aides meet secretly at Camp David

Iran war and escalating Yemen conflict dominate talks

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 4, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

Top national security officials in the Trump administration met for several hours at Camp David on Friday to discuss the next steps in the war with Iran and the rapidly escalating conflict between Saudi Arabia and Iran-backed Houthi forces in Yemen, according to three U.S. officials familiar with the meeting.

The gathering was chaired by Vice President JD Vance and was not publicly announced by the administration. Officials told Axios that Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, White House chief of staff Susie Wiles, special envoy Steve Witkoff, CIA Director John Ratcliffe and other senior officials participated. President Trump was not at the meeting.

The discussions came as Trump weighs whether to resume large-scale U.S. attacks against Iran after diplomatic efforts failed to produce an agreement. Iran has warned that another major U.S. attack would trigger a stronger response, while the Pentagon is sending approximately 9,000 additional troops, a third aircraft carrier and other military assets toward the Middle East.

Yemen was also a major focus. Saudi Arabia and Yemeni government forces are preparing a large offensive against the Houthis aimed at retaking territory along the Red Sea coast and the strategically important Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The United States has so far declined Saudi requests to participate directly in the offensive, although it is providing intelligence and other support.

The Camp David session was unusual both for its secrecy and its timing. Axios reported that the last comparable gathering occurred in June 2025, shortly before Israel went to war with Iran. No final decisions from Friday's meeting have been publicly announced, and reporting does not establish that the administration has decided to launch new military operations.

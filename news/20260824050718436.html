Phoenix fires and arrests 2 officers after hidden traffic stop

Kidnapping and assault charges follow alleged body camera cover-up

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 24, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

The Phoenix Police Department has fired and arrested former officers Luis Vasquez Jr. and Antonio Felix after investigators concluded they carried out a violent, undocumented traffic stop that allegedly included assaults, Taser use and threats against a man and woman. Police Chief Matt Giordano announced the dismissals and arrests, saying detectives established probable cause despite the officers failing to activate their body-worn cameras or report the incident through normal department channels.

According to investigators, the incident occurred around 11:30 p.m. on August 5 near 19th Avenue and Broadway Road in south Phoenix. Prosecutors allege the officers smashed the driver's side window, forcibly removed the female passenger, handcuffed her, repeatedly punched the male driver and deployed a Taser against both victims. Investigators also allege Vasquez threatened to shoot the driver during the encounter. The officers now face multiple felony charges, including aggravated assault, aggravated robbery, kidnapping, felony assault involving a bound or restrained person, threatening and intimidating, criminal damage, hindering prosecution, and tampering with evidence.

The case only came to light after the injured driver escaped and abandoned the damaged vehicle in neighboring Tempe. A Tempe police officer found no dispatch records showing any legitimate traffic stop and initially suspected the victims had been targeted by people impersonating police officers. Phoenix detectives later used the department's automatic vehicle locator (AVL) system to place the officers' marked patrol vehicle at the reported location, leading to a criminal investigation. Officials said there is no indication the officers knew either victim before the stop.

Vasquez, an eight-year veteran, has been released from custody after posting bond, while Felix, a nine-year veteran, remained jailed following his initial court appearance. Phoenix police said both officers had previous policy violations but nothing comparable to the allegations in this case. The department's criminal investigation and internal audit remain ongoing as officials determine whether additional misconduct or policy failures occurred.

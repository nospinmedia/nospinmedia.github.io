19 Killed in Crush at Durga Puja Festival in Kolkata

Heavy Rain and Collapse Warning Trigger Panic at Crowded Pandal

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 18, 2026 at 8:46 PM ET

At least 19 people were killed and 25 injured in a crowd crush at a Durga Puja celebration in Kolkata, India, after heavy rain and a warning that a festival structure might collapse triggered panic among people gathered at the site.

The disaster occurred on the evening of October 17 at the Chorbagan Sarbojanin Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata. The temporary religious pavilion had attracted a large crowd when a heavy downpour sent people rushing toward covered areas and exits.

Witnesses and local reporting said panic intensified after an announcement over a microphone warned that part of the structure might collapse. People surged toward an exit as others attempted to escape the rain, creating a deadly bottleneck. Victims were knocked down and trampled as the crowd pressed through the confined area.

Emergency crews transported injured attendees to hospitals across Kolkata. Authorities said the victims included men and women who had been visiting the pandal as part of the city's annual Durga Puja celebrations. The casualty count rose from an initially reported 17 deaths to 19 as additional information became available.

Police detained organizers and security personnel connected to the event as investigators examined crowd-control arrangements, emergency exits and the announcement that contributed to the panic. Authorities are also investigating whether required safety procedures were followed at the heavily attended venue.

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari announced the formation of a Special Investigation Team led by a High Court judge to investigate the disaster. The state government also announced compensation for families of those killed and financial assistance for people who were injured.

Durga Puja is one of the largest annual festivals in West Bengal, with millions of people visiting temporary pandals throughout Kolkata during the celebration.

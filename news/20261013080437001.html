14-Year-Old Disarmed After Shooting Principal and Teacher at French School

Staff Member Intervenes Before Police Arrive at Middle School Near Marseille

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 13, 2026 at 11:45 AM ET

A 14-year-old student was disarmed by a school staff member and taken into police custody Monday, October 12, 2026, after opening fire inside Joseph-Lakanal Middle School in Aubagne, near Marseille in southern France. The school's principal and a teacher were wounded, while a third person was grazed during the shooting.

Authorities said the student brought a 12-gauge hunting shotgun onto the school grounds concealed inside a guitar case. He then fired multiple shots inside the building before a school staff member intervened and managed to disarm him. Police arrived afterward and took the teenager into custody.

The school's 53-year-old principal and a 37-year-old teacher suffered gunshot wounds to their lower extremities. Their injuries were not considered life-threatening. A third person was grazed during the incident but did not suffer serious injuries. No deaths were reported.

The intervention by school personnel prevented the student from continuing to fire before armed officers reached the scene. Students and staff were secured as emergency personnel responded and authorities searched the school following the attack.

The Marseille prosecutor's office opened an investigation into attempted murder. Authorities had not announced a motive as investigators began examining the circumstances surrounding the shooting and how the teenager obtained the weapon.

French Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said the suspect's father was also taken into police custody as investigators sought to determine how the 14-year-old gained access to the hunting shotgun. His detention places additional focus on the weapon's ownership, storage and accessibility as authorities reconstruct events leading to the shooting.

French officials praised the response of school personnel. Education Minister Édouard Geffray highlighted the actions of staff members who intervened during the shooting, while President Emmanuel Macron also recognized their response.

The shooting has drawn renewed attention to school security and access to firearms in France, where attacks involving guns inside schools remain relatively uncommon. Investigators are expected to question the suspect, his father and witnesses as they work to establish a motive and determine how the weapon was obtained.

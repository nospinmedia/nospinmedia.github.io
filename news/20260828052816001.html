Norway begins a new royal era

King Haakon VIII succeeds Harald V after monarch dies at 89

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished August 28, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

King Harald V of Norway, Europe's longest-serving reigning monarch, died Friday at the age of 89, the Royal Palace announced. Harald had led the Scandinavian nation since 1991, becoming a widely respected constitutional monarch whose reign spanned decades of political stability, social change and modernization. Under Norway's constitution, the crown passed immediately upon his death to his son, who now becomes King Haakon VIII.

Harald had been hospitalized earlier this month after developing haemolytic anaemia and a bloodstream infection. The Royal Palace said he died peacefully at Oslo University Hospital, prompting an official period of national mourning. Flags across Norway were lowered to half-staff as mourners gathered outside the Royal Palace in Oslo to leave flowers and pay tribute. Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre suspended official travel to oversee state transition arrangements and praised Harald's decades of public service.

Harald ascended the throne on January 17, 1991, following the death of his father, King Olav V. During his 35-year reign, he became known for helping modernize Norway's monarchy while maintaining broad public support. His marriage to Queen Sonja, a commoner, was viewed as a turning point for the royal family and reflected a more modern approach to the institution. Throughout his reign, Harald emphasized national unity while serving as Norway's ceremonial head of state under its constitutional system.

The accession of King Haakon VIII begins a new chapter for the Norwegian monarchy. His daughter, Princess Ingrid Alexandra, now becomes the direct heir to the throne. Messages of condolence poured in from European leaders and royal families following the announcement, recognizing Harald's decades of leadership and his role in strengthening Norway's standing on the international stage. State ceremonies and funeral arrangements are expected to be announced by the Royal Palace in the coming days.

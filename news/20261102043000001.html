Halloween weekend shootings leave at least 6 dead

Dallas nightlife district hit hardest with 4 killed

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 2, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Mass shootings across several U.S. cities over Halloween weekend left at least six people dead and dozens injured, with much of the violence occurring in nightlife districts and near university communities.

The deadliest incidents occurred in Dallas, where three separate shootings unfolded in the Deep Ellum entertainment district over roughly 75 minutes early Sunday. Police said four people were killed and about 15 others injured. Dallas Police Chief Daniel Comeaux said investigators were working to determine whether the shootings were connected. Police made arrests and were interviewing people as the investigation continued.

In Birmingham, Alabama, one person was killed and eight others were wounded in two shootings outside Legion Field following the Magic City Classic between Alabama A&M and Alabama State. Nearly 70,000 fans attended the game. The shootings occurred along Graymont Avenue, where vendors had been set up outside the stadium. City officials suspended all post-game tailgating following the violence.

In Newark, Delaware, six people between the ages of 15 and 21 were shot near the University of Delaware campus. The victims included three teenagers and one university student, and a 15-year-old boy was critically injured. Police said a firearm was recovered from a 16-year-old victim, who faces weapons charges.

In Bloomington, Illinois, eight people were shot during a residential party in the 100 block of East Division Street near Illinois Wesleyan University. A 19-year-old woman was killed, another woman was critically injured and six others suffered injuries that were not considered life-threatening. Police said victims included students from Illinois Wesleyan University and Illinois State University.

In Fayetteville, Arkansas, six people were hospitalized with gunshot wounds after police said an altercation preceded a shooting near the University of Arkansas. Separate shootings Friday at Spirit Halloween stores in Fairfield, California, and Madison Heights, Michigan, also left two people dead. Police described the California shooting, which killed a 31-year-old pregnant woman and wounded six others, as a targeted attack.

Authorities have not identified any connection linking the separate shootings around the country, and investigations remain underway.

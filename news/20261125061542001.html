House Democrat expands probe into Trump Jr.-linked investment firm

Four companies asked for records after investments preceded federal actions

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 25, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Rep. Jamie Raskin, the top Democrat on the House Judiciary Committee and its incoming chairman, is expanding an investigation into 1789 Capital, the venture capital firm where Donald Trump Jr. is a partner, seeking records from four companies that received investments before later benefiting from federal contracts or regulatory decisions.

In letters made public Tuesday, November 24, Raskin requested two years of communications, investment agreements, valuations and other records from defense manufacturer Hadrian Automation, e-cigarette maker Juul Labs, prediction-market company Polymarket and online firearms retailer GrabAGun. The companies were given a December 8, 2026, deadline to produce the requested records.

Raskin described what he called the "serendipitous timing" of several investments and government actions and said the records could help determine whether political connections or advance knowledge played any role. The investigation itself does not establish wrongdoing.

Among the examples cited, Hadrian Automation received nearly $1 billion in Navy and Army manufacturing contracts in March 2026 after 1789 Capital invested in the company in 2025. Hadrian also has backing from major investors including Morgan Stanley, T. Rowe Price, Andreessen Horowitz and JPMorganChase, providing broader context for its financing.

Juul Labs received FDA authorization in July 2025 allowing certain e-cigarette products to remain on the U.S. market, about four months after 1789 acquired a stake. Polymarket received investment from 1789 before federal investigations involving the company were closed and it received regulatory clearance to return to the U.S. market.

Raskin also cited GrabAGun, which went public with 1789 Capital's backing and added Trump Jr. to its board before federal regulators proposed changes affecting firearm shipments. GrabAGun said its only involvement with the ATF proposal was through the public-comment process and denied receiving improper assistance.

The inquiry, launched in August 2026, also examines 1789 Capital's rapid growth after Trump Jr. joined the firm. Raskin cited reports that assets under management grew from roughly $150 million to about $3 billion, while its flagship fund reportedly generated an approximately 200% return as of June 2026.

Trump Jr. and 1789 Capital deny wrongdoing. Attorney Bill Burck called Raskin's allegations "personal attacks untethered to actual evidence."

Ukraine Launches More Than 1,000 Drones at Russia

St. Petersburg Oil Facilities Burn as One Person Is Killed

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 19, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

Ukraine launched more than 1,000 drones at Russia in one of its largest aerial attacks of the war, setting an oil export terminal and fuel depot ablaze in St. Petersburg. The attack killed one person and wounded five others, according to St. Petersburg Gov. Alexander Beglov.

Fires broke out at the Petersburg Oil Terminal and the city's Ruchi oil depot during the October 18 attack. A logistics center operated by Russian retailer Ozon and a data center belonging to Russian technology company VK were also targeted. Operations at St. Petersburg's main airport were restricted for safety reasons during the attack.

Russia's Defense Ministry said its forces neutralized 1,015 Ukrainian drones over 15 Russian regions, Russian-occupied Crimea and the Black Sea. The figure is a Russian government claim and does not establish how many drones were destroyed or reached their targets. In the neighboring Leningrad region, Gov. Alexander Drozdenko said 145 drones were shot down and about 20,000 people lost electricity after the attack.

The operation came days after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Kyiv was ending its pause on attacks against Russian energy facilities. President Trump had announced an energy ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine on October 11, but Zelenskyy said Russia continued striking Ukrainian energy infrastructure during the pause. He said Russia carried out 76 attacks against Ukraine's energy, oil and gas sectors during the following week.

The new strikes mark a significant escalation from the earlier announcement that Ukraine would resume attacks on Russian refineries: Ukrainian drones have now reached major energy facilities in Russia's second-largest city while Moscow and Kyiv continue exchanging attacks against energy infrastructure.

Ukraine says sea drones sank Russian warship in Libya

Strike extends Kyiv's campaign far beyond the Black Sea

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 23, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy says Ukrainian forces carried out a sea-drone attack that sank the Russian warship Merkury at the Benghazi Naval Base in eastern Libya, extending Kyiv's maritime campaign roughly 2,400 kilometers from Ukraine's front lines.

The attack occurred on November 22, when sea drones struck the Merkury while it was docked in Benghazi for refueling and supplies. The vessel is a Project 20380 Steregushchiy-class guided-missile corvette commissioned in 2023, making it one of the Russian Navy's newer surface combatants. Satellite imagery from Vantor later showed the warship capsized and largely submerged alongside the pier.

On November 23, Zelenskyy confirmed Ukraine's role and praised the country's Security Service, or SBU, and other forces involved. Ukraine said the operation also struck the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara and destroyed a Ka-27 military helicopter. Russia had not immediately provided its own account of the Ukrainian claims.

Libyan military officials told the Associated Press that the sea drones were launched from western Libya. The operation represents a significant geographic expansion of Ukraine's campaign against Russian naval and logistics assets, which has previously concentrated heavily around the Black Sea and Russian-controlled territory.

Benghazi has also taken on greater strategic importance for Moscow. The eastern Libyan port is controlled by authorities aligned with military commander Khalifa Haftar, who maintains close ties with Russia. Following changes to Russia's military access in Syria, Benghazi has become an increasingly important Mediterranean refueling and resupply point supporting Russian activities in Africa.

The strike followed a separate November 21 drone attack on the sanctioned Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Baltic Sea. Libyan authorities and the U.N. Support Mission in Libya have warned against allowing Libya to become an arena for foreign conflicts and called for an investigation into the Benghazi attack.

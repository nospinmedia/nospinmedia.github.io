Manhunt expands after convicted murderer escapes Tennessee prison

Stolen truck found abandoned hundreds of miles away in Illinois

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished October 28, 2026 at 6:15 AM ET

A multi-state manhunt is underway for Jacob Allen Morley, a convicted murderer serving a life sentence who escaped from a Tennessee prison and is now believed to have traveled hundreds of miles after authorities found a stolen pickup truck abandoned in southern Illinois.

Morley, 28, escaped from the Northwest Correctional Complex in Tiptonville, Tennessee, late October 25, according to the Tennessee Department of Correction. Surveillance footage showed him breaching the prison's perimeter fence at approximately 11:30 p.m. while still wearing his prison uniform.

Authorities said Morley then made his way to a nearby farm in Lake County, where a white 2008 Ford F-350 pickup truck was reported stolen early October 26. The discovery of that truck approximately 180 miles away in Marion, Illinois, on October 27 significantly expanded the search area. The vehicle was found abandoned behind a shopping center, and authorities have been investigating where Morley may have gone after leaving it.

The Tennessee Bureau of Investigation added Morley to its Most Wanted list and is offering a reward of up to $2,500 for information leading to his arrest. Federal, state and local agencies are participating in the search. Authorities consider Morley dangerous and have warned members of the public not to approach him if they see him.

Morley is serving a life sentence for the 2022 murder of Cody Birchfield, a former co-worker. Prosecutors said Morley killed Birchfield and concealed his body beneath Morley's home. He was convicted of first-degree murder and sentenced in November 2025, meaning he had been serving the sentence for less than a year when he escaped.

Morley is described as approximately 5 feet 11 inches tall and 240 pounds, with brown hair and green eyes. Authorities have cautioned that his appearance or clothing may have changed since the prison surveillance footage was recorded.

The victim's sister, Tiffany Birchfield, has also publicly urged anyone who encounters Morley to stay away and immediately contact authorities, saying she believes he poses a serious danger while free.

Investigators have not publicly explained how Morley was able to breach the prison perimeter or whether security failures contributed to the escape. The Tennessee Department of Correction said an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the escape is underway while the search continues.

Powerful Earthquake Strikes Peru's Andes

Magnitude 6.7 Quake Sends Residents Into Streets As Shaking Reaches Lima

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 21, 2026 at 10:16 AM ET

A powerful earthquake struck Peru's Andean region, sending residents into the streets as shaking was felt hundreds of miles away in the capital, Lima.

The U.S. Geological Survey measured the earthquake at magnitude 6.7, locating its epicenter about 9 kilometers northeast of Carhuamayo, Peru. Initial news reports described the quake as magnitude 6.8, a difference that can occur as seismic agencies analyze additional data and revise preliminary measurements.

The earthquake was strong enough to be felt in Lima, where residents left buildings and gathered outside following the shaking, according to NBC News. Peru sits along the seismically active western edge of South America, where powerful earthquakes are a recurring hazard.

The quake's location in Peru's central Andes means the immediate concern is whether communities closer to the epicenter suffered structural damage, injuries or disruptions that were not apparent in the first reports. Early information following a major earthquake can change rapidly as authorities reach affected areas and conduct inspections.

The USGS classified the event as a significant earthquake worth monitoring for potential impacts and aftershocks. Earthquakes of this magnitude can produce damaging shaking, although the severity at any particular location depends on factors including depth, distance from the epicenter, local geology and building construction.

As of the initial reports, the available source information did not establish a major casualty toll or widespread destruction. Authorities and seismic agencies were continuing to assess the earthquake's effects, making reports of injuries and damage the key developments to watch next.

The event comes as residents across the region remain vulnerable to seismic activity generated by the boundary between tectonic plates along South America's Pacific coast. Peru has experienced numerous destructive earthquakes during its history, making emergency preparedness a continuing concern for communities throughout the country.

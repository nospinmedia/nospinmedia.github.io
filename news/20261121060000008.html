Nasdaq Wipes Out All Of Its 2026 Gains As Stocks Plunge

S&P 500 Falls 3.7% As AI Selloff Pushes Major Indexes Into Correction

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished November 21, 2026 at 11:45 AM ET

The Nasdaq Composite has erased all of its gains for 2026, turning negative for the year as a sharp technology selloff drove another major decline across Wall Street and pushed other major indexes into correction territory.

The S&P 500 plunged 3.7% in the latest session, while the technology-heavy Nasdaq suffered an even steeper decline. The losses left the S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average in correction territory, generally defined as a decline of at least 10% from a recent high. The Nasdaq 100 was approaching the 20% decline commonly associated with a bear market.

The latest drop extends a market retreat NSM reported earlier this week, when the S&P 500 recorded its longest losing streak since 2023. The new decline represents a significant escalation: rather than simply extending that streak, the selloff has now erased the Nasdaq's entire advance since the beginning of the year.

Technology and artificial-intelligence stocks were at the center of the latest losses. Investors have increasingly questioned whether the enormous spending on AI infrastructure can generate profits quickly enough to justify valuations that rose sharply during the industry's investment boom.

Newly public Anthropic became one focus of those concerns as its shares fell sharply following its large initial public offering. The company's debut had been closely watched as a test of investor enthusiasm for generative AI, but it failed to calm broader concerns about technology-sector valuations and spending.

The retreat has spread beyond individual AI companies. Major technology stocks have fallen as investors reduce exposure to higher-valued companies, while uncertainty over interest rates has added pressure to the broader market.

The scale of the reversal is notable because technology and AI enthusiasm had been major drivers of stock-market gains earlier in 2026. With the Nasdaq now negative for the year, investors who bought an index-tracking investment at the beginning of 2026 would, as of the latest close, no longer have a year-to-date gain.

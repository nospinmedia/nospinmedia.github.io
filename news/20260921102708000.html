Trump says planned Washington arch will double as military complex

250-foot monument would house drones, snipers and ammunition

By No Spin Media NewsroomPublished September 21, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

President Trump says his proposed 250-foot triumphal arch near Arlington National Cemetery will also serve as a military complex capable of housing drones, sniper positions and ammunition, adding a national-security role to the monument planned near Washington, D.C.

Trump said the change came at the "strong request" of the military and described the facility as a "top grade Military Complex/Triumphal Arch." According to his announcement, the structure would store large numbers of drones, provide sniper positions on the roof and plaza areas, and maintain supplies of sniper ammunition. A U.S. military official said the Defense Department had nothing to add beyond Trump's statement, and Trump did not identify a specific threat requiring those capabilities.

The arch is planned for a traffic circle near Arlington National Cemetery, approximately 1 mile from Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall and 2 miles from the Pentagon. Retired Marine Col. Mark Cancian, a senior adviser at the Center for Strategic and International Studies, questioned why another military facility would be needed so close to existing installations and raised logistical questions about storing ammunition and positioning snipers at the monument.

The project already faces legal and aviation scrutiny. U.S. District Judge Tanya Chutkan has ordered the administration to provide 48 hours' notice before most activity at the site while litigation continues. The Federal Aviation Administration separately determined that the arch would not pose an aviation hazard if it includes required lighting, including an "eternal flame" on top. The proposed site is about 3,000 feet from Reagan Washington National Airport and lies near its main approach and departure corridor.

Trump originally proposed the arch to commemorate the nation's 250th anniversary. The newly announced military functions represent a significant expansion of the purpose envisioned for the structure.

🔥 Man and Two Dogs Killed in Stark, New Hampshire House FireWoman Seriously Injured After Escaping Early-Morning Blaze 👇

HealthOctober 19, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 19, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A man and two dogs were killed and a woman was seriously injured after a fire swept through a home in Stark, New Hampshire, early Sunday morning.

Firefighters were called to the home on Montgomery Road shortly before 1:30 a.m. Sunday, October 18, according to New Hampshire fire officials. The woman was able to escape the burning home and went to a neighboring residence for help, but the man remained trapped inside.

A neighbor attempted to enter the burning house before firefighters arrived but was forced back by intense heat and flames. Fire crews from several surrounding communities responded as the fire spread through the residence.

After firefighters brought the blaze under control, they found the man and two dogs dead inside the home. The surviving woman suffered serious injuries and was transported by medical helicopter to Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston for treatment. Authorities had not publicly released the identities of the two residents as of the initial reports.

The New Hampshire State Fire Marshal's Office is investigating alongside local fire officials. Investigators said preliminary evidence indicated the fire was not suspicious. Officials were examining a wood stove as part of their investigation into where and how the fire began, but the final cause remained under investigation.

The fire required assistance from departments outside Stark, a small Coos County community in northern New Hampshire. Crews remained at the scene for several hours, with the fire reported under control at approximately 4:30 a.m.

Officials have not announced additional injuries to firefighters or neighbors. The investigation will include examination of the fire scene and circumstances surrounding the blaze before authorities make a final determination about its origin and cause.

The incident is separate from another recent house fire in North Conway and had not previously appeared in No Spin Media's New England coverage.

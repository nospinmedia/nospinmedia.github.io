☘️ Jaylen Brown Returns to Boston for Community FestivalMayor Wu Honors Former Celtic With Street Sign 👇

EducationSeptember 1, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 1, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Former Boston Celtics star Jaylen Brown returned to Boston Common on August 30 to host 741 Frequency Fest, a free community celebration that drew thousands of fans for an afternoon of music, family activities, and back-to-school support. The event marked Brown's first major public appearance in Boston since his July 2026 trade to the Philadelphia 76ers after spending 10 seasons with the Celtics. Families attending the festival received free backpacks, school supplies, and haircuts through partnerships with The 7uice Foundation and local community organizations.

During the event, Boston Mayor Michelle Wu presented Brown with an honorary "Jaylen Brown Way" street sign in recognition of his contributions to the city both on and off the basketball court. Brown was also recognized for community initiatives developed during his time in Boston, including youth education and economic opportunity programs. Fans had the opportunity to view the Larry O'Brien Championship Trophy and Brown's 2024 NBA Finals MVP trophy before he thanked supporters for embracing him throughout his career with the Celtics.

The five-hour festival featured live music, local food vendors, games, community organizations, and a pop-up shop for Brown's 741 brand. City officials implemented temporary traffic and parking restrictions around Boston Common to accommodate the large crowd. Brown told attendees that Boston would always remain an important part of his life and thanked residents for supporting both his basketball career and his community work over the past decade.

Brown was traded to Philadelphia earlier this summer in one of the NBA's biggest offseason moves, ending a tenure that included multiple All-Star selections, an NBA championship, and the 2024 Finals MVP award. Sunday's event focused less on basketball and more on Brown's long-standing commitment to community engagement, giving fans an opportunity to celebrate his legacy in Boston while wishing him well in the next chapter of his career.

🏈 Drake Maye Has Fractured Fibula, Could Miss Rest Of SeasonPatriots QB Won't Need Surgery As Tommy DeVito Prepares To Take Over 👇

EconomyNovember 21, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 21, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye has a fractured right fibula and could miss the remainder of the 2026 regular season, according to multiple reports, providing a more serious diagnosis after initial reports indicated he would be sidelined with an ankle injury.

ESPN's Adam Schefter and Mike Reiss reported that Maye will not require surgery, although the Patriots have not established a definitive timetable for his return. The injury occurred during the first quarter of Sunday's 35–10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Germany, when Maye was sacked by defensive end Aidan Hutchinson.

The diagnosis is a significant development from earlier reports that Maye was expected to be out "a while" while undergoing additional testing. New England is currently on its bye week, giving Maye additional recovery time before the Patriots return against the Los Angeles Chargers on Nov. 29.

Backup quarterback Tommy DeVito, who replaced Maye against Detroit, is expected to start against Los Angeles. Rookie Behren Morton is currently the only other quarterback on the Patriots' roster. New England enters the game with a 5–5 record.

The Patriots also received another significant injury update from the Germany game. Wide receiver A.J. Brown is expected to miss multiple weeks with a hamstring injury suffered during the third quarter, potentially leaving DeVito without one of New England's primary offensive targets as he takes over the offense.

Maye's fractured fibula does not require surgery, but reports that he could miss the rest of the regular season remain a projection rather than a confirmed shutdown. His recovery over the coming weeks will determine whether a late-season return remains possible.

The Patriots now face the possibility of pursuing a playoff spot without their starting quarterback and with Brown sidelined for multiple games.

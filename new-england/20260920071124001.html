🔴 UMass Dartmouth student charged in teammate’s stabbing deathFreshman football player killed, another wounded in Providence 👇

EducationSeptember 20, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 20, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A 19-year-old UMass Dartmouth student has been charged with murder after a stabbing in Providence, Rhode Island, killed freshman football player Marvins Antoine and wounded another teammate early September 19. Providence police identified the suspect as Chukuwnonso Uzoma-eze, who was taken into custody on the UMass Dartmouth campus following the attack. The charge is an allegation, and the case has not been adjudicated.

Police said Antoine, 18, and other members of the UMass Dartmouth football team had gone to Mezzo Lounge in Providence after the team's Friday night game. An altercation began around midnight after the nightclub reached capacity and continued across the street into the parking lot of a Wendy's restaurant on Charles Street, according to investigators.

Antoine was stabbed multiple times in the chest and transported to Rhode Island Hospital, where he died. His teammate, 19-year-old Kensley Macean, a freshman defensive back, suffered stab wounds to his head, neck and left arm. Police described Macean's injuries as non-life-threatening.

Providence Police Chief Oscar Perez said investigators believe a woman leaving the nightclub became involved in an altercation with the victims and knew Uzoma-eze. Police said they had no indication that Uzoma-eze and the victims knew each other before the encounter. Investigators used surveillance and Flock camera footage to identify a vehicle associated with the suspect and trace him to the UMass Dartmouth campus.

Uzoma-eze, who is not a member of the football team, was initially detained by university police and later transferred to Massachusetts State Police custody while authorities work to return him to Rhode Island to face the murder charge. Police said the investigation remains active and they are reviewing surveillance footage and interviewing witnesses.

Antoine was a freshman running back from Boston and a graduate of B.M.C. Durfee High School in Fall River. UMass Dartmouth said counseling and support services are available and that officials have no reason to believe there is an ongoing threat to the campus community.

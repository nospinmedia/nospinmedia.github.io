🚨 Woman killed by New Hampshire state trooper after police pursuitOfficials say driver appeared to be armed with handgun when officers approached 👇

HealthOctober 9, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 9, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A woman was shot and killed by a New Hampshire State Police trooper in Bath following a vehicle pursuit on Thursday, October 8, 2026, according to updated information from the New Hampshire Attorney General's Office.

Authorities said state troopers and local police were attempting to locate a vehicle that officers had pursued earlier in the day. Police eventually encountered the vehicle near the intersection of Route 302 and Route 112 in Bath, a small Grafton County community near the Vermont border.

According to state officials, officers made contact with the female driver and determined that she was armed with what appeared to be a handgun. During the encounter, a New Hampshire State Police trooper discharged a firearm, striking the woman.

Emergency medical care was provided and the woman was transported to an area hospital, where she was later pronounced dead. Authorities said no law enforcement officers were physically injured during the incident.

The New Hampshire Attorney General's Office and its Major Crime Unit are investigating the shooting, as is standard following a deadly use of force involving law enforcement in the state. Investigators are working to establish the complete sequence of events, including what occurred during the earlier pursuit and immediately before the trooper fired.

Officials had not initially released the woman's identity, pending notification of her family. The name of the trooper who fired the weapon also had not been publicly released.

Although authorities have now confirmed that the woman appeared to have a handgun, officials have not yet provided a complete account of her actions immediately before the shooting or said whether she pointed or fired the weapon. Those details will be important in determining whether the trooper's use of deadly force was legally justified.

The investigation is expected to include interviews with the officers involved and other witnesses, along with examination of physical evidence and any available video or audio recordings. An autopsy will also help establish the woman's official cause and manner of death.

The New Hampshire Attorney General's Office reviews fatal police shootings to determine whether an officer's use of deadly force complied with state law. A final report is typically released after the investigation is completed.

The updated account substantially changes the initial picture of the incident by establishing that the encounter followed a police pursuit, occurred at the junction of Routes 302 and 112, and involved a driver who authorities say appeared to be armed.

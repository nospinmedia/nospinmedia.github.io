🗳️ JD Vance Campaigns in Brewer Ahead of MidtermsVice president backs Maine Republicans as voters cite affordability 👇

HealthAugust 25, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 25, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Vice President JD Vance campaigned in Brewer, Maine, on August 24 as part of the Republican Party's push to strengthen its position ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. Speaking at Compotech, a defense manufacturing company, Vance urged supporters to back Republican candidates and argued the administration's economic agenda is focused on rebuilding domestic manufacturing, strengthening national security and expanding opportunities for American workers. The event drew roughly 200 attendees, many of whom said inflation and the rising cost of living remain their top concerns.

During the event, Vance endorsed former Maine Governor Paul LePage in his campaign for Maine's 2nd Congressional District and criticized the policies of Democratic leaders in both Washington and Augusta. He also renewed criticism of MaineCare, calling for closer scrutiny of the state's Medicaid program while arguing that government spending and regulation have contributed to economic challenges facing working families.

Several prominent Maine Republicans did not attend the event. U.S. Senator Susan Collins, who is seeking reelection this year, cited a scheduling conflict, while Republican gubernatorial candidate Bobby Charles was also absent. Even so, Vance praised Collins' record in the Senate and encouraged voters to support Republican candidates across the ballot in November.

Maine Democratic leaders criticized the visit, arguing that federal tariff policies and rising household costs have placed additional financial pressure on families and businesses across the state. The Brewer rally highlighted the competing economic messages both parties are expected to emphasize as Maine prepares for several closely watched statewide and congressional races this fall.

🚨 Two adults found dead, 8-year-old shot in Manchester homeAuthorities investigate possible murder-suicide on city’s West Side 👇

HealthOctober 28, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 28, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

New Hampshire authorities are investigating a possible murder-suicide after a man and woman were found dead and an 8-year-old boy was discovered with a gunshot wound inside a home in Manchester on Tuesday morning.

Manchester police responded to a residence on Wheelock Street on the city's West Side at approximately 9:34 a.m. on October 27 after receiving a welfare-check request from someone concerned about the family. Officers entered the home and found the two adults dead and the child injured.

Authorities said the woman had suffered multiple gunshot wounds, while the man had suffered a single gunshot wound to the head. Both were pronounced dead at the scene. The boy had been shot in the torso but was conscious and alert when officers reached him. Police carried him from the house before he was transported to a regional trauma center. Authorities said his injuries were considered serious but that he was in stable condition.

The New Hampshire Attorney General's Office and Manchester Police Department are jointly investigating the deaths. Officials said preliminary information indicates the incident may have been a murder-suicide, but investigators had not announced a final determination about what occurred inside the home.

Authorities said there was no known threat to the general public connected with the incident. Investigators remained at the property Tuesday gathering evidence and interviewing people as they worked to establish the sequence of events leading to the shootings.

Autopsies were scheduled for October 28 at the New Hampshire Office of the Chief Medical Examiner in Concord. Those examinations are expected to help determine the official causes and manners of death. Authorities had not publicly released the names of the two adults as of the initial investigation.

The shooting drew a substantial emergency response to the residential neighborhood, with Manchester police securing the area while investigators worked inside and around the home. Officials have asked anyone with information that could assist the investigation to contact authorities.

The case remains active, and additional information is expected after the autopsies and further investigative work are completed.

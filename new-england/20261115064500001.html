💻 Passenger tackled after trying to grab computer at Boston LoganPolice arrest Boston man during confrontation at American Airlines counter 👇

LegalNovember 15, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 15, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A passenger at Boston Logan International Airport was tackled and arrested Saturday evening after becoming upset over damaged luggage and trying to grab a computer from behind an American Airlines baggage support counter, according to WCVB.

The confrontation happened inside Terminal B while travelers were still dealing with delays and cancellations following Friday's nationwide FAA ground stop. Witnesses said the man became frustrated during the dispute at the baggage counter and attempted to reach behind the counter for the computer.

Another man intervened and tackled him before police arrived. Witness Rommel Marson, who saw the confrontation, told WCVB that the passenger appeared angry and that people wrestled with him until officers reached the scene.

Massachusetts State Police identified the passenger to WCVB as Andrew Kipchirchir, 43, of Boston. State Police confirmed that he was arrested and charged with disorderly conduct and also had an outstanding warrant.

Authorities did not report a theft charge, and the available reporting does not indicate that Kipchirchir successfully took the computer. The incident was reported as an attempt to grab the equipment from behind the airline counter rather than a completed theft.

The confrontation occurred during an unusually difficult weekend at Logan. The FAA had ordered a nationwide ground stop Friday because of a telecommunications problem affecting the air traffic control system. Although flights later resumed, airlines continued working through thousands of cancellations and delays Saturday.

Travelers interviewed at Logan described difficulty getting replacement flights, with some preparing to spend the night at the airport. Some passengers said they were provided cots and quiet areas to sleep, while others complained that they had not received hotel accommodations or meal vouchers from their airlines.

There was no indication that Saturday's confrontation caused a broader security problem or disrupted airport operations. No additional charges related to the incident had been reported as of the latest available information.

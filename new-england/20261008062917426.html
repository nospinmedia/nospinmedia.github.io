🚨 Mother and 2-year-old daughter stabbed on Providence streetPolice charge 46-year-old man and say victims apparently did not know him 👇

HealthOctober 8, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 8, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A 46-year-old man has been charged after a mother and her 2-year-old daughter were stabbed Wednesday afternoon near Broad and Public streets in Providence, according to police. Investigators identified the suspect as Nelson Blanco, who was taken into custody shortly after the attack and is scheduled to be arraigned Thursday in Providence District Court.

Providence Police Chief Oscar Perez said officers responded at about 1:20 p.m. and found a woman in her late 20s and the young girl suffering from stab wounds. Both were transported to hospitals for treatment. Police initially said the child's injuries were serious, while later local reporting described her as being in critical condition at Hasbro Children's Hospital. The mother's injuries were reported as non-life-threatening.

Perez said investigators had found no evidence that Blanco knew the mother or child, making the circumstances surrounding the attack a major focus of the continuing investigation. Earlier reporting said the mother and daughter appeared to have been waiting for a bus when the stabbing occurred. Other children were nearby but were not injured.

Providence police say Blanco faces two counts of assault with intent to commit murder, one involving the mother and one involving the child. He also faces charges of assault with a deadly weapon, possession of a knife during a crime of violence and possession of a knife with a blade longer than three inches. Police also listed a separate breaking-and-entering charge involving a dwelling occupied by a person over 60. Authorities have not publicly explained how that charge relates to the stabbing investigation.

The charges are allegations, and Blanco is presumed innocent unless proven guilty. Police said the investigation remains active as detectives work to establish what led to the attack.

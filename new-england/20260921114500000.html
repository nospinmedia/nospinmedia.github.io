🐺 Coyote attacks man after approaching 2-year-old in HaverhillPolice search with drone after animal survives being hit by two vehicles 👇

HealthSeptember 21, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 21, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Police in Haverhill, Massachusetts, are urging residents to remain cautious after a coyote approached a 2-year-old child and then attacked a man who intervened Friday evening. The animal escaped and was later struck by two vehicles before disappearing into a wooded area.

The encounter happened around 6:30 p.m. Friday near Briarwood Road, as neighbors were preparing for a child's birthday party. Yolanda Delgado told Boston 25 News she was holding her neighbor's 2-year-old daughter when they noticed the coyote about 10 to 15 feet away. Her husband, Julio Delgado, told them to run and attempted to draw the animal's attention away from the child.

The coyote charged Julio, who fell during the confrontation. Accounts differ on whether he was bitten. Haverhill Police said the coyote bit him on the arm and that he fought it off with a shovel, while his wife told Boston 25 that he suffered scratches and bruises but was not bitten. Julio was treated at a hospital and received rabies and tetanus shots as precautions. The child was not injured.

Video recorded roughly three hours before the attack showed what was believed to be the same coyote biting a chair and a child's basketball hoop in a nearby backyard. After the confrontation with Delgado, police said the animal fled and was struck by a vehicle. It got up, ran away and was then struck by a second vehicle before again getting up and continuing into a wooded area near Broadway and Forest Street.

Haverhill Police deployed a drone to search for the coyote but were unable to locate it. Officers said they believe the animal may have died from its injuries, but because that has not been confirmed, residents are being asked to remain vigilant outdoors, particularly around children and pets.

⚖️ Randolph man arraigned in fatal Roxbury shootingBoston police charge 23-year-old with murder and firearm offenses 👇

HealthSeptember 1, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 1, 2026 at 11:43 AM ET

A 23-year-old Randolph man has been arraigned on a murder charge following the fatal shooting of Junior Manuel Sosa Fernandez, 28, in Roxbury over the weekend. According to the Boston Police Department, officers responded to a ShotSpotter alert shortly before 9:23 p.m. on August 29 near 306 Warren Street, where they found Sosa Fernandez suffering from multiple gunshot wounds. He was transported to a nearby hospital, where he was later pronounced dead.

Investigators said officers quickly identified a vehicle matching the suspect description and located it near Martin Luther King Boulevard. During a high-risk stop, police observed evidence inside the vehicle consistent with the shooting and arrested Jarrid Campbell without incident. Authorities said officers recovered a concealed Glock 43X 9mm handgun with an obliterated serial number from Campbell's clothing.

Campbell was arraigned in Roxbury District Court on August 31 on charges including murder, unlawful possession of a firearm, possession of a firearm with an obliterated serial number, and possession of ammunition without a firearm identification card. Prosecutors requested that he be held as the case moves forward through the court system.

The Boston Police Homicide Unit and the Suffolk County District Attorney's Office continue to investigate the circumstances surrounding the shooting. Detectives are reviewing surveillance video and interviewing witnesses as they work to determine what led to the fatal confrontation.

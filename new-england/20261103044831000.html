🔥 New Hampshire wildfire grows to about 300 acresCrews battle rugged terrain near Maine border 👇

File photoWildfire burning through forest.Credit: Salam2009
EnvironmentNovember 3, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 3, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A wildfire burning in northern New Hampshire has grown to approximately 300 acres, with firefighters working in steep and difficult terrain near the Maine border as dry conditions continue across the region.

The Wild River Fire is burning in the White Mountain National Forest in Coos County, southeast of Gorham and near the Wild River and Evans Notch areas. The fire was first reported Sunday and expanded significantly as crews worked to establish containment lines around the blaze.

More than 40 firefighters have been involved in the response, according to officials. The remote location and rugged terrain have complicated efforts to reach portions of the fire, with crews working through areas containing heavy timber, fallen trees and dry leaf litter.

Officials said the wildfire was not immediately threatening homes or private property. The fire is burning largely on federal forest land near the New Hampshire-Maine border. Authorities have not announced evacuations associated with the blaze.

The fire comes amid unusually dry conditions that have increased wildfire concerns across parts of New England. Officials have urged residents and visitors to use extra caution with outdoor fires and other potential ignition sources while vegetation remains dry.

The cause of the Wild River Fire remains under investigation. Firefighters are expected to continue containment work while monitoring weather and ground conditions that could influence the fire's spread.

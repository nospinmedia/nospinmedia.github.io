🔥 Wild River Fire reaches 59% containment in White Mountains821-acre fire remains under closure order as crews continue monitoring burn area 👇

EnvironmentNovember 18, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 18, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

The Wild River Fire in New Hampshire's White Mountain National Forest reached 59% containment on November 11, marking substantial progress against the unusually large late-season wildfire while a federal closure order remains in effect around the burn area.

The fire began November 1 in the Androscoggin Ranger District, about 7 miles southeast of Gorham, New Hampshire. Updated U.S. Forest Service mapping places the fire at 821 acres, replacing preliminary aerial estimates that had initially put its size near 1,000 acres.

Containment increased rapidly after wet weather and additional work by fire crews. The fire was reported at 29% containment before increasing to 53% on November 10 and 59% on November 11. Crews used equipment including leaf blowers to clear leaves and other combustible material from containment lines as they strengthened the perimeter.

The U.S. Forest Service says investigators believe the fire was human-caused. The specific circumstances that started the fire have not been publicly identified.

Although containment has improved substantially, restrictions remain around the affected area. A Forest Service closure order prohibits public entry into portions of the burn zone and includes all of Wild River Road, the Shelburne Trail and sections of the High Water Trail. Visitors should continue to follow posted closures while fire activity and conditions are monitored.

The fire developed during deep drought conditions that left forest vegetation unusually dry across parts of New Hampshire and contributed to an uncommon wildfire threat this late in the year. Rain and wetter conditions later helped crews make significant progress containing the blaze.

While the 59% containment milestone was reached on November 11, active management of the 821-acre burn area and federal access restrictions remain ongoing.

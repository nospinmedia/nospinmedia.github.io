🚨 Woman Found Dead Outside South Burlington CVSPolice See Signs Of Drug Use And Say Foul Play Is Not Suspected 👇

HealthNovember 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 22, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

South Burlington police are investigating after a woman believed to be in her 30s was found dead in a ditch outside a CVS on Dorset Street on Saturday, November 21.

Police responded to the area around 10 a.m. after the woman was discovered near the roadway outside the store. Investigators examined the scene and found no indications that she had been involved in a physical confrontation before her death.

Sgt. Dave MacDonough of the South Burlington Police Bureau of Criminal Investigations told WCAX that detectives found no signs of a struggle or physical injuries and observed signs of drug use. Police said they do not suspect foul play. WPTZ reported that the circumstances appeared consistent with a possible drug overdose.

An official cause and manner of death have not yet been determined. Those findings will depend on an examination by the Vermont Chief Medical Examiner's Office, meaning an overdose has not been formally established as the cause of death.

Police are asking anyone who may have seen activity around the Dorset Street CVS during the night before the discovery or Saturday morning to contact investigators with information that could help establish what happened before the woman's death.

The woman's identity had not been publicly released in the initial reports.

A separate Seven Days report concerning a Burlington woman killed in a domestic-violence case is not connected to this incident, despite the two stories being associated during automated news discovery.

⚖️ Brockton mayor charged with assault and batteryCase involves alleged contact with 17-year-old student 👇

EducationSeptember 17, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 17, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Brockton Mayor Moises Rodrigues is facing assault and battery charges stemming from an alleged encounter with a 17-year-old Brockton High School student during a city parade in May. Court records say the student was marching with the school's band when Rodrigues allegedly touched her around the waist and shoulder without her consent.

The alleged incident occurred during Brockton's Huntington Parade on May 22. According to a Massachusetts State Police investigation, the student said Rodrigues grabbed her around the waist while she was playing trombone and spoke into her ear as she attempted to move away. Investigators interviewed 13 witnesses and reviewed other evidence as part of the investigation.

Rodrigues has denied intentionally making the student uncomfortable. He told investigators that the band was playing "Anchors Aweigh" and, as a U.S. Navy veteran, he was walking alongside the band and acting as though he were conducting the song. He said none of his interactions that day initially stood out to him and later sought to apologize after learning that the student had been uncomfortable.

The student's family obtained a temporary harassment-prevention order against Rodrigues in June after the girl's mother confronted him during Brockton High School's graduation ceremony. A judge later declined to extend that order, finding that the legal requirements had not been met, while describing the alleged conduct as unwelcome and unprofessional. That proceeding was separate from the subsequent criminal case.

Court records reported by WBUR and The Boston Globe show Rodrigues is now facing misdemeanor assault and battery charges. The case was transferred out of Brockton because of a conflict, and Rodrigues is scheduled to be arraigned in Hingham District Court on September 28. The charges are allegations, and Rodrigues has not been convicted.

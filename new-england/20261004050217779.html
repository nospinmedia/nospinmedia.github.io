🎸 Zach Bryan wears 'Free Palestine' shirt at Gillette StadiumPerformance follows Macklemore controversy at same venue 👇

LegalOctober 4, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 4, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Country singer Zach Bryan performed at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Massachusetts, on Friday, October 2, wearing a black shirt displaying the words "Free Palestine" and "An appeal to heaven" with a pine tree. Several members of Bryan's band also wore clothing with pro-Palestinian messages, including Palestinian soccer jerseys.

Bryan did not make a reported speech about the Israel-Palestinian conflict during the performance. The message instead appeared on his clothing as he played the first of two scheduled concerts at Gillette Stadium. The phrase "An appeal to heaven" and its pine tree imagery date to the Revolutionary War and appeared on the Massachusetts maritime flag until 1971.

The appearance drew additional attention because it came weeks after a dispute involving rapper Macklemore and Robert Kraft, whose Kraft Group owns Gillette Stadium. Macklemore had been scheduled to open for Ed Sheeran at the Foxborough venue in September but was removed from Sheeran's tour after making pro-Palestinian remarks during an earlier concert in New Jersey. Kraft said at the time that Macklemore's participation at Gillette would cross the venue's line against providing a platform for hate speech, citing Macklemore's recent actions and what Kraft described as a broader history of antisemitic rhetoric and imagery.

Macklemore disputed the idea that saying "Free Palestine" or criticizing Israel should be equated with antisemitism. Sheeran later said venues had told him shows would be canceled if Macklemore remained on the tour and said the decision to remove him was made by the tour promoter, not Sheeran himself.

Bryan was scheduled to return to Gillette Stadium for a second performance on Saturday, October 3. Gillette Stadium had not publicly responded to the shirt when CBS Boston reported on the Friday performance.

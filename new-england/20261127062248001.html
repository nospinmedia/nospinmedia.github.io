🚒 Four Weymouth firefighters injured in engine rolloverTruck crashed while responding to Thanksgiving morning house fire 👇

HealthNovember 27, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 27, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Four Weymouth, Massachusetts, firefighters were injured Thanksgiving morning when their fire engine rolled over while responding to a three-alarm house fire that ultimately destroyed a home in North Weymouth.

The firefighters were aboard Engine 2 when the truck overturned on Commercial Street while responding to a fire at 14 Babcock Avenue around 10 a.m. Thursday, November 26. The crash occurred roughly 1.5 miles from the burning home.

All four firefighters were transported to South Shore Hospital. Weymouth Fire Chief Matthew Harding said two firefighters suffered serious injuries and two suffered minor injuries. Harding said the two seriously injured firefighters appeared to be in stable condition at the hospital.

Other firefighters continued responding to the Babcock Avenue fire while emergency crews assisted the injured Engine 2 crew. The home was already heavily involved when firefighters arrived, and the fast-moving blaze eventually caused part of the structure to collapse.

Officials said everyone inside the house was accounted for. One occupant escaped and was taken to a hospital for evaluation. The home was considered a total loss, while heat from the intense fire also damaged a neighboring residence.

The rollover left emergency crews simultaneously managing two major incidents: an injured four-person fire crew and the three-alarm residential fire they had been responding to. Surrounding fire departments provided assistance as crews worked to control the blaze.

Investigators are now examining both incidents separately. Massachusetts State Police collision reconstruction investigators are investigating what caused Engine 2 to roll over on Commercial Street. The State Fire Marshal's Office, working with local investigators, is examining the cause and origin of the Babcock Avenue fire.

Officials had not announced a cause for either incident as of the latest reports.

🚨 3 charged in shooting near Lawrence High SchoolThree teenage victims were wounded, including a 13-year-old walking home 👇

EducationSeptember 25, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 25, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Three people have been charged in connection with an August 31 shooting near Lawrence High School that left three teenagers wounded, including a 13-year-old boy who was shot while walking home from school. Prosecutors said the shooting happened at about 3 p.m. on East Dalton Street in Lawrence, during school dismissal, but was not on school property.

Jesus Emmanuel Garcia Acosta, 19, of Lawrence, was indicted in Essex County Superior Court on three counts of armed assault with intent to murder and three counts of assault and battery with a firearm causing serious bodily injury. He also faces charges of carrying a firearm without a license and carrying a loaded firearm without a license. Two other Lawrence residents, Raymond Santos Sosa, 17, and William Guerrero Amador, 16, were indicted as youthful offenders on the same charges in Lawrence Juvenile Court, according to the Essex County District Attorney's Office.

All three pleaded not guilty on September 24 and were ordered held without bail pending dangerousness hearings. The hearings for Sosa and Guerrero Amador are scheduled for September 29, while Garcia Acosta is scheduled to appear for a dangerousness hearing on October 1.

Boston.com reported that one of the victims was a 13-year-old boy named Deandres who was walking home from school when he was shot in the face. According to a fundraiser organized by his mother, he suffered a broken jaw and another serious internal injury that caused significant blood loss. The fundraiser says he was a bystander and was not involved in the incident. Lawrence police said all three victims continue to recover from their injuries.

The shooting remains under investigation by the Lawrence Police Department and the Massachusetts State Police Detective Unit assigned to the Essex County District Attorney's Office. Prosecutors said the three defendants are presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in court.

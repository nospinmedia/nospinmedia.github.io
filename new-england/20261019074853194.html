🏈 Patriots Hold Off Jets 26-19 for Third Straight WinDrake Maye Throws Three Touchdowns as New England Improves to 4-2 👇

New EnglandOctober 19, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 19, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

The New England Patriots won their third consecutive game Sunday, beating the New York Jets 26-19 at Gillette Stadium despite injuries that forced New England to shuffle its offensive line during the game.

Quarterback Drake Maye completed 18 of 32 passes for 235 yards and three touchdowns without an interception. It was his third consecutive game with at least three touchdown passes, extending a strong stretch that has helped New England rebound from a 1-2 start to improve to 4-2.

Maye connected with Romeo Doubs for a 42-yard touchdown in the first quarter before finding DeMario Douglas and Hunter Henry for additional scores. The Patriots did not allow Maye to be sacked despite losing three offensive linemen during the game and facing a Jets defense that entered Sunday among the NFL's more aggressive blitzing teams.

New England's defense also delivered several important stops. The Patriots stopped the Jets twice on fourth down in the first half, and cornerback Charles Woods intercepted quarterback Geno Smith in the fourth quarter. It was Smith's first interception of the season and Woods' first NFL interception.

New York remained within striking distance late and recovered enough momentum to make it a one-score game, but New England recovered the Jets' final onside-kick attempt to preserve the victory.

The win leaves the Patriots 3-0 at Gillette Stadium and 2-0 against AFC East opponents this season. New England has also beaten the Jets four consecutive times and has won 19 of the teams' last 21 meetings.

The performance follows Maye's four-touchdown passing game in New England's 45-17 victory over Las Vegas the previous week. The Patriots now face a short turnaround before traveling to Chicago to play the Bears on Thursday night.

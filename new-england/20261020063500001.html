🗳️ Minogue says he would govern Massachusetts without a lieutenant governorShawn Oliver remains on ballot despite suspending campaign after OUI arrest 👇

LegalOctober 20, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 20, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Massachusetts Republican gubernatorial nominee Mike Minogue says he plans to govern without a lieutenant governor if elected after running mate Shawn Oliver suspended his campaign following his arrest on drunken-driving and other charges.

Oliver was arrested after a late-night crash Saturday in New Bedford in which police say his SUV struck several parked vehicles and overturned. He pleaded not guilty Monday to operating under the influence of liquor, negligent operation of a motor vehicle, speeding and a marked-lanes violation. He was released on the condition that he not drive while the case is pending.

Minogue had called on Oliver to suspend his campaign following the arrest. On Monday, he went further, saying Oliver would not serve as lieutenant governor even if the Republican ticket wins in November.

There is an unusual complication: Oliver's name will remain on the ballot alongside Minogue's. Ballots have already been printed, and more than 200,000 voters had already cast ballots when Minogue announced his plan. Minogue urged supporters to vote for the ticket normally and said he would ask Oliver to resign if they are elected.

Massachusetts does not have a procedure allowing a governor to appoint a replacement when the lieutenant governor resigns. If Oliver resigned after taking office, the position could remain vacant until the next regular election. A similar vacancy occurred after Lt. Gov. Tim Murray resigned during Gov. Deval Patrick's administration.

The lieutenant governor normally assumes the governor's duties when necessary and presides over the Governor's Council. If the office is vacant, Massachusetts law provides a succession order beginning with the secretary of state.

Oliver's next court date is scheduled for November 16. His criminal case remains pending, and the charges against him have not been proven in court.

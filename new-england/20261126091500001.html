🚨 Man killed during fight at Chelsea high school football gamePolice investigate fatal sideline shooting after Thanksgiving rivalry game 👇

EducationNovember 26, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 26, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A man in his late 20s was shot and killed during a fight along the sidelines of a high school football game in Chelsea, Massachusetts, Wednesday night, sending students, families and other spectators fleeing the stadium.

Chelsea Police responded shortly before 7:45 p.m. on November 25 during the annual Thanksgiving Eve rivalry game between Chelsea High School and East Boston High School at Chelsea Memorial Stadium. East Boston was leading 24–9 when the game was called early with about three minutes remaining as the sideline altercation turned violent.

Authorities said the victim became involved in a physical confrontation with other male spectators along the sidelines. He was shot during the fight and received emergency treatment at the stadium before being transported to Massachusetts General Hospital, where he was pronounced dead.

The victim had not been publicly identified as of late Wednesday pending notification of his family. Police confirmed that he was not a student at Chelsea Public Schools or Boston Public Schools.

Witnesses described a chaotic scene as people ran from the field after hearing gunfire. One student told local television reporters that spectators initially thought the shots were fireworks before realizing something more serious had happened. Video from the scene showed a physical confrontation along the sidelines before people began running away.

Chelsea Police and Massachusetts State Police detectives assigned to the Suffolk County District Attorney's Office are investigating the killing. No arrests had been announced as of Thursday morning, and authorities had not publicly identified a suspect or explained what caused the initial confrontation.

Suffolk County District Attorney Kevin Hayden condemned the violence at a traditional sporting event attended by hundreds of spectators and expressed sympathy for the victim's family and the Chelsea and East Boston communities.

Chelsea Public Schools said counseling and emotional support services would be available to students, staff and community members affected by the shooting.

The investigation remains active as authorities review evidence and witness accounts to determine who fired the fatal shot and what led to the confrontation.

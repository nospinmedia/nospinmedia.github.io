🚨 Woman killed while sitting outside Boston hospital after SUV crashes into buildingTwo people in vehicle injured; gas line rupture forced evacuation of 32 patients and staff 👇

EducationOctober 2, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 2, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A woman sitting on a bench outside a building on the Brigham and Women's Faulkner Hospital campus in Boston was killed Thursday when an SUV struck her before crashing into the building and rupturing a gas line. Two people inside the vehicle suffered non-life-threatening injuries.

The crash occurred shortly before 11 a.m. October 1 at Belkin House, a standalone building on the hospital's Jamaica Plain campus. Boston police said the vehicle struck the pedestrian outside the building before hitting the structure and a gas line. The woman was taken into the hospital's emergency department but did not survive. Her name had not been publicly released as of Thursday evening.

The two people who were inside the SUV were treated for non-life-threatening injuries. Boston police Superintendent Paul McLaughlin said investigators were confident based on the preliminary evidence that the crash was not deliberate and had no connection to terrorism. Police described it as a tragic accident, although the precise cause remained under investigation.

The collision also caused significant disruption at the hospital. Thirty-two patients and staff members were evacuated from Belkin House after the gas line was damaged. Four Boston EMS paramedics were evaluated for possible exposure, and National Grid responded to secure the gas system.

Belkin House includes administrative offices and a dialysis clinic. Hospital officials said the building would remain closed Friday, requiring dialysis patients to be redirected to other locations.

Boston police homicide detectives and the department's Fatal Collision Team responded as part of the investigation. Police said Thursday that they were not investigating the crash as a crime.

🚨 Fights erupt at Whitman-Hanson football gameStudent allegedly stomped in head as police investigate report of knife 👇

EducationOctober 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 5, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Police are pursuing criminal charges after two separate fights involving juveniles disrupted a football game at Whitman-Hanson Regional High School Friday night, including one altercation in which a student allegedly repeatedly kicked and stomped another juvenile in the head and a knife was reportedly brandished.

The incidents occurred during Friday night's game between Whitman-Hanson and Silver Lake Regional High School. Hanson police officers were already at the game and initially assisted school staff with removing several students for inappropriate behavior.

Police said a fight then broke out among students. During officers' efforts to break it up, one juvenile allegedly refused to comply with police commands and assaulted officers despite attempts to de-escalate the situation. The juvenile was taken into custody and brought to the Hanson Police Department, where police said the student was released to a parent or guardian. Authorities are pursuing criminal charges, and the juvenile is expected to be summonsed to Juvenile Court. Police have not publicly specified the exact charges.

A second fight erupted later in the game. Police said one juvenile allegedly repeatedly kicked and stomped another juvenile's head. Investigators also received reports that a knife was brandished, although police said it was not used during the fight. Several people involved fled before officers arrived, and investigators are working to identify them and determine their individual roles. Authorities have not released information about injuries.

Police said the people involved were spectators and that no Silver Lake students or football players were involved.

Whitman-Hanson Principal Christopher Jones condemned the violence while emphasizing that the actions did not represent the overwhelming majority of students at the game. Jones said students involved will face school consequences and that spectators will see changes to supervision and expectations at future home games.

The second fight remains under investigation, and additional criminal charges could follow as police identify those involved.

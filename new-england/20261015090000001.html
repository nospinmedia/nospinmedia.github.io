🏛️ Boston School Security Official Charged With Rape at State Police BarracksWoman Found Outside Martha's Vineyard Facility as Internal Investigation Opens 👇

EducationOctober 15, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 15, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A Boston Public Schools security official has pleaded not guilty to raping a woman inside a Massachusetts State Police barracks on Martha's Vineyard, raising questions about how a civilian guest was allowed to stay overnight at the law enforcement facility.

Edward J. Brown, 27, of Boston, was arrested October 9 at Boston Latin Academy, where he worked as an assistant director of security. He was arraigned October 13 in Edgartown District Court on one count of rape.

The charge stems from an alleged assault during the early morning hours of August 15 at the State Police barracks in Oak Bluffs.

According to prosecutors, Brown met the woman at a local bar before the two traveled to the barracks, where he was staying as a guest of a state trooper he knew from college.

The woman told investigators she remembered being at the bar before waking up naked in an unfamiliar bed. She alleged that Brown restrained and sexually assaulted her as she attempted to escape.

Prosecutors said the woman eventually fled the building and was discovered outside, naked and calling for help. Bystanders assisted her, and she was taken to a hospital.

Medical personnel documented bruising on her legs and a red mark on her neck, according to statements presented in court.

Investigators subsequently obtained a warrant to search the barracks. Prosecutors also described text messages between Brown and the trooper concerning the woman's presence at the facility.

Brown's attorney, Anthony Ellison, challenged the prosecution's account and questioned the strength of the evidence. He emphasized that Brown has no prior criminal record and had continued reporting to work while aware of the investigation.

Brown was ordered held on $10,000 bail. His next court appearance is scheduled for December 3.

Boston Public Schools placed Brown on administrative leave following his arrest. District officials said the allegations do not involve students and that the school system is cooperating with investigators.

The Massachusetts State Police confirmed that its internal affairs division is investigating the circumstances surrounding the incident. The department has said it is cooperating with the criminal investigation, which is being conducted independently by Oak Bluffs police.

Neither Brown nor the woman involved is a State Police employee. No state trooper has been charged in connection with the alleged assault.

The criminal allegations remain unproven, and Brown is presumed innocent unless convicted.

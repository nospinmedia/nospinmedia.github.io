🎨 Vermont Unveils First-Ever State Tourism Brand'Vermont: State of Inspiration' campaign aims to attract more visitors 👇

EconomyAugust 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 5, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

The Vermont Department of Tourism and Marketing has unveiled the state's first dedicated tourism brand, introducing the slogan "Vermont: State of Inspiration" alongside a new hand-drawn logo designed to strengthen Vermont's identity among prospective visitors. Officials said the branding will appear in advertising, digital campaigns, and promotional materials while the state's official seal and government branding remain unchanged.

The new identity was developed by international place-branding agency DNCO following extensive market research commissioned by the state. Tourism officials said the research found that while 79% of U.S. travelers view Vermont as an attractive destination, only 15% spontaneously name it when planning a trip. The branding effort is intended to improve that conversion by giving Vermont a recognizable tourism identity similar to those used by other destinations.

Tourism Commissioner Heather Pelham said the campaign will initially target major metropolitan markets including Boston and New York City. The logo draws inspiration from traditional hand-painted Vermont roadside signs, while campaign imagery highlights outdoor recreation, local food, artisans, and small communities. Officials emphasized that the new tourism branding is separate from the state's official government identity and will not replace the iconic "moon over mountains" state seal.

Tourism remains one of Vermont's largest industries, accounting for approximately 9% of the state's gross domestic product and generating nearly $295 million annually in state and local tax revenue. State leaders said strengthening Vermont's tourism identity is part of a broader strategy to remain competitive with neighboring destinations and support businesses that rely on visitor spending.

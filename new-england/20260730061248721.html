🏠 New Hampshire Home Prices Rise Above $614,000 Average As Sales And Listings Increase 👇

EconomyJuly 30, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published July 30, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

New Hampshire's housing market became more active during the first half of 2026, but rising prices continued to place pressure on buyers. A newly released midyear report based on PrimeMLS data found that the average sales price across single-family homes, condominiums and multifamily properties reached $614,218 between January and June, an increase of 5.1% from $584,260 during the same period in 2025.

Single-family homes recorded the largest price increase, rising 6.5% year over year. Condominium prices increased 2.3%, while multifamily property prices rose 2.4%. The figures represent average sales prices across the three property categories and should not be confused with the statewide median price for a single-family home.

Housing activity also increased. The report counted 7,636 completed sales during the first six months of the year, up 4.3% from 7,323 in 2025. Single-family sales rose 3.3%, condominium sales increased 9.3% and multifamily sales declined 6.9%.

More properties also entered the market. New listings increased 6.2% to 12,060, while pending sales climbed 6.4% to 9,829. Analysts said the increases suggest buyers are continuing to absorb much of the additional inventory rather than allowing homes to remain unsold.

Despite the improved supply, affordability remains a major obstacle. The New Hampshire Association of Realtors reported that the median price of a single-family home stood at $575,000 in June. Earlier in the spring, inventory remained far below pre-pandemic levels, with roughly 2,400 homes available in May compared with about 4,600 in May 2019.

Mortgage rates remained generally in the mid-6% range during much of the first half of the year, adding to monthly ownership costs even as buyers gained somewhat more selection.

💰 Massachusetts consumers to get $11.5 million in lease reliefAll six New England states join settlement over alleged rent-to-own practices 👇

EconomyNovember 16, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 16, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Massachusetts consumers will receive more than $11.5 million in lease forgiveness and restitution under a multistate settlement resolving allegations that rent-to-own finance company Acima Digital LLC misled customers about the cost and terms of its agreements.

Massachusetts Attorney General Andrea Campbell joined attorneys general from 42 other states in the $183.5 million settlement with Acima and its parent company, Acima Holdings LLC. All six New England states — Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Connecticut, Maine, Rhode Island and Vermont — participated in the agreement.

Massachusetts consumers will receive $10.7 million in forgiveness of lease-to-own balances and another $830,000 in restitution. The Commonwealth will receive an additional $163,000.

Nationwide, the settlement provides $130 million in lease balance reductions, $43 million in consumer restitution and $10.5 million in payments to participating states. The multistate action followed a federal enforcement case against Acima that was dropped.

The states alleged that Acima advertised rental-purchase agreements as 90-day payoff programs without adequately disclosing that customers were automatically placed into maximum 12-month terms. Under those longer agreements, consumers could ultimately pay approximately twice the advertised cash price of merchandise.

Investigators also alleged that Acima used terms such as "credit" in ways that could suggest traditional financing, marked up merchandise prices and obscured terms during electronic signing.

Under the settlement, Acima must improve retailer training and disclosures, stop cash-price markups and properly address late deliveries, merchandise damaged upon delivery and required repairs during lease terms. The company also cannot report rental-purchase agreements to the three major credit reporting agencies for five years.

Eligible Massachusetts consumers do not need to take action now. A settlement administrator will contact those eligible for restitution with instructions, while Acima will contact customers who qualify for lease forgiveness.

🚨 Three-alarm fire brings HazMat teams to Newburyport chemical plantNo injuries reported as investigators work to determine what burned 👇

Illustration of hazmat crews responding to a refinery chemical leak.Credit: AI-generated illustration (No Spin Media)
New EnglandOctober 25, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 25, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A three-alarm fire at a chemical plant in Newburyport, Massachusetts, brought firefighters and a state hazardous-materials team to the facility late Friday night, with crews working for several hours before clearing the scene early Saturday.

Newburyport firefighters were called to 7 Mulliken Way, the location of Strem Chemicals, at approximately 11:10 p.m. Friday. Fire officials said crews arrived to find heavy smoke coming from the building and its sprinkler system already activated.

Because the fire was inside a chemical facility, officials declared a hazardous-materials incident and requested the Massachusetts Department of Fire Services Hazardous Materials Emergency Response Division. The fire department also struck three alarms to bring additional personnel and equipment to the scene.

Officials reported no injuries. Firefighters and hazardous-materials personnel remained at the facility until approximately 2:30 a.m. Saturday, when the scene was cleared.

Despite the HazMat response, authorities have not said that chemicals were released or even that chemicals were involved in the fire itself. Officials said the hazardous-materials declaration was made because of the type of facility involved. It remained unclear Saturday whether chemicals stored or used at the plant were affected by the blaze.

Fire departments from Newbury, Merrimac, West Newbury, Amesbury and Salisbury provided mutual aid. Departments from Groveland, Georgetown, Ipswich and Seabrook, New Hampshire, provided station coverage while Newburyport crews handled the incident.

Strem Chemicals produces chemicals used in research and commercial manufacturing as well as laboratory equipment, according to the company.

The cause of the fire remains under investigation by the Newburyport Fire Department and District 6 fire investigators. Authorities had not announced a cause as of the latest update.

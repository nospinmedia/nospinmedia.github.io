🛩️ Teen Arrested After Crashing Onto Pease Airport TarmacPolice Say Stolen Car Hit Tractor and Parked Plane 👇

HealthAugust 31, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 31, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A 17-year-old from Rochester, New Hampshire, is facing multiple charges after New Hampshire State Police say he fled a traffic stop, crashed through a security fence at Portsmouth International Airport at Pease, and struck both a utility tractor and a parked aircraft. Troopers said the incident began around 8:09 p.m. on August 29, when a member of Troop A attempted to stop a black 2008 Ford Mustang on Durham Street in Portsmouth. Investigators said the vehicle was being sought in connection with reported offenses in neighboring communities before the attempted stop.

According to police, the driver, identified as Wyatt Seely, turned off the vehicle's headlights and accelerated away rather than stopping. The Mustang broke through the airport's perimeter fence, crossed the airfield, collided with a Kubota utility tractor, and then struck an unoccupied parked airplane. Troopers arrested Seely at the scene without further incident. He was taken to a local hospital for evaluation, and authorities said no other injuries were reported.

State police charged Seely with aggravated driving while intoxicated, driving while intoxicated, disobeying a police officer, conduct after an accident, and reckless driving. Multiple agencies, including the Portsmouth Police Department, Portsmouth Fire Department, Newington Police Department, and Pease Fire and Emergency Services, responded to secure the airfield and assess damage to airport property. Officials said there was no continuing threat to airport operations following the incident.

The investigation remains active as authorities continue reviewing the circumstances surrounding the pursuit and security breach. Seely is scheduled to be arraigned in Portsmouth District Court on September 9. Police have not released an estimate of the damage to the aircraft or airport infrastructure.

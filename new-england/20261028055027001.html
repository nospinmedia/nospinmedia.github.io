🏫 Brown University classrooms closed after deadly shooting to reopenRenovated Barus & Holley spaces will return to use in spring 2027 👇

EducationOctober 28, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 28, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Brown University plans to reopen classrooms inside its Barus & Holley building in spring 2027, more than a year after a deadly campus shooting led the university to close the affected instructional spaces.

The university announced the reopening timeline on October 27, saying renovation work is underway in portions of the Providence, Rhode Island, building that have remained closed since the December 13, 2025 shooting. Other areas of Barus & Holley, including research laboratories and faculty offices, returned to use earlier as Brown gradually restored access to the complex.

Two Brown students were killed and nine other people were wounded when a gunman opened fire inside a classroom during a review session ahead of final exams. The attack prompted an extensive law enforcement response and temporarily disrupted campus operations while authorities investigated the shooting.

The classrooms most directly associated with the attack were kept out of service as Brown considered how the spaces should be changed before students and faculty returned. University officials consulted members of the campus community while developing plans for the renovation, recognizing both the practical needs of the heavily used academic building and the emotional significance of returning to the location.

The renovation includes changes to the classroom interiors and surrounding spaces rather than simply restoring them to their previous appearance. Brown has also been reviewing campus safety measures following the shooting, including building access, emergency communications and other security procedures.

Barus & Holley is a major academic facility used by Brown's engineering and physics programs. Keeping its principal classrooms closed has required the university to relocate classes elsewhere on campus while research and other activities continued in portions of the building that were cleared for occupancy.

University officials said the remaining renovation work is expected to be completed in time for classes to resume there during the spring 2027 semester. The reopening will mark one of the final stages in returning the building to regular academic use following the shooting.

The university has emphasized that the process involves more than physical reconstruction, with administrators working with students, faculty and staff as the community continues dealing with the effects of the attack.

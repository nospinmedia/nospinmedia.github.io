🚨 18-Year-Old Surrenders After Fatal Boston Gas Station ShootingSuspect Pleads Not Guilty to Murder and Is Held Without Bail 👇

An 18-year-old Boston man has surrendered to police in connection with the fatal shooting of a 20-year-old man inside a gas station convenience store in Boston's Hyde Park neighborhood.

Christian Peebles turned himself in to Boston Police after investigators identified him as a suspect in the killing of Darryl Aleo, according to authorities. Peebles was arraigned on a murder charge and pleaded not guilty. A judge ordered him held without bail while the case proceeds.

The shooting happened shortly after 5 p.m. Wednesday, October 7, inside the Akiki Gas Station on Hyde Park Avenue. Boston Police officers responding to reports of gunfire found Aleo suffering from life-threatening gunshot wounds.

Emergency medical crews transported Aleo to a hospital, where he was later pronounced dead. Police homicide detectives and prosecutors with the Suffolk County District Attorney's Office subsequently began investigating the circumstances surrounding the shooting and searching for a suspect.

Authorities said the shooting did not appear to be a random attack, and investigators believe Peebles and Aleo knew one another before the confrontation. Officials have not publicly detailed what they believe led to the shooting.

Peebles' surrender marks a major development in the investigation, but the criminal case remains at an early stage. The murder charge is an allegation, and Peebles is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

The killing is another case under investigation by Boston authorities as city officials and law enforcement continue addressing gun violence across Boston's neighborhoods. The fact that the shooting occurred inside a neighborhood business during the early evening also drew attention from residents and customers in the Hyde Park area.

Investigators may release additional details as prosecutors present evidence and the case moves through court.

🗳️ New Hampshire Absentee Ballots Now AvailableNew voter ID requirements take effect for September primary 👇

PoliticsAugust 12, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 12, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

New Hampshire voters who plan to cast absentee ballots in the September 8, 2026 state primary can now request their ballots, but new identification requirements mean many voters will need to complete additional steps before receiving one. The changes stem from Senate Bill 287, which now requires every absentee voter to verify their identity when requesting a ballot.

Under the new law, voters requesting an absentee ballot must either present a valid photo ID in person at their local town or city clerk's office, submit a photocopy of their identification with their absentee ballot application, or have their signature notarized. The previous option allowing voters to verify their identity by signing an affidavit under penalty of voter fraud has been eliminated.

New Hampshire continues to require voters to qualify for absentee voting by providing an approved reason, such as being away from their municipality on Election Day, illness or disability, work obligations, caregiving responsibilities, religious observance, incarceration, or weather-related concerns. Once an application is approved, election officials will mail the voter an absentee ballot along with instructions and return envelopes.

The New Hampshire Secretary of State's Office recommends requesting absentee ballots well before Election Day, ideally by August 25, although applications submitted by mail should arrive no later than September 7. Completed absentee ballots must be received by local election officials no later than 5 p.m. on Election Day to be counted. Voters can also track the status of their absentee ballots through the Secretary of State's online ballot tracking system.

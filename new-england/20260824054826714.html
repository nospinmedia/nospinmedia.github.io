💵 Vermont issues $192M in tax refunds under 2025 relief lawExpanded credits boost families, veterans and low-income workers 👇

EconomyAugust 24, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 24, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Vermont has issued approximately $192 million in state income tax refunds for tax year 2025, with Governor Phil Scott and the Vermont Department of Taxes highlighting faster processing times and expanded tax credits enacted through last year's tax relief package. Tax Commissioner Bill Shouldice said nearly 240,000 of the roughly 370,000 tax returns filed so far have resulted in refunds, with most taxpayers who filed by the standard deadline receiving their refunds in a median of 12 days.

State officials said the 2025 tax package has delivered about $10 million in targeted relief to Vermont households through expanded tax credits and exemptions. The Vermont Child Tax Credit expansion to families with children up to age six provided approximately $3.6 million to about 4,000 households. Changes to the Earned Income Tax Credit returned roughly $2.4 million to about 11,000 lower-income workers without dependent children. Additional tax relief included $1.9 million in military pension exemptions benefiting more than 2,400 veterans, $1.3 million in expanded Social Security exclusions for about 8,000 retirees, and $440,000 in credits for nearly 1,900 lower-income veterans.

Governor Scott said the results demonstrate that targeted tax relief can help Vermonters manage higher living costs while allowing the state to maintain a balanced budget. Vermont Adjutant General Henry "Hank" Harder joined the announcement to support proposals for broader military retirement tax exemptions, calling them important for retaining veterans and attracting retired service members to the state.

The Vermont Department of Taxes expects total refund payments to increase as additional returns are processed ahead of the October 15, 2026, filing extension deadline. Officials encouraged taxpayers who have not yet filed to submit returns electronically to receive refunds more quickly.

🚔 'Operation Wicked Safe' nets thousands of citations across New EnglandThree-day crackdown results in 36 impaired-driving arrests 👇

LegalOctober 21, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 21, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A three-day traffic enforcement campaign across six Northeastern states resulted in thousands of citations and 36 impaired-driving arrests, according to results released following Operation Wicked Safe.

The coordinated enforcement effort ran from October 13 through October 15 and included state police agencies in Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Connecticut, Rhode Island, Vermont and New York. Troopers focused on speeding, distracted and aggressive driving, impaired driving and other violations along major highways and roads connecting the participating states.

In Massachusetts, State Police conducted 3,714 traffic stops, issued nearly 2,000 citations and arrested 16 drivers for OUI. The citations included 1,110 speeding violations and 418 violations involving the state's hands-free driving law. Troopers also cited motorists for seat belt and move-over law violations.

Commercial vehicle enforcement was another part of the operation. Massachusetts State Police said specialized units inspected 577 commercial vehicles and identified more than 1,300 violations. Some vehicles and drivers were taken out of service after inspections, and the enforcement resulted in more than $72,000 in fines.

Operation Wicked Safe was announced earlier this month as a regional effort to reduce dangerous driving through increased police visibility and coordinated enforcement across state lines. The participating agencies said concentrating enforcement during the same period was intended to discourage drivers from assuming that traffic laws would be enforced differently after crossing into another state.

The newly released numbers provide the first results from the operation after authorities initially announced the campaign before it began.

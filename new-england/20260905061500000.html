💉 Mass. med spa owner gets 46 months for counterfeit injectionsMore than 900 clients paid over $1 million for bogus Botox and fillers 👇

HealthSeptember 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 5, 2026 at 2:39 PM ET

A Massachusetts med spa owner who falsely represented herself as a nurse was sentenced to 46 months in federal prison for performing thousands of cosmetic procedures using counterfeit Botox and dermal fillers. Rebecca Fadanelli, 40, of Stoughton, was sentenced on September 2 by U.S. District Judge Julia E. Kobick, who also ordered her to serve two years of supervised release and pay $1,001,562 in restitution and the same amount in forfeiture.

Fadanelli owned and operated Skin Beaute Med Spa in Randolph and South Easton. Federal prosecutors said she was an aesthetician who was never licensed to perform injections or administer prescription drugs or devices but told clients and employees she was a nurse. Between approximately March 2021 and June 2024, business records show she performed more than 2,700 injection procedures using counterfeit products and collected more than $1 million from over 900 clients.

Investigators said Fadanelli represented the products as authentic Botox, Sculptra, Restylane and Juvéderm, while counterfeit versions were imported from China and Brazil. Products seized during federal searches in June 2024 were subsequently identified as counterfeit by manufacturers. Laboratory testing also found Methylobacterium, bacteria capable of causing infection, in bacteriostatic water recovered during the searches and used to dilute Botox.

Federal prosecutors documented serious health problems among clients, including infections, severe swelling, facial and eye paralysis, double vision, impaired eyesight and hard lumps beneath the skin. Multiple clients required hospitalization or additional medical treatment, and one underwent corrective surgery that left scarring.

Fadanelli continued performing injections despite seizures of international shipments, federal searches and her November 2024 arrest. Prosecutors said she even performed another injection at her home while on pretrial release in May 2025. She pleaded guilty in April 2026 to eight federal counts involving illegal imports and counterfeit drugs and devices.

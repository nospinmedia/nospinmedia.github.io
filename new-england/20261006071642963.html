🎤 Former 'American Idol' contestant gets life for wife's murderProsecutors say Caleb Flynn killed her and staged a home invasion 👇

LegalOctober 6, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 6, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Former American Idol contestant and Ohio music pastor Caleb Flynn, 40, was sentenced on October 5 to life in prison without the possibility of parole for murdering his wife and attempting to make the killing look like a home invasion.

Flynn was convicted last week of aggravated murder, murder, felonious assault and tampering with evidence in the February 16 killing of Ashley Flynn, 37, a schoolteacher and volleyball coach. Prosecutors said Ashley was shot in the head while she slept at the couple's home in Tipp City, Ohio. Their two daughters were home when police arrived.

Flynn initially told investigators that a burglar had killed his wife. Prosecutors argued that he instead staged the scene after the shooting. The murder weapon was never recovered, but investigators presented digital evidence showing Flynn moving around the house during a period when he claimed he had been asleep. Prosecutors also said his fingerprints and DNA were found at the scene.

The trial focused heavily on Flynn's affair with Alleigha Botner, who testified that she met him while working as a 20-year-old intern at his church in 2022. Jurors heard messages in which Flynn repeatedly expressed hatred toward his wife and discussed wanting her dead. Prosecutors also presented a message Flynn sent Botner the night before Ashley's death saying he had chosen her and that actions would follow the next day.

Miami County Common Pleas Judge Jeannine Pratt imposed the life-without-parole sentence for aggravated murder. Flynn also received additional prison terms for evidence-tampering convictions and firearm specifications. During sentencing, Flynn continued to deny killing Ashley while apologizing to his daughters and other family members.

Flynn appeared briefly on American Idol in 2013, where he spoke publicly about his love for Ashley before performing during the audition process. He did not advance to the show's main competition.

🚨 Boston man identified as victim in Chelsea football game shooting29-year-old Robert Acosta was killed during sideline fight after rivalry game 👇

EducationNovember 27, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 27, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Authorities have identified the man fatally shot during a fight after a Thanksgiving Eve high school football game in Chelsea, Massachusetts, as Robert Acosta, 29, of Boston, while investigators continue searching for whoever fired the fatal shot.

Chelsea police said they responded around 7:44 p.m. Wednesday, November 25, to a report of a fight at Chelsea High School's stadium at 299 Everett Avenue. The violence occurred along the track and sidelines shortly after East Boston High School defeated Chelsea High School 24–9 in the annual rivalry game.

Authorities said Acosta became involved in a physical altercation with other males and was shot during the fight. An off-duty Everett Police officer performed CPR on Acosta at the scene before EMS transported him to Massachusetts General Hospital, where he died from his injuries.

Authorities said Acosta was not a student in either the Chelsea or Boston public school systems. Witnesses described a chaotic scene as spectators began running after hearing gunfire, with families and young children among those attending the game.

Suffolk County District Attorney Kevin Hayden called the shooting "terrible, senseless violence" at a traditional sporting event attended by hundreds of spectators and extended his sympathies to Acosta's family, witnesses and the Chelsea and East Boston communities.

Chelsea Public Schools Superintendent Almi Abeyta addressed the district's grief following the shooting and said counseling and emotional support would be available to students and staff affected by the incident.

Acosta's brother told local television station WHDH that he and their mother were working when they received the call from the hospital that Acosta had died. He described his brother as a social person who had many friends in Chelsea, explaining why he was attending the game.

The investigation is being conducted by Chelsea Police and Massachusetts State Police detectives assigned to the Suffolk County District Attorney's Office. As of the latest reports, authorities had announced no arrests and had not publicly identified a suspect.

🛒 Cyber Incident Disrupts Stop & Shop and Hannaford Online ServicesParent Company Takes Systems Offline as Grocery Chains Across U.S. and Europe Are Affected 👇

EconomyOctober 13, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 13, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A cybersecurity incident involving the parent company of Stop & Shop and Hannaford disrupted grocery websites, mobile applications and online shopping services across New England and other parts of the United States on Monday, October 12, 2026. The disruption also affected several European grocery brands operated by the same company.

Ahold Delhaize, the Netherlands-based grocery corporation, confirmed that it was experiencing a security issue within its network and had taken some computer systems offline as a precaution. The company said its teams were working to assess the problem, protect affected systems and restore services. Physical grocery stores remained open while the investigation continued.

The disruption affected websites for five major American grocery chains: Stop & Shop, Hannaford, Giant Food, The GIANT Company and Food Lion. Online grocery ordering and delivery services were interrupted, creating difficulties for customers who rely on digital shopping and home delivery.

For New England residents, the incident directly affected two familiar supermarket chains. Stop & Shop operates throughout Massachusetts, Connecticut and Rhode Island, while Hannaford serves customers across Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts and New York.

The company's international operations also experienced disruptions. Websites and digital services associated with several European brands, including Albert Heijn and Delhaize, were affected, demonstrating the broad reach of the network security issue.

Ahold Delhaize said protecting customers, employees and business partners remained a priority. The company had not provided a timetable for restoring all affected systems or publicly established the full scope of the security incident.

The company also had not confirmed whether customer information had been compromised. The cause of the incident and whether attackers gained access to sensitive data remained under investigation.

Ahold Delhaize experienced a separate cybersecurity incident in 2024 that disrupted business operations and later resulted in notifications concerning potentially exposed employee information. Authorities have not established a connection between that earlier incident and the latest disruption.

The outage highlights how a cybersecurity problem affecting one parent company's technology infrastructure can disrupt services across numerous grocery brands and geographic regions, even while individual stores remain open.

Customers who depend on online ordering may need to check service availability or shop in person until affected systems are restored.

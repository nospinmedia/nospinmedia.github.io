🥫 Hannaford Recalls Deli Burritos, Wraps Over Salmonella RiskProducts sold across New England linked to jalapeño outbreak 👇

New EnglandAugust 11, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 11, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Hannaford has recalled several prepared deli and produce items sold in stores across Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, and New York because they may contain jalapeños linked to a multistate Salmonella outbreak. The precautionary recall includes selected deli burritos, wraps, diced jalapeños, and fresh pico de gallo after the ingredients were traced to Coast Citrus Distributors, whose imported jalapeños have been identified as the source of the expanding outbreak.

Affected Hannaford products include the Beans Rice and Cheese Burrito, Classic Beef Burrito, Chicken Verde Burrito, Chipotle Chicken Tortilla Wrap, Diced Jalapeños, and Pico de Gallo Salsa with specified best-by dates in early to mid-August. Hannaford said the products have been removed from store shelves and advised customers not to consume them. Consumers may discard the items or return them to any Hannaford store for a full refund.

The recall is part of a broader investigation involving contaminated jalapeños distributed by Coast Citrus Distributors. Federal health officials have linked the outbreak to 345 illnesses and 36 hospitalizations across 27 states. While no illnesses have been specifically connected to the recalled Hannaford products, officials issued the recall as a precaution because the affected ingredients were supplied through the same distribution chain.

Customers who purchased the recalled products should check product labels and best-by dates, dispose of any affected items, clean surfaces that may have come into contact with them, and seek medical advice if they develop symptoms consistent with Salmonella infection, including fever, diarrhea, vomiting, or severe abdominal cramps.

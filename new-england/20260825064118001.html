🕊️ Body Found Ends Search in Worcester Officer's DeathPolice investigating case as apparent murder-suicide 👇

HealthAugust 25, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 25, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Authorities have found the body of Karen Solomon, ending a six-day search following the death of her husband, Worcester Police Officer Kurt Solomon. Worcester Police Chief Paul Saucier said two employees from Fairlawn Rehabilitation Hospital discovered her body in a wooded area near May Street shortly before 1 p.m. on August 24. Investigators also recovered a firearm near her body that was not a department-issued service weapon.

Police said the investigation now points to an apparent murder-suicide, although the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner will make the final determination regarding the causes and manners of death. Authorities previously obtained an arrest warrant charging Karen Solomon with assault and battery with a dangerous weapon causing serious bodily injury in connection with her husband's death. Officials also said there had been no previous domestic violence calls involving the couple.

The investigation began during the early morning hours of August 18, when officers responded to the couple's Wildwood Avenue home and found 57-year-old Kurt Solomon dead from injuries caused by an edged weapon. Surveillance footage later showed Karen Solomon leaving the neighborhood on foot, prompting an extensive search involving the Worcester Police Department, Massachusetts State Police, the U.S. Marshals Service, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, and other agencies. Search efforts focused on areas including Coes Pond before her body was discovered.

Kurt Solomon served with the Worcester Police Department for 31 years. Karen Solomon was known nationally as a co-founder of First H.E.L.P., a nonprofit organization that tracks law enforcement suicides and supports officers and their families through mental health advocacy. Authorities said the investigation remains active while forensic testing and autopsy results are completed.

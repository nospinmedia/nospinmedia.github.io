🚨 Driver accused of intentionally hitting 2 Bridgewater State studentsEast Weymouth man faces two attempted murder charges 👇

EducationOctober 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 5, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A Massachusetts man is facing two counts of attempted murder after authorities say he intentionally drove into two Bridgewater State University students on campus Saturday morning during the school's Homecoming weekend.

The incident happened on Burrill Avenue near the Moakley Center. According to university officials, surveillance video reviewed during the investigation showed the driver steering his vehicle into the two students. One student suffered serious but non-life-threatening injuries and was taken to a hospital, while the second suffered minor injuries.

Bridgewater State University Police arrested the driver near the scene. University President Frederick W. Clark Jr. described him as a man in his early 40s from East Weymouth who has no affiliation with the university. His identity had not been publicly released in the initial reports.

University officials said the driver had been involved in an earlier road-rage incident in East Bridgewater before the campus collision. Although investigators believe the act of striking the students was intentional, officials emphasized that there is no evidence the driver specifically targeted the two students.

CBS News Boston reported that the man was transported for a psychiatric evaluation following his arrest. In addition to the two attempted murder counts, officials said he faces two counts of reckless operation of a motor vehicle, leaving the scene of personal injury and a marked-lanes violation.

Bridgewater State University Police continue to investigate the incident with assistance from an accident reconstruction team. The university's Homecoming celebrations were taking place on campus Friday and Saturday when the incident occurred.

What led to the earlier road-rage incident and the alleged intentional attack on two students remains a key unanswered question as the investigation continues.

🛍️ Massachusetts Sales Tax Holiday UnderwayMost Purchases Under $2,500 Tax-Free Through Sunday 👇

EducationAugust 9, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 9, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Massachusetts began its annual Sales Tax Holiday on Saturday, August 8, 2026, temporarily suspending the state's 6.25% sales tax on most eligible retail purchases of $2,500 or less through Sunday, August 9. Administered by the Massachusetts Department of Revenue (DOR), the two-day exemption applies to qualifying purchases made in stores, online, or by telephone, provided the transaction is completed before the holiday ends Sunday night.

Under Department of Revenue rules, the $2,500 limit applies to each individual item rather than the total purchase, allowing shoppers to buy multiple qualifying items without paying sales tax. Eligible purchases include most clothing, electronics, furniture, appliances, school supplies, and other personal-use goods. However, the exemption does not apply to motor vehicles, motorboats, meals, telecommunications services, tobacco products, marijuana products, or any single item priced above $2,500, which remains fully taxable.

State lawmakers authorize the annual tax holiday to provide temporary consumer savings while encouraging retail activity during the busy back-to-school shopping season. Massachusetts retailers participating in the state's sales tax system are required to honor the exemption on qualifying purchases during the designated weekend, regardless of whether the transaction occurs in person or through an online order completed during the holiday period.

With the exemption ending at 11:59 p.m. on Sunday, August 9, shoppers planning larger purchases are encouraged to review eligibility requirements before checkout to ensure items qualify for the tax-free weekend. The Department of Revenue has also reminded consumers that the exemption applies automatically and does not require coupons, rebate forms, or any special registration.

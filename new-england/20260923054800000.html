🚨 ICE arrests surge across New Bedford areaChild waited at school after father was detained 👇

IllustrationICE immigration enforcement jacket.Credit: AI-generated (Gemini)
EducationSeptember 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 23, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Federal immigration agents carried out a multi-day enforcement operation across the New Bedford, Massachusetts, area this week, with arrests reported at workplaces, transit areas and other locations. Reporting Tuesday indicated the operation had expanded beyond the initial arrests Monday, while state and local officials said they had received little information from U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement about who was being detained or why.

Gov. Maura Healey said her office had heard from families and advocates that at least a dozen people were arrested Monday. Among the cases described by the governor was an 8-year-old boy who was waiting for his father to pick him up from school, but the father had been detained. Healey also said another arrested father had several children at home, including a one-month-old baby. Many of those detained were reportedly headed to jobs at a fishing plant, supermarket and other businesses in the region.

The operation continued Tuesday in New Bedford and neighboring communities. Local reporting documented immigration agents at several locations, while Dartmouth Police confirmed officers responded after a caller reported masked individuals conducting a traffic stop on Faunce Corner Road. Police determined the individuals were federal immigration agents and said the department had not been notified beforehand about the operation.

New Bedford Mayor Jon Mitchell said there was no indication that people arrested in the city had criminal records beyond minor motor vehicle violations. He also raised concerns that local police were not notified about the federal activity. ICE did not provide detailed information about individual arrests, saying its agents routinely conduct enforcement operations across New England and that it does not disclose information about ongoing operations for security reasons.

The arrests prompted a rally outside New Bedford City Hall Tuesday night, where residents and immigrant advocates called for greater protections for families and more information about those detained.

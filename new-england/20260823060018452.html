🚢 Federal Agents Detain 11 Cruise Ship Crew Members in BostonOperation Targets Child Sexual Abuse Material Investigations 👇

LegalAugust 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 23, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Federal authorities have detained 11 foreign crew members from two cruise ships that docked at Flynn Cruiseport Boston as part of a multi-agency investigation into alleged child sexual abuse material offenses. The enforcement operation took place between August 14 and August 16 aboard Holland America Line's Volendam and Norwegian Cruise Line's Norwegian Breakaway. According to U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), the operation was conducted with assistance from Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) and other federal partners after investigators developed intelligence identifying crew members suspected of possessing or distributing illegal material.

CBP said agents searched crew living quarters and electronic devices during the operation. The individuals taken into custody include nine citizens of the Philippines, one citizen of Indonesia, and one citizen of Nicaragua. Federal officials said the investigation was carried out under Operation Tidal Wave, an initiative focused on identifying individuals involved in child sexual abuse material offenses within the maritime industry. Authorities emphasized that protecting children and disrupting exploitation networks remains a priority for the participating agencies.

Rather than pursuing criminal prosecution in the United States, officials said the case was coordinated with the U.S. Attorney's Office and immigration authorities to begin removal proceedings. CBP stated the crew members were processed under provisions of the Immigration and Nationality Act and are expected to be removed from the country. Acting Boston Field Office Director Julio Caravia said the operation demonstrates continued cooperation among federal agencies working to identify and remove individuals suspected of crimes involving child exploitation.

The arrests occurred during the busy summer cruise season, when thousands of passengers pass through Boston's cruise terminal each week. Federal officials said the investigation remains ongoing and declined to provide additional details about the evidence recovered or whether further enforcement actions involving other vessels are anticipated.

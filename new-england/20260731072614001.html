⚖️ Federal Grand Jury Indicts Three Men In Alleged Interstate Fentanyl Ring Serving BurlingtonProsecutors Say Texas-Based Suspect Directed Vermont Drug Distribution 👇

IllustrationIllustration representing court rulings and legal proceedings.Credit: AI-generated illustration
HealthJuly 31, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published July 31, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A federal grand jury has indicted three men accused of operating an interstate drug trafficking conspiracy that allegedly distributed fentanyl and crack cocaine in the Burlington area. The indictment, announced by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Vermont, charges Angel Louis Concepcion (also known as "Cito"), Troy McIlwain (also known as "Breeze"), and David Lauderbach (also known as "Cane") with conspiracy to distribute controlled substances in violation of federal law.

According to prosecutors, Concepcion coordinated the alleged operation from Texas while McIlwain and Lauderbach distributed narcotics in Vermont. Court documents allege that when local drug sales slowed, Concepcion traveled to Vermont to personally assist with distribution activities. Authorities say the investigation uncovered an operation that moved fentanyl and crack cocaine into northern New England through an organized distribution network.

McIlwain was arrested in Vermont and entered a plea of not guilty during his initial appearance in federal court. Lauderbach was also taken into custody, while proceedings involving Concepcion are expected to continue in federal court. The case is being prosecuted in the U.S. District Court for the District of Vermont.

The investigation was conducted by the Drug Enforcement Administration, the Vermont State Police, the Burlington Police Department and other law enforcement agencies. Federal prosecutors said the charges carry significant penalties if the defendants are convicted.

An indictment is a formal accusation and is not evidence of guilt. All three defendants are presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in court.

📱 Connecticut Board Adopts 'Bell-to-Bell' School Phone GuidanceState urges districts to limit personal devices and review classroom screen use 👇

EducationAugust 7, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 7, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

The Connecticut State Board of Education unanimously adopted new statewide guidance on August 5, 2026, encouraging public school districts to implement "bell-to-bell" restrictions on student use of personal electronic devices throughout the school day. The recommendations come after the General Assembly adjourned without passing statewide legislation on school cellphone use, leaving local districts responsible for setting their own policies.

The updated position statement, "Personal and Purposeful Technology Use in Connecticut Schools," expands previous recommendations beyond smartphones to include smartwatches, personal tablets, and other personal electronic devices. The guidance also encourages districts to evaluate how school-issued technology is used in classrooms, promoting more direct instruction and reducing unnecessary screen time where appropriate.

According to the Connecticut State Department of Education, about 71% of middle schools already enforce bell-to-bell cellphone restrictions, compared with approximately 31% of high schools. State officials cited a 2025 Connecticut Education Association survey in which 57% of educators identified personal technology as a major classroom distraction. The recommendations include exceptions for students with documented disabilities, individualized education programs (IEPs), and medical monitoring needs.

Because the guidance is advisory rather than mandatory, local boards of education will decide whether and how to adopt the recommendations. State education leaders said the framework is intended to help districts create more consistent policies while balancing student learning, classroom management, and legitimate educational uses of technology.

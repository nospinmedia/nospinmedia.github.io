🚨 Quinnipiac lockdown lifted after hourslong search for possible gunmanPolice find no threat after room-by-room sweep as university investigates possible swatting 👇

EducationNovember 20, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 20, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Quinnipiac University lifted its final campus lockdown early Friday, November 20, after an hourslong police search prompted by a report of a person with a gun on the university's Mount Carmel campus in Hamden, Connecticut. No one was physically harmed, and university officials said authorities are investigating whether the report was a swatting incident — a false emergency report intended to trigger a major police response.

The university initially locked down all of its campuses shortly after 8:30 p.m. Thursday and instructed students, faculty and staff to shelter in secure locations. Officials said Hamden Police received a call reporting an individual with a gun on the Mount Carmel campus, triggering the university's emergency alert system and a large law-enforcement response.

Quinnipiac Public Safety, Hamden Police and a Connecticut Special Weapons and Tactics team conducted a room-by-room search of the Mount Carmel campus. The FBI was also alerted and participated in the coordinated response, according to university President Marie Hardin. Authorities also investigated claims and images that appeared on social media after the original threat was reported.

The lockdowns at the York Hill and North Haven campuses were lifted at approximately 10:30 p.m., while Mount Carmel remained locked down as officers continued clearing buildings. At approximately 2:28 a.m. Friday, the university announced that emergency responders had completed their sweep and determined it was safe to resume normal activities.

The security alert also affected a men's basketball game between Quinnipiac and the University of Maine at the York Hill campus. Maine's team remained in its locker room for roughly two hours following its 69–67 victory before safely leaving after restrictions there were lifted.

Quinnipiac canceled all Friday classes and made counseling available to students, faculty and staff beginning at 9 a.m. University officials said the investigation into the origin of the gunman report will continue.

☕ Manchester coffee shop vandalized with racist graffitiPolice investigate smashed windows and swastikas as suspected hate crime 👇

EconomySeptember 17, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 17, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Police in Manchester, New Hampshire, are investigating vandalism at The Moka Pot coffee shop as a suspected hate crime after several windows were smashed and racist and white supremacist graffiti was spray-painted on the downtown business early September 16.

The graffiti included swastikas and the phrase "white power" on the exterior of the Hanover Street shop. Manchester police said people were last known to be inside the business around 11:30 p.m. Tuesday following a comedy show, and investigators believe the vandalism occurred sometime between midnight and 4 a.m. Wednesday. Police were notified shortly before 7 a.m.

Manchester Police Chief Peter Marr said detectives are investigating the incident as a hate crime and are working to identify whoever was responsible. Because of the nature of the graffiti, police also notified the New Hampshire Department of Justice Civil Rights Unit. No suspect had been publicly identified as of the latest reports, and the investigation remains active.

The shop's owners said they do not know why their business was targeted. Co-owner Nick Carnes said the vandalism would not change The Moka Pot's commitment to being a welcoming community space. The business remained open Wednesday as customers and other Manchester residents came to show their support, and community members helped remove or cover the graffiti.

The incident also comes as police in Concord investigate separate racist and antisemitic graffiti discovered this week along the Merrimack River Greenway Trail. Concord police said they were not aware of a connection between the incidents, and authorities have not established that they are related.

Anyone with information about the Manchester vandalism is being asked to contact Manchester police.

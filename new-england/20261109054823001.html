🚒 Walpole firefighter-paramedic Elizabeth Hunt dies at 35Fire service rallies to support her wife and family 👇

EducationNovember 9, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 9, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Firefighters across Massachusetts are mourning the death of Elizabeth Marie Hunt, an active-duty firefighter and paramedic with the Walpole Fire Department, who died last week at age 35.

Hunt, an Abington resident, died Tuesday, November 3. The circumstances and cause of her death have not been publicly disclosed. Walpole firefighters announced her death and described the loss as deeply felt throughout their department and the wider fire service.

Hunt joined the Walpole Fire Department in April 2022, fulfilling what her obituary described as a lifelong dream of becoming a firefighter. She also worked as a paramedic and had previously served as an EMT. Outside the fire service, Hunt earned a master's degree from Waldorf University and was a fifth-degree master-level black belt in karate, according to her obituary.

The Professional Fire Fighters of Massachusetts Charitable Foundation established a fundraiser following Hunt's death to help her wife, Stephanie McKinnon, and their family with immediate expenses. McKinnon works as a dispatcher for the Massachusetts State Police. The fundraiser had collected thousands of dollars as fellow firefighters, first responders and community members contributed to the family.

Walpole Firefighters IAFF Local 2464 said Hunt's death was being felt throughout the department and the larger firefighter community. The union and other Massachusetts fire organizations have shared tributes and information about the fundraiser.

Visiting hours are scheduled for Monday, November 9, from 4 to 7 p.m. at Blanchard Funeral Chapel in Whitman, with a funeral service scheduled afterward at 7 p.m. Her obituary asks those wishing to honor her memory to consider donations to organizations including the Professional Fire Fighters of Massachusetts Charitable Foundation.

Although Hunt was an active-duty firefighter, available reporting does not indicate that she died while responding to a fire or other emergency, and no official cause of death has been released.

🤝 Massachusetts Teens Invite 7-Year-Old Sitting Alone to Join ThemBoy's Mother Says Simple Act of Inclusion Made a Lasting Difference 👇

New EnglandOctober 18, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 18, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A group of teenagers from Milford, Massachusetts, is receiving widespread praise after inviting a 7-year-old boy with autism who was sitting alone at an indoor adventure park to join them for nearly an hour of games and play.

Tristan Caron, whose family is from Foxboro, was visiting Urban Air Adventure Park in Bellingham with his mother, Christina Caron, when teenager Livy Molinari noticed him sitting by himself. Tristan had won a ball, and Molinari approached him and started a conversation about it.

The brief interaction quickly became a game of toss. Molinari's friends Kelsey Zozzaro, Anna Hele and Kenny Leite joined them, and the group spent about 40 minutes playing hide-and-seek, catch and going on the slides with Tristan.

The teenagers told WBZ-TV that they did not know Tristan had autism when they approached him. They simply saw that he was alone and wanted to include him in what they were doing.

Christina Caron said the interaction had a significant impact on her son, who is often left out by other children. After the visit, she turned to Facebook in hopes of identifying the teenagers so she could thank them. She eventually connected with the group, and her account of what happened began drawing attention throughout the community.

The teenagers said they did not expect recognition for what they had done. Instead, they said they hoped the attention might encourage other young people to notice when someone is being left out and invite them to participate.

Urban Air Adventure Park owner Chad Darling confirmed that the park is organizing a special celebration day for Tristan, his mother and all four teenagers in recognition of their time together.

The encounter began with one teenager noticing a child sitting by himself and deciding to say hello.

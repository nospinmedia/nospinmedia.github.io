⚖️ Matthew Farwell’s twin testifies under immunity in murder trialFormer Stoughton officer admits sexual relationship with Sandra Birchmore 👇

HealthOctober 27, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 27, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

William Farwell, the twin brother of former Stoughton police officer Matthew Farwell, has begun testifying under a federal grant of immunity in the ongoing trial over the death of Sandra Birchmore. The immunity prevents his compelled testimony from being used against him in a federal criminal case.

William Farwell, who also served as a Stoughton police officer, acknowledged on the stand Monday that he had a sexual relationship with Birchmore. He testified that the relationship began in 2018, when Birchmore was about 20 or 21, and acknowledged having sexual contact with her while he was on duty. He said he did not know at that time that his brother was also having a sexual relationship with her.

Matthew Farwell is accused of killing 23-year-old Birchmore and her unborn child in her Canton apartment in February 2021 and staging her death to appear to be a suicide. Prosecutors allege he had groomed and sexually abused Birchmore after meeting her through the Stoughton Police Explorers program when she was a teenager. Farwell has pleaded not guilty, and his defense maintains that Birchmore died by suicide.

William Farwell's testimony followed testimony Monday from the medical examiner who performed Birchmore's original autopsy. The examiner initially classified the death as a suicide by hanging but later changed the cause to asphyxia and the manner of death to undetermined after receiving additional information. She testified that she was not pressured to make the change.

The trial has already included extensive testimony about Birchmore's relationships, electronic communications and the circumstances surrounding her death. William Farwell is expected to continue testifying as prosecutors move closer to completing their case.

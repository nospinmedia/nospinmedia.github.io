🚨 Three teens shot near Lawrence High School after dismissalPolice search for person of interest as investigation expands 👇

EducationSeptember 1, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 1, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Three teenage boys were shot near Lawrence High School shortly after dismissal on August 31, 2026, prompting a large police response and a multi-agency investigation. The shooting occurred shortly after 3 p.m. near the intersection of Exeter Street and East Daulton Street, close to Lawrence Housing Authority property. Although the gunfire happened off school grounds, it occurred as students, staff, and families were leaving the campus.

Emergency responders treated the three victims at the scene before transporting them to area hospitals. Authorities said all three suffered gunshot wounds but have not publicly released additional information about their conditions. Investigators believe the shooting may have followed an altercation and said there is currently no indication that the attack was random or specifically targeted at the school.

The Lawrence Police Department, assisted by the Massachusetts State Police, FBI, and the Essex County Sheriff's Office K-9 Unit, spent hours processing evidence and searching for a person of interest. Police have not announced any arrests. Lawrence Public Schools Superintendent Ralph Carrero said the district is working closely with law enforcement and will provide additional security and support for students and staff following the incident.

The investigation remains active as detectives review surveillance footage, interview witnesses, and gather forensic evidence. Authorities urged anyone with information about the shooting to contact investigators. Police also asked residents in the area to check home or business security cameras that may have captured activity before or after the gunfire.

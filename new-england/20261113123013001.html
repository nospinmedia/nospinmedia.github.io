🚨 New Bedford standoff ends with suspect found dead3-year-old left in SUV unharmed after man allegedly fired at police 👇

HealthNovember 13, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 13, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A 39-year-old New Bedford man was found dead after authorities say he fired at police during a traffic stop and later from inside his home, triggering a multi-agency SWAT response Wednesday night. A 3-year-old relative who had been left inside the man's vehicle was found unharmed.

Bristol County District Attorney Thomas M. Quinn III identified the man as Derek Rego, who had recently moved to New Bedford from Fall River. The incident began shortly after 7 p.m. November 11 when a New Bedford police detective attempted to stop Rego's red Jeep Cherokee near South First Street.

Authorities say Rego did not stop and eventually came to a halt near Blackmer and South Second streets, where he allegedly fired at the detective. The detective returned fire. Rego then ran into his residence at 153 Blackmer Street, leaving the child inside the Jeep.

Police established a perimeter, evacuated nearby residents and brought in negotiators and the Southeastern Massachusetts Law Enforcement Council Regional SWAT team. Investigators say Rego later fired additional rounds at officers from an upper floor of the residence, and a Massachusetts State Police trooper returned fire.

After officers heard another gunshot inside the home, a tactical drone was sent into the building and located Rego dead in a stairwell. Authorities said a firearm was found resting on his abdomen and that he appeared to have suffered a self-inflicted gunshot wound to the head. A formal determination remains pending with the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner.

No officers, other public safety personnel or bystanders were injured. Massachusetts State Police detectives assigned to the Bristol County District Attorney's Office and New Bedford police are continuing to investigate the incident and the exchanges of gunfire.

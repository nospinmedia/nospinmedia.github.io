🚨 Boston councilor seeks hate-crime probe after elderly woman attackedPolice charged suspect after unprovoked South End shove 👇

HealthSeptember 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 5, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Boston City Councilor Ed Flynn is asking authorities to investigate whether racial bias motivated an apparently unprovoked assault on an elderly Chinese woman in Boston's South End. The woman, who is in her 80s, was crossing West Canton Street near Tremont Street shortly before 9 a.m. Wednesday when another woman allegedly shoved her to the pavement, according to a Boston police report and surveillance video of the incident.

Police said the video showed the suspect walk in front of the victim, stop and then shove her to the ground for no apparent reason before leaving. Passersby helped the woman to her feet. She reported knee pain and a cut to her thumb and was taken to Tufts Medical Center for evaluation. Officers later located a woman matching the suspect's description and identified her as Kaitlyn Ann Quick, 38, who police said was homeless.

Quick was arrested on two outstanding warrants and charged with assault and battery on a person over 60 or disabled with injury. She pleaded not guilty during her arraignment Thursday in Boston Municipal Court, was ordered held on $1,500 bail and is scheduled to return to court on September 29.

Police initially marked the incident as not a suspected hate crime, and authorities have not publicly identified evidence establishing a racial motive. Flynn nevertheless asked the Boston Police Department's civil rights unit to investigate whether bias played a role. In a letter to Mayor Michelle Wu and Police Commissioner Michael Cox, Flynn also requested that the Boston Human Rights Commission examine the assault as a possible hate crime and civil rights violation.

Flynn, whose district includes the South End and Chinatown, cited broader concerns about anti-Asian racism while emphasizing the need for investigators to determine the motive in this particular case.

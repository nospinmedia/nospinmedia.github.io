🎰 Encore Boston Harbor Workers Set Friday Strike DeadlineMore Than 1,300 Employees Could Walk Off Job 👇

EconomySeptember 2, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 2, 2026 at 9:42 AM ET

More than 1,300 unionized employees at Encore Boston Harbor in Everett, Massachusetts, say they will begin a strike at 7 a.m. on Friday, Sept. 4, unless Wynn Resorts reaches a new labor agreement before the deadline. The workers, represented by UNITE HERE Local 26 and Teamsters Local 25, have continued working since their contracts expired at the end of Aug. 31, but negotiations have not yet produced a new deal.

The unions voted overwhelmingly in August to authorize a strike after months of bargaining. The authorization passed by a 1,006-53 margin, reflecting strong support among employees across multiple departments, including housekeeping, food service, bartending, warehouse operations, slot attendants and valet services. Union leaders are seeking higher wages, continued employer-funded health insurance and increased pension contributions, arguing that compensation should better reflect the casino's financial performance.

According to union officials, Encore Boston Harbor generated approximately $847 million in revenue last year, making it the highest-grossing commercial casino in Massachusetts. Wynn Resorts has said it remains committed to reaching a fair agreement and has described its proposals as financially responsible while negotiations continue. Bargaining is expected to continue ahead of Friday's deadline in hopes of avoiding a work stoppage.

If no agreement is reached, a strike could affect hotel, restaurant and casino operations at one of New England's largest entertainment destinations heading into the Labor Day weekend period. Union leaders have also said Teamsters members who deliver goods to the property are expected to honor any active picket line, potentially affecting deliveries if a strike begins. Whether a walkout occurs will depend on the outcome of negotiations over the next two days.

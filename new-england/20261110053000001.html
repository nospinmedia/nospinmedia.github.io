🦠 Vermont confirms multiple measles cases in Orleans CountyHospital begins screening visitors as health officials investigate 👇

HealthNovember 10, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 10, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

The Vermont Department of Health has confirmed a small number of connected measles cases in Orleans County, prompting an investigation, increased monitoring by health care providers and new screening precautions at a northern Vermont hospital.

State laboratory testing confirmed the cases on Monday, November 9. Officials have not disclosed the exact number of people infected, citing patient privacy, but WCAX reported that multiple positive tests were confirmed. The Health Department says the current risk to the general Vermont public remains low.

North Country Hospital in Newport has begun screening visitors for possible measles, including temperature checks and questions about symptoms. The hospital said the screening will continue for the foreseeable future.

State health officials are providing testing, vaccinations and guidance to people involved in the outbreak while epidemiologists investigate potential exposures and contacts. Vermont also issued guidance to health care providers asking them to watch for possible measles infections and immediately report suspected cases, even before laboratory confirmation.

Measles is highly contagious and typically begins with fever, cough, runny nose and red or watery eyes before a rash develops. The Health Department is urging Vermonters to check that they and their families are current on the measles, mumps and rubella, or MMR, vaccine. Anyone uncertain about their vaccination history is advised to contact their health care provider and request their records.

The Orleans County cases come during a sharp national increase in measles. Vermont previously confirmed one measles case in February 2026, following two cases in 2025 and two in 2024. Before that, the state recorded only two cases during the previous decade, in 2011 and 2018.

Health officials have not announced specific public exposure locations connected to the new Orleans County cases and say the investigation remains ongoing.

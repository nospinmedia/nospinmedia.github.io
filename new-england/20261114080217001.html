🚨 Malden man charged with murder in sister's deathProsecutors say he cleaned the scene and tried to make killing appear a suicide 👇

LegalNovember 14, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 14, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A 43-year-old Malden, Massachusetts, man has been ordered held without bail after prosecutors accused him of fatally stabbing his sister and attempting to make her death appear to be a suicide.

Jean Eddy Noel was charged with murder in the death of his 38-year-old sister, Kenia Noel, following an investigation at their home on Lombard Court. According to Middlesex District Attorney Marian Ryan and Malden Police Chief Marc Gatcomb, Noel called 911 on Thursday, November 12, and told authorities that his sister had died by suicide.

When officers arrived, they found Kenia Noel dead in a bedroom with severe trauma to her neck. Investigators reported finding three knives in the room. One had been placed in the victim's hand, another covered in blood was on a nearby table, and a third was found on the floor, according to prosecutors.

Authorities allege Jean Eddy Noel left the residence after his sister's death to shower and that investigators found indications someone had attempted to clean portions of the home before police arrived. Investigators also observed lacerations on Noel's hands and what appeared to be blood inside his vehicle.

Noel was arraigned Friday in Malden District Court on a murder charge. A judge ordered him held without bail, and his next court appearance is scheduled for January 5, 2027.

The allegations against Noel have not been proven in court, and the investigation remains ongoing. Authorities have not publicly identified a motive for the killing.

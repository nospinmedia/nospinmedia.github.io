🚨 20-Year-Old Seriously Injured in Lewiston StabbingPolice Arrest 18-Year-Old After Fight Near Main Street Dunkin' 👇

HealthOctober 19, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 19, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A 20-year-old man was seriously injured after being stabbed in the back and struck with brass knuckles during a fight in Lewiston, Maine, Saturday afternoon, according to police.

Lewiston police responded at approximately 3:30 p.m. Saturday, October 17, to reports of a fight near the Dunkin' at 420 Main Street. Officers found the victim with a single stab wound to his back and additional injuries that police said appeared to have been caused by brass knuckles.

Officers provided aid at the scene before the man was transported to Central Maine Medical Center. Police described his injuries as serious but not life-threatening. Authorities have not publicly identified the victim.

Police arrested 18-year-old Alexander Keith of Lewiston at the scene in connection with the attack. Keith was charged with aggravated assault and criminal threatening and taken to the Androscoggin County Jail.

Authorities said Keith was not eligible for bail because he was already on a deferred disposition at the time of his arrest. The new charges are allegations, and Keith has not been convicted in connection with Saturday's incident.

Lewiston Police Department spokesperson Derek St. Laurent said investigators believe Keith and the victim knew each other and that the attack was not random. Police have not publicly detailed what led to the confrontation or described the relationship between the two men.

The investigation remains ongoing, and authorities have not announced whether additional charges could be filed. No other injuries were reported in the initial accounts of the incident.

The stabbing occurred during the afternoon near a business on one of Lewiston's major roads, but police have indicated that investigators do not believe there is a broader threat to the public because the people involved were known to each other.

🔎 Missing Massachusetts lawyer found dead after weekend searchAuthorities say no foul play suspected in death of 54-year-old Melissa Dragon 👇

HealthNovember 16, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 16, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A 54-year-old Massachusetts lawyer who disappeared Friday and prompted a public search on the North Shore has been found dead, according to authorities.

Melissa Dragon of Swampscott was reported missing on Friday, November 13. The Essex County District Attorney's Office and Swampscott police announced Sunday that she had been located deceased.

Dragon was the founding partner and chief litigator of Family Law Practitioners, P.C. in Woburn, where her work focused on family law and divorce litigation. She had been admitted to the Massachusetts bar since 1998.

Dragon was last seen at approximately 10:30 a.m. Friday driving west on Lynnfield Street near Den Quarry Road in neighboring Lynn. Police had asked the public for help locating both Dragon and her blue 2015 Lexus RX 350.

Authorities have not disclosed where Dragon or her vehicle was ultimately found or provided details about the circumstances surrounding her death.

The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner is investigating the official cause and manner of death. Authorities said foul play is not suspected at this time.

Essex County District Attorney Paul Tucker and Swampscott Police Chief Ruben Quesada thanked law enforcement agencies and members of the community who assisted with efforts to locate Dragon.

Officials said they do not expect to release additional information about the case out of respect for Dragon's family and their privacy.

Dragon's disappearance prompted a search across the North Shore after police released information about her last known location and vehicle. Her death ends that search, while the official cause and manner remain pending the medical examiner's investigation.

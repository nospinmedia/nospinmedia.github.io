🏈 Drake Maye Scores 5 Touchdowns as Patriots Crush Raiders 45–17New England Piles Up 536 Yards in Dominant Home Victory 👇

New EnglandOctober 12, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 12, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

The New England Patriots delivered one of their most impressive offensive performances in recent years Sunday, October 11, defeating the Las Vegas Raiders 45–17 at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Massachusetts. Quarterback Drake Maye accounted for five touchdowns as New England improved to 3–2 and moved above .500 for the first time this season.

Maye completed 26 of 34 passes for 374 yards, throwing four touchdown passes without an interception. He also scored on a three-yard run in the third quarter, finishing with five total touchdowns and a passer rating of 150.9.

Wide receiver DeMario Douglas caught two touchdown passes, while Kyle Williams and tight end Hunter Henry each added a touchdown reception. Running back Rhamondre Stevenson contributed another score on a five-yard run.

The Patriots finished with 536 total offensive yards and 29 first downs, their highest totals in both categories this season. The 45 points represented New England's highest scoring output since its 50–10 victory over Jacksonville in January 2022.

New England's defense also played an important role, limiting the Raiders to just 38 rushing yards on 18 attempts. Las Vegas entered the game averaging nearly 118 rushing yards per contest, making the defensive performance particularly notable.

The Patriots established control early, building a 21–7 halftime lead before extending their advantage in the second half. Las Vegas quarterback Kirk Cousins completed 19 of 28 passes for 243 yards and one touchdown, but the Raiders struggled to keep pace with New England's offense.

The victory also ended a three-game losing streak against the Raiders and gave New England its first 2–0 start at Gillette Stadium since 2020.

There were concerns despite the convincing win. Running back TreVeyon Henderson left with an ankle injury, while starting left tackle Will Campbell suffered a knee injury. Neither returned to the game, leaving their availability for upcoming contests uncertain.

For Maye, the performance marked his second consecutive game with at least three touchdown passes, a feat no Patriots quarterback had accomplished since Tom Brady in 2018.

New England will look to build on the victory when it hosts the New York Jets on October 18.

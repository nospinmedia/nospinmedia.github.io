🚨 Arkansas man charged with threatening two Massachusetts lawmakersVoicemails referenced Oklahoma City bombing and warned officials to “duck” 👇

EconomyOctober 8, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 8, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Federal authorities arrested David Czerniak, 62, of Hatfield, Arkansas, after a Boston grand jury indicted him on two counts of interstate transmission of threatening communications involving two Massachusetts members of Congress. Prosecutors have not publicly identified the lawmakers, but the indictment describes both as Democrats and says the alleged messages were left at their Massachusetts offices.

The first voicemail was allegedly left on February 7, 2025. According to the indictment, Czerniak complained about immigration policy and government spending before telling the lawmaker that the official and family members would “pay the consequences” if an undocumented immigrant harmed his family or friends. He allegedly described his response as “an eye for an eye.”

A second voicemail allegedly left for another Massachusetts lawmaker on December 6, 2025 was more explicit. Prosecutors say Czerniak warned Democrats to “watch your backs,” referenced Timothy McVeigh, who carried out the 1995 Oklahoma City bombing, and mentioned the assassination of Charlie Kirk. The message ended with a warning that the next time the lawmakers stood at a podium, they should “duck.”

Czerniak was arrested in Arkansas on October 7 and made an initial appearance in federal court in the Western District of Arkansas. He is expected to appear in federal court in Boston at a later date. Each charge carries a maximum sentence of five years in prison, up to three years of supervised release and a fine of up to $250,000. The charges are allegations, and Czerniak is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.

The investigation involved the FBI, U.S. Capitol Police, Massachusetts State Police and the Polk County Sheriff's Office in Arkansas.

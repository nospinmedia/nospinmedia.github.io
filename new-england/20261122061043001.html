🌧️ Coastal Storm Targets New England With Heavy Rain And Gusty WindsMassachusetts, New Hampshire And Maine Face Sunday Night Downpours Before Thanksgiving 👇

EnvironmentNovember 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 22, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A coastal storm will bring widespread rain and gusty winds across Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Maine Sunday into Monday, creating the potential for localized flooding and difficult travel as the Thanksgiving holiday week begins.

The Weather Prediction Center includes portions of southern New England in a Slight Risk for excessive rainfall, with widespread totals around 1 to 2 inches and locally higher amounts possible. Along the corridor of heaviest precipitation, forecasts indicate a 60% to 80% or greater chance of rainfall rates exceeding 1 inch per hour. The heaviest rain is expected Sunday evening into early Monday.

In Massachusetts, rain will intensify Sunday afternoon and evening before tapering Monday. Coastal areas will also face strengthening winds. The National Weather Service Boston/Norton has issued a Gale Watch for waters south and east of Massachusetts from Sunday night through Monday morning, with rough seas potentially reaching around 15 feet.

The storm will track toward the Gulf of Maine overnight, spreading moderate to heavy rain across New Hampshire and Maine. Southeastern New Hampshire and much of coastal and interior Maine have a strong chance of receiving at least 1 inch of rain, with higher totals possible in parts of Maine.

Conditions should improve Monday, followed by quieter weather Tuesday into Wednesday. Attention will then shift to another potentially more significant system approaching Wednesday night into Thanksgiving Day.

That second storm remains uncertain, but current guidance shows the potential for an impactful Nor'easter with heavy precipitation and strong coastal winds. NWS Boston/Norton says there is a low risk of storm-force gusts reaching 55 knots, while offshore seas could potentially build to around 24 feet. Some interior areas could initially see frozen precipitation depending on the storm's eventual track.

For Sunday night's storm, the main concerns are heavy rain, localized flooding, gusty coastal winds and hazardous marine conditions. The Thanksgiving system could pose a broader travel threat if stronger forecasts materialize.

How the Thanksgiving storm develops will be especially important for holiday travel across New England.

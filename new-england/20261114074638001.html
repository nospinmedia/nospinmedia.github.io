🚢 Connecticut company's river cruise ship struck by tanker in New OrleansAll aboard survive as Coast Guard investigates collision and closes river 👇

HealthNovember 14, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 14, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A river cruise ship operated by Connecticut-based American Cruise Lines capsized on the Mississippi River in New Orleans after being struck by a tanker Friday, sending people into the water and triggering a major rescue operation. Officials later confirmed that everyone aboard survived and was accounted for.

The American Heritage was docked near the Port of New Orleans when the 520-foot Atlantic Asphalt, a Marshall Islands-flagged tanker, struck it at approximately 12:30 p.m. Friday, November 13. About 129 passengers, along with crew members, were aboard the riverboat. A local river pilot was aboard the tanker at the time of the collision.

The impact caused the American Heritage to list heavily and capsize. New Orleans Mayor Helena Moreno said 32 passengers were taken to hospitals, with no critical injuries reported. Two Port of New Orleans Harbor Police officers were also hospitalized after participating in rescue efforts and were reported in good condition.

Moreno said preliminary information indicated the tanker may have experienced "some sort of loss of steering," but investigators have not established the cause. The U.S. Coast Guard closed a three-mile section of the Mississippi River between mile markers 93 and 96 while crews investigated the collision and coordinated salvage operations. Sector New Orleans Commander Capt. Melanie Burnham said officials were evaluating how to reopen the waterway, potentially beginning with controlled one-way vessel traffic.

American Cruise Lines, based in Guilford, Connecticut, said its passengers and crew were evacuated and that it was cooperating with authorities.

Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry traveled to New Orleans for an emergency briefing and said he had spoken with Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin and President Trump about the federal response.

🚢 Electric Boat Begins Crystal Mall TransformationFormer Connecticut Shopping Center to Become Campus for 5,000 Workers 👇

EducationAugust 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 22, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

General Dynamics Electric Boat officially broke ground on August 21 to convert the former Crystal Mall in Waterford, Connecticut, into an 814,000-square-foot office, engineering, and workforce training campus. The redevelopment marks the next phase in repurposing the long-vacant shopping mall into a major support center for the nation's submarine industrial base. Company officials expect the campus to open in stages, with full operations anticipated by mid-2027.

When completed, the facility is expected to accommodate between 4,000 and 5,000 employees, making Electric Boat the town's largest employer. The campus will consolidate a wide range of operations, including engineering, information technology, procurement, finance, contracts, and human resources. The project also includes laboratories for testing shipboard systems and collaborative workspaces designed to support the company's growing submarine production programs.

A centerpiece of the redevelopment is the Shipbuilding Advanced Institute for Learning (SAIL), which will significantly expand workforce training capacity. Plans call for 73 welding booths, 40 classrooms, submarine training mockups, and virtual reality instruction areas capable of training up to 400 workers per day across multiple shifts. Electric Boat President Mark Rayha said the expanded training center is essential to meeting hiring demands and increasing production of the Virginia-class attack submarine and Columbia-class ballistic missile submarine programs for the U.S. Navy.

Federal, state, and local officials attending the groundbreaking said the project represents one of southeastern Connecticut's largest economic development initiatives in recent years. Electric Boat has said it remains on track to hire approximately 8,000 new employees across Connecticut and Rhode Island by the end of 2026, with portions of the new Waterford campus expected to begin supporting workforce training before the end of this year.

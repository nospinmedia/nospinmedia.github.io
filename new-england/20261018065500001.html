🚨 Hateful Graffiti Found Across Three Manchester NeighborhoodsPolice Investigate 15 Incidents Involving Racist, Antisemitic and Homophobic Slurs 👇

EconomyOctober 18, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 18, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Manchester police are investigating 15 incidents of vandalism after residents in three areas of the city awoke Saturday to find racist, antisemitic and homophobic graffiti spray-painted on homes, vehicles and other property.

Police said the vandalism occurred overnight in the areas of Candia Road, Gabrielle Street and South Beech Street. Investigators currently believe the incidents were random, with no indication that particular residents or properties were deliberately selected because of their identity.

Surveillance video obtained by a neighborhood resident showed people running down a street shortly after 2:30 a.m. The footage is among the information available as police investigate who was responsible for the overnight vandalism.

Manchester Police Chief Peter Marr condemned the vandalism, saying acts involving antisemitic, racist and homophobic slurs are unacceptable and have no place in Manchester. He said the department is actively investigating and intends to hold those responsible accountable.

Neighbors spent part of Saturday helping one another clean graffiti from affected properties. Police have not announced any arrests or identified suspects in connection with the 15 incidents.

Police are asking anyone who witnessed suspicious activity or has surveillance footage from the affected neighborhoods to contact the Manchester Police Department at 603-668-8711.

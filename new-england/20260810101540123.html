🎓 Rhode Island Awards $1.6 Million For Afterschool LearningFifth Learn365RI grant round expands student programs statewide 👇

EducationAugust 10, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 10, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Governor Dan McKee and Education Commissioner Angélica Infante-Green announced $1.629 million in Learn365RI grants on August 7, 2026, providing new funding for afterschool, weekend, and summer learning programs across Rhode Island. The fifth round of municipal grants supports partnerships among school districts, local governments, libraries, nonprofit organizations, and community groups to expand educational opportunities outside the traditional school day.

The grants are administered by the Rhode Island Department of Education (RIDE) and are intended to help communities strengthen academic achievement, improve student well-being, and increase access to enrichment opportunities throughout the year. Programs supported through Learn365RI include tutoring, STEM education, literacy initiatives, career exploration, arts programming, and other activities designed to keep students engaged beyond regular classroom hours.

State officials said the latest funding continues Rhode Island's broader effort to provide year-round learning opportunities while helping communities address learning gaps that emerged during recent years. The initiative places particular emphasis on expanding access for underserved students through collaborations between schools and local organizations that can offer additional educational and mentoring resources.

Since its launch, Learn365RI has funded multiple rounds of competitive grants aimed at building sustainable out-of-school learning networks across the state. Officials said participating municipalities are expected to use the latest funding to expand existing programs and develop new partnerships that give students additional opportunities for academic support, enrichment, and workforce readiness.

🏛️ N.H. man charged after Election Day assault at polling siteVideo shows 70-year-old striking Turning Point volunteer during argument 👇

EducationNovember 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 5, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A 70-year-old Laconia man has been charged after police say he struck an 18-year-old Turning Point volunteer during an argument outside a New Hampshire polling location on Election Day.

Stephen Redic was charged Wednesday with misdemeanor simple assault and violation-level disorderly conduct following the confrontation at Woodland Heights Elementary School, Laconia's Ward 5 polling location.

Police said the volunteer was working at a Turning Point informational booth outside the polls when Redic approached after voting and wanted to read a poem about rich people. The two began debating, according to the volunteer's account to police, and Redic became increasingly agitated, raised his voice and eventually struck the volunteer across the face with the back of his right hand.

Video of the confrontation circulated widely online and appears to show Redic arguing with the volunteer, making an obscene gesture and then backhanding him across the face before walking away. Police said investigators reviewed video evidence and spoke with several witnesses before identifying Redic.

An arrest warrant was prepared Tuesday night. Police went to Redic's home Wednesday morning and, after finding he was not there, said he turned himself in a short time later. He was released on a promise to appear in court and is scheduled for arraignment in Laconia District Court on December 10.

New Hampshire Secretary of State David Scanlan, who had visited the polling location shortly before the incident, said political disagreements do not justify assaults at polling places. He also said Election Day was otherwise conducted smoothly across the state.

The charges remain allegations unless proven in court.

🎓 Saint Anselm expands free tuition program to VermontQualifying students from NH, Maine and Vermont can now attend tuition-free 👇

EducationSeptember 27, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 27, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Saint Anselm College has expanded its free-tuition program to Vermont, giving qualifying students from all three northern New England states an opportunity to attend the private New Hampshire college without paying undergraduate tuition.

The Manchester-area college announced that students from Vermont and Maine are now included in its Anselmian Community Commitment, which originally launched for New Hampshire students. To qualify, incoming first-year students must have a family income of $100,000 or less, earn at least a 3.25 high school GPA, complete the FAFSA and plan to live on campus.

The program covers tuition rather than the entire cost of attending college. Students remain responsible for expenses including housing, food, fees, books and personal costs. NHPR reports that attending Saint Anselm can cost as much as about $70,000 annually, with qualifying students under the program paying roughly $20,000 for room, board and fees instead.

Eligibility also includes residency requirements. The student and parent contributors must have their permanent and primary residence in New Hampshire, Maine or Vermont and must have been legal residents of one of those states for at least one year before the student enrolls.

The commitment can continue for four years. Students must remain full-time residential students, maintain at least a 2.5 cumulative GPA, meet satisfactory academic progress requirements and continue to have family income below $100,000.

Saint Anselm says the program has already attracted significant interest in New Hampshire. According to figures provided to NHPR, 148 New Hampshire applicants qualified last year and 55 enrolled. This year, 75 qualified and 54 enrolled. NHPR reports the college has waived tuition for 109 New Hampshire students since the program began.

College President Mike Lewis said extending the commitment beyond New Hampshire is intended to make a Saint Anselm education accessible to more families across the region as higher-education costs remain a concern.

The expansion means eligible families in New Hampshire, Maine and Vermont now have access to the same tuition guarantee at Saint Anselm, although housing and other college expenses remain outside the waiver.

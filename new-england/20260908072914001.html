💻 Cyber incident shuts down Springfield Public SchoolsStudents and staff told to stay off district networks 👇

EducationSeptember 8, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 8, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Springfield Public Schools canceled classes and after-school activities Tuesday after a cyber incident disrupted computer systems needed for essential school operations, forcing one of Massachusetts' largest school districts into an emergency shutdown.

Superintendent Dr. Sonia Dinnall announced the closure Monday and said the exact scope and size of the incident remain under investigation. All district employees have been instructed to stay off Springfield Public Schools network systems until further notice, while students with district-issued laptops have been told not to use them.

District officials have not publicly identified the cause of the incident or said whether it involved ransomware, data theft or another form of cyberattack. They also have not announced whether personal information belonging to students or employees was compromised.

The disruption is affecting systems the district considers necessary for normal school operations. Dinnall said closing schools gives administrators time to address the incident while developing backup procedures for technology-dependent functions such as attendance reporting. The district's Central Office remains open Tuesday, although officials warned that some services, including phone lines, may be affected.

Dinnall said safety was the primary reason for canceling classes, explaining that the district was unwilling to risk operating schools if disrupted systems could interfere with normal procedures.

The incident comes after other Massachusetts school systems have experienced significant cybersecurity problems. Fall River Public Schools disclosed a ransomware attack earlier this year, while school districts including Nantucket and Swansea have previously canceled classes following cyberattacks.

For now, Springfield officials have not announced when affected systems will be fully restored or whether schools will reopen Wednesday. The district says its investigation and recovery work are continuing while staff and students remain disconnected from the network.

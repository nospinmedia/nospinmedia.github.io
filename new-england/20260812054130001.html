⚖️ Gov. Healey Signs Massachusetts Abortion LawState replaces 24-week statutory limits with physician judgment 👇

HealthAugust 12, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 12, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Massachusetts Gov. Maura Healey has signed legislation changing how abortions performed after 24 weeks of pregnancy are regulated, replacing the state's previous list of statutory medical exceptions with a standard based on the professional judgment of the treating physician. Supporters said the change gives physicians greater flexibility to respond to complex medical situations, while opponents argued it significantly expands access to later-term abortions.

Under the previous law, abortions after 24 weeks were generally permitted only when necessary to preserve the life or physical or mental health of the pregnant patient or in cases involving specified severe fetal diagnoses. The new law removes those specific statutory conditions and instead allows physicians to determine whether an abortion is appropriate based on their professional medical judgment. The legislation was approved by the Democratic-controlled Legislature before being signed by Healey.

Supporters, including physicians, hospitals, and reproductive rights organizations, said the previous law could delay or prevent care for patients facing serious pregnancy complications that did not clearly fit the existing legal categories, sometimes forcing families to seek treatment outside Massachusetts. Opponents argued the legislation removes important legal safeguards and gives physicians broad discretion without a defined statutory gestational limit.

The change makes Massachusetts one of a small number of states that do not set a statutory gestational limit for abortion in state law after viability, instead relying on physician judgment and existing medical standards of care. The new policy takes effect immediately and reflects the state's continued expansion of abortion protections following the U.S. Supreme Court's 2022 decision overturning Roe v. Wade.

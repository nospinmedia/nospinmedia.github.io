💧 Kittery Imposes Mandatory Water RestrictionsLevel 3 conservation rules limit outdoor watering as drought persists 👇

EnvironmentAugust 18, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 18, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

The Kittery Water District (KWD) has implemented mandatory Level 3 Water Conservation restrictions effective August 17, 2026, citing prolonged dry weather and declining reservoir levels across southern Maine. Officials said the measures are intended to reduce water demand and help preserve the community's drinking water supply while dry conditions continue.

Under the mandatory restrictions, outdoor lawn and landscape watering is limited to two designated days per week between 8:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. Properties with odd-numbered addresses may water only on Mondays and Thursdays, while even-numbered addresses are limited to Tuesdays and Fridays. No outdoor watering is permitted on Saturdays or Sundays. Residents are also encouraged to minimize other non-essential outdoor water use whenever possible.

The water district has established an escalating enforcement policy for violations. A first violation will result in a written warning, a second violation carries a $100 fine, and a third violation may result in a $500 fine along with the possibility of water service being disconnected. Officials said enforcement is intended to ensure the restrictions are followed fairly and to protect available water supplies for essential household and emergency needs.

KWD said it will continue monitoring reservoir conditions and weather forecasts before determining when restrictions can be eased or lifted. Similar conservation measures have been adopted by several New England water systems during periods of below-average rainfall, as utilities work to balance water demand with available supplies.

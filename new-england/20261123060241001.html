🤖 Logan Airport tests autonomous wheelchairs and cleaning robotsMassport hopes to expand passenger mobility technology in 2027 👇

New EnglandNovember 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 23, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

The Massachusetts Port Authority is testing autonomous wheelchairs and robotic cleaners at Boston Logan International Airport, exploring whether the technology can improve passenger mobility and airport cleanliness.

Massport CEO Rich Davey told the authority's board on November 19 that a demonstration of autonomous wheelchairs had begun about two weeks earlier. Davey emphasized that the technology is intended to supplement, not replace, existing wheelchair attendants.

Massport says the experiment partly addresses a physical limitation at Logan. While some large airports use golf carts to transport passengers who need assistance, Davey said most of Logan's corridors are too narrow for them. Autonomous wheelchairs could provide another way for passengers to travel through the terminals without requiring the space needed for larger carts.

The authority plans to issue a request for information inviting additional companies to test autonomous wheelchairs, with the goal of potentially deploying more units at Logan in 2027. Airports including Miami International and Los Angeles International already use autonomous wheelchairs.

Massport has not publicly identified the vendor supplying Logan's current wheelchair demonstration, how many chairs are being tested, which terminals they are operating in or how passengers access them. Details about their operating procedures and safety systems also have not been released in the reporting reviewed.

Automation is also being tested for cleaning. Massport is working with its existing cleaning contractor, ABM, to operate four CenoBots autonomous cleaning robots in pre-security areas across all Logan terminals. The machines are being evaluated as another tool for maintaining airport facilities.

The trials remain demonstrations rather than a full deployment, and Massport has not announced results or costs for either program.

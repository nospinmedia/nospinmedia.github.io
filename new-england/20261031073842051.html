🚨 Man dies after Boston police shooting in BrightonPolice say he charged officers with knife after 20-minute standoff 👇

Police station exteriorCredit: AI-generated illustration (No Spin Media)
HealthOctober 31, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 31, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A man has died after a Boston police officer shot him in Brighton during what authorities described as a mental health crisis that escalated after officers spent about 20 minutes trying to de-escalate the situation.

Police responded to Selkirk Road near Cleveland Circle shortly before 7 a.m. Friday after receiving a report of a man armed with what Boston Police Commissioner Michael Cox described as a "very large knife" and threatening to harm himself. Officers were told before arriving that the man was experiencing a mental health crisis.

According to Cox, officers engaged with the man for approximately 20 minutes while additional police and Boston EMS personnel staged nearby. Police said the man initially directed the knife toward himself but eventually moved toward officers while still armed.

Cox said officers retreated before the man charged them on an outside porch and chased an officer. One officer then fired a single shot, striking the man in the stomach. Police and EMS personnel immediately provided medical aid before he was transported to a hospital in critical condition. Authorities later confirmed that he died from his injuries. His identity had not been publicly released as of Friday.

The officer who fired the shot has been with the Boston Police Department for a little more than two years, according to Cox. Two officers involved in the confrontation were also taken to a hospital for evaluation. Officials said neither suffered serious physical injuries.

Cox acknowledged that officers at the scene had access to less-lethal equipment, but said determining what equipment was available and how officers responded will be part of the investigation.

Suffolk County District Attorney Kevin Hayden's office is investigating the shooting and will review body-camera footage and other evidence to determine whether the use of deadly force was legally justified.

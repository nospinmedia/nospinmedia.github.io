🔎 Human remains found along power line corridor in BowAutopsy extended into Monday as investigators seek answers 👇

HealthNovember 2, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 2, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

An autopsy is continuing Monday after a hunter discovered human remains in heavy brush along a power line corridor in Bow, New Hampshire, prompting an investigation by local and state authorities.

Bow police responded shortly before 7:30 a.m. Saturday to a 911 call from a hunter who reported finding what appeared to be human remains off South Bow Road. First responders arrived and confirmed that the remains were those of a deceased person.

Authorities have not publicly identified the person or released an age or gender. The New Hampshire Attorney General's Office said an autopsy originally scheduled for Sunday at the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner in Concord was extended into Monday. Officials have not said when the results will be released.

The Bow Police Department is leading the investigation with assistance from New Hampshire State Police and the Attorney General's Office. Police said the investigation remains in its early stages and that, based on information currently available, there is no known threat to the public.

Investigators are asking for help from people who may have been in the area before the remains were discovered. Authorities specifically want to hear from anyone who walked the trails or other areas near the power lines stretching from South Bow Road in Bow to Twist Hill Road in Dunbarton during the previous week.

Officials have not announced a suspected cause or manner of death, and authorities have not publicly connected the remains to a missing-person case. Investigators said additional information is expected after the medical examiner's work is completed.

The discovery has brought multiple law enforcement agencies to an area of trails and utility corridors between Bow and neighboring Dunbarton as investigators work to determine who the person was and how the death occurred.

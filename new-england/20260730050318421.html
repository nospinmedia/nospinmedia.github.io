🚒 Vacationing Connecticut Firefighters Rescue Sleeping Brothers From Burning Maine Home 👇

New EnglandJuly 30, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published July 30, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Two off-duty Connecticut firefighters are being credited with helping rescue two men from a burning home while vacationing in Scarborough, Maine. Brandon Seney and Dylan Vincent, members of the East Putnam Fire Department, were driving toward Old Orchard Beach on July 28 when they noticed heavy smoke coming from a home on Sea Meadows Lane.

After stopping to investigate, the firefighters found flames spreading up the exterior of the two-and-a-half-story house toward the roof. Without their turnout gear or self-contained breathing apparatus, they entered the home and discovered two brothers asleep on the first floor who were unaware of the fire. The firefighters woke the men and helped them safely evacuate before conditions worsened.

After getting the occupants outside, Seney and Vincent used a garden hose and a portable fire extinguisher in an effort to slow the fire's spread until crews from the Scarborough Fire Department arrived. Local firefighters then extinguished the blaze and prevented it from spreading further.

Scarborough fire officials praised the quick actions of the visiting firefighters, saying their training and rapid response helped alert the occupants before the fire could grow more dangerous. Officials said the incident serves as a reminder of how quickly residential fires can spread and the importance of early detection and prompt evacuation.

The East Putnam Fire Department also recognized Seney and Vincent for their actions, noting that their professionalism and instincts carried over even while they were off duty and away from home.

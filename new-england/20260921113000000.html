🚗 Grandmother and two grandchildren found dead in submerged carCellphone tracking led police to vehicle in Roque Bluffs 👇

HealthSeptember 21, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 21, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Maine State Police are investigating after 61-year-old Sharon Bukulmez of Mount Desert and her two young grandchildren, 5-year-old Kinsley Grubb and 3-year-old Sawyer Grubb, both of Bar Harbor, were found dead inside a submerged vehicle in Roque Bluffs on Sunday, September 20.

The three were last seen on Saturday, September 19, leaving a family function in Jonesboro and heading toward Machias. When they did not arrive as expected, authorities began searching for them.

The investigation led Bar Harbor Police to track Bukulmez's cellphone location. At approximately 7 a.m. Sunday, officers traced the phone to the area of the Schoppee Point Road boat ramp at Pond Cove in Roque Bluffs, in Washington County. The vehicle was subsequently located underwater near the boat ramp, and divers recovered the three victims.

State and local authorities processed the scene and removed the vehicle for further examination as investigators began working to determine how it entered the water. The Maine State Police Major Crimes Unit is involved in the investigation, along with local law enforcement.

The victims' bodies were taken to the Office of Chief Medical Examiner in Augusta for autopsies to determine their official causes and manners of death. Authorities had not announced a final determination about how the vehicle ended up submerged or what caused the deaths in the reporting reviewed for this story.

Investigators are continuing to reconstruct the family's movements between the time they left Jonesboro Saturday night and the discovery of the vehicle Sunday morning.

🏁 NASCAR Moves Up New Hampshire Race Start Due to WeatherDollar Tree 301 Will Begin One Hour Earlier Sunday 👇

EconomyAugust 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 22, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Officials at New Hampshire Motor Speedway and NASCAR have moved Sunday's Dollar Tree 301 start time up by one hour because of a forecast for afternoon and evening storms. The NASCAR Cup Series race will now begin at 2:00 p.m. on August 23, with the green flag scheduled for approximately 2:10 p.m. Speedway officials said the adjustment is intended to improve the chances of completing the full race before deteriorating weather reaches the Loudon area.

Under the revised schedule, grandstand gates will open at 10:00 a.m., and driver introductions will begin at 1:15 p.m. The weekend also features the Team EJP 175 for the NASCAR CRAFTSMAN Truck Series and the Mohegan Sun 100 for the NASCAR Whelen Modified Tour, drawing thousands of fans to the speedway for New England's only annual NASCAR Cup Series weekend. Local and state officials have coordinated traffic management plans along Route 106 as campers and race fans continue arriving throughout the weekend.

The Dollar Tree 301 is the penultimate race of the 2026 NASCAR Cup Series regular season, making it especially important for drivers still competing for playoff positions before the postseason begins. Speedway officials said the revised start time was coordinated with NASCAR, race teams, and broadcast partners to minimize the risk of weather delays affecting the event.

Race weekend provides a significant economic boost for central New Hampshire, with hotels, restaurants, campgrounds, and local businesses benefiting from the annual influx of visitors. Track officials encourage fans to arrive early, monitor weather forecasts, and check the speedway's official channels for any additional schedule updates before traveling to the race.

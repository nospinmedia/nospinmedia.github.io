🏛️ Rhode Island Democratic Party chair resigns after husband's indictmentOhio grand jury alleges $20 million fraud involving federal student aid 👇

EducationOctober 21, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 21, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Rhode Island Democratic Party Chair Liz Beretta-Perik has resigned after her husband was indicted on felony charges in Ohio over an alleged scheme involving nearly $20 million in federal grants and financial aid intended for students at a community college.

Her husband, Michael Perik of Jamestown, is one of three people indicted by an Ohio grand jury in connection with the now-closed Eastern Gateway Community College. Ohio Auditor Keith Faber announced the indictment on October 8. Perik faces 12 felony charges, including theft, fraud, bribery, money laundering and engaging in a pattern of corrupt activity. He has pleaded not guilty, and his attorney denies wrongdoing.

Perik formerly led the Student Resource Center, an education consulting company that worked with Eastern Gateway on a tuition program for union members and their families. Prosecutors allege Perik, a business partner and the college's former president used business entities to improperly obtain federal grants and financial aid over a six-year period ending in 2022. The allegations include misuse of Pell Grant funding and pandemic-era Paycheck Protection Program money.

Beretta-Perik has not been charged with wrongdoing and said Monday that her husband's case had nothing to do with her or her position. She resigned Tuesday after calls to step aside from Democratic gubernatorial nominee Helena Buonanno Foulkes and Republican gubernatorial nominee Aaron Guckian ahead of the November 3 election.

Beretta-Perik was elected unanimously as party chair in 2023, becoming the first woman to lead the Rhode Island Democratic Party. As chair, she played a central role in fundraising and supporting Democratic candidates across the state.

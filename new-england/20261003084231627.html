🎆 Two men avoid jail after setting off firework inside Harvard Medical School labFederal judge orders probation, community service and $28,517 restitution 👇

IllustrationIllustration representing court rulings and legal proceedings.Credit: AI-generated illustration
EducationOctober 3, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 3, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Two Massachusetts men avoided jail after pleading guilty to setting off a large commercial firework inside a Harvard Medical School research building following Halloween celebrations last year. Logan David Patterson, 19, of Plymouth, and Dominick Frank Cardoza, 21, of Bourne, were each sentenced in federal court to three years of probation and 300 hours of community service. They were also ordered to pay $28,517 in restitution to Harvard Medical School.

The sentencing was handed down by U.S. District Judge Angel Kelley in Boston. Patterson and Cardoza pleaded guilty in April to one count each of conspiracy to damage a building by means of an explosive. Federal prosecutors had sought six months of home confinement as part of their recommended sentences, but Kelley declined to impose home confinement.

The case stems from the early morning hours of November 1, 2025. Surveillance cameras captured Patterson and Cardoza walking toward the Harvard Medical School campus wearing dark clothing and face coverings. At about 2:24 a.m., video showed them lighting what appeared to be Roman candle fireworks.

Minutes later, the two climbed over a chain-link fence into a construction area surrounding the Goldenson Building and used scaffolding to reach the roof. At approximately 2:45 a.m., campus police received a fire alarm indicating an explosion on the building's fourth floor, which houses a research laboratory within Harvard Medical School's Department of Neurobiology.

Investigators determined that Patterson and Cardoza had detonated a large commercial firework inside a wooden locker in the fourth-floor research laboratory. Surveillance footage subsequently captured them visiting the fifth floor before leaving through a first-floor emergency exit. Authorities said they then fled in opposite directions, discarded clothing they had worn on campus and returned to nearby Wentworth Institute of Technology, where they had been visiting for Halloween social activities.

No one was injured in the explosion, but the incident prompted a substantial law-enforcement response. Patterson and Cardoza were arrested and federally charged in November 2025 before pleading guilty in April 2026.

The case was prosecuted by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Massachusetts' National Security Unit and investigated by the FBI's Boston Division.

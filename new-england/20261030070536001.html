⚖️ Family sues Shaw's after Saco truck driver's deathLawsuit follows 2024 accident involving falling bags of rock salt 👇

Illustration representing court rulings and legal proceedings.Credit: AI-generated illustration
HealthOctober 30, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 30, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

The family of a Saco, Maine, truck driver who died months after being severely injured while making a delivery has filed a wrongful death lawsuit against Shaw's Supermarkets.

Randy Rackliffe, 59, was delivering rock salt to a Shaw's store in Raynham, Massachusetts, on January 16, 2024, when bags of salt fell on him, according to the lawsuit and news reports. Rackliffe was working as a truck driver for Pottle's Transportation.

The lawsuit says Rackliffe was struck and crushed by falling 50-pound bags of Morton's Safe-T-Salt, causing catastrophic injuries. He was hospitalized following the accident and later received additional medical care.

Rackliffe died on September 12, 2024, about eight months after the accident, at Maine Medical Center in Portland. His estate is now pursuing claims against Shaw's over the circumstances surrounding the unloading of the shipment.

The lawsuit alleges Shaw's failed to provide reasonably safe conditions for Rackliffe while he was making the delivery. Those allegations are claims in a civil case and have not been established by a court.

The newly filed lawsuit brings attention back to an accident that occurred more than two years ago rather than reporting a new workplace death. The case also raises questions about responsibility for the safety of commercial drivers and other outside contractors while they are working on a company's property.

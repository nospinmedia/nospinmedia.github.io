🏍️ Three killed in Laconia motorcycle collisionFourth person hospitalized after apparent head-on crash 👇

HealthSeptember 14, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 14, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Police have identified the three people killed when two motorcycles collided in Laconia, New Hampshire, late Saturday night, leaving a fourth person seriously injured. The crash happened at about 11 p.m. on September 12, 2026, along Route 3 on Endicott Street North near Weirs Beach.

Laconia Police Chief Matthew Canfield said the initial investigation indicates the motorcycles were traveling in opposite directions when they collided. Each motorcycle was carrying an operator and a passenger. One of the motorcycles caught fire following the impact, and emergency crews encountered two heavily damaged motorcycles and debris spread across the crash scene.

Police identified the motorcycle operators as Cameron Mosher, 28, of Dennis, Massachusetts, and Miles Collette, 32, of Gilford, New Hampshire. Both died at the scene. Passenger Jeanine Fereshetian, 33, of Laconia, was also pronounced dead. A fourth person, an adult woman whose identity has not been released, was flown by DHART medical helicopter to Concord Hospital with serious injuries.

Canfield said the surviving woman was wearing a helmet, while the three people who died were not wearing helmets. Investigators have not determined whether speed or impairment contributed to the collision, and authorities emphasized that the investigation remains ongoing.

The crash occurred during Biketemberfest weekend in the Weirs Beach area, but police said there was no indication that the event or nearby businesses were directly connected to the collision. The Belknap Regional Accident Investigation Team is assisting Laconia police with reconstructing what happened. Route 3 remained closed for several hours while investigators documented the scene and emergency crews cleared the roadway.

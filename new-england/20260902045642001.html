⚖️ Massachusetts DA Primaries Keep Incumbents in OfficeHayden and Ryan Advance Unopposed to November 👇

PoliticsSeptember 2, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 2, 2026 at 4:25 PM ET

Voters in Massachusetts backed incumbent district attorneys in the state's two largest counties during the Sept. 1, 2026, Democratic primary, giving Suffolk County District Attorney Kevin Hayden and Middlesex County District Attorney Marian Ryan victories that leave both unopposed in the Nov. 3 general election. With no Republican candidates on the ballot for either office, Tuesday's results effectively determine who will lead two of the Commonwealth's busiest prosecutor's offices for the next four-year term.

In Suffolk County, Hayden defeated former U.S. Attorney Rachael Rollins and attorney Linda Champion in a closely watched three-way race. Hayden received about 51% of the vote, while Champion finished with approximately 27% and Rollins received about 23%. The race marked a political rematch after Hayden was appointed district attorney by then-Gov. Charlie Baker in 2022 following Rollins' departure to become U.S. attorney for Massachusetts. Rollins later resigned that federal position in 2023 after an ethics investigation.

In Middlesex County, Ryan turned back a challenge from former assistant district attorney David Solet, who previously led the office's cold-case homicide unit before leaving to run against his former boss. Ryan, who has served as district attorney since 2013, campaigned on her office's work involving wrongful conviction reviews, juvenile diversion programs and efforts to reduce gun violence. Solet argued the office needed new leadership and received support from several police organizations during the campaign.

The results ensure continuity in two offices that together oversee criminal prosecutions across much of eastern Massachusetts. Suffolk County includes Boston, Chelsea, Revere and Winthrop, while Middlesex County serves 54 cities and towns and is the state's most populous county. The primary outcomes also conclude two of Massachusetts' most closely watched local contests during this year's election cycle.

🔎 Search underway for missing 18-year-old in NeedhamPolice deploy K-9 teams, drones and aircraft while asking residents to stay clear 👇

EducationNovember 13, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 13, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Police in Needham, Massachusetts, are conducting an intensive search for 18-year-old Cole Davison, a Needham High School senior who was reported missing Wednesday night. Authorities say the operation is focused on locating Davison and checking on his well-being, and there is no known threat to the public.

Davison was last reported seen near 1000 South Street in Needham on November 11, an area that includes residential neighborhoods and wooded terrain near the Dover town line. Police said he may be wearing a blue flannel jacket with gold or yellow coloring.

The search expanded Thursday with assistance from Metro-LEC Search and Rescue, K-9 teams, thermal-imaging drones and the Massachusetts State Police Air Wing. Authorities established a staging area near South Street and sent wireless emergency alerts to phones in the surrounding area asking anyone with information to contact police.

Needham Police Chief John Schlittler said there is no indication Davison is armed and that he has made no threats toward anyone. Police have emphasized that there is no known danger to the public associated with the search.

Authorities are also asking residents and other volunteers not to enter the wooded search area on their own. Additional people moving through the area can interfere with K-9 scent tracking and make it more difficult for teams using thermal-imaging equipment to distinguish search personnel from other people.

Police said falling overnight temperatures increased concern about finding Davison quickly. Officials have not released private details about the circumstances surrounding his disappearance and have asked that the family's privacy be respected.

Anyone with information about Davison's location is asked to contact Needham Police at 781-444-1212.

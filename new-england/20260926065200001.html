🔥 Maine heating oil hits record $6.02 per gallonPrices surpass 2022 peak as winter heating season approaches 👇

EconomySeptember 26, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 26, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

The average price of heating oil in Maine has climbed to a record $6.02 per gallon, surpassing the previous high reached during the energy-price surge of 2022 and raising concerns about household heating costs as colder weather approaches.

The latest statewide survey found the average cash price for No. 2 heating oil at $6.02 per gallon, above the previous record of $5.92 set in May 2022 following Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Maine Public reported that prices have risen sharply amid disruptions in global energy markets connected to the ongoing conflict involving Iran.

The increase has an outsized impact in Maine because the state remains heavily dependent on heating oil. Roughly half of Maine households use oil as their primary source of home heating, making changes in fuel prices particularly important to household budgets during the winter.

At an average price of $6.02 per gallon, filling a typical 275-gallon heating oil tank from empty would cost about $1,656. Actual household costs vary depending on tank size, fuel consumption, delivery prices and how much fuel remains when a delivery is made.

Other heating fuels are also expensive. The latest survey put the statewide average price of K-1 kerosene at $5.97 per gallon and propane at approximately $3.20 per gallon. Prices can vary considerably between dealers and regions of the state.

The new record comes before the heart of Maine's heating season, when household fuel consumption typically rises substantially. Low-income residents who need help paying heating bills can apply for assistance through Maine's home energy assistance programs and local Community Action Agencies.

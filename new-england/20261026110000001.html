👮 Westborough police chief placed on leave after Shrewsbury police callTown hires outside investigator but has not disclosed what prompted the response 👇

PoliticsOctober 26, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 26, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Westborough Police Chief Todd Minardi has been placed on administrative leave after police in neighboring Shrewsbury responded to a call for service involving him Friday night, town officials announced over the weekend.

Westborough officials said they learned about the Shrewsbury police response and placed Minardi on leave Saturday. The town has begun taking steps to retain an outside investigator to examine the matter, but officials have not disclosed what prompted the original call or explained Minardi's involvement.

Deputy Police Chief Steve Tompkins is overseeing the Westborough Police Department's day-to-day operations while Minardi remains on leave. The town said it would not provide additional information because the situation is an ongoing personnel matter.

Shrewsbury police have also released few details. The department has not publicly explained the nature of the call, whether Minardi was at a private residence or another location, or whether investigators believe any criminal conduct occurred. No arrest or criminal charge involving Minardi had been publicly announced as of Sunday evening.

Minardi has spent more than 30 years with the Westborough Police Department. He joined the department in the mid-1990s and worked his way through the ranks before becoming chief in 2023. He is also a lifelong Westborough resident.

The administrative action comes shortly after Minardi received the 2026 Massachusetts Chiefs of Police Association Community Policing Award in September. The recognition honored his work in community policing before the still-undisclosed Shrewsbury incident occurred.

Placing an employee on administrative leave does not by itself establish wrongdoing, and Westborough officials have not announced any findings against Minardi. The outside investigation is expected to give town officials an independent review of the circumstances surrounding the police response before decisions are made about his status.

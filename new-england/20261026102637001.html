🚨 Boston mother charged with manslaughter after 4-year-old dies from fentanylProsecutors say Liyanna Bautista Familia died after ingesting the drug 👇

HealthOctober 26, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 26, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A Boston mother is facing manslaughter and other charges after prosecutors said her 4-year-old daughter died from ingesting fentanyl earlier this year.

Dayanna Familia, 35, was arrested over the weekend by Boston police in connection with the death of her daughter, Liyanna Bautista Familia. According to the Suffolk County District Attorney's Office, Liyanna died on August 18 as a result of ingesting fentanyl.

Boston police said officers responded to an apartment on Fuller Street in Dorchester after receiving a report of an unresponsive child. Officers and emergency medical personnel found Liyanna unresponsive, and she was transported to Boston Children's Hospital, where she was pronounced dead.

The child's death led to an investigation by Boston police and prosecutors. Authorities obtained an arrest warrant for Familia on October 16, and police arrested her in Dedham on Saturday.

Familia faces charges of manslaughter, possession of a Class A substance, withholding evidence, intimidation and reckless endangerment of a child, according to court records. She is scheduled to be arraigned Monday in Dorchester Municipal Court.

Authorities had not publicly released detailed allegations explaining how Liyanna came into contact with the fentanyl as of Sunday. Prosecutors also had not publicly detailed the evidence underlying the additional charges of withholding evidence and intimidation. An attorney representing Familia was not immediately identified in initial reports.

The case comes amid continuing concern over accidental exposure to fentanyl, a powerful synthetic opioid that can be deadly in small amounts. The specific circumstances surrounding Liyanna's exposure, however, remain part of the criminal case and have not yet been fully disclosed publicly.

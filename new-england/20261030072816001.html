🎓 Dartmouth provost resigns after review of undisclosed AI useCommittee says Santiago Schnell fell short of academic standards 👇

Dartmouth College Provost Santiago Schnell has resigned and is leaving the university after a faculty-led review concluded that he used artificial intelligence to significantly alter some of his writing without properly disclosing that use.

The controversy began after reporting raised questions about Schnell's use of AI in published work, including an August Washington Post opinion column about AI and academic cheating. Schnell had said he used ChatGPT to refine arguments and check grammar while maintaining that the underlying ideas were his own.

Dartmouth President Sian Leah Beilock appointed an independent faculty-led committee to examine Schnell's AI use and disclosures. The committee said Schnell used AI to meaningfully alter his writing, failed to disclose AI use as required by at least one publication and was not forthcoming about the extent of that use. It also concluded that his conduct fell short of Dartmouth's ethics and professionalism standards.

The committee said it did not rely on AI-detection software for its findings. Instead, reviewers examined emails and available records from Schnell's Dartmouth Claude account. In one academic paper, the committee found he used Claude to substantially edit a manuscript, troubleshoot computer code and generate figures after peer reviewers requested major revisions. The publication required disclosure of that type of AI assistance, according to the committee.

The report also said Schnell did not fully cooperate with the investigation, including declining to provide requested logs from personal AI accounts. The committee did not recommend disciplinary action.

Beilock said Schnell's resignation was his decision and emphasized that university leaders must model the transparency expected of students and scholars. History professor Matthew Delmont will serve as interim provost through June 2028.

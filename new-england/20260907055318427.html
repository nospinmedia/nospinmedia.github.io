🚨 1 killed, 7 wounded in Taunton house party shooting22-year-old Brockton man identified as homicide investigation continues 👇

HealthSeptember 7, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 7, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A 22-year-old Brockton man was killed and seven other people were wounded in a shooting during a large house party in Taunton, Massachusetts, early September 6, prompting a homicide investigation involving local and state authorities. The Bristol County District Attorney's Office identified the man who died as Zirovick Depina.

Police responded to the area of Weir Street shortly after 12:30 a.m. following multiple reports of gunfire. Officers arriving at the scene found several people suffering from gunshot wounds, while other victims were taken to hospitals separately. Authorities determined that a total of eight people had been shot. Depina was pronounced dead after being transported for medical treatment.

The seven surviving victims were also taken to area hospitals. One was reported in serious but stable condition, while the injuries suffered by the other six were described as non-life-threatening. Authorities said the victims were young adults who had been attending a large gathering at the residence when the shooting occurred.

The Taunton Police Department, Massachusetts State Police detectives assigned to the Bristol County District Attorney's Office and other investigators are working to determine what led to the gunfire and who was responsible. No arrest had been announced in the latest reporting. Authorities have asked anyone with information about the shooting to contact investigators as the homicide investigation continues.

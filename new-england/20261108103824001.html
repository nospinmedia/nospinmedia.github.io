🚧 I-195 reopens after East Providence bridge demolitionPotter Street Bridge rebuild will widen westbound highway 👇

EducationNovember 8, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 8, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Interstate 195 has fully reopened in East Providence, Rhode Island, after crews demolished the Potter Street Bridge as part of work connected to the larger Washington Bridge reconstruction project.

The Rhode Island Department of Transportation closed I-195 in both directions beginning at 10 p.m. Friday, November 6, so crews could safely demolish the bridge over the interstate. Eastbound lanes reopened at approximately 11 a.m. Saturday, while westbound lanes began reopening around noon after cleanup work was completed.

The closure was designed to allow crews to remove the bridge in one operation rather than demolishing it in stages. RIDOT said a phased approach would have required temporary structural supports and multiple lane closures, creating traffic disruptions over a longer period.

The demolition is part of a plan to rebuild the Potter Street Bridge and widen the I-195 corridor. RIDOT expects construction of the replacement bridge to take approximately 12 to 18 months, with the new structure scheduled to reopen in summer 2028.

Once the work is complete, I-195 West is expected to have four continuous travel lanes between the Broadway interchange and the Washington Bridge. A fifth lane will lead to a new exit ramp at Waterfront Drive. RIDOT says the changes are intended to reduce congestion that has historically backed traffic toward the Massachusetts state line.

Potter Street itself will remain closed at the interstate while the replacement bridge is constructed. Drivers crossing I-195 can use the nearby Purchase Street Bridge, while a separate pedestrian detour uses School, Purchase and Freeborn streets.

The reopening restores interstate traffic after the temporary shutdown, but construction associated with the broader Washington Bridge project will continue.

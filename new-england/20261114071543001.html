🖥️ Microsoft outage disrupts public services across New EnglandServices fully restored after courts, towns and universities lost access 👇

EducationNovember 14, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 14, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Microsoft says all services affected by this week's Microsoft 365 outage were fully restored Saturday morning after a multi-day disruption that affected municipal governments, courts, universities and public safety agencies across New England.

In Massachusetts, state employees received alerts Thursday that government email and Microsoft services were unavailable. Municipalities including Arlington, Wellesley and Orange also reported email outages and directed residents who needed assistance to contact town departments by telephone.

The consequences were more serious in Maine, where the Maine Judicial Branch warned that emergency protection-from-abuse and protection-from-harassment requests emailed after 12:30 p.m. Thursday might not have reached the courts. Officials instructed people who submitted those requests to resend them or deliver paper copies directly to a courthouse. The U.S. District Court for the District of Maine also reported disruption to its email intake system, while Ogunquit said municipal employees could not send or receive email or access Microsoft 365 tools.

In Rhode Island, the Narragansett Police Department reported losing access to Outlook email, while the state's judiciary experienced Microsoft Office and email disruptions. In Vermont, the University of Vermont reported problems affecting Outlook, Teams and Copilot.

Microsoft said Saturday morning that all affected services had been restored. The company narrowed the technical cause to an issue introduced during an earlier network capacity upgrade that surfaced following a recent network configuration change. That combination caused servers to fail automated health checks and drop out of service.

No evidence has emerged that the disruption resulted from a cyberattack. The outage demonstrated how technical problems at one major cloud provider can simultaneously affect unrelated government offices, courts, police departments and universities across the region.

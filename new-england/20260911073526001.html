🚌 MBTA bus crashes into six vehicles after driver's apparent medical emergencyThree hospitalized as out-of-service bus ends up on Medford lawn 👇

HealthSeptember 11, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 11, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

An out-of-service MBTA bus crashed into six vehicles before jumping a curb and coming to rest on the lawn of a Medford home Thursday after its driver suffered an apparent medical emergency, according to transit authorities.

The crash happened shortly after 11 a.m. on September 10 along Fellsway West near Salem Street. MBTA Transit Police said the 62-year-old bus operator apparently experienced a medical emergency while driving eastbound toward vehicles waiting at a traffic light.

The bus first struck a vehicle stopped in traffic, setting off a chain-reaction collision involving several other vehicles. It continued through the intersection, where it struck additional vehicles and a light pole before traveling onto the lawn of a nearby home and stopping near a tree.

Authorities said a total of six other vehicles were damaged. Photographs and video from the scene showed several heavily damaged cars along the roadway and the MBTA bus sitting well beyond the street on the residential property.

The bus driver and two people from the other vehicles were transported to a hospital. Transit Police said their injuries were not considered life-threatening and all three were expected to recover. The bus was not carrying passengers when the crash occurred.

Emergency crews temporarily closed portions of the surrounding roadway while damaged vehicles were removed and crews dealt with the downed light pole. The collision caused significant traffic disruption around the busy Fellsway West and Salem Street intersection.

The MBTA said the bus had been traveling out of service at the time of the crash. Authorities are continuing to investigate the incident, and officials have not announced a final determination beyond the driver's reported apparent medical emergency. No confirmed mechanical failure has been publicly identified as the cause.

The incident ended with extensive property and vehicle damage but, despite the path traveled by the bus, no life-threatening injuries were reported.

💔 15-year-old Uxbridge High School student killed in crashTeen was ejected after car struck utility pole Saturday night 👇

EducationOctober 5, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 5, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A 15-year-old Uxbridge High School student died after being ejected from a car that crashed into a utility pole in Uxbridge, Massachusetts, Saturday night, leaving the school community mourning as investigators work to determine what caused the crash.

Uxbridge Police said emergency crews responded to a single-vehicle crash near 685 Chocolog Road on the evening of October 3. A Ford Focus carrying three people had left the roadway and struck a utility pole.

The 15-year-old girl was ejected from the vehicle and transported to Rhode Island Hospital in Providence, where she was pronounced dead. Authorities have not publicly identified her out of respect for her family. Police also have not released information about whether the other two occupants of the vehicle were injured.

The circumstances leading to the crash remain under investigation. The Central Massachusetts Law Enforcement Council Accident Reconstruction Team is assisting Uxbridge Police in determining what happened. Authorities have not announced a cause or said what caused the Ford Focus to leave the roadway.

Uxbridge Public Schools Superintendent Dave Ljungberg confirmed that the teenager was a student at Uxbridge High School. The district activated its crisis-response team following her death and said counselors and other support staff would be available when students returned to school Monday.

Uxbridge officials have asked the public to respect the family's privacy and avoid speculation while investigators establish the facts. Police are also asking anyone who witnessed the crash to contact the department.

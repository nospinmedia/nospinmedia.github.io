🏭 Lowell Residents Seek Court Order To Pause Data Center Expansion 👇

EnvironmentJuly 30, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published July 30, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A group of Lowell, Massachusetts, residents has asked a judge to temporarily halt the planned expansion of a Markley Group data center while a legal challenge over state permits moves forward. The proposed project would add approximately 352,000 square feet to the existing facility, which neighbors say has already affected nearby homes through persistent noise, vibrations and emissions from backup generators.

During a hearing in Middlesex Superior Court on July 29, attorneys representing nearby residents requested a preliminary injunction to stop construction and equipment testing until the court reviews the project's environmental approvals. The lawsuit argues that the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection did not adequately evaluate the cumulative environmental and public health impacts before issuing permits for the expansion.

The Markley Group and state regulators dispute the claims and have defended the permitting process, arguing the project complies with applicable environmental requirements. The court has not ruled on the merits of the allegations, and no decision has yet been issued on the request for a preliminary injunction.

The case reflects a growing debate in Massachusetts and elsewhere over where large data centers should be located as demand for cloud computing and artificial intelligence infrastructure continues to increase. Supporters say such facilities are critical to the digital economy, while opponents argue they can create significant impacts for nearby residential neighborhoods.

Judge Douglas Wilkins is expected to issue a decision on the request for a preliminary injunction in the coming days. The broader lawsuit challenging the project's permits will continue regardless of how the court rules on the temporary request.

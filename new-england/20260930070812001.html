🏥 $20 million gift expands health care at home in rural New EnglandDartmouth program will support patients and family caregivers 👇

EducationSeptember 30, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 30, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Dartmouth Health and Dartmouth's Geisel School of Medicine are launching a major effort to bring more medical care directly into patients' homes, backed by a $20 million gift from Dartmouth alumnus and former biotech executive Richard "Dick" Levy. The Dartmouth Health at Home initiative is aimed particularly at rural communities in New Hampshire and Vermont, where long distances and shortages of health care providers can make receiving care more difficult.

The initiative will focus on three areas. Dartmouth plans to expand primary and palliative care in patients' homes, establish a new Family Caregiver Institute, and study the results to determine whether the approach can be replicated by other health systems. The caregiver institute will provide hands-on training, help families navigate available services and connect them with home care aides and other resources.

The program grew from the Susan & Richard Levy Health Care Delivery Incubator, established in 2020. The incubator has funded 22 projects examining new ways to deliver health care, many focused on providing services and caregiver support at home. Levy's interest in the issue became personal after his wife, Susan, developed Lewy body dementia and he became her primary caregiver. She died in 2023.

Rural health care presents particular challenges in northern New England. About 20% of Americans live in rural areas, while only about 9% of physicians practice there, according to a 2025 Dartmouth report. Dartmouth also says approximately 740,000 people in New Hampshire, Maine and Vermont provide care for a family member with a long-term illness or disability.

The Dartmouth Institute for Health Policy and Clinical Practice will evaluate the initiative by measuring patient outcomes, costs and patient experiences. Dartmouth says the findings will be used to develop a toolkit that other health systems could use to create similar programs. The broader Dartmouth Health at Home effort also includes the previously announced Vickie French Hospital at Home, which is being developed to provide hospital-level care in patients' homes.

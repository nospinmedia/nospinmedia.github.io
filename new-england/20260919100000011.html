⚖️ Suspended Plymouth officer, husband indicted on child rape chargesCase moves to Superior Court after grand jury indictments 👇

IllustrationIllustration representing court rulings and legal proceedings.Credit: AI-generated illustration
LegalSeptember 19, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 19, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A Plymouth County grand jury has indicted suspended Plymouth police officer Samantha Pelrine, 31, and her husband, Daniel Forand, 37, on charges stemming from the alleged sexual abuse of a boy who lived in their home after the couple became his guardians. Both defendants have previously pleaded not guilty to the allegations.

Pelrine and Forand each face multiple counts of aggravated child rape. Prosecutors say Forand also faces charges including indecent assault and battery, rape, and assault and battery. The indictments move the criminal case from District Court to Superior Court, where the couple will be arraigned at a later date.

The case began publicly in March, after the alleged victim, now an adult in his early 20s, sought a restraining order against the couple. According to court records reviewed by the Boston Globe, he alleged that Pelrine and Forand sexually abused him after taking him into their Plymouth home in 2018 and later becoming his legal guardians. Prosecutors allege the sexual assaults occurred on numerous occasions between 2019 and 2025.

Pelrine and Forand were arrested in March and released on bail after their arraignments. Pelrine was suspended from the Plymouth Police Department shortly after the allegations surfaced. At the time, the department said it was deeply disturbed by the allegations and would conduct an internal investigation.

Many details of the criminal case remain unavailable publicly because court documents have been placed under a protective order. The indictments are formal criminal accusations and do not establish guilt; both defendants are presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

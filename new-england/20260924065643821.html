🌧️ Weekend nor’easter targets southern New EnglandGusts could reach 70 mph as offshore seas build above 20 feet 👇

EnvironmentSeptember 24, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 24, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A slow-moving coastal storm is expected to bring heavy rain, damaging winds and dangerous surf to southern New England, with forecasters warning of possible flooding, tree damage, power outages and travel disruptions across parts of Massachusetts, Rhode Island and Connecticut.

The National Weather Service Boston/Norton office has issued High Wind Watches and Coastal Flood Watches for coastal portions of Massachusetts and Rhode Island beginning Friday morning. Marine advisories have also escalated to Storm Watches across coastal waters as the system strengthens and moves slowly through the region.

Wind will be a major concern. Peak gusts could reach 50 to 70 mph across Cape Cod, Cape Ann and the Islands, while other areas could experience gusts of 40 to 50 mph. Meteorologists warn that trees remain fully leaved in September, increasing wind resistance and making branches and trees more susceptible to falling. That means winds that might cause fewer problems during winter could produce more extensive tree damage and power outages now.

Marine conditions could become particularly dangerous Friday night into Saturday, with offshore seas forecast to build to 17 to 22 feet. Ferry operators serving Martha's Vineyard, Nantucket and Block Island have warned travelers to expect service disruptions and cancellations beginning Friday. Coastal flooding and erosion are also possible in vulnerable locations, particularly around high tide.

Widespread rainfall totals of 1 to 3 inches are forecast, with localized amounts reaching as much as 6 inches along eastern coastal areas. Heavy downpours could produce urban, poor-drainage and localized flash flooding, although exact totals will depend on the storm's track and speed.

Residents are being advised to secure outdoor objects, prepare for possible power outages, monitor ferry schedules and reconsider coastal or marine activities during the worst conditions. Forecast details could still change as the storm develops.

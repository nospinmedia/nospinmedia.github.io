🚨 Medford officers rescue seven residents from intentionally set house fireThree officers hospitalized for smoke inhalation as arson investigation continues 👇

CrimeNovember 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 23, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Police officers rescued seven residents from a burning home in Medford, Massachusetts, after authorities say a person broke into the occupied residence and intentionally set a fire on Saturday night.

Medford police responded to a home on Crescent Street at approximately 7:13 p.m. for a report of an active break-in. When officers arrived, they discovered the house was on fire and found the front door locked. Officers forced open the door and evacuated seven people from the residence as firefighters responded.

Three Medford police officers were hospitalized with minor injuries from smoke inhalation sustained during the rescue. Available reporting did not indicate that any of the seven residents suffered serious injuries.

Police took a suspect into custody at the scene. Authorities said the person was known to the residents, and police described the incident as isolated, saying there was no continuing threat to the public. Officials have not publicly established a motive for the alleged break-in and fire.

The blaze brought a substantial mutual-aid response to Medford. Firefighters from Somerville, Arlington, Everett, Malden and Chelsea assisted local crews at the scene.

The Massachusetts State Police Fire Marshal's office also joined the investigation into the fire. Authorities are treating the blaze as intentionally set while investigators work to establish the circumstances surrounding the incident and determine the appropriate criminal charges.

The combination of an occupied burning home and locked entrance forced responding officers to act before the full fire response was in place, resulting in the evacuation of all seven occupants but exposing the officers to smoke.

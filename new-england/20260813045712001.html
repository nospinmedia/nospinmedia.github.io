🚨 Suspicious Package Evacuates Maine Revenue ServicesBomb squad finds no explosive or biological hazard 👇

EconomyAugust 13, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 13, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A suspicious package opened inside Maine Revenue Services in Augusta prompted an evacuation and a multi-agency emergency response late Wednesday, August 12, before investigators determined the item contained no explosive or biological hazards. No injuries were reported, and authorities said the incident remains under investigation.

Capitol Police responded at approximately 4:56 p.m. to the state office at 51 Commerce Drive after receiving a report that a suspicious package had been opened inside the building. According to authorities, the person who handled the package moved it outside and contacted police. Capitol Police then requested assistance from the Maine State Police Bomb Squad, while the building was evacuated as a precaution.

Bomb technicians evaluated the package using X-ray imaging and presumptive field testing. Those tests found no evidence of explosives or biological hazards. The Augusta Fire Department and the 11th Army National Guard Civil Support Team also assisted at the scene during the response.

Officials have not publicly identified what was inside the package, where it originated or why it was considered suspicious. Maine State Police said no additional information was being released while investigators continue examining the circumstances surrounding the incident.

The response temporarily disrupted operations at one of Maine's state government facilities but ended without any reported injuries or identified hazardous material. Authorities have not announced whether the investigation has determined who sent the package or whether any criminal conduct was involved.

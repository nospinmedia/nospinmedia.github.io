⚖️ Lindsay Clancy due back in court Tuesday after mistrialDefense seeks dismissal as prosecutors consider another trial 👇

HealthSeptember 28, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 28, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Lindsay Clancy is scheduled to return to Plymouth Superior Court on Tuesday, September 29, for the next stage of the criminal case involving the deaths of her three young children. The hearing comes after her first trial ended in a mistrial earlier this month when jurors were unable to reach a unanimous verdict.

Clancy was charged in connection with the January 2023 deaths of her children, Cora, 5, Dawson, 3, and Callan, 8 months, at the family's home in Duxbury, Massachusetts. Prosecutors alleged that she strangled the children with exercise bands before attempting to take her own life. Her attorneys argued that severe mental illness left her not criminally responsible for the killings.

The trial ended in a mistrial on September 4 after jurors could not unanimously decide whether Clancy was criminally responsible. The case included extensive testimony about her mental health, medications and psychiatric condition surrounding the children's deaths.

Defense attorney Kevin Reddington has filed a motion under Massachusetts criminal procedure asking the judge for post-verdict relief, including dismissal or reduction of the charges. The defense argues that the evidence presented at trial supports such action following the deadlocked jury. Prosecutors can oppose that request and pursue another trial if the charges remain in place.

Tuesday's hearing is expected to address the defense's post-mistrial motions and the next steps in the prosecution. No second trial has yet taken place, and the court has not dismissed the charges.

The case has drawn widespread attention across New England because of its focus on severe mental illness, postpartum psychiatric conditions and the legal standard for criminal responsibility.

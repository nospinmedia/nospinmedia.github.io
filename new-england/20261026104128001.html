🏦 Man accused of targeting three Boston banks in just 27 minutesPolice say two tellers refused his demands before officers arrested him at third bank 👇

Illustration representing court rulings and legal proceedings.Credit: AI-generated illustration
LegalOctober 26, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 26, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A 68-year-old New Jersey man was arrested after Boston police say he attempted to rob three banks in the city's Chinatown area within just 27 minutes on Saturday, succeeding in getting money from one before officers detained him at the third location.

Police identified the suspect as Anthony Matarazzo. The sequence began at approximately 12:17 p.m. on October 24, when officers responded to East West Bank at 68 Harrison Avenue. An employee told police that a man passed a note demanding $200. The employee refused to give him the money, and the man eventually left. Police distributed his description and a photograph to officers in the area.

At approximately 12:38 p.m., officers were called to Cathay Bank at 621 Washington Street. Police said a man matching the earlier description again passed a note demanding $200 and told the employee he had a gun. This time, he left with an undisclosed amount of money.

Six minutes later, at about 12:44 p.m., police received another robbery report from Bank of America at 630 Washington Street. An employee there told investigators the man passed a note indicating that he intended to rob the bank, but the employee refused his demand.

Responding officers found a man matching the description from the previous incidents and detained him. During the booking process, police said they recovered a note from Matarazzo's chest pocket along with an undisclosed amount of money. No firearm was recovered, according to police.

Matarazzo was arrested and is expected to be arraigned in Boston Municipal Court on an armed robbery charge. Initial reports did not identify an attorney representing him.

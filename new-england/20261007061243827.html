💣 New Hampshire man gets 5 years for building and detonating pipe bombsTwo devices exploded in Weare before police recovered a third 👇

EconomyOctober 7, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 7, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Dale G. Stewart Jr., 57, of Webster, New Hampshire, was sentenced Monday to five years in federal prison for building three pipe bombs and detonating two of them in Weare, according to the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of New Hampshire.

U.S. District Judge Samantha D. Elliott sentenced Stewart to 60 months in prison, followed by three years of supervised release. He pleaded guilty in June 2026 to three counts of possessing unregistered firearms. Under federal law, the homemade explosive devices qualify as destructive devices and therefore as firearms subject to registration requirements under the National Firearms Act.

The case began after Weare police responded to reports of explosions in April 2023. Investigators determined that Stewart had built three pipe bombs at his Webster home using galvanized steel pipe, threaded end caps and explosive powder. Prosecutors said he transported two of the devices to Weare and detonated them near sugar houses.

Investigators later searched Stewart's property and recovered a third pipe bomb that had not been detonated. Federal prosecutors said the device contained more than four ounces of explosive powder. Bomb technicians rendered it safe before investigators collected it as evidence.

Authorities have not said that anyone was injured in either explosion. Prosecutors also have not alleged that Stewart was carrying out a politically motivated or terrorist attack. The federal case focused on his manufacture, possession and detonation of the unregistered destructive devices.

The investigation involved the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, FBI, New Hampshire State Police, Weare Police Department and other local agencies. Stewart will also be subject to federal supervision for three years after completing his prison sentence.

🗳️ Luke Bronin Defeats Rep. John Larson in Connecticut PrimaryFormer Hartford mayor advances to November after unseating 14-term incumbent 👇

EconomyAugust 12, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 12, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Former Hartford Mayor Luke Bronin defeated U.S. Rep. John Larson in Connecticut's Democratic primary for the 1st Congressional District on August 11, 2026, ending Larson's nearly three decades in Congress. The victory sends Bronin to the November general election against Republican Dr. Amy Chai in a district that has long favored Democratic candidates.

Bronin, 47, served as Hartford's mayor from 2016 through 2024 and entered the race promising new leadership for the district. Larson, 78, had represented Connecticut's 1st District since 1999 and was seeking a 15th term. Bronin secured the Democratic Party's endorsement earlier this year before defeating the longtime incumbent in the primary.

Campaign finance reports showed Bronin entered the final weeks of the race with a fundraising advantage, helping finance an aggressive campaign focused on economic opportunity, housing, and government effectiveness. Larson emphasized his seniority and experience in Congress, but Democratic primary voters ultimately chose a new nominee to represent the district.

The result marks one of Connecticut's most significant political developments of the 2026 election cycle and ensures a new Democratic nominee will appear on the November ballot for the first time in nearly three decades. With Democrats historically performing well in the district, the primary effectively determined one of the state's highest-profile congressional contests.

🚨 Massachusetts woman killed when gun fires during family disputeAuthorities say Andry McFall was struck as two other family members argued 👇

EducationNovember 16, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 16, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A 49-year-old Massachusetts woman was fatally shot during a dispute between other family members inside a Rockland home Saturday evening, according to authorities.

The victim was identified as Andry McFall. Rockland police received a 911 call at approximately 5:51 p.m. Saturday reporting a domestic disturbance involving a firearm at 31 School Street.

Responding officers found McFall suffering from a gunshot wound. She was transported to South Shore Hospital, where she was pronounced dead.

According to preliminary findings released by the Plymouth County District Attorney's Office, an argument was taking place between other family members when a gun discharged and McFall was struck by a bullet. Initial reporting indicated two men were arguing inside the residence when the gun went off.

The two men were questioned by investigators. Authorities have not announced any arrests or charges, and investigators have not publicly determined whether the firearm was discharged intentionally or accidentally.

The Massachusetts State Police Detective Unit assigned to the Plymouth County District Attorney's Office is investigating alongside Rockland police. Plymouth County District Attorney Timothy Cruz and Rockland Police Chief Nicholas Zeoli identified the investigation as active in statements released following the shooting.

Officials described the shooting as an isolated incident and said there was no continuing threat to the public.

Authorities have not released additional information about the relationship between McFall and the two people involved in the dispute or identified who was handling the firearm when it discharged. The circumstances surrounding the shooting remain under investigation.

🔥 New England heating oil prices surge above $6 a gallonMaine hits record high as NH and neighboring states face similar winter costs 👇

An oil pumpjack operates at a petroleum production site.Credit: PickPik
EconomyOctober 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 23, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Heating oil prices have climbed above $6 a gallon across New England, creating a major increase in home heating costs as the region heads toward winter. Maine has now recorded a new all-time high, while prices in New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Connecticut and Rhode Island are at similarly elevated levels.

The U.S. Energy Information Administration reported a New England average of $6.112 per gallon for residential No. 2 heating oil for the week of October 19. Rhode Island had the highest statewide average at $6.380, followed by Connecticut at $6.154, Massachusetts at $6.115, Vermont at $6.097, New Hampshire at $6.033 and Maine at $6.026.

Maine's own weekly fuel survey, which uses a different methodology and reporting period, put the statewide average at about $6.04 per gallon, surpassing the previous state record of approximately $6.02 set in September. Maine Public reported that the latest figure is the highest average recorded by the state since it began tracking heating oil prices.

The increase is particularly important in northern New England because heating oil remains much more common there than in most of the country. In Maine, roughly six in 10 households use fuel oil as their primary heating source. New Hampshire also has one of the country's highest rates of reliance on heating oil.

For a household buying 500 gallons over a heating season, every $1 increase in the average price adds about $500 to its fuel bill. At $6 per gallon, 500 gallons would cost approximately $3,000, although actual household consumption and delivered prices vary considerably.

The latest increase comes as federal heating assistance is being distributed across the region. Maine recently secured approximately $38 million in early federal Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program funding, an issue No Spin Media previously reported as heating oil prices moved above $6.

While Maine has confirmed that its latest statewide average is a record, current prices are extremely high throughout New England. Comparable historical records vary by state, so the latest figures do not establish that every New England state has individually reached an all-time high.

⚓ Electric Boat lands record $76.6 billion Navy packageSubmarine contracts reinforce 8,000-worker hiring drive in Connecticut and Rhode Island 👇

EducationAugust 3, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 3, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

General Dynamics Electric Boat has received a record package of U.S. Navy submarine contracts valued at $76.6 billion, providing long-term work for its facilities and workforce in Connecticut and Rhode Island. Electric Boat President Mark Rayha said the awards are the largest contracts in the Groton-based company's 127-year history.

The package announced on July 29, 2026, includes $29.5 billion for Electric Boat to construct five additional Columbia-class ballistic-missile submarines. It also includes $42.1 billion for nine Virginia-class fast-attack submarines covering fiscal years 2025 through 2029, with Electric Boat sharing that construction work with HII-Newport News Shipbuilding in Virginia.

The combined total also incorporates approximately $5 billion for shipyard infrastructure and productivity improvements. Electric Boat plans projects supporting its shipyards in Groton and Quonset Point, Rhode Island, along with redevelopment of the former Crystal Mall in Waterford into offices, laboratories and training space. The company expects the Waterford campus to open in mid-2027.

Electric Boat employs more than 27,000 people across Connecticut and Rhode Island and is maintaining its goal of hiring 8,000 workers during 2026. Rayha said more than 4,000 employees had already been hired by late July. The company expects the contracts to provide steadier demand for its regional suppliers while it works toward eventually delivering two Virginia-class submarines and one Columbia-class submarine annually.

The Navy said the awards bring the number of Columbia-class submarines under contract to seven. Columbia-class vessels are intended to replace the aging Ohio-class fleet as the sea-based component of the nation's nuclear deterrent, while Virginia-class submarines perform intelligence, surveillance, strike and other tactical missions.

Construction schedules for the newly contracted submarines were not immediately disclosed, and Electric Boat continues working through earlier production groups for both classes.

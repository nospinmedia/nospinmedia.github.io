🥗 New Hampshire Added To CDC's Lettuce Parasite OutbreakCyclospora investigation expands to 15 states after six-state update 👇

HealthAugust 6, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 6, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention has added New Hampshire to its official multistate Cyclospora outbreak investigation, marking the first time the CDC has officially linked illnesses in New Hampshire to the lettuce-related outbreak. The August 5, 2026 update expanded the investigation from 9 to 15 states after investigators connected additional illnesses through laboratory testing, patient interviews, and food traceback.

The six newly added states are Arkansas, Iowa, Missouri, Nebraska, New Hampshire, and North Carolina. The official outbreak now includes Arkansas, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Hampshire, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, and West Virginia. Although recalled lettuce had previously been distributed into New Hampshire, this is the first CDC confirmation linking illnesses in the state to the multistate outbreak.

Federal investigators continue tracing the outbreak to shredded iceberg lettuce supplied by Taylor Farms de Mexico, which was served at Taco Bell restaurants and distributed to other food-service customers. The company issued a voluntary recall in July 2026, and officials say the affected product is no longer being shipped. However, new states may continue to be added because confirming Cyclospora infections often takes several weeks.

Health officials advise anyone experiencing prolonged diarrhea, stomach cramps, fatigue, or loss of appetite after eating fresh produce to seek medical evaluation, as laboratory confirmation is needed before illnesses can be linked to the outbreak. The CDC and Food and Drug Administration continue investigating whether additional illnesses or locations should be added as more evidence becomes available.

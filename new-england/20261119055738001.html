🦌 Maine hunter who vanished in 1975 was killed, investigators sayPolice identify now-deceased suspect as search for father's remains continues 👇

LegalNovember 19, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 19, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Maine investigators say they have determined who killed 25-year-old Ludger Belanger, a married father of three young daughters who disappeared while deer hunting more than 50 years ago, but the identified suspect has since died and Belanger's remains have still not been found.

Belanger disappeared on the morning of November 25, 1975. His wife and brother dropped him off near his home along Route 105 in Washington, Maine, in Knox County, so he could hunt deer. He never returned.

The Maine State Police Major Crimes Unit – Unsolved, working with the Maine Attorney General's Office, announced November 18 that investigators now consider Belanger's disappearance a homicide and have identified the person responsible for his death. Authorities are not publicly releasing the suspect's identity because that person is deceased.

The announcement follows an intensified review of the cold case during 2025 and 2026. Investigators conducted additional interviews and followed leads across multiple Maine communities and other states. Detectives also searched wooded areas, bodies of water and wells while attempting to determine what happened to Belanger and locate his remains.

The finding resolves two major questions that remained unanswered for decades: investigators now say Belanger was killed and that they know who was responsible. Because the identified suspect is dead, however, there will be no criminal prosecution unless authorities uncover evidence implicating another person.

One crucial part of the case remains unresolved. Police are still trying to find Belanger's body, and investigators say their work will continue in hopes of returning his remains to his family.

Belanger's disappearance left his wife and three daughters without answers for more than half a century. The new findings provide the family with an explanation for his disappearance, but without his remains, investigators say the search is not finished.

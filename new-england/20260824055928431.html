🗳️ Boston advances to final three for 2028 Democratic conventionDNC narrows host city search ahead of final selection 👇

EconomyAugust 24, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 24, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Boston has advanced to the final round of bidding to host the 2028 Democratic National Convention, with the Democratic National Committee narrowing its list of candidates to Boston, Denver, and Philadelphia. DNC Chair Ken Martin announced that Atlanta and Chicago were eliminated following months of site evaluations, leaving the remaining cities to compete for the party's presidential nominating convention scheduled for August 7-10, 2028.

Boston's proposal centers on TD Garden as the convention arena, with related events planned at the Boston Convention and Exhibition Center. The local bid is being led by Boston 2028 President Steve Kerrigan, who said the city offers the facilities, transportation network, hotel capacity, and experience needed to host one of the nation's largest political events. Massachusetts lawmakers, including U.S. Reps. Ayanna Pressley and Jim McGovern, welcomed Boston's selection as a finalist, calling it an opportunity to showcase the region while generating significant economic activity.

The DNC's selection process included technical reviews, fundraising assessments, security planning, and site visits to all five applicant cities earlier this year. Boston last hosted the Democratic National Convention in 2004, when the event attracted tens of thousands of visitors and delivered an estimated $150 million in regional economic impact. If chosen again, the convention would bring delegates, media organizations, campaign staff, and visitors from across the country to Massachusetts during the height of the presidential election season.

The Democratic National Convention Committee will continue evaluating each finalist's venues, hotel capacity, transportation plans, public safety coordination, and fundraising commitments before recommending a host city. Party officials expect to announce the winning bid before the end of 2026, while the Republican National Committee has already selected Houston to host its 2028 national convention.

🔥 Teenager unaccounted for after three-alarm Montpelier apartment fireThree units heavily damaged as investigators examine cause 👇

HealthNovember 25, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 25, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

A teenager remained unaccounted for Tuesday evening after a three-alarm fire tore through part of a public apartment complex in Montpelier, Vermont, heavily damaging three adjoining units and displacing residents.

Firefighters were called around 2:30 p.m. Tuesday, November 24, to the Cummings Street Apartments at 23 Cummings St., a 20-unit complex managed by the Montpelier Housing Authority. The roofs of three adjoining apartments collapsed or were severely damaged as crews worked to contain the fire.

Fire departments from several surrounding communities assisted the Montpelier Fire Department, while Vermont State Police, Vermont Hazmat and Green Mountain Power also responded. Authorities closed Elm Street/Route 12 and nearby roads during the emergency response. Two people appeared to have been transported from the scene by ambulance, while one teenager remained unaccounted for into the evening.

Witnesses described hearing a loud bang or explosion shortly before seeing flames. Residents said the building shook, and fire spread through the apartments and into nearby brush. The most severe damage appeared to be around Unit 2, where windows were blown out and the interior was heavily blackened. Authorities had not established the cause of the fire.

One resident, Julie Griffin, told VTDigger she escaped with her dog after hearing someone calling for help, but said her belongings were lost when flames spread into her apartment.

Another resident told The Montpelier Bridge that she had previously reported smelling propane near Unit 2 to Montpelier Housing Authority Director Jo Ann Troiano. Troiano confirmed that she had responded to earlier concerns but said detectors in the apartments, including four in Unit 2, had not indicated a problem. She declined additional comment while the investigation was beginning.

The reported explosion and previous propane concerns have not been established as the cause of the fire, and investigators had not announced an official cause Tuesday night.

What investigators determine caused the fire — and what happened to the missing teenager — remain the central unanswered questions.

🌉 Manchester closes Queen City Bridge over structural concernsRoute 3 crossing over Merrimack River shut down until further notice 👇

HealthSeptember 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 22, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

The Queen City Bridge in Manchester, New Hampshire, remains closed in both directions after inspectors identified potential structural deficiencies during a routine inspection, cutting off a prominent Route 3 crossing over the Merrimack River. City officials said the bridge will remain closed until further notice as a safety precaution while inspectors determine the extent of the problem.

The New Hampshire Department of Transportation notified Manchester's Department of Public Works on Monday, September 21, after the potential deficiencies were discovered. Officials said a full inspection had not yet been completed when the closure was announced, and the city has not specified what part of the structure prompted the concern or how long the shutdown could last. Manchester police advised motorists to seek alternate routes.

The closure affects an important connection across the city. The Queen City Bridge carries Route 3 across the Merrimack River, linking traffic with Elm Street on the east side and Second Street on the west side. The crossing also provides access to the Elliot Hospital at River's Edge area, making the indefinite closure potentially significant for commuters and other travelers moving through Manchester.

The bridge is 1,186 feet long and was built in 1923, according to state information reported by New Hampshire Public Radio. Its most recent rehabilitation was completed in 2020. A previous inspection was conducted in 2024.

Manchester officials said they will provide additional information and traffic plans once the inspection is completed. Mayor Jay Ruais said he has been in contact with Gov. Kelly Ayotte and the city's Public Works director as officials monitor the situation. As of Tuesday morning, the city continued to list the bridge as closed to traffic in both directions until further notice.

🚨 Susan Collins' Biddeford office vandalized with vulgar messageDemocratic opponent Troy Jackson condemns the act 👇

EconomySeptember 29, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 29, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

U.S. Sen. Susan Collins' constituent service office in Biddeford, Maine, was vandalized with a vulgar message painted across its glass doors Monday, prompting a police response and condemnation from both Collins' staff and her Democratic opponent in November's Senate election.

The graffiti, painted in red, included an obscenity linking the Republican senator to President Donald Trump. Biddeford Police Chief JoAnne Fisk confirmed that police received a call about the vandalism but did not provide additional details about the investigation or identify a suspect.

Collins' chief of staff, Katie Brown, condemned the vandalism and said it would not disrupt the senator's work. Brown said Collins and her staff would not be intimidated and that the Biddeford constituent service office remained open to the public.

The incident comes during an increasingly heated Senate campaign between Collins and Democratic nominee Troy Jackson, a former Maine Senate president. Jackson also condemned the vandalism, saying there was no place for intimidation or property destruction in Maine and that Collins and her staff should be able to do their jobs without their office being defaced.

The Biddeford office has previously been the site of protests, including demonstrations following the July fatal shooting of a man by an Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer in the city. Police said, however, that the office had not recently experienced similar vandalism.

The vandalism also comes as Collins' relationship with Trump has become a major issue in the Senate campaign. Collins has supported many of the president's policies while also breaking with him on some high-profile votes and actions. Jackson and other Democrats have sought to tie Collins more closely to Trump, while Collins has emphasized instances in which she has opposed the administration.

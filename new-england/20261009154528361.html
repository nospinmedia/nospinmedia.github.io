🚨 Son captured in Vermont after mother of three found shot dead in MassachusettsPolice had warned 22-year-old could be armed and dangerous during multistate search 👇

LegalOctober 9, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 9, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A 22-year-old Massachusetts man who became the subject of a multistate search after his mother was found fatally shot inside her Reading home has been taken into custody in Vermont.

Authorities identified the suspect as Ryan Hanafin, the son of 49-year-old Andria Hanafin, a mother of three who was discovered suffering from an apparent gunshot wound at her home on Center Avenue in Reading, Massachusetts.

According to Middlesex District Attorney Marian Ryan and Reading Police Chief David Clark, police were called to the residence at approximately 12:38 a.m. Thursday after Hanafin's husband discovered her. Emergency responders pronounced her dead, and investigators began treating the death as a homicide.

Police soon began searching for Ryan Hanafin and issued a public warning that he could be armed and dangerous. Authorities also sought a black 2010 Dodge Charger associated with him, expanding the search beyond Reading as investigators attempted to determine where he had gone after the shooting.

The search ultimately reached Vermont, where Vermont State Police located Hanafin and took him into custody. He was arrested as a fugitive from justice, allowing authorities to hold him while Massachusetts officials pursue his return to the state.

The arrest ended the immediate public safety concern surrounding the search, but the investigation into Andria Hanafin's death remains active. Authorities have not yet publicly detailed what they believe led to the shooting or disclosed the evidence connecting Ryan Hanafin to his mother's death.

A fugitive-from-justice arrest is not itself a determination that Hanafin committed the homicide. Additional criminal charges could follow as Massachusetts investigators complete their work and prosecutors determine what charges are supported by the evidence. Hanafin is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

The case crossed state lines within hours, requiring coordination among Massachusetts investigators and Vermont authorities. Officials are expected to release additional information as extradition proceedings and the homicide investigation move forward.

For Reading residents, the arrest brings an end to an urgent search that began with a fatal shooting inside a residential neighborhood and quickly expanded across New England.

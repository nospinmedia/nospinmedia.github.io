🏈 Drake Maye reportedly expected to be out 'a while' after ankle injuryPatriots have bye week before Chargers as quarterback awaits further testing 👇

HealthNovember 16, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 16, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye is reportedly expected to be sidelined for a period of time after injuring his right ankle during Sunday's 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich, although the team has not announced a timetable for his return.

Maye was injured on the 10th play of the game when Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson sacked him. Maye initially remained in the game but was later evaluated in the medical tent before being taken to the locker room and ruled out. Hutchinson also suffered a knee injury while celebrating after the sack and was ruled out.

Head coach Mike Vrabel said Maye would undergo additional testing after the team returned to New England. A subsequent MassLive report said the quarterback is expected to be out "a while," but the severity of the injury and an official recovery timeline remain uncertain pending further evaluation.

Backup quarterback Tommy DeVito replaced Maye against Detroit and is in line to start if Maye cannot play, with Behren Morton available behind him.

The Patriots enter their Week 11 bye before facing the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 12, giving Maye additional recovery time before New England's next game.

The loss dropped the Patriots to 5-5 and added to mounting offensive injuries. Wide receiver A.J. Brown exited the game with a hamstring injury, while running back TreVeyon Henderson was placed on injured reserve Saturday before the game.

Maye's availability could have significant implications for the second half of New England's season. An extended absence would leave the Patriots relying on DeVito while trying to remain in playoff contention.

The Patriots are expected to have a clearer picture of Maye's status after additional imaging and medical evaluation.

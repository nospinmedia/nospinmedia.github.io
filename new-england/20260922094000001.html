📞 Mass. audit finds long waits and delayed unemployment benefitsMore than half of callers waited over 30 minutes for help 👇

EconomySeptember 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 22, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

A new state audit found serious problems with the Massachusetts Department of Unemployment Assistance, including long call-center waits, delayed benefit payments and inadequate access to in-person help. State Auditor Diana DiZoglio's office found that more than half of callers reviewed were kept on hold for more than 30 minutes, while about 24% of all callers waited more than an hour before reaching an agent or receiving an automated voice prompt.

The audit found the department had not established formal performance standards for its call center or documented procedures for monitoring whether callers received timely assistance. Call-log data covering August 26, 2024, through August 26, 2025 was used because the agency had not retained older call records required for the original review period.

Auditors also found that DUA failed to meet federal timeliness standards for initial unemployment payments and violated state law by failing to provide required in-person assistance at three regional offices. With face-to-face assistance available only at the Boston Re-Employment Center, some claimants elsewhere in Massachusetts had to rely on the same telephone and online systems auditors found were experiencing problems.

DUA says conditions have improved since the periods examined. The agency told the Boston Globe that current call-center waits have fallen to about 4 to 11 minutes. It also said nearly 70% of claims were processed within 30 days in July 2026, compared with about 53% during the summer and fall of 2025. The auditor's office said it plans to follow up on its recommendations in approximately six months.

The audit recommended establishing measurable call-center standards, routinely reviewing wait-time and abandoned-call data, improving payment processing and ensuring required in-person assistance is available at regional offices.

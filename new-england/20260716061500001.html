🚨 Fugitive Arrested In North CarolinaBrockton Man Faces 2017 Boston Murder Charge 👇

LegalJuly 16, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published July 16, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

A 40-year-old Brockton man wanted in connection with a 2017 Boston homicide has been arrested in Charlotte, North Carolina, following a joint investigation involving the Boston Police Fugitive Unit, Massachusetts State Police, and the U.S. Marshals Service. Authorities identified the suspect as Alan Marcus Lewis, who was taken into custody as part of the U.S. Marshals' Operation 250th, a multi-agency fugitive initiative that has resulted in the arrests of more than 110 violent fugitives. Lewis is awaiting extradition to Massachusetts, where he will face a murder charge.

The arrest stems from the fatal shooting of 37-year-old Brent Stevenson on November 6, 2017, near the intersection of Massachusetts Avenue and Boylston Street in Boston. Investigators said Stevenson attempted to drive away after being shot but crashed his vehicle a short distance later because of his injuries. Authorities identified Lewis as a suspect during the homicide investigation, and he was later indicted by Suffolk Superior Court on a charge of murder.

Officials credited the successful arrest to the coordinated efforts of local, state, and federal law enforcement agencies. The U.S. Marshals Service had previously offered a $5,000 reward for information leading to Lewis' capture while investigators continued pursuing leads over the years. Authorities have not released additional details about the circumstances that ultimately led investigators to locate him in North Carolina.

The arrest marks a significant breakthrough in a long-running homicide investigation that remained active for nearly nine years. Lewis is expected to be returned to Suffolk County to face prosecution, while Boston police emphasized that unresolved violent crime investigations remain active regardless of how much time has passed.

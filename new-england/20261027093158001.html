⚖️ Maine fines Bobby Charles campaign $22,658 over contributionsEthics panel found $45,316 was accepted above state limits 👇

PoliticsOctober 27, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published October 27, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Maine's campaign finance regulators have fined Republican gubernatorial nominee Bobby Charles' campaign $22,657.99 after finding it accepted more than $45,000 in contributions above the state's individual donation limit.

The Maine Commission on Governmental Ethics and Election Practices voted 5-0 to find that the campaign violated state law by receiving $45,315.98 in over-the-limit contributions. Commissioners then voted 4-1 to impose a penalty equal to 50% of the excess contributions. Republican Commissioner Dennis Keschl opposed setting the penalty immediately, saying he wanted more information about steps the campaign had taken to prevent future problems.

Maine limits individual contributions to a gubernatorial candidate to $2,075 per election. Commission staff identified dozens of contributions exceeding that limit. Staff said it was unusual to find a gubernatorial campaign with so many excessive contributions during a reporting period.

The case began after the Maine Democratic Party filed a complaint in early October. Charles' campaign subsequently returned the excess money to donors and amended its campaign finance reports. The campaign did not dispute that it had accepted over-the-limit contributions but opposed the size and timing of the penalty.

Campaign treasurer Bill Riley told commissioners that a national compliance consultant had filed reports despite his warnings about excessive contributions. An attorney representing the campaign said it had worked diligently with regulators and requested additional time to respond, including delaying a final decision until after the November 3 election.

Commissioners rejected delaying the matter. Several said returning the money did not eliminate the original violation and that a meaningful penalty was necessary to deter similar violations by other campaigns.

Charles is running against Democrat Hannah Pingree and independent Rick Bennett in the race to succeed Gov. Janet Mills.

⚖️ Vermont jury convicts man who shot three Palestinian studentsJason Eaton found guilty on three attempted-murder counts 👇

IllustrationIllustration representing court rulings and legal proceedings.Credit: AI-generated illustration
EducationSeptember 22, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 22, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

A Vermont jury has convicted Jason Eaton, 51, on three counts of attempted second-degree murder for shooting three Palestinian college students in Burlington in November 2023, rejecting his defense that he was legally insane at the time of the attack.

The victims — Hisham Awartani, Kinnan Abdalhamid and Tahseen Aliahmad — were all 20 years old when they were shot on November 25, 2023. They were visiting Awartani's grandmother for Thanksgiving and walking near the University of Vermont campus while speaking a mix of English and Arabic. Two were wearing traditional Palestinian keffiyehs. Awartani was left paralyzed, while Abdalhamid and Aliahmad were also wounded.

Eaton did not dispute that he was the shooter. His attorneys argued that he should be found not guilty by reason of insanity, presenting evidence that he believed U.S. and Israeli intelligence agencies were communicating with him through FM radio and directing his actions. Prosecutors argued that Eaton understood his actions were criminal. After a weeklong trial in Chittenden Superior Court, jurors deliberated for about two hours before rejecting the insanity defense and returning guilty verdicts on all three counts.

The shootings drew international attention and prompted calls for hate-crime charges. Eaton was not charged with a hate crime, although prosecutors argued during the trial that prejudice played a role in the attack. The victims' families said after the verdict that they believed the shooting was motivated by anti-Palestinian hatred.

Eaton was ordered jailed pending sentencing, which has not yet been scheduled. Each attempted second-degree murder conviction carries the possibility of a life sentence, and prosecutors said the presumptive minimum is 20 years on each count.

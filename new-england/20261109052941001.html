🚨 Body found along walking trail in Palmer, MassachusettsPolice investigate unattended death but say foul play is not suspected 👇

HealthNovember 9, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published November 9, 2026 at 12:30 PM ET

Police are investigating after a person was found dead Saturday morning near a walking trail and pavilion in Palmer, Massachusetts, prompting authorities to close the area for several hours while investigators examined the scene.

The Palmer Police Department said emergency crews were notified at about 9 a.m. Saturday that an unresponsive person was lying near the walking trail off First Street. Police and firefighters responded and determined that the person had died. The individual's identity has not been publicly released.

The trail and nearby pavilion were temporarily closed as Palmer police investigated with assistance from Massachusetts State Police detectives. The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner responded to the scene and removed the body. Authorities reopened the area after investigators completed their work there.

Police described the case as an unattended death investigation and said there was no threat to the public. Authorities also said foul play was not suspected based on the information available during the initial investigation. No arrests or criminal charges have been announced.

The medical examiner's office will work to determine the person's cause and manner of death. Police have not released additional information about the circumstances surrounding the death or said how long the person may have been near the trail before being discovered.

An additional report initially grouped with this story described a woman who allegedly was killed by her husband after sending her sister a message saying she was "not good." That case occurred in Quitman, Texas, and is unrelated to the Palmer death. The Massachusetts case remains an unattended-death investigation, and available local reporting does not support describing it as a homicide.

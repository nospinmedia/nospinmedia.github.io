🚨 Lakeville man killed during exchange of gunfire with policeBody cameras captured encounter after family requested well-being check 👇

HealthSeptember 28, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 28, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Massachusetts State Police are investigating after Lakeville police officers shot and killed 31-year-old Gregory Buckley during an exchange of gunfire inside a home Saturday afternoon. The confrontation began after a family member went to the Lakeville Police Department at approximately 1:58 p.m. on September 26 and requested a well-being check, telling police Buckley had a firearm and was "disturbed and acting erratically."

According to the Plymouth County District Attorney's Office, Lakeville officers went with the reporting family member to the residence at 8 Heritage Hill Drive. The family member allowed officers inside. When police reached a second-floor bedroom, authorities say they encountered Buckley pointing a firearm at them.

Police ordered Buckley to drop the weapon, but authorities say he refused. Gunfire was then exchanged and Buckley was struck. Officials have not publicly detailed who fired first or how many shots were fired. Officers immediately provided first aid before Buckley was transported to Morton Hospital in Taunton, where he was pronounced dead.

Four Lakeville police officers were also taken to a hospital for evaluation and later released. Authorities have not reported that any of the officers suffered gunshot wounds. Police said there was no continuing threat to the public following the confrontation.

The encounter was recorded by the officers' body-worn cameras, according to District Attorney Timothy Cruz's office. Massachusetts State Police detectives assigned to the Plymouth County District Attorney's Office are conducting the investigation into the fatal shooting. Authorities had not announced conclusions about the officers' use of force as of Sunday, and the investigation remained ongoing.

The case leaves investigators with body-camera footage and other evidence to reconstruct the sequence of events inside the home before the fatal exchange of gunfire.

🚨 Cambridge Police Shooting Leaves Armed Man DeadMiddlesex DA Opens Investigation Into Officer-Involved Shooting 👇

IllustrationPolice station exteriorCredit: AI-generated illustration (No Spin Media)
HealthAugust 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 23, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

The Middlesex District Attorney's Office is investigating a fatal officer-involved shooting after Cambridge Police shot and killed a man during an encounter in Central Square on the evening of August 21. According to a joint statement from District Attorney Marian Ryan and Cambridge Police Commissioner Pauline Wells, officers responded shortly after 8 p.m. to a report of suspicious activity near Temple Street and Massachusetts Avenue. Authorities said a man believed to be in his 60s exited a vehicle and pointed a handgun at responding officers, prompting an attempt to physically disarm him before officers fired their weapons. The man was pronounced dead at the scene despite immediate medical aid, and investigators said a loaded firearm was recovered nearby.

Officials said several responding officers were taken to local hospitals for evaluation and were later released. No officers were reported to have suffered life-threatening injuries. Authorities have not publicly identified the man pending notification of family members. The incident occurred in a busy commercial area of Central Square, where portions of the neighborhood were temporarily closed while investigators documented the scene and collected evidence.

As required under Massachusetts law, the investigation is being led by the Middlesex District Attorney's Office with assistance from the Massachusetts State Police Detective Unit assigned to the district attorney. Investigators are reviewing body-worn camera footage, interviewing witnesses, and examining forensic evidence to determine the sequence of events leading to the shooting. Officials have not announced when the body-camera footage may be released or whether additional information will be made public during the investigation.

The shooting is the latest officer-involved fatality in Cambridge to receive independent review under state protocol. Authorities emphasized that the investigation remains active and urged the public to avoid drawing conclusions until all available evidence has been analyzed.

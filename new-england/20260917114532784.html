🦠 Maine confirms new measles case in unvaccinated adultWaterville clinic visitors warned of possible exposure 👇

HealthSeptember 17, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 17, 2026 at 8:45 AM ET

Maine health officials have confirmed a new case of measles in an unvaccinated adult from Somerset County and are warning people who visited a Waterville medical clinic that they may have been exposed. The case, confirmed September 15 and announced the following day, is Maine's first new measles case since a five-case cluster was reported in February.

The Maine Center for Disease Control and Prevention said anyone who was at MaineGeneral Express Care, 211 Main Street in Waterville, on September 12 between 8 a.m. and 11 a.m. may have been exposed. Those individuals are being advised to review their vaccination history and monitor for symptoms for 21 days after the potential exposure. The exposure information could change as the investigation continues.

Measles is a highly contagious viral disease spread through the air when an infected person coughs or sneezes. Maine CDC says about 90% of people who are not immune and are close to someone with measles will become infected. Symptoms can include fever, cough, runny nose, red or watery eyes and a rash that spreads from the head downward. The virus can also cause serious complications including pneumonia and encephalitis.

Health officials are asking anyone who develops symptoms to contact a health care provider before arriving at a doctor's office or hospital, allowing medical staff to take precautions that can reduce exposure to other patients.

The latest infection brings Maine's confirmed 2026 measles total to six cases. Before this year, Maine had not reported a confirmed measles case since 2019. Nationally, the U.S. CDC had recorded 3,294 confirmed cases in 2026 through September 11.

Maine CDC says vaccination remains the best protection against measles. Two doses of the MMR vaccine are about 97% effective at preventing the disease, while one dose is about 93% effective.

People who were at the Waterville clinic during the identified exposure period should monitor for symptoms and contact a health care provider if they become ill.

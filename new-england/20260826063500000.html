⚖️ Opening Statements Begin in Suffolk Sheriff Tompkins TrialFederal extortion case centers on disputed $50,000 cannabis investment 👇

EducationAugust 26, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published August 26, 2026 at 6:30 PM ET

Opening statements have begun in the federal criminal trial of Suffolk County Sheriff Steven W. Tompkins, marking the start of what is expected to be a two-week proceeding in U.S. District Court in Boston. Tompkins faces two counts of extortion under color of official right, charges that carry a maximum penalty of 20 years in prison per count if he is convicted. He has pleaded not guilty.

Federal prosecutors allege Tompkins improperly used the authority of his office to obtain a $50,000 pre-public investment opportunity in Ascend Wellness Holdings, a cannabis company connected to political operative Frank Perullo. Prosecutors contend Tompkins later pressured Perullo to fully reimburse the investment after the company's stock declined, threatening to end a prisoner re-entry partnership between the Suffolk County Sheriff's Department and the cannabis company if he was not repaid.

Defense attorney Martin G. Weinberg told jurors the investment was a lawful private business transaction between longtime associates and argued there was no criminal coercion. Following opening statements, prosecutors called Ascend co-founder Abner Kurtin as one of the trial's first witnesses to testify about the investment and the company's relationship with the sheriff's office. The prisoner re-entry partnership was part of the company's social equity obligations under Massachusetts Cannabis Control Commission regulations.

Tompkins, who has served as Suffolk County sheriff since 2013, stepped aside from his official duties after being indicted in 2025, with Assistant Superintendent Mark Lawhorne assuming day-to-day leadership of the department. The outcome of the trial could have significant implications for one of Massachusetts' largest county law enforcement agencies and is expected to be closely followed throughout the region.

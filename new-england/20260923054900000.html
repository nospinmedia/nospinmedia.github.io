⚖️ ‘Clavicular’ charged with rape on Cape CodInfluencer accused of assaulting 17-year-old girl 👇

LegalSeptember 23, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 23, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

Social media influencer Braden Peters, known online as Clavicular, has been charged in Massachusetts with rape and other offenses stemming from an alleged encounter with a 17-year-old girl at his family's Cape Cod home. Peters, 20, faces charges of rape, administering a substance for sexual intercourse and procuring alcohol for a person under 21, according to court records.

The charges concern an alleged May 23, 2025, assault in Chatham, Massachusetts. A clerk-magistrate found probable cause for the charges on September 4, and a criminal complaint was issued September 8. Peters is not in custody and is scheduled to be arraigned October 14 in Orleans District Court.

According to a police report, the teenager told Chatham police that she met Peters through Instagram and was later paid to appear in his online "looksmaxxing" videos. She alleged that Peters knew she was 17 but instructed her to tell viewers she was 18. She said she traveled to his family's home on Morris Island, where Peters was livestreaming, and drank what she believed was two or three shots of vodka that he provided. She told investigators she became increasingly intoxicated and later fell asleep before awakening to Peters sexually assaulting her.

The allegations had previously surfaced in a civil lawsuit filed against Peters in Florida in April. That lawsuit also alleges Peters later injected the teenager with a fat-dissolving substance during a livestream without her consent. Peters has denied the allegations in the civil lawsuit. His publicist did not immediately respond to a request for comment about the criminal charges Tuesday.

Peters has developed a large social-media following through content centered on "looksmaxxing," an online trend focused on efforts to improve physical appearance. He regularly livestreams and has built audiences on platforms including TikTok, Instagram and Kick.

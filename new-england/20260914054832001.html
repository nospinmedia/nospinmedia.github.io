🏈 A.J. Brown sidelined with severe ankle sprainPatriots receiver now expected to miss at least six weeks 👇

EconomySeptember 14, 2026By No Spin Media Newsroom · Published September 14, 2026 at 3:45 PM ET

New England Patriots wide receiver A.J. Brown is expected to miss at least six weeks with a high-ankle sprain suffered in the team's season-opening loss to the Seattle Seahawks, extending his projected absence beyond the minimum four games required by his placement on injured reserve. NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported on September 13, 2026, that Brown's injury is on the more severe end of the recovery range, although surgery is not expected.

Brown injured his right ankle during the third quarter of New England's 13-10 loss in Seattle on September 9. He was attempting to catch a third-down pass from quarterback Drake Maye when Seahawks cornerback Nehemiah Pritchett came down across his lower leg. Brown left the game and did not return. Initial X-rays showed no fracture, while additional evaluation determined he had suffered a high-ankle sprain.

Before leaving the game, Brown caught three passes for 26 yards in his Patriots debut. He also drew a 34-yard defensive pass interference penalty that helped set up New England's only touchdown.

The Patriots placed Brown on injured reserve, automatically ruling him out for at least four games. The updated six-week projection means his absence is now expected to extend beyond that minimum, although New England has not announced a specific date for his return. His rehabilitation and how the ankle responds will determine when he can resume football activities.

With Brown unavailable, the Patriots will have to rely more heavily on receivers Mack Hollins, Kyle Williams and Romeo Doubs as Maye and the offense move through the opening portion of the schedule. Brown, 29, joined New England during the offseason in a trade with the Philadelphia Eagles and was expected to serve as one of Maye's primary receiving targets.
